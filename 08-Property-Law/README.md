# Property Law
# 財產法

## What this course is about
## 本課程內容

Begin after you understand ownership, agreements, and civil disputes.
先理解所有權、協議及民事爭議，再開始本課。

This is an introductory course guide, not a complete textbook or legal opinion.
本頁是入門課程指南，並非完整教科書或法律意見。

## Start with three ideas
## 從三個概念開始

### 1. Ownership and possession
### 1. 所有權與占有

Learn the difference between owning property and physically possessing it.
學習擁有財產與實際占有財產之間的差別。

### 2. Land and leases
### 2. 土地與租賃

Study basic landlord-tenant relationships and property interests.
學習房東與房客關係及基本財產權益。

### 3. Property limits
### 3. 財產權限制

Understand that property rights operate within statutes and constitutional rules.
理解財產權仍受到制定法及憲法規則限制。


## Beginner practice
## 初學者練習

Explain the difference between owning a bicycle and borrowing a bicycle.
解釋擁有一輛自行車與借用一輛自行車的差別。

## How to continue
## 如何繼續學習

Study one topic at a time and write down unfamiliar terms.
一次只學習一個主題，並記錄不熟悉的術語。

For every case, identify the court, jurisdiction, date, issue, rule, holding, and reasoning.
閱讀每一宗判例時，辨識法院、司法管轄區、日期、爭點、規則、裁判要旨及推理。

Always verify the law that applies to the relevant jurisdiction and date.
務必核實相關司法管轄區及日期所適用的法律。

**僅供教育學習，不構成法律意見。**
