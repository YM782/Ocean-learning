# Property Law
# 財產法

**Level: beginner | Focus: ownership, possession, land, and property rights**
**程度：初學者｜重點：所有權、占有、土地及財產權利**

Property law studies legal rights in land and personal property. In the United States, much property law is state law, so rules and terminology can vary.
財產法研究土地及個人財產上的法律權利。在美國，大部分財產法屬於州法，因此規則及術語可能有所不同。
