# Landlord-Tenant Law and Property Transfers
# 房東租客法及財產轉讓

## 1. The lease relationship
## 1. 租賃關係

A lease gives a tenant a possessory interest in property for a defined period or under a defined arrangement, in exchange for rent or other consideration.
租約使租客在特定期間或安排下取得財產占有權，通常以支付租金或其他對價作為交換。

A lease may create contractual duties and property-law rights. The landlord's and tenant's obligations are affected by the lease, state statutes, and local law.
租約可能同時產生契約義務及財產法權利。房東與租客的義務會受到租約、州制定法及地方規則影響。

## 2. Common leasehold forms
## 2. 常見租賃權益類型

A fixed-term tenancy lasts for a stated period. A periodic tenancy renews for recurring periods under the agreement or law. A tenancy at will may continue while both sides permit it, subject to applicable termination rules.
定期租賃持續至約定期限。週期性租賃依協議或法律按固定週期續期。任意租賃可能在雙方允許時持續，但仍須遵守適用終止規則。

## 3. Duties and remedies
## 3. 義務及救濟

A landlord may have duties involving possession, habitability, repairs, and compliance with housing statutes. A tenant may have duties to pay rent, avoid waste, and comply with lawful lease terms.
房東可能負有交付占有、維持適居性、修繕及遵守住房制定法等義務。租客可能負有支付租金、避免損害財產及遵守合法租約條款等義務。

The implied warranty of habitability is recognized in many states for residential leases, but its scope, remedies, and exceptions vary.
許多州在住宅租賃中承認默示適居性保證，但其範圍、救濟及例外可能不同。

## 4. Deeds and transfer
## 4. 契據及財產轉讓

A deed is a formal instrument used to transfer an interest in real property. State law governs deed requirements, delivery, acceptance, recording, and related issues.
契據是用來轉讓不動產權益的正式文件。州法規範契據要求、交付、接受、登記及相關問題。

A deed may contain warranties about title, but the type and extent of those warranties depend on the deed form and law.
契據可能包含有關產權的保證，但保證類型及範圍取決於契據形式及法律。

## 5. Recording systems
## 5. 不動產登記制度

Recording statutes help determine priority among competing claims to property. States use different recording systems and notice rules.
不動產登記制定法有助於決定相互競爭的財產權利主張之間的優先順序。各州採用不同登記制度及通知規則。

Recording a document does not necessarily cure every defect in the transfer. A buyer or lawyer must examine the chain of title and applicable statute.
登記文件不一定能補正轉讓中的所有瑕疵。買方或律師必須檢查產權鏈及適用制定法。

## 6. Mortgages and security interests
## 6. 抵押及擔保權益

A mortgage or deed of trust commonly secures repayment of a loan with real property. If the borrower defaults, the lender may seek foreclosure under state law and the loan documents.
抵押或信託契據通常以不動產作為貸款還款擔保。如果借款人違約，貸款人可能依州法及貸款文件尋求止贖。

Foreclosure procedures, notice, redemption rights, and deficiency judgments vary significantly among states.
止贖程序、通知、贖回權及不足額判決在各州之間可能有很大差異。

   租約只產生契約，還是也可能產生財產權益？
