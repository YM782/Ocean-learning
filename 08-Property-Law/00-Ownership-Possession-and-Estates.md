# Ownership, Possession, and Estates
# 所有權、占有及土地權益

## 1. What is property?
## 1. 什麼是財產？

Property is not merely a physical object. It is a collection of legally recognized rights concerning an object or resource, such as the rights to possess, use, exclude others, transfer, or receive income.
財產不只是實體物品。它是一組法律承認的權利，涉及某項物品或資源，例如占有、使用、排除他人、轉讓或取得收益的權利。

Different people may hold different rights in the same property. A landlord may own a building while a tenant has a lawful right to possess an apartment under a lease.
不同的人可能對同一財產享有不同權利。房東可能擁有建築物，而租客則依租約合法占有其中一間公寓。

## 2. Real property and personal property
## 2. 不動產及動產

Real property generally includes land and certain interests attached to land. Personal property generally includes movable property and intangible rights.
不動產通常包括土地及附著於土地的某些權益。動產通常包括可移動財產及無形權利。

The classification can matter for transfers, taxation, security interests, and remedies.
財產分類可能影響轉讓、稅務、擔保權益及法律救濟。

## 3. Possession
## 3. 占有

Possession is a legally significant relationship of control over property. Possession and ownership are related but not identical.
占有是對財產具有法律意義的控制關係。占有與所有權有關，但並不相同。

A person may possess property without owning it, and an owner may have legal title while another person has lawful possession.
一個人可能占有財產但並非所有人；所有人可能持有法律所有權，而另一個人則合法占有該財產。

## 4. Estates in land
## 4. 土地權益類型

An estate in land describes the nature and duration of a person's interest in real property.
土地權益用來描述一個人對不動產所享有權益的性質及期限。

A fee simple estate is generally the broadest inheritable ownership interest recognized by common law. A life estate lasts for the life of a specified person and then passes according to the instrument or applicable law.
Fee simple estate（完全所有權）通常是普通法承認範圍最廣、可繼承的不動產所有權。Life estate（終身土地權益）則持續至特定人士死亡，之後依文件或適用法律轉移。

Future interests identify who may receive possession or ownership after a present estate ends. Their names and rules can be technical.
未來權益用來辨識目前土地權益終止後，誰可能取得占有或所有權。相關名稱及規則可能相當技術性。

## 5. Concurrent ownership
## 5. 共同所有權

Two or more people may own property together. Common forms include tenancy in common and joint tenancy; some states also recognize tenancy by the entirety for certain married couples.
兩人或多人可以共同擁有財產。常見形式包括按份共有及共同共有；某些州也承認特定已婚夫妻之間的夫妻整體共有。

The rights to transfer, inherit, partition, and manage the property depend on the form of ownership and state law.
轉讓、繼承、分割及管理財產的權利，取決於所有權形式及州法。

## 6. Adverse possession
## 6. 逆權占有

Adverse possession may allow a possessor to acquire title after satisfying strict statutory and common-law requirements for a required period. Common elements may include actual, open and notorious, exclusive, hostile, and continuous possession.
如果占有人在規定期間內符合嚴格的制定法及普通法要求，逆權占有可能使其取得所有權。常見要件可能包括實際、公開且明顯、排他、對抗性及持續占有。

The required period and details vary by state. Do not assume that simply using another person's land creates ownership.
所需期間及具體要求因州而異。不要假設單純使用他人的土地就會產生所有權。

## Check your understanding
## 理解測驗

1. Is possession always the same as ownership?
   占有是否永遠等同所有權？
2. What is the difference between real and personal property?
   不動產與動產有什麼不同？
3. Does adverse possession have the same time limit in every state?
   逆權占有在每個州都有相同期限嗎？

**Answers:** 1. No. 2. Real property concerns land and land interests; personal property generally concerns movable property and intangible rights. 3. No.
**答案：** 1. 不是。2. 不動產涉及土地及土地權益；動產通常涉及可移動財產及無形權利。3. 不是。
