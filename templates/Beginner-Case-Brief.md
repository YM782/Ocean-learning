## 9. Source check
## 9. 資料核實

Did you read the opinion itself rather than only a headnote or summary?

Did you record the court, jurisdiction, and decision date?
你是否記錄了法院、司法管轄區及判決日期？

Do not assume that every case is binding in every court.
不要假設每一個判例都對所有法院具有拘束力。
