# Beginner IRAC Worksheet
# 初學者 IRAC 練習表

IRAC stands for Issue, Rule, Application, and Conclusion.
IRAC 代表 Issue（爭點）、Rule（規則）、Application（適用）及 Conclusion（結論）。

## Step 1 — Facts
## 第一步——事實

Write down only facts that may affect the legal question.
只記錄可能影響法律問題的事實。

[Important facts]
[重要事實]

## Step 2 — Issue
## 第二步——爭點

What precise legal question must be answered?
必須回答的具體法律問題是什麼？

[Write one question.]
[寫出一個問題。]

## Step 3 — Rule
## 第三步——規則

What legal rule applies, and where did you find it?
適用哪一項法律規則？你在哪裡找到它？

[State the rule and source.]
[陳述規則及資料來源。]

## Step 4 — Application
## 第四步——適用

Explain how the rule applies to the facts.
解釋法律規則如何適用於事實。

[Apply the rule.]
[適用規則。]

What is the strongest argument for the other side?
對方最有力的論點是什麼？

[Counterargument.]
[相反論點。]

## Step 5 — Conclusion
## 第五步——結論

Give a conclusion supported by your analysis.
提出一個由你的分析所支持的結論。

[Conclusion.]
[結論。]

## Final checklist
## 最後檢查清單

- [ ] I identified a specific legal issue. / 我辨識出具體法律爭點。
- [ ] I stated a rule and its source. / 我陳述了規則及其來源。
- [ ] I applied the rule to the facts. / 我將規則適用於事實。
- [ ] I considered the other side's argument. / 我考慮了對方的論點。
- [ ] I gave a reasoned conclusion. / 我提出有理由支持的結論。
- [ ] I checked the jurisdiction and date. / 我核對了司法管轄區及日期。

A good legal answer explains the reasoning, not just the result.
好的法律答案必須解釋推理過程，而不只是給出結果。
