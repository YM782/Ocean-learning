# Legal Research and Writing
# 法律研究與寫作

**Level: beginner | Focus: primary authority, legal research, case reading, and clear analysis**
**程度：初學者｜重點：第一手法律權威、法律研究、判例閱讀及清晰分析**

Legal research identifies the law that governs a question. Legal writing explains that law accurately, applies it to facts, and supports conclusions with reliable authority.
法律研究是找出某個問題所適用的法律。法律寫作則準確解釋法律、將法律適用於事實，並以可靠法律權威支持結論。

## Research principle
## 研究原則

Start with the legal question, identify the jurisdiction and date, locate controlling primary authority, check whether it remains good law, and then explain the result in plain language.
先界定法律問題，確認司法管轄區及日期，找出具有控制力的第一手法律權威，核實其是否仍然有效，最後以淺白語言解釋結果。
