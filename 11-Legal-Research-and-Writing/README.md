# Legal Research and Writing
# 法律研究與寫作

## What this course is about
## 本課程內容

Begin after you can identify a legal issue and read a short case.
先學會辨識法律爭點及閱讀簡短判例，再開始本課。

This is an introductory course guide, not a complete textbook or legal opinion.
本頁是入門課程指南，並非完整教科書或法律意見。

## Start with three ideas
## 從三個概念開始

### 1. Finding primary sources
### 1. 尋找第一手資料

Learn to locate constitutions, statutes, regulations, and court opinions.
學習查找憲法、制定法、行政規章及法院判決。

### 2. Case briefs and IRAC
### 2. 判例摘要與 IRAC

Practice organizing facts, issues, rules, and reasoning.
練習整理事實、爭點、規則及推理。

### 3. Legal writing
### 3. 法律寫作

Learn to write clear, sourced, and balanced explanations.
學習撰寫清楚、有來源且兼顧雙方觀點的說明。


## Beginner practice
## 初學者練習

Write a short paragraph that states an issue, identifies one source, and explains one side's argument.
撰寫一段短文，提出法律爭點、指出一項資料來源，並解釋其中一方的論點。

## How to continue
## 如何繼續學習

Study one topic at a time and write down unfamiliar terms.
一次只學習一個主題，並記錄不熟悉的術語。

For every case, identify the court, jurisdiction, date, issue, rule, holding, and reasoning.
閱讀每一宗判例時，辨識法院、司法管轄區、日期、爭點、規則、裁判要旨及推理。

Always verify the law that applies to the relevant jurisdiction and date.
務必核實相關司法管轄區及日期所適用的法律。

**僅供教育學習，不構成法律意見。**
