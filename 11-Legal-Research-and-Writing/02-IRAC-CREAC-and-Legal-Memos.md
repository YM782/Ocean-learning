# IRAC, CREAC, and Legal Memoranda
# IRAC、CREAC 及法律備忘錄

## 1. IRAC
## 1. IRAC

IRAC means Issue, Rule, Application, and Conclusion. It is a simple structure for analyzing a legal question.
IRAC 代表爭點、規則、適用及結論，是分析法律問題的簡單架構。

**Issue:** What legal question must be answered?
**爭點：** 必須回答什麼法律問題？

**Rule:** What law controls, and where does it come from?
**規則：** 哪項法律具有控制力，來源是什麼？

**Application:** How do the facts satisfy or fail the rule's elements?
**適用：** 事實如何符合或不符合法律規則的要件？

**Conclusion:** What is the most defensible answer based on the available facts?
**結論：** 根據現有事實，最有理由支持的答案是什麼？

## 2. CREAC
## 2. CREAC

CREAC is another common structure: Conclusion, Rule, Explanation, Application, and Conclusion.
CREAC 是另一種常見架構：結論、規則、解釋、適用及結論。

It often works well in legal memoranda because the reader sees the short answer first, then the rule and analysis supporting it.
它通常適用於法律備忘錄，因為讀者會先看到簡短答案，再看到支持答案的規則及分析。

## 3. Objective legal memorandum
## 3. 客觀法律備忘錄

A predictive memorandum explains how a court is likely to resolve a legal issue. It should discuss arguments on both sides rather than act as a persuasive brief for one party.
預測性法律備忘錄解釋法院可能如何解決法律問題。它應討論雙方論點，而不是充當某一方的說服性訴狀。

A basic memorandum may contain: question presented, brief answer, facts, discussion, and conclusion.
基本備忘錄可能包括：提出的問題、簡短答案、事實、分析及結論。

## 4. Writing clearly
## 4. 清晰寫作

Use short sentences, define technical terms, cite the authority supporting each legal rule, and distinguish facts from assumptions.
使用簡短句子、定義專業術語、引用支持每項法律規則的權威來源，並區分事實與假設。

Avoid claiming that a case “proves” a broad proposition unless the holding actually supports that proposition.
除非判例的裁判要旨確實支持某項廣泛命題，否則不要聲稱該判例「證明」了該命題。

## 5. Short practice
## 5. 簡短練習

**Facts:** A city denies a permit for a public event because officials dislike the organizer's political viewpoint.
**事實：** 某城市因官員不喜歡活動主辦人的政治觀點，而拒絕發出公共活動許可。

**Issue:** Does the denial raise a First Amendment viewpoint-discrimination issue?
**爭點：** 這項拒絕是否引發第一修正案上的觀點歧視問題？

**Rule:** Government generally may not discriminate against speech based on viewpoint, subject to the applicable forum and regulatory framework.
**規則：** 政府通常不得基於觀點歧視言論，但仍須考慮適用的言論場所及規管架構。

**Application:** The stated reason directly concerns the organizer's viewpoint. The full permit rules, official authority, and factual record must still be reviewed.
**適用：** 已說明的理由直接涉及主辦人的觀點。但仍須檢查完整許可規則、官員權限及事實紀錄。

**Conclusion:** The denial presents a substantial constitutional concern, but a final legal opinion requires jurisdiction-specific research.
**結論：** 該拒絕提出重要憲法疑慮，但最終法律意見仍需要針對司法管轄區進行研究。

**Educational use only; not legal advice.**
**僅供教育學習，不構成法律意見。**
