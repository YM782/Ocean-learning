# Researching Cases, Statutes, and Citations
# 判例、制定法及法律引證研究

## 1. Start with a precise question
## 1. 從具體問題開始

A broad question such as “Is this legal?” is difficult to research. Rewrite it as a focused question that identifies the conduct, parties, jurisdiction, and relevant time.
「這樣合法嗎？」這類廣泛問題很難研究。應將問題改寫為具體問題，指出行為、當事人、司法管轄區及相關時間。

## 2. Find the governing statute
## 2. 找出適用制定法

Search official statutory sources and read the entire relevant section, including definitions, exceptions, cross-references, and amendments.
搜尋官方制定法來源，並閱讀整個相關條文，包括定義、例外、交叉引用及修正內容。

Do not rely only on a search result or a quotation in a blog post. Verify the current text and effective date.
不要只依賴搜尋結果或部落格引用。應核實現行文本及生效日期。

## 3. Find cases interpreting the rule
## 3. 尋找解釋規則的判例

Search for decisions interpreting the statute or constitutional provision. Read the holding and reasoning, not only the headnote or summary.
搜尋解釋該制定法或憲法條文的判決。應閱讀裁判要旨及推理，而不只是判例摘要或標題。

Check whether the decision is from the U.S. Supreme Court, a federal court of appeals, a federal district court, or a state court. The court affects precedential force.
確認判決來自美國最高法院、聯邦上訴法院、聯邦地方法院或州法院。法院層級會影響判例效力。

## 4. Verify whether a case remains good law
## 4. 核實判例是否仍然有效

A later court may affirm, distinguish, limit, criticize, or overrule an earlier case. Use citators or reliable case histories and read the later decisions.
後來的法院可能維持、區別、限制、批評或推翻先前判例。應使用判例追蹤工具或可靠判例歷史資料，並閱讀後續判決。

A case can remain valid for one proposition while being limited on another. Verify the exact proposition you intend to cite.
一宗判例可能對某一法律命題仍然有效，卻在另一命題上受到限制。引用時應核實你真正要引用的具體命題。

## 5. Basic case citation
## 5. 基本判例引證

A U.S. Supreme Court citation commonly includes the case name, reporter volume, reporter abbreviation, first page, and year.
美國最高法院判例引證通常包括案件名稱、判例集卷號、判例集縮寫、起始頁及年份。

Example: *Brown v. Board of Education*, 347 U.S. 483 (1954).
例子：*Brown v. Board of Education*, 347 U.S. 483（1954年）。

The citation helps readers locate the decision. Pinpoint citations identify the specific page supporting a proposition.
引證可以幫助讀者找到判決。精確頁碼引證則指出支持某項法律命題的具體頁面。

## 6. Basic statutory citation
## 6. 基本制定法引證

A federal statutory citation usually identifies the title of the United States Code and the section number.
聯邦制定法引證通常指出《美國法典》的篇號及條文號。

Example: 42 U.S.C. § 1983.
例子：42 U.S.C. § 1983。

## Research log
## 研究紀錄

For each source, record the title, court or issuing body, date, jurisdiction, URL, relevant section or page, and the date you accessed it.
對每一項來源，記錄標題、法院或發布機關、日期、司法管轄區、網址、相關條文或頁碼，以及查閱日期。
