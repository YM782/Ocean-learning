# Primary and Secondary Legal Authority
# 第一手及第二手法律權威

## 1. Primary authority
## 1. 第一手法律權威

Primary authority is law issued by a body with legal authority. Examples include constitutions, statutes, regulations, court rules, and judicial opinions.
第一手法律權威是由具有法律權限的機關發布的法律。例子包括憲法、制定法、行政規章、法院規則及司法判決。

Primary authority may be binding or persuasive depending on the court, jurisdiction, issue, and hierarchy.
第一手法律權威可能具有拘束力或說服力，取決於法院、司法管轄區、爭點及層級。

## 2. Secondary sources
## 2. 第二手資料

Secondary sources explain, summarize, or critique the law. Examples include treatises, law-review articles, practice guides, legal encyclopedias, and textbooks.
第二手資料用來解釋、摘要或評論法律。例子包括法律專論、法律評論期刊文章、實務指南、法律百科全書及教科書。

Secondary sources can help a beginner understand a subject and locate primary authority, but they do not replace the controlling law.
第二手資料可以幫助初學者理解主題及尋找第一手法律權威，但不能取代具有控制力的法律。

## 3. Binding and persuasive authority
## 3. 拘束性及說服性法律權威

A source is binding when the relevant hierarchy and jurisdiction require the court to follow it. A source is persuasive when the court may consider its reasoning but is not required to follow it.
如果相關層級及司法管轄區要求法院遵循某項資料，該資料便具有拘束力。如果法院可以考慮其推理但不必遵循，該資料便具有說服力。

Always identify the issuing court, the legal question decided, and whether the decision is still controlling.
務必辨識發布判決的法院、所裁決的法律問題，以及該判決是否仍具有控制力。

## 4. Official versus unofficial sources
## 4. 官方及非官方來源

Official government websites are important for current statutes, rules, regulations, and court opinions. Commercial databases and academic websites may provide useful search tools and annotations.
官方政府網站是查閱現行制定法、規則、行政規章及法院判決的重要來源。商業資料庫及學術網站可能提供有用的搜尋工具及註釋。

A search-result snippet is not a substitute for reading the source itself. Open the full text and verify the relevant section.
搜尋結果摘要不能取代閱讀來源本身。應打開完整文本並核實相關條文。

## 5. A source hierarchy checklist
## 5. 來源層級檢查表

1. Constitution or controlling constitutional precedent.
   憲法或具有控制力的憲法判例。
2. Applicable statutes and regulations.
   適用的制定法及行政規章。
3. Binding court rules and controlling judicial opinions.
   具有拘束力的法院規則及司法判決。
4. Persuasive opinions from other jurisdictions.
   其他司法管轄區具有說服力的判決。
5. Secondary commentary.
   第二手評論資料。

This is a practical learning order, not a universal ranking that resolves every conflict. The exact hierarchy depends on the legal issue.
這是一種實用學習順序，不是能解決所有衝突的普遍排名。具體層級取決於法律問題。
