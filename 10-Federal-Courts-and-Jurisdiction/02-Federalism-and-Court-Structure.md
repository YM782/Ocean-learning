# Federalism and the U.S. Court Structure
# 聯邦制及美國法院架構

## 1. Two court systems
## 1. 兩套法院制度

The United States has a federal court system and separate court systems in each state. State courts hear most criminal prosecutions and many civil disputes.
美國有聯邦法院制度，各州也有各自的法院制度。州法院審理大多數刑事追訴及許多民事爭議。

Federal courts hear cases within federal jurisdiction, including certain federal constitutional, statutory, treaty, and diversity cases.
聯邦法院審理屬於聯邦管轄權範圍的案件，包括某些聯邦憲法、制定法、條約及多元國籍案件。

## 2. The federal hierarchy
## 2. 聯邦法院層級

The main federal trial courts are U.S. District Courts. Most appeals from district courts go to one of the U.S. Courts of Appeals.
主要聯邦初審法院是美國聯邦地方法院。地方法院的大多數上訴會交由其中一個美國聯邦上訴法院審理。

The U.S. Supreme Court is the highest federal court. It has limited original jurisdiction and primarily reviews selected cases through appellate jurisdiction.
美國最高法院是最高聯邦法院。它具有有限的初審管轄權，主要透過上訴管轄權審查經選擇的案件。

## 3. State court hierarchy
## 3. 州法院層級

A typical state system may include trial courts, intermediate appellate courts, and a state supreme court. The names and organization differ by state.
典型州法院制度可能包括初審法院、中級上訴法院及州最高法院。名稱及組織方式因州而異。

A state supreme court is generally authoritative on that state's law, while the U.S. Supreme Court may review a final state judgment that presents a properly preserved federal question.
州最高法院通常是該州法律的權威；如果州法院最終判決涉及適當保留的聯邦問題，美國最高法院可能進行審查。

## 4. Federalism
## 4. 聯邦制

Federalism divides governmental authority between the national government and the states. The Constitution assigns certain powers to the federal government and reserves other powers to the states or the people.
聯邦制將政府權限分配給國家政府及各州。憲法將某些權力授予聯邦政府，並將其他權力保留給各州或人民。

The Supremacy Clause makes valid federal law controlling when it conflicts with state law within the scope of federal constitutional authority.
在聯邦憲法權限範圍內，有效聯邦法律與州法衝突時，最高法條款使聯邦法律具有控制效力。

## 5. The Supreme Court and certiorari
## 5. 最高法院及調卷令

The Supreme Court receives thousands of petitions for review but grants only a small fraction. A petition for a writ of certiorari asks the Court to review a lower-court decision.
最高法院每年收到數千份審查請求，但只批准其中一小部分。調卷令申請是要求最高法院審查下級法院判決。

The Court often selects cases involving important federal questions, conflicts among lower courts, or issues of broad national significance.
最高法院經常選擇涉及重要聯邦問題、下級法院之間法律衝突，或具有廣泛全國重要性的案件。
