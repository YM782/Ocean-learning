# Federal Subject-Matter Jurisdiction
# 聯邦事項管轄權

## 1. Why jurisdiction comes first
## 1. 為什麼先看管轄權？

Jurisdiction is a court's legal authority to hear and decide a case. A federal court cannot decide a dispute merely because the parties prefer federal court or because the issue seems important.
管轄權是法院審理及裁決案件的法律權限。聯邦法院不能只因當事人偏好聯邦法院，或爭議看起來很重要，就取得審理權限。

Federal courts are courts of limited jurisdiction. A party must identify a constitutional and statutory basis for federal jurisdiction.
聯邦法院屬於有限管轄權法院。當事人必須指出聯邦管轄權的憲法及制定法依據。

## 2. Federal-question jurisdiction
## 2. 聯邦問題管轄權

Under 28 U.S.C. § 1331, federal district courts have original jurisdiction over civil actions arising under the Constitution, laws, or treaties of the United States.
依28 U.S.C. § 1331，聯邦地方法院對源自美國憲法、法律或條約的民事訴訟具有初審管轄權。

A case does not become a federal-question case merely because a federal issue is mentioned. The claim must satisfy the governing arising-under doctrine.
案件不會只因為提及聯邦問題就自動成為聯邦問題案件。請求必須符合適用的「源自」法律原則。

## 3. Diversity jurisdiction
## 3. 多元國籍管轄權

Under 28 U.S.C. § 1332, diversity jurisdiction generally requires complete diversity between opposing sides and an amount in controversy exceeding $75,000, exclusive of interest and costs.
依28 U.S.C. § 1332，多元國籍管轄權通常要求對立雙方之間具有完整國籍多元性，且爭議金額超過75,000美元，不包括利息及訴訟費用。

Citizenship is a legal concept, not always the same as residence. A corporation generally has citizenship in its state of incorporation and the state of its principal place of business.
公民身分是法律概念，不一定等同居住地。公司通常具有其註冊州及主要營業地所在州的公民身分。

## 4. Supplemental jurisdiction
## 4. 補充管轄權

Under 28 U.S.C. § 1367, a federal court may hear certain additional claims that are part of the same Article III case or controversy as a claim within original jurisdiction, subject to statutory limits.
依28 U.S.C. § 1367，聯邦法院可能審理某些額外請求，只要它們與具有初審管轄權的請求屬於同一第三條案件或爭議，但仍受到制定法限制。

## 5. Removal
## 5. 移送聯邦法院

Removal may allow a defendant to move a qualifying case from state court to federal court. The case must satisfy the federal jurisdictional basis and the removal statute's procedural requirements.
移送制度可能允許被告將符合條件的案件從州法院移至聯邦法院。案件必須符合聯邦管轄權依據及移送制定法的程序要求。

Removal is not the same as appeal. It changes the court in which the case proceeds under statutory conditions.
移送不等於上訴。它是在符合法定條件下改變案件繼續進行的法院。

## 6. Quick comparison
## 6. 快速比較

| Type | Core question | 核心問題 |
|---|---|---|
| Federal question | Does the claim arise under federal law? | 請求是否源自聯邦法律？ |
| Diversity | Are the parties sufficiently diverse and is the amount over $75,000? | 當事人是否符合國籍多元性，金額是否超過75,000美元？ |
| Supplemental | Is the additional claim part of the same case or controversy? | 額外請求是否屬於同一案件或爭議？ |
| Removal | Could the case have been filed in federal court, and are removal rules met? | 案件原本能否在聯邦法院提出，且是否符合移送規則？ |
