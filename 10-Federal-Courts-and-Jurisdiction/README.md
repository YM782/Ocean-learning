# Federal Courts and Jurisdiction
# 聯邦法院與管轄權

**Level: beginner | Focus: Article III courts, federal jurisdiction, and federalism**
**程度：初學者｜重點：第三條法院、聯邦管轄權及聯邦制**

This course explains why federal courts may hear some disputes but not others, how the federal court system is organized, and how federal and state courts interact.
本課程解釋為什麼聯邦法院可以審理某些爭議但不能審理其他爭議、聯邦法院制度如何組織，以及聯邦與州法院如何互動。
