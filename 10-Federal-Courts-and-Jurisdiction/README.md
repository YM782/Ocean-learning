# Federal Courts and Jurisdiction
# 聯邦法院與管轄權

## What this course is about
## 本課程內容

Begin after the basic court-system and federalism lessons.
先完成法院制度及聯邦制的基礎課，再開始本課。

This is an introductory course guide, not a complete textbook or legal opinion.
本頁是入門課程指南，並非完整教科書或法律意見。

## Start with three ideas
## 從三個概念開始

### 1. Federal court structure
### 1. 聯邦法院架構

Learn district courts, courts of appeals, and the Supreme Court.
學習地方法院、上訴法院及最高法院。

### 2. Subject-matter jurisdiction
### 2. 事物管轄權

Understand why federal courts can hear only cases authorized by law.
理解為什麼聯邦法院只能審理法律授權其受理的案件。

### 3. Standing
### 3. 訴訟資格

Learn the basic requirement that a plaintiff have a legally sufficient stake in a dispute.
學習原告必須對爭議具有足夠法律利害關係的基本要求。


## Beginner practice
## 初學者練習

Explain why a federal court cannot automatically hear every dispute in the country.
解釋為什麼聯邦法院不能自動審理全國所有爭議。

## How to continue
## 如何繼續學習

Study one topic at a time and write down unfamiliar terms.
一次只學習一個主題，並記錄不熟悉的術語。

For every case, identify the court, jurisdiction, date, issue, rule, holding, and reasoning.
閱讀每一宗判例時，辨識法院、司法管轄區、日期、爭點、規則、裁判要旨及推理。

Always verify the law that applies to the relevant jurisdiction and date.
務必核實相關司法管轄區及日期所適用的法律。
