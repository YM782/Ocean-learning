# Article III and Justiciability
# 美國憲法第三條及可裁判性

## 1. Article III courts
## 1. 第三條法院

Article III of the Constitution establishes the federal judicial power and protects important features of federal judicial independence, including life tenure during good behavior and salary protection.
美國憲法第三條建立聯邦司法權，並保障聯邦司法獨立的重要特徵，包括法官在行為良好期間任職及薪酬保障。

Congress creates lower federal courts and defines much of their jurisdiction within constitutional limits.
國會在憲法限制內設立下級聯邦法院，並界定其大部分管轄權。

## 2. Case or controversy
## 2. 案件或爭議

Federal courts generally decide concrete legal disputes rather than issue advisory opinions about hypothetical questions.
聯邦法院通常裁決具體法律爭議，而不是對假設性問題發布諮詢意見。

The case-or-controversy requirement helps ensure that courts resolve disputes presented by parties with a real stake in the outcome.
案件或爭議要求有助於確保法院解決的是由真正受到結果影響的當事人提出的爭議。

## 3. Standing
## 3. 訴訟資格

Article III standing generally requires injury in fact, traceability, and redressability.
第三條訴訟資格通常要求存在事實損害、可追溯性及可救濟性。

The injury must be concrete and particularized and actual or imminent. A generalized disagreement with government policy usually is not enough.
損害必須具體且個別化，並且已經發生或迫在眉睫。單純普遍地不同意政府政策，通常並不足夠。

The injury must be fairly traceable to the challenged conduct, and a favorable court decision must likely provide relief.
損害必須公平地可追溯至受到挑戰的行為，而且有利的法院判決必須很可能提供救濟。

## 4. Ripeness and mootness
## 4. 成熟性及失去實際爭議

Ripeness asks whether a dispute is sufficiently developed for judicial decision. Courts generally avoid deciding disputes that depend on uncertain future events.
成熟性是問爭議是否已充分發展到可以由法院裁決。法院通常避免裁決依賴不確定未來事件的爭議。

Mootness asks whether a live controversy still exists. If later events eliminate the parties' concrete dispute, the case may become moot, subject to recognized exceptions.
失去實際爭議是問是否仍然存在真實爭議。如果後續事件消除了當事人之間的具體爭議，案件可能失去實際爭議，但仍存在法律承認的例外。

## 5. Political question doctrine
## 5. 政治問題原則

Some disputes are treated as political questions that courts are not institutionally suited to resolve. The doctrine is limited and depends on constitutional structure and the nature of the issue.
某些爭議會被視為法院在制度上不適合裁決的政治問題。該原則適用範圍有限，取決於憲法架構及爭議性質。

## 6. Why justiciability matters
## 6. 為什麼可裁判性重要

A person may believe government action is wrong but still lack standing to sue. A court may agree that an issue is serious but lack authority to issue an advisory opinion.
一個人可能認為政府行為錯誤，卻仍然沒有提起訴訟的資格。法院可能認為某個問題很嚴重，但仍沒有權限發布諮詢意見。

## Check your understanding
## 理解測驗

1. What are the three familiar elements of Article III standing?
   第三條訴訟資格常見的三個要素是什麼？
2. Does a court decide every important political disagreement?
   法院會裁決每一項重要政治分歧嗎？
3. What does mootness ask?
   失去實際爭議會問什麼？

**Answers:** 1. Injury, traceability, and redressability. 2. No. 3. Whether a live controversy still exists.
**答案：** 1. 損害、可追溯性及可救濟性。2. 不會。3. 是否仍然存在真實爭議。
