# Administrative Law
# 行政法

**Level: beginner | Focus: federal agencies, rulemaking, adjudication, and judicial review**
**程度：初學者｜重點：聯邦行政機關、規則制定、行政裁決及司法審查**

Administrative law governs how government agencies receive authority, make rules, decide cases, and are reviewed by courts. This course focuses on the federal Administrative Procedure Act and constitutional limits.
行政法規範政府機關如何取得權限、制定規則、裁決案件，以及接受法院審查。本課程以聯邦《行政程序法》及憲法限制為重點。

**Important update:** *Loper Bright Enterprises v. Raimondo* (2024) overruled Chevron deference. Courts must exercise independent judgment when interpreting statutes; agency views may still receive respect according to their persuasive power.
**重要更新：** 2024年的 *Loper Bright Enterprises v. Raimondo* 推翻了 Chevron 尊重原則。法院解釋制定法時必須行使獨立判斷；行政機關的意見仍可能依其說服力獲得尊重。
