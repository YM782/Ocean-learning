# Judicial Review of Agency Action
# 行政機關行為的司法審查

## 1. Judicial review
## 1. 司法審查

Judicial review allows a court to examine whether an agency acted within its legal authority and followed required procedures. The court does not simply replace the agency's policy judgment with its own preference.
司法審查讓法院檢查行政機關是否在法律授權範圍內行事，以及是否遵守必要程序。法院不會單純以自己的政策偏好取代行政機關的政策判斷。

The Administrative Procedure Act provides important review provisions, but other statutes may limit or structure review.
《行政程序法》提供重要的審查條文，但其他制定法也可能限制或規範審查。

## 2. Final agency action
## 2. 最終行政機關行為

Under the APA, judicial review is generally directed at final agency action for which there is no other adequate remedy in court, subject to statutory exceptions.
依《行政程序法》，司法審查通常針對沒有其他充分法院救濟途徑的最終行政機關行為，但仍須考慮制定法例外。

A preliminary step or informal staff communication may not qualify as final agency action.
初步步驟或非正式職員溝通可能不符合最終行政機關行為的要求。

## 3. Standing and other justiciability limits
## 3. 訴訟資格及其他可裁判性限制

A plaintiff generally must establish Article III standing: a concrete and particularized injury that is actual or imminent, fairly traceable to the defendant, and likely to be redressed by a favorable decision.
原告通常必須確立第三條訴訟資格：存在具體且個別化、已發生或迫在眉睫的損害；該損害公平地可追溯至被告；而有利判決很可能救濟該損害。

Other limits may include ripeness, mootness, exhaustion requirements, and statutory preclusion of review.
其他限制可能包括成熟性、失去實際爭議、救濟用盡要求，以及制定法排除司法審查。

## 4. Arbitrary-and-capricious review
## 4. 恣意及反覆無常審查

Under APA § 706(2)(A), a court may set aside agency action that is arbitrary, capricious, an abuse of discretion, or otherwise not in accordance with law.
依《行政程序法》第706(2)(A)條，如果行政機關行為屬於恣意、反覆無常、濫用裁量權或其他不符合法律的情況，法院可以撤銷該行為。

The agency must examine relevant data and provide a reasoned explanation connecting the facts found with the choice made. A court does not ordinarily substitute its own policy judgment for the agency's.
行政機關必須檢查相關資料，並提出有理由的解釋，將所認定事實與所作選擇連結起來。法院通常不會以自己的政策判斷取代行政機關的判斷。

## 5. Loper Bright and statutory interpretation
## 5. Loper Bright 與制定法解釋

In *Loper Bright Enterprises v. Raimondo* (2024), the Supreme Court overruled Chevron deference. Courts must exercise independent judgment when deciding whether an agency has acted within its statutory authority.
在2024年的 *Loper Bright Enterprises v. Raimondo* 案中，最高法院推翻了 Chevron 尊重原則。法院判斷行政機關是否在制定法授權範圍內行事時，必須行使獨立判斷。

An agency's expertise and interpretation may still be persuasive depending on its thoroughness, consistency, and power to persuade. This is not the same as automatically deferring to the agency.
行政機關的專業及解釋仍可能因其完整程度、一致性及說服力而具有參考價值。但這不等於自動尊重行政機關的解釋。

## 6. Exercises
## 6. 練習題

**Question 1:** Congress authorizes an agency to regulate a defined subject. The agency issues a rule outside that subject. What issue arises?
**問題一：** 國會授權某行政機關規範特定事項，但該機關發布的規則超出該事項範圍。會產生什麼問題？

**Answer:** The agency may have exceeded its statutory authority. The court must interpret the statute independently under current doctrine.
**答案：** 行政機關可能超越制定法授權。依現行法律原則，法院必須獨立解釋制定法。

**Question 2:** An agency gives no explanation for ignoring important evidence in the record. What review issue may arise?
**問題二：** 行政機關沒有解釋為什麼忽略紀錄中的重要證據。可能產生什麼審查問題？

**Answer:** The action may be challenged as arbitrary and capricious under APA § 706(2)(A).
**答案：** 該行為可能依《行政程序法》第706(2)(A)條被挑戰為恣意及反覆無常。

**Question 3:** Does *Loper Bright* mean courts must ignore agency expertise?
**問題三：** *Loper Bright* 是否代表法院必須忽略行政機關的專業？

**Answer:** No. Agency reasoning may remain persuasive, but the court must exercise independent judgment on statutory meaning.
**答案：** 不是。行政機關的推理仍可能具有說服力，但法院必須對制定法含義行使獨立判斷。
