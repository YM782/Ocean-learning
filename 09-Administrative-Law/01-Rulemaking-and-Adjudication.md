# Rulemaking and Agency Adjudication
# 規則制定及行政裁決

## 1. Rulemaking
## 1. 規則制定

Rulemaking is the process by which an agency creates regulations under authority granted by statute.
規則制定是行政機關依制定法授予的權限建立行政規章的程序。

Under the federal Administrative Procedure Act, informal notice-and-comment rulemaking generally requires notice of proposed rulemaking, an opportunity for interested persons to submit comments, and a concise general statement of the basis and purpose of the final rule.
依聯邦《行政程序法》，非正式通知及評論規則制定通常要求發布擬議規則通知、提供利害關係人提交評論的機會，並對最終規則的依據及目的作出簡明一般性說明。

Not every agency action is a legislative rule. Guidance documents, policy statements, interpretive rules, and adjudicative orders may have different legal status.
並非每一項行政機關行為都是立法性規則。指引文件、政策聲明、解釋性規則及行政裁決命令可能具有不同法律地位。

## 2. Formal rulemaking
## 2. 正式規則制定

Formal rulemaking is required only when the governing statute requires a hearing on the record under the relevant APA provisions. It is more trial-like than ordinary notice-and-comment rulemaking.
只有在適用制定法依相關《行政程序法》條文要求進行紀錄聽證時，才需要正式規則制定。它比一般通知及評論程序更接近審判。

## 3. Agency adjudication
## 3. 行政裁決

Adjudication is an agency process for applying law to particular facts and resolving a dispute or determining a person's legal status.
行政裁決是行政機關將法律適用於特定事實，並解決爭議或決定某人的法律地位的程序。

Some agency adjudications involve administrative law judges, hearings, evidence, and written decisions. The exact process depends on the statute and agency rules.
某些行政裁決涉及行政法官、聽證、證據及書面決定。具體程序取決於制定法及行政機關規則。

## 4. Due process
## 4. 正當法律程序

The Fifth and Fourteenth Amendments may require fair procedures when government deprives a person of life, liberty, or property interests protected by due process.
當政府剝奪受到正當法律程序保障的生命、自由或財產利益時，第五及第十四修正案可能要求公平程序。

The analysis often asks whether a protected interest exists, what procedures are due, and how the government's interests and risk of error affect the required process.
分析通常會問：是否存在受保障的利益、應提供哪些程序，以及政府利益與錯誤風險如何影響所需程序。

## 5. Ex parte communications and impartiality
## 5. 單方溝通及公正性

Agency adjudicators may be subject to rules designed to protect impartiality and fair hearings. The restrictions depend on the type of proceeding and governing law.
行政裁決者可能受到旨在保障公正及公平聽證的規則限制。相關限制取決於程序類型及適用法律。

## 6. Building an administrative record
## 6. 建立行政紀錄

The administrative record is the material the agency considered or was required to consider when taking the challenged action, subject to the governing review framework.
行政紀錄是行政機關在採取受到挑戰的行為時實際考慮或依法必須考慮的資料，但仍須遵守適用審查架構。

The record matters because a reviewing court usually evaluates the agency action based on the legally relevant record rather than conducting a completely new hearing.
行政紀錄十分重要，因為審查法院通常依法律相關紀錄評估行政機關行為，而不是重新舉行完整聽證。
