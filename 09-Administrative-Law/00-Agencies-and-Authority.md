# Agencies and Delegated Authority
# 行政機關及授權權力

## 1. What is an administrative agency?
## 1. 什麼是行政機關？

An administrative agency is a government body created or authorized by law to administer a program, enforce statutes, issue regulations, or decide certain disputes.
行政機關是依法律設立或授權的政府機構，負責管理某項計畫、執行制定法、發布行政規章，或裁決某些爭議。

Agencies may be located within executive departments or structured as independent agencies. Their legal authority comes from statutes and the Constitution.
行政機關可能設於行政部門之內，也可能採取獨立機關架構。其法律權限來自制定法及憲法。

## 2. Delegation
## 2. 權力授權

Congress may authorize an agency to implement a statute and fill in details within the limits of the law. The agency cannot simply invent power that Congress did not give it.
國會可以授權行政機關執行制定法，並在法律限制內補充細節。行政機關不能自行創造國會未授予的權力。

The nondelegation doctrine concerns constitutional limits on Congress's transfer of legislative power. The Supreme Court has generally allowed delegations when Congress supplies an intelligible principle, though the doctrine's application is debated.
不授權原則涉及國會轉移立法權的憲法限制。最高法院通常在國會提供可理解原則時允許授權，但該原則的適用仍存在爭論。

## 3. Agency functions
## 3. 行政機關職能

Agencies may perform legislative-like functions by issuing regulations, executive functions by enforcing statutes, and adjudicative functions by resolving disputes under statutory schemes.
行政機關可能透過制定規章履行類似立法的職能，透過執行制定法履行行政職能，並透過依制定法架構解決爭議履行裁決職能。

These functions remain subject to constitutional structure, statutory limits, procedural requirements, and judicial review.
這些職能仍受到憲法架構、制定法限制、程序要求及司法審查的約束。

## 4. Enabling statutes
## 4. 授權制定法

An enabling statute establishes an agency or grants it authority. To understand an agency action, begin by identifying the statute and the specific section that authorizes the action.
授權制定法設立行政機關或授予其權限。要理解行政機關的行為，應先找出相關制定法及授權該行為的具體條文。

An agency action outside its statutory authority may be challenged as ultra vires or unlawful under the applicable framework.
如果行政機關的行為超出制定法授權，可能依適用架構被挑戰為越權或違法。

## 5. Separation of powers
## 5. 權力分立

Administrative agencies operate within a constitutional system that divides power among Congress, the President, and the courts. The Constitution and statutes determine how agency officials may be appointed, supervised, and removed.
行政機關在一個將權力分配給國會、總統及法院的憲政制度中運作。憲法及制定法決定行政官員如何任命、監督及免職。

## Beginner checklist
## 初學者檢查表

When reading about an agency decision, ask:
閱讀行政機關決定時，請問：

1. Which statute created or authorized the agency?
   哪一項制定法設立或授權該機關？
2. Which provision authorizes the specific action?
   哪一條文授權這項具體行動？
3. Did the agency follow required procedures?
   行政機關是否遵守必要程序？
4. Is the action subject to judicial review?
   該行為是否可以接受司法審查？
