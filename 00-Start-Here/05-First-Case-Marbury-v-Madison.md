# Lesson 05 — Your First Case: Marbury v. Madison
# 第 05 課——你的第一個判例：Marbury v. Madison

## 1. Case information
## 1. 案件資料

**Case:** Marbury v. Madison, 5 U.S. (1 Cranch) 137 (1803).
**案件：** Marbury v. Madison, 5 U.S. (1 Cranch) 137 (1803)。

**Court:** Supreme Court of the United States.
**法院：** 美國聯邦最高法院。

**Year:** 1803.
**年份：** 1803 年。

**Topic:** Judicial review and the relationship between the Constitution and federal statutes.
**主題：** 司法審查，以及憲法與聯邦制定法之間的關係。

## 2. Background
## 2. 案件背景

William Marbury had been appointed to a public office near the end of President John Adams's administration.
在 John Adams 總統任期即將結束時，William Marbury 獲任命擔任一項公職。

His commission was not delivered before the new administration began.
他的任命證書在新政府上任前並未完成交付。

James Madison, the new Secretary of State, did not deliver the commission.
新任國務卿 James Madison 沒有交付任命證書。

Marbury asked the Supreme Court for a writ of mandamus ordering delivery.
Marbury 請求最高法院核發職務執行令（writ of mandamus），命令交付任命證書。

## 3. The legal question
## 3. 法律問題

The Court considered whether Marbury had a right to the commission, whether the law provided a remedy, and whether the Supreme Court could issue the requested writ in its original jurisdiction.
法院考慮 Marbury 是否有權取得任命證書、法律是否提供救濟，以及最高法院能否在其原始管轄權範圍內核發所請求的命令。

The jurisdiction question required comparison of Section 13 of the Judiciary Act of 1789 and Article III of the Constitution.
管轄權問題要求比較《1789 年司法法》第 13 條與憲法第三條。

## 4. The Court's answer
## 4. 法院的答案

Chief Justice John Marshall wrote the Court's opinion.
首席大法官 John Marshall 撰寫了法院的判決意見。

The Court concluded that Marbury had a legal right and that the law could provide a remedy for a violation of that right.
法院認為 Marbury 具有法律權利，而且該權利受到侵害時，法律可以提供救濟。

However, the Court concluded that it could not issue the requested writ under its original jurisdiction in the circumstances of the case.
然而，法院認為，在本案情況下，它不能依其原始管轄權核發所請求的命令。

The Court interpreted Section 13 as attempting to authorize original jurisdiction beyond the categories permitted by Article III.
法院將第 13 條解釋為試圖授予超出憲法第三條所允許類別的原始管轄權。

To the extent the statute conflicted with the Constitution, the conflicting provision could not be applied.
在制定法與憲法相牴觸的範圍內，該相牴觸條文不能適用。

## 5. What is judicial review?
## 5. 什麼是司法審查？

Judicial review is the power of courts to decide whether government action or a law is consistent with the Constitution in a case properly before them.
司法審查是指法院在依法受理的案件中，判斷政府行為或法律是否符合憲法的權力。

Marbury v. Madison is a foundational Supreme Court decision associated with judicial review of federal legislation.
Marbury v. Madison 是與聯邦立法司法審查相關的重要最高法院基礎判例。

It does not mean that the Supreme Court can decide every political disagreement or hear every dispute directly.
這並不代表最高法院可以裁決所有政治分歧，或直接受理所有爭議。

Courts must have jurisdiction and a proper case or controversy.
法院必須具有管轄權，而且案件必須符合適當的司法裁判要求。

## 6. Beginner case brief
## 6. 初學者判例摘要

**Facts:** Marbury's commission was not delivered, and he asked the Court for an order.
**事實：** Marbury 的任命證書沒有交付，因此他請求法院發布命令。

**Issue:** Could the Supreme Court issue the requested writ under its original jurisdiction?
**爭點：** 最高法院能否依其原始管轄權核發所請求的命令？

**Rule:** Article III defines the Supreme Court's original jurisdiction, and a statute cannot validly expand it beyond the Constitution.
**規則：** 憲法第三條界定最高法院的原始管轄權，制定法不能有效地將其擴大至憲法允許範圍之外。

**Holding:** The Court could not issue the requested writ in its original jurisdiction because the relevant statutory provision conflicted with the Constitution.
**裁判要旨：** 由於相關制定法條文與憲法相牴觸，最高法院不能依其原始管轄權核發所請求的命令。

**Reasoning:** The Constitution controls when a statute conflicts with it.
**推理：** 制定法與憲法相牴觸時，應以憲法為準。
