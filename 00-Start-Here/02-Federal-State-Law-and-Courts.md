# Lesson 02 — Federal Law, State Law, and U.S. Courts
# 第 02 課——聯邦法、州法與美國法院

## 1. Two levels of government
## 1. 兩個層級的政府

The United States has a national government and fifty state governments.
美國有一個聯邦政府及五十個州政府。

The Constitution gives certain powers to the federal government and reserves other powers to the states or the people.
憲法將某些權力授予聯邦政府，並將其他權力保留給各州或人民。

This system is called federalism.
這套制度稱為聯邦制（Federalism）。

Federal law governs subjects such as immigration, naturalization, federal taxes, bankruptcy, and many aspects of interstate commerce.
聯邦法規範移民、歸化、聯邦稅收、破產及州際商業的許多方面。

States regulate many matters such as local public safety, family relationships, property, contracts, and much ordinary criminal law.
各州規範許多事項，例如地方公共安全、家庭關係、財產、契約及許多一般刑法。

State law can differ from one state to another.
各州法律可能彼此不同。

## 2. What if federal and state law conflict?
## 2. 聯邦法與州法發生衝突時怎麼辦？

The Supremacy Clause makes valid federal law supreme over conflicting state law.
最高效力條款規定，有效的聯邦法律優先於與其牴觸的州法。

This does not mean that every federal rule automatically replaces every state rule.
這並不代表每一項聯邦規則都會自動取代所有州法規則。

Preemption is the doctrine used to determine when federal law displaces state law.
聯邦優先適用（Preemption）是用來判斷聯邦法何時排除州法適用的法律原則。

The result depends on the federal law, its scope, and the nature of the conflict.
結果取決於聯邦法律、其適用範圍及衝突性質。

## 3. What does a court do?
## 3. 法院的工作是什麼？

A court resolves legal disputes by applying law to facts.
法院透過將法律適用於事實來解決法律爭議。

A trial court is usually where a case begins and evidence is presented.
初審法院通常是案件開始及提出證據的地方。

An appellate court reviews a lower court's decision for claimed legal or procedural errors.
上訴法院審查下級法院的決定是否存在當事人主張的法律或程序錯誤。

An appeal is generally not a completely new trial.
上訴通常不是重新進行一次完整審判。

## 4. The federal court structure
## 4. 聯邦法院架構

**U.S. District Courts** are the principal general federal trial courts.
**美國聯邦地方法院**是主要的一般聯邦初審法院。

**U.S. Courts of Appeals** review many decisions from federal district courts.
**美國聯邦上訴法院**審查許多聯邦地方法院的決定。

**The Supreme Court of the United States** is the highest federal court.
**美國聯邦最高法院**是聯邦司法體系中的最高法院。

The Supreme Court chooses most cases it hears through discretionary review.
最高法院透過裁量審查決定受理大部分案件。

State courts have their own structures and names, which vary by state.
州法院有各自的架構及名稱，而且因州而異。

## 5. Three important words
## 5. 三個重要詞語

**Jurisdiction** means a court's legal authority to hear a case.
**Jurisdiction（管轄權）** 是指法院審理案件的法律權限。

**Venue** usually concerns the proper geographic location for a case.
**Venue（審判地）** 通常涉及案件適當的地理審理地點。

**Appeal** means asking a higher court to review a lower court's decision.
**Appeal（上訴）** 是請求上級法院審查下級法院的決定。

## 6. Quick exercise
## 6. 快速練習

**Question:** Does every state have exactly the same contract law?
**問題：** 每一州的契約法都完全相同嗎？

**Answer:** No. State law can differ, so identify the state whose law governs the dispute.
**答案：** 不相同。各州法律可能不同，因此必須確認哪一州的法律適用於該爭議。

**Question:** Does an appellate court normally repeat the entire trial?
**問題：** 上訴法院通常會重新進行整個審判嗎？

**Answer:** No. It generally reviews the lower-court record and the claimed errors.
**答案：** 不會。上訴法院通常審查下級法院的訴訟紀錄及當事人主張的錯誤。
