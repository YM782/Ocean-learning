# First-Week Exercises and Answer Key
# 第一週練習題與答案解析

Try to answer each question before looking at the answer.
請先嘗試回答每一道題目，再查看答案。

## Multiple choice
## 選擇題

**1. What is the U.S. Constitution?**
**1. 美國憲法是什麼？**

A. A private company policy. / 私人公司的政策。
B. The foundational legal document of the United States. / 美國的根本法律文件。
C. A court's daily schedule. / 法院每日行程。

**2. Which branch is Congress part of?**
**2. 國會屬於哪一個部門？**

A. Executive. / 行政部門。
B. Judicial. / 司法部門。
C. Legislative. / 立法部門。

**3. Does every state have exactly the same state law?**
**3. 每一州的州法都完全相同嗎？**

A. Yes. / 是。
B. No. / 否。

**4. What does IRAC stand for?**
**4. IRAC 代表什麼？**

A. Issue, Rule, Application, Conclusion. / 爭點、規則、適用、結論。
B. Investigation, Report, Appeal, Court. / 調查、報告、上訴、法院。

## True or false
## 是非題

**5. An appeal is usually a completely new trial.**
**5. 上訴通常是一次完全重新進行的審判。**

**6. A dissenting opinion disagrees with the court's result or reasoning.**
**6. 反對意見不同意法院的判決結果或推理。**

**7. A moral belief automatically becomes a criminal law.**
**7. 道德觀念會自動成為刑法。**

## Short answers
## 簡答題

**8. Explain one difference between a civil case and a criminal case.**
**8. 解釋民事案件與刑事案件的一項差異。**

**9. Name the three branches of the federal government.**
**9. 說出聯邦政府的三個部門。**

**10. What are the four parts of IRAC?**
**10. IRAC 的四個部分是什麼？**

## Answer key
## 答案

**1. B.** The Constitution is the foundational legal document of the federal government.
**1. B。** 憲法是聯邦政府的根本法律文件。

**2. C.** Congress is the legislative branch.
**2. C。** 國會屬於立法部門。

**3. B.** State laws can differ from one state to another.
**3. B。** 各州法律可能彼此不同。

**4. A.** IRAC means Issue, Rule, Application, and Conclusion.
**4. A。** IRAC 代表爭點、規則、適用及結論。

**5. False.** An appellate court generally reviews the lower-court record and claimed errors.
**5. 錯。** 上訴法院通常審查下級法院的訴訟紀錄及當事人主張的錯誤。

**6. True.** A dissent explains disagreement with the majority's result or reasoning.
**6. 對。** 反對意見會解釋為什麼不同意多數意見的結果或推理。

**7. False.** Criminal conduct must satisfy the elements of an applicable criminal law.
**7. 錯。** 犯罪行為必須符合適用刑法所規定的構成要件。

**8. Sample answer:** A civil case commonly resolves a private legal dispute, while a criminal case is a government prosecution for an alleged crime.
**8. 參考答案：** 民事案件通常解決私人法律爭議，而刑事案件是政府對涉嫌犯罪行為提出的追訴。

**9. Legislative, executive, and judicial.**
**9. 立法、行政及司法部門。**

**10. Issue, Rule, Application, Conclusion.**
**10. 爭點、規則、適用、結論。**

If you missed more than three questions, review the lessons before moving forward.
如果你答錯超過三題，請先複習課程，再繼續往下學習。
