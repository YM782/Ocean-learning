# Lesson 03 — Reading Cases and Using IRAC
# 第 03 課——閱讀判例及使用 IRAC

## 1. What is a court case?
## 1. 什麼是法院判例？

A case is a legal dispute decided through a court proceeding.
判例是透過法院訴訟程序作出裁決的法律爭議。

A judicial opinion explains the court's decision and may explain the legal rules used.
司法判決意見會說明法院的決定，也可能解釋法院使用的法律規則。

Some decisions become precedent that later courts may need to follow.
某些判決會成為先例，後來的法院可能必須遵循。

## 2. The basic parts of a case
## 2. 判例的基本部分

**Facts:** What happened?
**Facts（事實）：** 發生了什麼事？

**Procedural history:** Which courts handled the case before this decision?
**Procedural history（程序經過）：** 在本次判決之前，哪些法院處理過本案？

**Issue:** What legal question did the court need to answer?
**Issue（爭點）：** 法院需要回答什麼法律問題？

**Rule:** What legal principle did the court use?
**Rule（規則）：** 法院使用了什麼法律原則？

**Holding:** What answer did the court give to resolve the issue?
**Holding（裁判要旨）：** 法院為解決爭點作出了什麼答案？

**Reasoning:** Why did the court reach that result?
**Reasoning（推理）：** 法院為什麼得出這個結果？

**Disposition:** What happened to the case?
**Disposition（裁判結果）：** 案件最後如何處理？

## 3. Different opinions
## 3. 不同類型的司法意見

A majority opinion states the position supported by the required majority of the deciding court.
多數意見表達作成裁判的法院中，獲得所需多數支持的立場。

A concurring opinion agrees with the result but may use different reasoning.
協同意見同意判決結果，但可能採用不同推理。

A dissenting opinion disagrees with the result or reasoning.
反對意見不同意判決結果或推理。

A headnote or syllabus is an editorial summary, not the court's full opinion.
編者標題或判決摘要是編輯整理的摘要，不是法院完整的判決意見。

## 4. What is IRAC?
## 4. 什麼是 IRAC？

IRAC is a common structure for organizing legal analysis.
IRAC 是組織法律分析的常見架構。

**Issue** means the legal question that must be answered.
**Issue（爭點）** 是必須回答的法律問題。

**Rule** means the legal rule that applies.
**Rule（規則）** 是適用的法律規則。

**Application** means explaining how the rule applies to the facts.
**Application（適用）** 是解釋法律規則如何適用於事實。

**Conclusion** means the result supported by your analysis.
**Conclusion（結論）** 是由你的分析所支持的結果。

## 5. A very simple example
## 5. 一個非常簡單的例子

Alex pays Sam $300 for a laptop, but Sam does not deliver it on the agreed date.
Alex 向 Sam 支付 300 美元購買筆記型電腦，但 Sam 沒有在約定日期交貨。

**Issue:** Did the parties form a contract, and did Sam breach it?
**爭點：** 雙方是否成立契約，以及 Sam 是否違約？

**Rule:** A basic contract analysis often considers offer, acceptance, and consideration; the exact law depends on the jurisdiction and transaction.
**規則：** 基本契約分析通常考慮要約、承諾及對價；確切法律取決於司法管轄區及交易類型。

**Application:** The payment and promise to deliver may show an agreement, but more facts may be needed.
**適用：** 付款及交貨承諾可能顯示雙方已有協議，但仍可能需要更多事實。

**Conclusion:** Alex may have a contract claim, subject to the governing law and any defenses.
**結論：** Alex 可能享有契約請求權，但仍須考慮適用法律及任何抗辯。

## 6. Beginner reading method
## 6. 初學者閱讀方法

First identify the court, date, and parties.
先辨識法院、日期及當事人。

Then find the facts and the legal question.
接著找出案件事實及法律問題。

Next identify the rule and the court's answer.
然後辨識法律規則及法院的答案。

Finally explain why the court reached that result.
最後解釋法院為什麼得出該結果。

Do not write only a conclusion; explain your reasoning.
不要只寫結論；必須解釋你的推理。
