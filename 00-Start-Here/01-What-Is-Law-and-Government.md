# Lesson 01 — What Is Law and How Government Works
# 第 01 課——什麼是法律，以及政府如何運作

## 1. What is law?
## 1. 什麼是法律？

Law is a system of public rules recognized and enforced by legal institutions.
法律是一套由法律機關承認並執行的公共規則。

These rules help people understand what they may do, what they must do, and what may happen if they break a rule.
這些規則幫助人們了解自己可以做什麼、必須做什麼，以及違反規則可能產生什麼後果。

Law is not exactly the same as morality, custom, or a company's private policy.
法律並不完全等同於道德、習俗或公司的內部政策。

A moral belief may influence law, but it does not automatically become a legal rule.
道德觀念可能影響法律，但不會自動成為法律規則。

## 2. Why do societies use law?
## 2. 社會為什麼需要法律？

Law can protect people, resolve disputes, organize public institutions, and limit government power.
法律可以保護人民、解決爭議、組織公共機關，並限制政府權力。

Contract law helps people understand when an agreement may create enforceable duties.
契約法幫助人們了解協議在什麼情況下可能產生可執行的義務。

Criminal law identifies conduct that the government may prosecute as a crime.
刑法界定政府可以作為犯罪加以追訴的行為。

Constitutional law limits government power and protects certain individual rights.
憲法學限制政府權力，並保障某些個人權利。

## 3. Civil cases and criminal cases
## 3. 民事案件與刑事案件

A civil case commonly concerns a legal dispute between people, businesses, or other legal entities.
民事案件通常涉及個人、企業或其他法律主體之間的法律爭議。

A criminal case is brought by the government against a person accused of violating criminal law.
刑事案件由政府對涉嫌違反刑法的人提出追訴。

In a criminal case, the prosecution generally must prove guilt beyond a reasonable doubt.
在刑事案件中，控方通常必須以排除合理懷疑的程度證明被告有罪。

In many ordinary civil cases, the plaintiff generally must prove the claim by a preponderance of the evidence.
在許多一般民事案件中，原告通常必須以證據優勢的標準證明其請求。

The exact standards depend on the type of case and the applicable jurisdiction.
確切標準取決於案件類型及適用的司法管轄區。

## 4. What is the U.S. Constitution?
## 4. 什麼是美國憲法？

The U.S. Constitution is the foundational legal document of the federal government.
美國憲法是聯邦政府的根本法律文件。

It creates the federal government, assigns powers, and places limits on government action.
它建立聯邦政府、分配權力，並對政府行為設下限制。

The Constitution is not a complete list of every law in the country.
憲法並不是全國所有法律的完整清單。

Congress, state legislatures, administrative agencies, and courts also play important roles.
國會、州議會、行政機關及法院也都扮演重要角色。

## 5. The three branches of the federal government
## 5. 聯邦政府的三個部門

**Legislative branch — Congress.**
**立法部門——國會。**

Congress makes federal statutes within the powers granted by the Constitution.
國會在憲法授予的權限範圍內制定聯邦法律。

Congress has two chambers: the House of Representatives and the Senate.
國會由兩院組成：眾議院與參議院。

**Executive branch — President and federal agencies.**
**行政部門——總統與聯邦行政機關。**

The executive branch carries out and administers federal law.
行政部門負責執行及管理聯邦法律。

Federal agencies must act within the authority granted by law.
聯邦行政機關必須在法律授予的權限範圍內行事。

**Judicial branch — Federal courts.**
**司法部門——聯邦法院。**

The judicial branch resolves legal disputes and interprets law in cases properly before the courts.
司法部門負責解決依法提交法院的法律爭議，並在案件中解釋法律。

## 6. Checks and balances
## 6. 制衡制度

Checks and balances means that each branch has certain powers to limit or review the actions of other branches.
制衡制度是指每個政府部門都具有某些限制或審查其他部門行為的權力。

For example, the President may veto a bill, and Congress may override a veto under constitutional requirements.
例如，總統可以否決法案，而國會可以依憲法規定的條件推翻否決。

Courts may decide whether government action violates the Constitution in a case within their jurisdiction.
法院可以在其管轄權範圍內的案件中，判斷政府行為是否違反憲法。

## 8. Quick check
## 8. 快速檢查

**Question:** Which branch is Congress part of?
**問題：** 國會屬於哪一個部門？

**Answer:** Congress is the legislative branch.
**答案：** 國會屬於立法部門。

**Question:** Does every immoral act automatically become a crime?
**問題：** 每一種不道德的行為都會自動構成犯罪嗎？

**Answer:** No. Criminal conduct must satisfy the elements of an applicable criminal law.
**答案：** 不會。犯罪行為必須符合適用刑法所規定的構成要件。
