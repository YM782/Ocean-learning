# Start Here — U.S. Law for Absolute Beginners
# 從這裡開始——美國法律零基礎課程

Welcome. This course assumes that you have never studied law before.
歡迎你。本課程假設你以前從未學過法律。

You do not need a law degree, legal work experience, or perfect English to begin.
開始學習不需要法律學位、法律工作經驗，也不需要完美的英語能力。

## Follow this learning order
## 請按照以下順序學習

1. Understand what law is and why societies use legal rules.
   了解什麼是法律，以及社會為什麼需要法律規則。
2. Learn the U.S. Constitution and the three branches of government.
   學習美國憲法及政府的三個部門。
3. Understand the difference between federal law and state law.
   理解聯邦法與州法的差別。
4. Learn what trial courts and appellate courts do.
   學習初審法院與上訴法院的工作。
5. Learn how a judicial opinion is organized.
   學習司法判決意見的基本結構。
6. Practice IRAC with short and simple examples.
   使用簡短、簡單的例子練習 IRAC。
7. Read one landmark Supreme Court case with guidance.
   在指引下閱讀一宗重要的最高法院判例。
8. Study the landmark laws and cases in American history.
   學習美國歷史上影響深遠的法律及判例。
9. Begin the 12 core law subjects only after the foundation lessons.
   完成基礎課程後，再開始 12 門核心法律科目。

## What each lesson gives you
## 每一課會提供什麼

A simple explanation comes before difficult terminology.
先提供簡單解釋，再介紹困難的專業術語。

Each English explanation is followed immediately by Traditional Chinese.
每段英文說明後面都會立即接續繁體中文。

Examples connect legal ideas to ordinary life.
透過例子將法律概念與日常生活連結起來。

Exercises include explanations so that you can understand why an answer is correct.
練習題附有解析，讓你理解答案為什麼正確。

## What not to do yet
## 現階段先不要做的事

Do not begin with a long Supreme Court opinion without a guide.
不要在沒有指引的情況下，直接閱讀篇幅很長的最高法院判決。

Do not memorize hundreds of legal terms in one day.
不要試圖在一天內背誦數百個法律術語。

Do not assume that one state's law applies in all fifty states.
不要假設某一州的法律適用於全美五十州。

## Your first-week goal
## 第一週的學習目標

You should be able to explain what law is, describe the three branches of government, distinguish federal and state law, and explain the basic role of a court.
你應該能夠解釋什麼是法律、描述政府三個部門、區分聯邦法與州法，並說明法院的基本職能。

You are not expected to analyze a difficult case independently yet.
目前不要求你獨立分析複雜判例。

**Start with Lesson 01. Do not rush.**
**請從第 01 課開始，不要急於求成。**
