# Jurisdiction, Venue, and Service
# 管轄權、審判地及訴訟送達

## 1. Subject-matter jurisdiction
## 1. 事項管轄權

Subject-matter jurisdiction is a court's legal authority to hear a particular type of dispute. A federal court must have a basis for subject-matter jurisdiction; the parties cannot create it merely by agreement.
事項管轄權是法院審理某一類爭議的法律權限。聯邦法院必須具有事項管轄權依據；當事人不能只靠協議創造這種權限。

Two common bases for federal subject-matter jurisdiction are federal-question jurisdiction and diversity jurisdiction.
聯邦事項管轄權的兩種常見依據是聯邦問題管轄權及多元國籍管轄權。

**Federal-question jurisdiction:** Under 28 U.S.C. § 1331, federal district courts have jurisdiction over civil actions arising under the Constitution, laws, or treaties of the United States.
**聯邦問題管轄權：** 依28 U.S.C. § 1331，聯邦地方法院可以審理源自美國憲法、法律或條約的民事訴訟。

**Diversity jurisdiction:** Under 28 U.S.C. § 1332, jurisdiction generally requires the required diversity of citizenship and an amount in controversy exceeding $75,000, exclusive of interest and costs. The precise citizenship rules are technical.
**多元國籍管轄權：** 依28 U.S.C. § 1332，通常必須符合特定的當事人國籍多元性，且爭議金額超過75,000美元，不包括利息及訴訟費用。具體國籍判斷規則相當技術性。

## 2. Personal jurisdiction
## 2. 對人管轄權

Personal jurisdiction concerns a court's authority over a defendant. It protects a defendant from being required to defend a lawsuit in a place with insufficient legal connection to the defendant or dispute.
對人管轄權涉及法院對被告行使審判權的能力。它保障被告不會被要求在與被告或爭議缺乏足夠法律聯繫的地方應訴。

For a nonresident defendant, the constitutional analysis often asks whether the defendant has sufficient minimum contacts with the forum and whether exercising jurisdiction is consistent with fair play and substantial justice.
對於非本地被告，憲法分析通常會問：被告是否與該司法管轄區具有足夠的最低聯繫，以及行使管轄權是否符合公平競爭及實質正義。

A defendant may consent to personal jurisdiction or waive an objection by failing to raise it properly and on time. The rules for preserving the objection matter.
被告可以同意法院行使對人管轄權，也可能因未依規則及時提出異議而放棄異議。如何保留這項異議十分重要。

## 3. Venue
## 3. 審判地

Venue determines the proper geographic federal district for a lawsuit. Venue is different from subject-matter jurisdiction and personal jurisdiction.
審判地用來決定訴訟應在哪一個聯邦地區提出。審判地與事項管轄權及對人管轄權不同。

Under 28 U.S.C. § 1391, venue commonly depends on where defendants reside or where a substantial part of the events or omissions giving rise to the claim occurred, subject to statutory details.
依28 U.S.C. § 1391，審判地通常取決於被告居住地，或引發請求的重大事件或不作為發生地，但仍須遵守制定法細節。

## 4. Service of process
## 4. 訴訟文件送達

Service of process is the formal delivery of a summons and complaint or other required documents. It gives the defendant notice and an opportunity to respond.
訴訟文件送達是正式交付傳票、起訴狀或其他必要文件的程序。它讓被告獲得通知及回應機會。

Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 4 governs service in federal civil cases. The permitted method may depend on whether the defendant is an individual, corporation, government entity, or person outside the United States.
《聯邦民事訴訟規則》第4條規範聯邦民事案件的送達。允許的送達方式可能取決於被告是個人、公司、政府機關，還是美國境外人士。

## 5. Removal and remand
## 5. 移送聯邦法院及發回州法院

A defendant may sometimes remove a case from state court to federal court if the case could originally have been brought in federal court and statutory requirements are met.
如果案件原本可以在聯邦法院提出，且符合法定要求，被告有時可以將案件從州法院移送到聯邦法院。

A plaintiff may seek remand when removal was improper. Removal deadlines and consent requirements can be strict.
如果移送不當，原告可以申請將案件發回州法院。移送期限及同意要求可能相當嚴格。

## 6. A beginner's jurisdiction checklist
## 6. 初學者管轄權檢查表

1. Does the court have subject-matter jurisdiction?
   法院是否具有事項管轄權？
2. Does the court have personal jurisdiction over each defendant?
   法院是否對每名被告具有對人管轄權？
3. Is venue proper in this district?
   在這個地區提出訴訟是否符合審判地規則？
4. Was each defendant properly served?
   每名被告是否都已依法送達？
5. Is there a removal or remand issue?
   是否存在移送聯邦法院或發回州法院的問題？
