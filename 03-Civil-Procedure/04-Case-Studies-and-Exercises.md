# Civil Procedure Case Studies and Exercises
# 民事訴訟判例及練習題

## Case 1 — International Shoe Co. v. Washington, 326 U.S. 310 (1945)
## 判例一——International Shoe Co. v. Washington（1945年）

**Question:** When may a state court exercise personal jurisdiction over an out-of-state company?
**問題：** 州法院在什麼情況下可以對州外公司行使對人管轄權？

**Core principle:** Due process permits jurisdiction when the defendant has sufficient minimum contacts with the forum and exercising jurisdiction is consistent with traditional notions of fair play and substantial justice.
**核心原則：** 如果被告與該司法管轄區具有足夠的最低聯繫，而且行使管轄權符合傳統公平競爭及實質正義觀念，正當法律程序允許法院行使管轄權。

**Beginner lesson:** A company does not automatically become subject to every state's courts merely because it sells products somewhere. The nature and quality of its contacts matter.
**初學者重點：** 公司不會只因為在某地銷售產品，就自動受到每個州法院的管轄。聯繫的性質及程度都很重要。

## Case 2 — Erie Railroad Co. v. Tompkins, 304 U.S. 64 (1938)
## 判例二——Erie Railroad Co. v. Tompkins（1938年）

**Question:** In a diversity case, does a federal court simply apply federal common law to every issue?
**問題：** 在多元國籍案件中，聯邦法院是否對所有問題都直接適用聯邦普通法？

**Core principle:** A federal court generally applies state substantive law in diversity cases, while applying federal procedural law, subject to the complex Erie doctrine.
**核心原則：** 在多元國籍案件中，聯邦法院通常適用州的實體法，同時適用聯邦程序法，但仍須遵守複雜的 Erie 法律原則。

**Beginner lesson:** The words “federal court” do not mean that every legal rule in the case is federal law.
**初學者重點：** 「聯邦法院」並不代表案件中的每一項法律規則都是聯邦法。

## Case 3 — Bell Atlantic Corp. v. Twombly, 550 U.S. 544 (2007), and Ashcroft v. Iqbal, 556 U.S. 662 (2009)
## 判例三——Twombly（2007年）及 Iqbal（2009年）

**Core principle:** A complaint must contain enough factual matter to state a plausible claim for relief. A bare legal conclusion is not automatically accepted as true.
**核心原則：** 起訴狀必須包含足夠事實，使請求具有合理可信性。單純的法律結論不會自動被視為真實。

**Beginner lesson:** Pleading is not proof, but a complaint still needs more than labels and conclusions.
**初學者重點：** 訴狀並不是證明，但起訴狀仍然不能只有標籤及結論。

## Exercise 1 — Identify the jurisdiction
## 練習一——辨識管轄權

A plaintiff from State A sues a defendant from State B for $100,000 on a state-law contract claim. Assume complete diversity and no other complication. What possible basis for federal subject-matter jurisdiction exists?
來自 A 州的原告，以州契約法請求來自 B 州的被告支付100,000美元。假設符合完整國籍多元性且沒有其他複雜情況，可能存在什麼聯邦事項管轄權依據？

**Answer:** Diversity jurisdiction under 28 U.S.C. § 1332 may exist because the parties are citizens of different states and the amount in controversy exceeds $75,000, assuming all statutory requirements are met.
**答案：** 依28 U.S.C. § 1332，可能存在多元國籍管轄權，因為雙方是不同州的公民，而且爭議金額超過75,000美元，但仍須符合全部法定要求。

## Exercise 2 — Motion to dismiss or summary judgment?
## 練習二——駁回動議還是簡易判決？

The defendant argues that, even if the complaint's factual allegations are true, those allegations do not state a legally recognized claim. Which motion is most directly relevant?
被告主張，即使起訴狀中的事實指控都是真的，這些事實仍未構成法律承認的請求。哪一種動議最直接相關？

**Answer:** A Rule 12(b)(6) motion to dismiss for failure to state a claim.
**答案：** 依第12(b)(6)條提出未能陳述可獲救濟請求的駁回動議。

## Exercise 3 — Summary judgment
## 練習三——簡易判決

The parties agree on all material facts, and the only remaining question is how a statute applies. Could summary judgment be appropriate?
雙方對所有重大事實都沒有爭議，唯一剩下的問題是某項制定法如何適用。法院是否可能作出簡易判決？

**Answer:** Yes, if the moving party is entitled to judgment as a matter of law under Rule 56.
**答案：** 可以，只要提出動議的一方依第56條規則在法律上有權取得判決。

## IRAC practice
## IRAC 練習

**Facts:** A company deliberately targets advertisements to residents of a state, makes repeated sales there, and is sued there over injuries allegedly caused by one of the products sold through those contacts.
**事實：** 某公司刻意向某州居民投放廣告，並在該州反覆銷售產品。有人因透過這些聯繫所售出的產品而受傷，並在該州起訴該公司。

**Issue:** Does the state court have personal jurisdiction over the company?
**爭點：** 該州法院是否對該公司具有對人管轄權？

**Rule:** The court examines minimum contacts, whether the claim relates to those contacts, and whether jurisdiction is consistent with fair play and substantial justice.
**規則：** 法院會檢查最低聯繫、請求是否與這些聯繫相關，以及行使管轄權是否符合公平競爭及實質正義。

**Application:** Repeated targeted sales and a claim connected to those sales support jurisdiction more strongly than an isolated, unrelated contact. The complete facts and controlling circuit or Supreme Court precedent must still be examined.
**適用：** 反覆針對性銷售，而且請求與這些銷售相關，比起一次性且無關的接觸，更有力地支持管轄權。但仍須檢查完整事實及具有控制力的巡迴法院或最高法院判例。

**Conclusion:** Personal jurisdiction may be proper, but the answer depends on the full record and governing doctrine.
**結論：** 對人管轄權可能成立，但答案仍取決於完整紀錄及適用法律原則。
