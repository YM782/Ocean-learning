# The Life Cycle of a Federal Civil Lawsuit
# 聯邦民事訴訟的完整流程

## 1. What is a civil lawsuit?
## 1. 什麼是民事訴訟？

A civil lawsuit is a legal dispute in which a person or organization asks a court to resolve a claim. Common examples include contract disputes, personal injury claims, property disputes, and some claims against government officials.
民事訴訟是個人或組織向法院提出請求，要求法院解決法律爭議的程序。常見例子包括契約糾紛、人身傷害請求、財產爭議，以及某些針對政府官員的請求。

A civil case is different from a criminal prosecution. A civil plaintiff usually seeks money damages, an injunction, a declaration of rights, or another civil remedy rather than criminal punishment.
民事案件與刑事追訴不同。民事原告通常尋求金錢賠償、禁制令、權利宣告或其他民事救濟，而不是刑事處罰。

## 2. The basic timeline
## 2. 基本流程

A typical federal civil case may include the following stages, although not every case reaches trial:
一宗典型的聯邦民事案件可能包括以下階段，但並非每宗案件都會進入審判：

1. **Complaint:** The plaintiff files a document stating the claim and requested relief.
   **起訴狀：** 原告提交文件，說明請求及希望法院提供的救濟。
2. **Service:** The defendant is formally served with the summons and complaint.
   **送達：** 被告正式收到傳票及起訴狀。
3. **Response:** The defendant files an answer or an appropriate motion.
   **回應：** 被告提交答辯狀或適當的動議。
4. **Initial case management:** The court and parties organize the schedule and issues.
   **初步案件管理：** 法院及當事人安排時間表及爭點。
5. **Discovery:** Parties exchange information and obtain evidence under the rules.
   **證據開示：** 當事人依照規則交換資料及取得證據。
6. **Pretrial motions:** Parties ask the court to resolve legal or evidentiary issues before trial.
   **審前動議：** 當事人要求法院在審判前解決法律或證據問題。
7. **Settlement or trial:** The case may settle, or proceed to a jury or bench trial.
   **和解或審判：** 案件可能和解，也可能進入陪審團審判或法官獨任審判。
8. **Judgment and possible appeal:** The court enters judgment, and a party may seek appellate review if permitted.
   **判決及可能上訴：** 法院作出判決，當事人可在法律允許時尋求上訴審查。

## 3. The parties
## 3. 訴訟當事人

The plaintiff is the party who brings the claim. The defendant is the party against whom the claim is brought.
原告是提出請求的一方；被告是被提出請求的一方。

A party may be represented by a lawyer. In federal court, an individual generally may represent themselves, but a corporation or other artificial entity generally must appear through licensed counsel.
當事人可以由律師代理。在聯邦法院，個人通常可以自行訴訟；但公司或其他法人通常必須由持牌律師代表出庭。

## 4. Burden of proof
## 4. 舉證責任

In many civil claims, the plaintiff must prove the elements of the claim by a preponderance of the evidence. This generally means the claim is more likely true than not, although some claims use a different standard.
在許多民事請求中，原告必須以優勢證據標準證明請求要件。一般而言，這表示該主張成立的可能性高於不成立，但某些請求會使用不同標準。

Do not confuse this with the criminal standard of proof beyond a reasonable doubt. The applicable standard depends on the type of claim and governing law.
不要將此標準與刑事案件的排除合理懷疑標準混淆。適用的證明標準取決於請求類型及相關法律。

## 5. Settlement
## 5. 和解

Many civil cases settle before trial. Settlement is a voluntary agreement that may resolve some or all claims, often in exchange for payment, promises, or other terms.
許多民事案件會在審判前和解。和解是一項自願協議，可能解決部分或全部請求，通常涉及付款、承諾或其他條件。

A settlement is not necessarily an admission that either side was legally wrong. The parties may settle to reduce cost, uncertainty, delay, or risk.
和解不一定代表任何一方承認自己在法律上有錯。當事人可能為了降低成本、不確定性、延誤或風險而選擇和解。

## 6. Why procedure matters
## 6. 為什麼程序重要

Procedure protects fairness by giving parties notice, an opportunity to respond, access to relevant information, and a neutral decision-maker.
程序透過通知當事人、提供回應機會、取得相關資料的機會，以及中立裁決者，保障訴訟公平。

A party can have a strong factual argument and still lose because the court lacks jurisdiction, the claim is filed too late, or a procedural requirement is not met.
即使一方在事實上具有很強的論點，仍可能因法院沒有管轄權、請求提出太晚，或未符合程序要求而敗訴。
