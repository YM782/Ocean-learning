# Trial, Judgment, and Appeal
# 審判、判決及上訴

## 1. Pretrial conference
## 1. 審前會議

Before trial, the court may hold conferences to simplify issues, discuss settlement, organize evidence, and establish a schedule.
審判前，法院可能召開會議，以簡化爭點、討論和解、整理證據及確定時間表。

The parties may prepare a pretrial order identifying claims, defenses, witnesses, exhibits, and disputed issues. The exact practice depends on the court and its local rules.
當事人可能準備審前命令，列明請求、抗辯、證人、證物及爭議事項。具體做法取決於法院及當地規則。

## 2. Jury trial or bench trial
## 2. 陪審團審判或法官獨任審判

In a jury trial, jurors decide disputed facts and apply the court's legal instructions. The judge decides legal questions and manages the trial.
在陪審團審判中，陪審員決定有爭議的事實，並依照法院的法律指示作出裁決。法官負責決定法律問題及管理審判。

In a bench trial, the judge decides both the facts and the law. The Seventh Amendment preserves a civil jury-trial right in certain federal cases at law, subject to historical and doctrinal limits.
在法官獨任審判中，法官同時決定事實及法律。第七修正案在某些聯邦普通法案件中保障民事陪審團審判權，但仍受到歷史及法律原則限制。

## 3. The burden of proof
## 3. 舉證責任

Many civil claims use the preponderance-of-the-evidence standard. The plaintiff must show that the claim is more likely true than not.
許多民事請求使用優勢證據標準。原告必須證明其主張成立的可能性高於不成立。

Some civil matters use a higher standard, such as clear and convincing evidence. Always identify the standard required for the particular claim.
某些民事事項會使用更高的標準，例如清楚且令人信服的證據。務必確認特定請求所要求的證明標準。

## 4. Judgment
## 4. 判決

A judgment is the court's formal resolution of claims or rights. A final judgment generally resolves all claims for all parties, subject to recognized exceptions.
判決是法院對請求或權利作出的正式裁決。最終判決通常會解決所有當事人的全部請求，但仍存在法律承認的例外。

A party may seek post-judgment relief under applicable rules, but deadlines can be short and strict. The exact motion and time limit must be checked in the current rules.
當事人可能依適用規則尋求判決後救濟，但期限可能短而嚴格。必須查閱現行規則，確認適當動議及期限。

## 5. Appeal
## 5. 上訴

An appeal asks a higher court to review alleged legal errors. It usually relies on the record created in the trial court rather than hearing the entire case again.
上訴是要求上級法院審查所主張的法律錯誤。它通常依據原審法院建立的紀錄，而不是重新聽取整宗案件。

Appellate courts use different standards of review. Questions of law are often reviewed de novo; factual findings may receive clear-error review; discretionary decisions may be reviewed for abuse of discretion.
上訴法院會使用不同審查標準。法律問題通常採取重新審查；事實認定可能採取明顯錯誤審查；裁量決定可能採取濫用裁量權審查。

An appellate court may affirm, reverse, vacate, or remand. It does not automatically order a new trial whenever a party disagrees with the result.
上訴法院可能維持、撤銷、廢棄或發回重審。當事人不同意結果時，上訴法院不會自動命令重新審判。

## 6. Why preserving objections matters
## 6. 為什麼保留異議很重要

A party generally must raise a legal objection in the trial court at the appropriate time to preserve the issue for appeal. A failure to object may lead to a more demanding standard of review or forfeiture.
當事人通常必須在適當時間向原審法院提出法律異議，才能保留該問題供上訴審查。未提出異議可能導致更嚴格的審查標準，或喪失提出該問題的機會。

The rules contain exceptions, so a lawyer must examine the procedural record and the applicable appellate doctrine.
規則中仍有例外，因此律師必須檢查程序紀錄及適用的上訴法律原則。

## Check your understanding
## 理解測驗

1. Who decides facts in a jury trial?
   在陪審團審判中，誰決定事實？
2. Does an appeal normally repeat all witness testimony?
   上訴通常會重新聽取所有證人證言嗎？
3. What is the difference between de novo review and clear-error review?
   重新審查與明顯錯誤審查有什麼不同？

**Answers:** 1. The jury. 2. No. 3. De novo review gives no deference on the legal question; clear-error review gives substantial respect to trial-level factual findings.
**答案：** 1. 陪審團。2. 不會。3. 重新審查法律問題時不給予原審法律結論尊重；明顯錯誤審查則會相當尊重原審事實認定。
