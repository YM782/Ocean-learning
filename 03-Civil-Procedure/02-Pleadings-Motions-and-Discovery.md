# Pleadings, Motions, and Discovery
# 訴狀、動議及證據開示

## 1. Pleadings
## 1. 訴狀

Pleadings are formal documents that state the parties' claims and defenses. In a typical federal civil action, the complaint begins the case and the answer responds to it.
訴狀是正式文件，用來說明當事人的請求及抗辯。在典型的聯邦民事訴訟中，起訴狀開始案件，答辯狀則回應起訴內容。

Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 8 requires a short and plain statement showing that the pleader is entitled to relief, along with a demand for the relief sought. The rule does not require a full trial brief in the complaint.
《聯邦民事訴訟規則》第8條要求以簡明陳述說明提出訴訟的一方有權獲得救濟，並提出所要求的救濟。起訴狀不需要寫成完整的審判法律意見書。

The answer admits or denies allegations and may state affirmative defenses. A failure to respond can create serious procedural consequences, including possible default.
答辯狀會承認或否認起訴狀中的指控，也可能提出積極抗辯。未作出回應可能產生嚴重程序後果，包括可能被作出缺席判決。

## 2. Motions to dismiss
## 2. 駁回動議

A motion is a request asking the court to take a particular action. A motion to dismiss under Rule 12(b) may raise defenses such as lack of subject-matter jurisdiction, lack of personal jurisdiction, improper venue, insufficient process, insufficient service, or failure to state a claim.
動議是要求法院採取特定行動的請求。依第12(b)條提出的駁回動議，可能主張法院欠缺事項管轄權、對人管轄權、審判地不當、訴訟文件不足、送達不足，或起訴狀未能提出可獲法律救濟的請求。

A Rule 12(b)(6) motion tests whether the complaint states a legally sufficient claim, assuming well-pleaded factual allegations are true for that analysis. It is not ordinarily a trial of which side's evidence is more believable.
第12(b)(6)條動議檢查起訴狀是否提出法律上足夠的請求；在這項分析中，法院通常先假設妥善陳述的事實指控為真。它通常不是判斷哪一方證據更可信的審判。

## 3. Amendments
## 3. 訴狀修正

Rule 15 governs amendments to pleadings. In some circumstances a party may amend once as a matter of course; otherwise the party may need the opposing party's written consent or the court's permission.
第15條規則規範訴狀修正。在某些情況下，當事人可以依法自行修正一次；其他情況則可能需要對方書面同意或法院許可。

Courts generally consider whether justice requires allowing an amendment, but timing, prejudice, bad faith, and futility may matter.
法院通常會考慮是否有必要為了公正而允許修正，但提出時間、對方是否受到不利影響、是否存在惡意及修正是否徒勞，都可能影響決定。

## 4. Discovery
## 4. 證據開示

Discovery is the pretrial process through which parties obtain relevant, nonprivileged information within the scope of the rules. It helps each side understand the evidence and prepare for trial or settlement.
證據開示是審判前的程序，當事人可以依照規則取得範圍內相關且不受特權保護的資料。它幫助雙方了解證據，並為審判或和解作準備。

Common discovery tools include interrogatories, requests for production, requests for admission, and depositions.
常見證據開示工具包括書面詢問、文件提出要求、承認請求及證言錄取。

Discovery is not unlimited. Rule 26 includes relevance, proportionality, privilege, and other limits. Courts can protect parties and nonparties from improper discovery burdens.
證據開示並非毫無限制。第26條規則包括相關性、比例原則、特權及其他限制。法院可以保護當事人及第三人免受不當的證據開示負擔。

## 5. Summary judgment
## 5. 簡易判決

Under Rule 56, summary judgment may be appropriate when there is no genuine dispute as to any material fact and the movant is entitled to judgment as a matter of law.
依第56條規則，如果不存在任何重大事實的真正爭議，而且提出動議的一方依法有權取得判決，法院可能作出簡易判決。

A genuine dispute means that the evidence could allow a reasonable factfinder to decide the issue for either side. The judge does not simply choose which witness seems more honest when a genuine material dispute exists.
真正爭議是指證據可能讓合理的事實裁決者對該問題作出有利於任一方的決定。如果重大事實確實存在真正爭議，法官不能只是選擇看起來比較誠實的證人。

## 6. Protective orders and sanctions
## 6. 保護令及制裁

A protective order may limit discovery to prevent annoyance, embarrassment, oppression, or undue burden or expense.
保護令可以限制證據開示，以防止騷擾、尷尬、壓迫或不合理的負擔及費用。

Sanctions may apply when a party or lawyer violates procedural duties. The specific sanction depends on the rule, the conduct, and the court's findings.
如果當事人或律師違反程序義務，可能受到制裁。具體制裁取決於適用規則、相關行為及法院認定。

## 理解測驗

1. Does a complaint need to contain every piece of trial evidence?
   起訴狀是否必須包含所有審判證據？
2. What does a Rule 12(b)(6) motion test?
   第12(b)(6)條動議檢查什麼？
3. Is discovery unlimited?
   證據開示是否毫無限制？
4. What is the key question under Rule 56?
   第56條規則下的核心問題是什麼？

**Answers:** 1. No. 2. Whether the complaint states a legally sufficient claim. 3. No. 4. Whether a genuine dispute of material fact exists and the movant is entitled to judgment as a matter of law.
**答案：** 1. 不是。2. 起訴狀是否提出法律上足夠的請求。3. 不是。4. 是否存在重大事實的真正爭議，以及提出動議的一方是否依法有權取得判決。
