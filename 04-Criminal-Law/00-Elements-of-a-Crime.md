# Legality and the Elements of a Crime
# 罪刑法定及犯罪構成要件

## 1. Criminal law versus criminal procedure
## 1. 刑法與刑事程序

Substantive criminal law defines crimes, defenses, and punishment. Criminal procedure governs how the government investigates, charges, and tries a person accused of a crime.
刑法界定犯罪、抗辯及刑罰。刑事程序則規範政府如何調查、起訴及審判被控犯罪的人。

For example, a statute may define theft as a crime. Criminal procedure determines how police may search, how charges are filed, and how guilt is decided.
例如，制定法可能將竊盜定義為犯罪。刑事程序則決定警方如何搜查、如何提出指控，以及如何判斷有罪與否。

## 2. The principle of legality
## 2. 罪刑法定原則

The principle of legality means that a person should not be punished for conduct that was not criminalized by law at the time it occurred. The Constitution restricts ex post facto criminal laws.
罪刑法定原則意味著，一個人不應因行為發生時尚未被法律規定為犯罪的行為而受到處罰。憲法限制具有追溯處罰效果的刑事法律。

Criminal statutes must also provide fair notice and cannot be so vague that people cannot reasonably understand what conduct is prohibited.
刑事制定法也必須提供公平通知，不能模糊到讓人無法合理理解哪些行為受到禁止。

## 3. Elements of an offense
## 3. 犯罪構成要件

A crime is defined by its legal elements. The prosecution generally must prove every required element beyond a reasonable doubt.
犯罪由其法律構成要件界定。控方通常必須排除合理懷疑地證明每一項必要要件。

Many offenses include a prohibited act, a required mental state, a connection between the act and result, and sometimes additional circumstances.
許多犯罪包括被禁止的行為、要求的主觀心理狀態、行為與結果之間的關係，有時還包括其他情況要件。

## 4. Actus reus
## 4. 犯罪行為要件

Actus reus refers to the prohibited conduct or legally relevant omission. A criminal statute may punish an act, a failure to act when there is a legal duty, or a specified result caused by conduct.
Actus reus 指受到禁止的行為，或具有法律意義的不作為。刑事制定法可能處罰某項行為、在負有法律義務時未採取行動，或由行為造成的特定結果。

A person is not usually criminally liable merely for having a bad thought. The law generally requires conduct or an omission that satisfies the offense definition.
一個人通常不會只因為有不好的想法就承擔刑事責任。法律通常要求存在符合犯罪定義的行為或不作為。

## 5. Mens rea
## 5. 主觀罪責要件

Mens rea refers to the mental state required by the offense. Depending on the statute, the required state may include purpose, knowledge, recklessness, or negligence.
Mens rea 指犯罪所要求的心理狀態。依制定法不同，所要求的心理狀態可能包括目的、明知、魯莽或過失。

Do not assume that every crime requires the same mental state. Read the exact statutory language and the controlling interpretation.
不要假設所有犯罪都要求相同的心理狀態。應閱讀確切法條文字及具有控制力的司法解釋。

## 6. Causation
## 6. 因果關係

For result crimes, the prosecution may need to prove both factual cause and proximate cause. Factual cause asks whether the result would have occurred without the defendant's conduct; proximate cause asks whether the result is sufficiently connected and foreseeable under the governing law.
對於結果犯，控方可能必須證明事實因果關係及法律上的近因。事實因果關係會問：如果沒有被告的行為，結果是否仍會發生；近因則會問：依適用法律，結果是否與行為具有足夠關聯及可預見性。

The exact causation test depends on the offense and jurisdiction.
具體因果關係測試取決於犯罪類型及司法管轄區。

## 7. Concurrence
## 7. 行為與主觀罪責的同時存在

Many offenses require the prohibited act and required mental state to exist together in the legally relevant way.
許多犯罪要求被禁止的行為與必要的主觀心理狀態，以法律上相關的方式同時存在。

## 8. Attempt and complicity
## 8. 未遂及共犯責任

Attempt generally requires the required intent and conduct that goes beyond mere preparation under the applicable test.
犯罪未遂通常要求具備必要故意，而且行為依適用測試已超越單純準備階段。

Aiding and abetting may impose liability on a person who intentionally assists another's crime, when the statutory and case-law requirements are met.
在符合制定法及判例要求時，故意協助他人犯罪的人可能依幫助及教唆原則承擔責任。
