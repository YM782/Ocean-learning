# Mental States, Causation, and Criminal Defenses
# 主觀心理狀態、因果關係及刑事抗辯

## 1. Four common mental states
## 1. 四種常見心理狀態

The Model Penal Code uses four important culpability terms: purposely, knowingly, recklessly, and negligently. The Model Penal Code is influential, but it is not itself a single criminal code binding every state.
《模範刑法典》使用四個重要罪責術語：有目的地、明知地、魯莽地及過失地。《模範刑法典》具有重要影響力，但它本身不是一部對所有州都具有拘束力的單一刑法典。

**Purpose:** The actor's conscious object is to engage in conduct or cause a result.
**有目的地：** 行為人的有意識目標是實施某項行為或造成某種結果。

**Knowledge:** The actor is aware that conduct or a circumstance exists, or is practically certain that a result will occur.
**明知地：** 行為人知道某項行為或情況存在，或實際上確信某種結果會發生。

**Recklessness:** The actor consciously disregards a substantial and unjustifiable risk.
**魯莽地：** 行為人有意識地漠視重大且不合理的風險。

**Negligence:** The actor should be aware of a substantial and unjustifiable risk but fails to perceive it.
**過失地：** 行為人理應察覺重大且不合理的風險，卻未能察覺。

## 2. Homicide categories
## 2. 殺人犯罪類型

Homicide means one person causes the death of another, but not every homicide is murder. The legal category depends on the statute, mental state, circumstances, and defenses.
Homicide 指一個人造成另一個人死亡，但並非所有殺人行為都構成謀殺。法律分類取決於制定法、主觀心理狀態、具體情況及抗辯。

Many jurisdictions distinguish forms of murder and manslaughter. The names and elements differ between states and federal statutes, so do not memorize one state's definitions as a nationwide rule.
許多司法管轄區區分不同類型的謀殺及過失殺人。各州及聯邦制定法中的名稱與要件可能不同，因此不要把某一州的定義當成全美統一規則。

## 3. Self-defense
## 3. 正當防衛

Self-defense may justify force when a person reasonably believes force is necessary to protect against an imminent unlawful threat, subject to the jurisdiction's rules on proportionality, retreat, and other limits.
如果一個人合理相信必須使用武力，才能防止迫在眉睫的非法威脅，正當防衛可能使其使用武力具有正當性，但仍須遵守司法管轄區對比例原則、退避義務及其他限制的規定。

Deadly force is subject to stricter rules than nondeadly force. The exact requirements differ among jurisdictions.
致命武力受到比非致命武力更嚴格的規則限制。具體要求因司法管轄區而異。

## 4. Insanity and mental condition
## 4. 精神失常及精神狀態

An insanity defense is a legal defense with a specific test set by the applicable jurisdiction. A medical diagnosis alone does not automatically establish legal insanity.
精神失常抗辯是一項法律抗辯，其具體測試由適用司法管轄區規定。單純取得醫學診斷並不會自動證明法律上的精神失常。

Mental condition may also be relevant to the required mens rea, competency to stand trial, or sentencing, but these are distinct legal questions.
精神狀態也可能與必要主觀罪責、受審能力或量刑有關，但這些是不同的法律問題。

## 5. Mistake and intoxication
## 5. 錯誤及醉酒或藥物影響

A mistake of fact may negate a required mental state if the governing law permits it. A mistake of law is generally not a defense, subject to narrow exceptions.
如果適用法律允許，事實錯誤可能否定犯罪所要求的心理狀態。法律錯誤通常不是抗辯理由，但存在狹窄例外。

Voluntary intoxication has limited and jurisdiction-specific effects. Never assume that being intoxicated automatically excuses criminal conduct.
自願醉酒的法律效果有限，而且因司法管轄區而異。不要假設醉酒會自動免除刑事責任。

## 6. Necessity and duress
## 6. 緊急避難及脅迫

Necessity may apply when a person chooses a lesser harm to avoid a greater imminent harm, if the legal test is met.
如果符合法律測試，當事人為避免更大的迫切危害而選擇較小危害時，可能適用緊急避難抗辯。

Duress may apply when unlawful threats leave a person with no reasonable alternative under the governing law. The defense has important limits and may be unavailable for some offenses.
如果非法威脅使當事人依適用法律沒有合理替代選擇，可能適用脅迫抗辯。這項抗辯有重要限制，對某些犯罪可能不適用。

## 7. Defense analysis
## 7. 抗辯分析

For every defense, ask: What elements must be shown? Who bears the burden? Does the defense negate an element, justify the conduct, or excuse the actor? What limits apply in this jurisdiction?
分析每一項抗辯時，請問：必須證明哪些要件？由誰承擔舉證責任？該抗辯是否否定犯罪要件、使行為正當化，或免除行為人的責任？本司法管轄區有哪些限制？
