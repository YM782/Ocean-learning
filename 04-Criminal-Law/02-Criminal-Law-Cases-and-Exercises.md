# Criminal Law Cases and Exercises
# 刑法判例及練習題

## Case 1 — Morissette v. United States, 342 U.S. 246 (1952)
## 判例一——Morissette v. United States（1952年）

**Core idea:** The Supreme Court discussed the importance of criminal intent and rejected treating certain traditional crimes as strict-liability offenses without clear congressional direction.
**核心概念：** 最高法院討論刑事故意的重要性，並拒絕在國會沒有明確指示的情況下，將某些傳統犯罪視為嚴格責任犯罪。

**Lesson:** Always ask what mental state the statute requires.
**學習重點：** 務必確認制定法要求什麼主觀心理狀態。

## Case 2 — Staples v. United States, 511 U.S. 600 (1994)
## 判例二——Staples v. United States（1994年）

**Core idea:** The Court considered whether the government had to prove knowledge of a weapon's characteristics under a federal firearms statute.
**核心概念：** 最高法院考慮政府是否必須依聯邦槍械制定法，證明被告知道武器的特定性質。

**Lesson:** The text and context of a criminal statute matter when identifying mens rea.
**學習重點：** 判斷主觀罪責時，刑事制定法的文字及上下文十分重要。

## Case 3 — Elonis v. United States, 575 U.S. 723 (2015)
## 判例三——Elonis v. United States（2015年）

**Core idea:** The Court held that negligence alone was not enough to satisfy the mental-state requirement for the federal threat statute at issue. The opinion did not decide the full First Amendment question.
**核心概念：** 最高法院裁定，單純過失不足以滿足該聯邦威脅制定法所要求的心理狀態。該判決並未完整決定第一修正案問題。

**Lesson:** Separate statutory mens rea from constitutional speech analysis.
**學習重點：** 必須區分制定法上的主觀罪責與憲法言論分析。

## Exercise 1 — Identify the elements
## 練習一——辨識犯罪要件

A statute criminalizes knowingly entering a restricted facility without authorization. Which elements should the prosecution identify?
某制定法規定，明知未獲授權而進入限制設施構成犯罪。控方應辨識哪些要件？

**Sample answer:** The prosecution must identify the prohibited entry, the restricted nature of the facility, lack of authorization, and the required knowledge, as defined by the statute and controlling cases.
**參考答案：** 控方必須辨識被禁止的進入行為、設施受到限制的性質、欠缺授權，以及制定法及具有控制力判例所界定的必要明知狀態。

## Exercise 2 — Recklessness or negligence?
## 練習二——魯莽還是過失？

A driver sees a serious risk but consciously ignores it. Which Model Penal Code mental state may be relevant?
駕駛人察覺到嚴重風險，卻有意識地忽視。哪一種《模範刑法典》心理狀態可能相關？

**Answer:** Recklessness, if the risk is substantial and unjustifiable and the remaining legal requirements are met.
**答案：** 可能是魯莽，但仍須符合風險重大且不合理，以及其他法律要求。

## Exercise 3 — Self-defense
## 練習三——正當防衛

A person uses force after an argument, but the other person is walking away and poses no immediate threat. What issue arises?
兩人爭吵後，一個人在對方已經離開且沒有立即威脅時使用武力。會產生什麼法律問題？

**Answer:** The imminence and reasonableness requirements for self-defense may not be satisfied. The full facts and jurisdiction's law must be examined.
**答案：** 正當防衛所要求的迫切性及合理性可能不符合。仍須檢查完整事實及司法管轄區法律。

## IRAC practice
## IRAC 練習

**Issue:** Did the defendant act with the mental state required by the statute?
**爭點：** 被告是否具有制定法要求的主觀心理狀態？

**Rule:** Identify the exact statutory mental-state term and its authoritative interpretation.
**規則：** 找出確切法定心理狀態用語及其權威解釋。

**Application:** Compare what the defendant knew, intended, perceived, or consciously disregarded with the legal definition.
**適用：** 將被告知道、意圖、察覺或有意識忽視的事情，與法律定義進行比較。

**Conclusion:** State whether the evidence could satisfy the required mental state, while identifying missing facts.
**結論：** 說明證據是否可能滿足必要心理狀態，同時指出缺少的事實。
