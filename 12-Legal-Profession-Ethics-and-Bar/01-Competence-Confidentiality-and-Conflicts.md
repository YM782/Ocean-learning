# Competence, Confidentiality, and Conflicts of Interest
# 勝任能力、保密義務及利益衝突

## 1. Competence
## 1. 勝任能力

ABA Model Rule 1.1 states the model duty of competent representation. Competence generally requires the legal knowledge, skill, thoroughness, and preparation reasonably necessary for the matter.
美國律師協會模範規則1.1條規定勝任代理的模範義務。勝任能力通常要求律師具備案件合理需要的法律知識、技能、徹底程度及準備。

A lawyer may need to learn an unfamiliar area, associate with experienced counsel, or decline a matter when competence cannot be provided.
如果律師不熟悉某個領域，可能需要學習、與有經驗的律師合作，或在無法提供勝任服務時拒絕案件。

## 2. Confidentiality
## 2. 保密義務

ABA Model Rule 1.6 generally restricts a lawyer from revealing information relating to the representation unless the client gives informed consent, disclosure is impliedly authorized, or an exception applies.
美國律師協會模範規則1.6條通常限制律師披露與代理有關的資料，除非委託人知情同意、披露屬於默示授權，或適用某項例外。

The ethical duty of confidentiality is broader than the attorney-client evidentiary privilege. The privilege generally concerns protected confidential communications in legal proceedings; the ethical duty covers a wider range of information.
倫理上的保密義務比律師與委託人證據特權更廣。證據特權通常涉及法律程序中受到保護的機密溝通；倫理保密義務則涵蓋更廣泛的資料。

## 3. Conflicts of interest
## 3. 利益衝突

A conflict may arise when a lawyer's responsibilities to one client, another client, a former client, a third person, or the lawyer's own interests materially limit the representation.
如果律師對某一委託人、另一委託人、前委託人、第三人或自身利益的責任，實質限制其代理工作，便可能產生利益衝突。

Model Rule 1.7 addresses current-client conflicts. Some conflicts may be consentable if the rule's conditions are met and affected clients give informed consent confirmed in writing.
模範規則1.7條處理現有委託人之間的利益衝突。如果符合規則條件，且受影響的委託人作出書面確認的知情同意，某些衝突可能可以同意處理。

Other conflicts are not consentable. A lawyer must analyze the specific rule and jurisdiction.
其他利益衝突不能透過同意解決。律師必須分析具體規則及司法管轄區。

## 4. Former and prospective clients
## 4. 前委託人及潛在委託人

Model Rule 1.9 addresses duties to former clients, including limits on representing another person in the same or a substantially related matter when interests are materially adverse, subject to informed consent.
模範規則1.9條處理對前委託人的義務，包括在相同或實質相關事項中，當利益存在重大對立時，限制律師代表另一人，但仍可能存在知情同意的情況。

Model Rule 1.18 addresses duties to prospective clients who consult a lawyer about forming a client-lawyer relationship.
模範規則1.18條處理潛在委託人的義務，即曾向律師諮詢是否建立律師與委託人關係的人。

## 5. Communication and diligence
## 5. 溝通及勤勉

A lawyer generally must keep a client reasonably informed, respond to reasonable requests for information, and act with reasonable diligence and promptness.
律師通常必須合理地向委託人提供最新資訊、回應合理的資料要求，並以合理勤勉及及時的方式處理案件。

## Check your understanding
## 理解測驗

1. Is the ethical duty of confidentiality identical to attorney-client privilege?
   倫理上的保密義務是否與律師及委託人證據特權完全相同？
2. Can every conflict be waived by client consent?
   每一種利益衝突都可以透過委託人同意而放棄嗎？
3. What should a lawyer do when unable to provide competent representation?
   律師無法提供勝任代理時應該怎麼做？

**Answers:** 1. No. 2. No. 3. Take appropriate steps, such as obtaining assistance or declining or withdrawing as permitted by the rules.
**答案：** 1. 不是。2. 不是。3. 採取適當措施，例如尋求協助，或依規則拒絕或退出代理。
