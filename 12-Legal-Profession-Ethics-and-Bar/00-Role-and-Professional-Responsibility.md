# The Lawyer's Role and Professional Responsibility
# 律師角色及專業責任

## 1. What does a lawyer do?
## 1. 律師做什麼？

Lawyers advise clients, research law, negotiate, draft documents, represent parties in court or before agencies, and help people understand legal rights and obligations.
律師為委託人提供意見、研究法律、進行談判、起草文件、在法院或行政機關代表當事人，並協助人們理解法律權利及義務。

Not every lawyer appears in court. Many work in companies, government, public-interest organizations, universities, compliance departments, or private practice.
並非每一位律師都會出庭。許多律師在公司、政府、公益組織、大學、合規部門或私人執業機構工作。

## 2. Lawyer and client
## 2. 律師與委託人

A lawyer-client relationship involves duties of loyalty, confidentiality, competence, communication, and diligence under applicable professional rules.
依適用專業規則，律師與委託人關係涉及忠誠、保密、勝任能力、溝通及勤勉等義務。

The exact duties depend on the jurisdiction's adopted rules, statutes, and case law.
具體義務取決於司法管轄區採納的規則、制定法及判例。

## 3. ABA Model Rules
## 3. 美國律師協會模範規則

The ABA Model Rules of Professional Conduct are influential model rules. They are not automatically binding law in every state; each jurisdiction decides which rules to adopt and how to modify them.
美國律師協會《職業行為模範規則》是具有重要影響力的模範規則。它們不會自動成為每個州的拘束性法律；各司法管轄區會決定採納哪些規則及如何修改。

A lawyer must check the professional rules adopted by the jurisdiction where the lawyer is licensed or practices.
律師必須核實其取得執照或執業所在司法管轄區所採納的專業規則。

## 4. Duties to the court and legal system
## 4. 對法院及法律制度的義務

Lawyers owe duties not only to clients but also to courts, opposing counsel, witnesses, and the administration of justice.
律師不僅對委託人負有義務，也對法院、對方律師、證人及司法公正負有義務。

Examples include candor to the tribunal, fairness to opposing parties, and restrictions on frivolous claims or misleading evidence.
例子包括對法庭誠實、對對方當事人公平，以及不得提出無理請求或使用誤導性證據。

## 5. Public service and access to justice
## 5. 公共服務及司法可及性

Lawyers may support access to justice through pro bono work, legal aid, public defense, community education, and policy work.
律師可以透過公益法律服務、法律援助、公設辯護、社區教育及政策工作，促進司法可及性。
