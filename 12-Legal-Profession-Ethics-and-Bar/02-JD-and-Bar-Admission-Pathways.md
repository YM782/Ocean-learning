# JD Education and U.S. Bar Admission
# JD 法律教育及美國律師准入

## 1. What is a JD?
## 1. 什麼是 JD？

The Juris Doctor (JD) is the professional law degree commonly used as the standard route to legal practice in many U.S. jurisdictions.
Juris Doctor（JD，法律博士）是美國許多司法管轄區通往法律執業的常見標準專業學位。

A JD is generally a graduate-level program. Applicants normally need an undergraduate degree or its recognized equivalent before enrolling, subject to the law school's admissions policies.
JD 通常屬於研究生階段課程。申請者通常需要先取得學士學位或獲認可的同等學歷，但仍須遵守法學院招生政策。

Most full-time JD programs take about three academic years. Part-time programs may take longer.
大多數全日制 JD 課程約需三個學年。兼讀制課程可能需要更長時間。

## 2. Choosing a law school
## 2. 選擇法學院

For a conventional route to bar eligibility, applicants should carefully verify whether a law school is approved by the American Bar Association and whether the target jurisdiction accepts its degree.
對於常見的律師資格途徑，申請者應仔細核實法學院是否獲美國律師協會認可，以及目標司法管轄區是否接受該學位。

Admission requirements vary. Schools may consider undergraduate records, LSAT or other accepted tests, recommendations, personal statements, work experience, and other factors.
入學要求因校而異。學校可能考慮大學成績、LSAT 或其他獲接受的考試、推薦信、個人陳述、工作經驗及其他因素。

## 3. Bar admission is state-specific
## 3. 律師准入資格因州而異

There is no single nationwide U.S. bar license. Each state or jurisdiction sets its own admission rules.
美國不存在一張全國通用的單一律師執照。每個州或司法管轄區都制定自己的准入規則。

Common requirements may include an approved legal education, a bar examination, the MPRE or another ethics requirement, a character-and-fitness review, and administrative steps.
常見要求可能包括獲認可的法律教育、律師考試、MPRE 或其他倫理要求、品格及適任性審查，以及行政程序。

## 4. Foreign legal education
## 4. 外國法律教育背景

Some jurisdictions provide limited routes for applicants educated in foreign law schools, sometimes involving evaluation of prior legal education or an LL.M. degree. Requirements differ substantially.
某些司法管轄區為在外國法學院受教育的申請者提供有限途徑，有時需要評估先前法律教育或取得 LL.M. 學位。各地要求差異很大。

A foreign undergraduate degree alone should not be assumed to qualify someone for a U.S. bar examination. The target jurisdiction's bar authority must evaluate the applicant's exact education and circumstances.
不要假設只有外國大學學士學位就能使某人取得參加美國律師考試的資格。目標司法管轄區的律師准入機關必須評估申請者的具體教育背景及情況。

## 5. A practical pathway
## 5. 實際規劃路線

1. Choose the state where you hope to practice.
   選擇你希望執業的州。
2. Read that state's official bar-admission rules.
   閱讀該州官方律師准入規則。
3. Determine whether you need a JD, an LL.M., or another qualifying education route.
   確認你需要 JD、LL.M.，還是其他合資格教育途徑。
4. Compare law-school admission requirements and costs.
   比較法學院入學要求及費用。
5. Confirm examination, ethics, character-and-fitness, and licensing requirements.
   確認考試、倫理、品格及適任性，以及執照要求。
6. Recheck the rules before applying because admission standards can change.
   申請前再次核實規則，因為准入標準可能改變。

## 6. 官方入門資源

  美國律師協會法學院資訊。

  你打算執業所在州的官方律師准入機關。

## Important caution
## 重要提醒

A JD or bar examination is not a guarantee of admission. Character-and-fitness review and every other jurisdiction-specific requirement must also be satisfied.
取得 JD 或通過律師考試並不保證獲准執業。申請者仍須通過品格及適任性審查，並符合司法管轄區的所有其他要求。
