# Legal Profession, Ethics and Bar
# 法律職業、倫理與律師資格

## What this course is about
## 本課程內容

Begin after learning the basic legal system and research methods.
先了解基本法律制度及研究方法，再開始本課。

This is an introductory course guide, not a complete textbook or legal opinion.
本頁是入門課程指南，並非完整教科書或法律意見。

## Start with three ideas
## 從三個概念開始

### 1. Professional duties
### 1. 專業義務

Learn competence, diligence, confidentiality, and conflicts of interest.
學習專業能力、勤勉、保密及利益衝突。

### 2. JD education
### 2. JD 法律教育

Understand what a U.S. JD program is and how admissions work.
了解美國 JD 課程是什麼，以及入學申請如何運作。

### 3. Bar admission
### 3. 律師准入

Learn that bar rules differ by state and must be checked with the official authority.
學習各州律師准入規則不同，必須向官方主管機關核實。


## Beginner practice
## 初學者練習

Choose one state and find its official bar-admission authority; do not rely only on a general website.
選擇一個州並找出該州官方律師准入機關；不要只依賴一般性網站。

## How to continue
## 如何繼續學習

Study one topic at a time and write down unfamiliar terms.
一次只學習一個主題，並記錄不熟悉的術語。

For every case, identify the court, jurisdiction, date, issue, rule, holding, and reasoning.
閱讀每一宗判例時，辨識法院、司法管轄區、日期、爭點、規則、裁判要旨及推理。

Always verify the law that applies to the relevant jurisdiction and date.
務必核實相關司法管轄區及日期所適用的法律。

**僅供教育學習，不構成法律意見。**
