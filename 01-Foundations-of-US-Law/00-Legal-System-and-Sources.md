# The U.S. Legal System and Sources of Law
# 美國法律制度與法律淵源

## 1. What is law?
## 1. 什麼是法律？

Law is a system of rules recognized and enforced by public institutions. It establishes rights, duties, procedures, and consequences.
法律是由公共機關承認並執行的一套規則，用來建立權利、義務、程序及法律後果。

A law may tell people what they must do, what they may do, what they must not do, and how disputes are resolved.
法律可以規定人們必須做什麼、可以做什麼、不得做什麼，以及爭議應如何解決。

Law is not exactly the same as morality. An action may be morally criticized without being illegal, and an action may be legal even when some people consider it unfair.
法律與道德並不完全相同。一項行為可能受到道德批評但並不違法；一項行為也可能合法，即使有人認為它不公平。

## 2. The Constitution
## 2. 憲法

The U.S. Constitution is the supreme law of the federal system. It creates the federal government, distributes governmental powers, and limits what government may do.
《美國憲法》是聯邦制度中的最高法律。它建立聯邦政府、分配政府權力，並限制政府可以採取的行動。

The Constitution establishes three federal branches: Congress makes federal statutes, the President leads the executive branch, and the federal judiciary interprets and applies law in cases.
憲法建立三個聯邦部門：國會制定聯邦法律、總統領導行政部門，而聯邦司法部門在案件中解釋及適用法律。

The Constitution also protects individual rights. The Bill of Rights contains the first ten amendments, while later amendments address subjects such as slavery, citizenship, equal protection, voting, and presidential terms.
憲法也保障個人權利。《權利法案》包含前十條修正案，後來的修正案則處理奴隸制度、公民身分、平等保護、投票及總統任期等議題。

## 3. Statutes
## 3. 制定法

A statute is a written law enacted by a legislative body. At the federal level, Congress passes bills that become federal statutes after the constitutional lawmaking process is completed.
制定法是由立法機關通過的成文法律。在聯邦層級，國會通過法案，完成憲法規定的立法程序後，法案便成為聯邦制定法。

States also have legislatures that enact state statutes. A federal statute does not automatically govern every subject; Congress must act within its constitutional authority.
各州也有自己的立法機關，可以制定州法律。聯邦制定法不會自動管轄所有事項；國會必須在憲法授予的權限範圍內立法。

## 4. Regulations
## 4. 行政規章

Congress sometimes gives an executive agency authority to implement a statute. The agency may issue regulations under the authority and procedures provided by law.
國會有時會授權行政機關執行某項制定法。行政機關可以依照法律授予的權限及程序制定行政規章。

A regulation is not simply an agency employee's personal opinion. Its legal force depends on statutory authority, required procedures, and judicial review.
行政規章並不是行政機關職員的個人意見。它的法律效力取決於制定法授權、法定程序及司法審查。

## 5. Judicial decisions
## 5. 司法判決

Courts resolve disputes by applying legal rules to facts. Their written opinions may explain the rule, interpret statutes, interpret the Constitution, and create precedent.
法院透過將法律規則適用於事實來解決爭議。法院的書面判決意見可能解釋法律規則、制定法、憲法，並形成判例先例。

A judicial opinion is not the same thing as a statute. A statute is enacted by a legislature; a judicial opinion explains how a court resolves a legal dispute.
司法判決意見與制定法不是同一回事。制定法由立法機關制定；司法判決意見則解釋法院如何解決法律爭議。

## 6. Federal law and state law
## 6. 聯邦法律與州法律

The United States is a federal system. Some powers belong to the federal government, some are exercised by states, and some areas involve both levels of government.
美國採取聯邦制度。有些權力屬於聯邦政府，有些由各州行使，另有一些領域涉及兩級政府。

The Supremacy Clause makes valid federal law supreme over conflicting state law when federal law is within constitutional authority. It does not mean that every federal policy overrides every state rule.
最高法條款規定，在聯邦法律具有憲法權限且與州法衝突時，有效的聯邦法律具有優先效力。這不代表每一項聯邦政策都會取代所有州規則。

A lawyer must identify the jurisdiction before applying a rule. A federal criminal procedure rule, for example, should not automatically be described as the procedure used in every state court.
律師在適用規則前必須先確認司法管轄區。例如，聯邦刑事訴訟規則不能自動被描述為每一個州法院都採用的程序。

## 7. A simple source-check method
## 7. 簡單的法律來源核查方法

When you read a legal statement, ask four questions: Who issued it? What authority supports it? Where does it apply? Is it still current?
閱讀一項法律說法時，請問四個問題：誰發布了它？它的權限依據是什麼？它在哪裡適用？它現在是否仍然有效？

Prefer the Constitution, official statutes, official court rules, and judicial opinions over unsourced social-media summaries.
相較於沒有來源的社群媒體摘要，應優先查閱憲法、官方制定法、官方法院規則及司法判決。

## Check your understanding
## 理解測驗

1. Is a judicial opinion the same as a statute?
   司法判決意見是否等同制定法？
2. Why must you identify whether a case is federal or state?
   為什麼必須確認案件屬於聯邦還是州？
3. Who usually issues regulations?
   通常由誰制定行政規章？

**Answers:** 1. No. 2. The applicable authority and procedure may differ. 3. Executive agencies acting under delegated statutory authority.
**答案：** 1. 不是。2. 適用的法律權限及程序可能不同。3. 依制定法授權行事的行政機關。
