# Common Law, Precedent, and Legal Interpretation
# 普通法、判例先例及法律解釋

## 1. What is common law?
## 1. 什麼是普通法？

Common law is a legal tradition in which judicial decisions play an important role in developing legal rules. It is not simply “law that is common” or a rule that applies everywhere.
普通法是一種法律傳統，司法判決在法律規則的發展中扮演重要角色。它並不是「普遍存在的法律」，也不是在任何地方都適用的規則。

In the United States, common-law reasoning operates alongside constitutions, statutes, regulations, and court rules. A statute may replace or modify a common-law rule.
在美國，普通法推理與憲法、制定法、行政規章及法院規則並行運作。制定法可能取代或修改普通法規則。

## 2. Stare decisis
## 2. 遵循先例原則

Stare decisis means that courts generally follow applicable prior decisions. The principle supports consistency, predictability, and equal treatment.
Stare decisis 意指法院通常遵循適用的先前判決。這項原則有助於法律一致性、可預測性及平等對待。

A precedent may be binding or persuasive. A binding precedent must be followed by a lower court within the relevant hierarchy and jurisdiction, unless a recognized exception applies.
判例先例可能具有拘束力，也可能只有說服力。具有拘束力的先例通常必須由相關法院層級及司法管轄區內的下級法院遵循，除非存在法律承認的例外。

A persuasive decision may help a court understand an issue, but the court is not required to follow it. Decisions from another jurisdiction are often persuasive rather than binding.
具有說服力的判決可以幫助法院理解法律問題，但法院不一定必須遵循。來自其他司法管轄區的判決通常具有說服力，而非拘束力。

## 3. Court hierarchy matters
## 3. 法院層級十分重要

A U.S. Supreme Court holding on federal law binds lower federal courts and state courts on the federal issue. A federal court of appeals generally binds federal district courts within its circuit on questions of federal law.
美國最高法院對聯邦法律作出的裁判要旨，會在相關聯邦法律問題上拘束下級聯邦法院及州法院。聯邦上訴法院通常會在聯邦法律問題上拘束其巡迴區內的聯邦地方法院。

A state supreme court is generally the final authority on that state's law, subject to review by the U.S. Supreme Court on an applicable federal question.
州最高法院通常是該州法律的最終權威；如果涉及適用的聯邦問題，仍可能受到美國最高法院審查。

## 4. Holding and dicta
## 4. 裁判要旨與附帶意見

The holding is the legal rule or conclusion necessary to decide the case. It is the part most likely to carry precedential force.
Holding 是解決案件所必需的法律規則或結論，也是最可能具有判例效力的部分。

Dicta are statements that are not necessary to the decision. Dicta may be informative or persuasive, but they generally do not have the same precedential force as the holding.
Dicta 是作出判決時並非必要的陳述。附帶意見可能具有參考或說服價值，但通常不具有與裁判要旨相同的判例效力。

## 5. Distinguishing a case
## 5. 區別判例

A court may distinguish a precedent when a legally important fact is different. The court explains why the earlier rule does not control the new case.
如果某項具有法律重要性的事實不同，法院可能區別先前判例。法院會解釋為什麼先前規則不控制目前案件。

Distinguishing is not the same as overruling. Overruling occurs when a court rejects a prior precedent as no longer controlling law.
區別判例不等於推翻判例。推翻判例是法院認定先前判例不再是具有控制力的法律。

## 6. Statutory interpretation
## 6. 制定法解釋

When interpreting a statute, courts often begin with the enacted text, reading words in context and considering the structure of the statute.
解釋制定法時，法院通常先從正式通過的法條文字開始，並結合上下文及整部法律的結構理解用語。

Courts may consider canons of interpretation, definitions, related provisions, and the legal context. The weight given to legislative history and other materials can vary by court and issue.
法院可能考慮法律解釋原則、法定定義、相關條文及法律背景。立法歷史及其他資料的權重，可能因法院及爭點而不同。

A beginner should not assume that a dictionary definition alone resolves a legal dispute. Legal words can have specialized meanings in context.
初學者不應假設只查字典定義就能解決法律爭議。法律用語在特定上下文中可能具有專門含義。

## 7. Constitutional interpretation
## 7. 憲法解釋

Courts use different interpretive approaches, including textual, historical, structural, doctrinal, and precedent-based reasoning. Judges may disagree about how these approaches apply.
法院會採用不同的解釋方法，包括文本、歷史、結構、法律原則及判例推理。法官可能對這些方法應如何適用存在分歧。

The fact that judges disagree does not mean that legal reasoning is arbitrary. A sound opinion identifies the legal materials, explains the reasoning, and responds to competing arguments.
法官存在分歧並不代表法律推理完全任意。一份有說服力的判決意見會指出法律資料、解釋推理，並回應相反論點。

## 8. Example: a precedent in practice
## 8. 實例：實務中的判例先例

Suppose a federal appellate court holds that a particular federal statute covers conduct X. A district court within that appellate circuit generally must apply that holding to materially similar cases.
假設某聯邦上訴法院裁定某項聯邦制定法涵蓋行為 X。該上訴法院巡迴區內的地方法院，通常必須將這項裁判要旨適用於具有實質相似性的案件。

A district court in another circuit may consider the first court's reasoning, but it may not be bound by it. The U.S. Supreme Court may later resolve a conflict between circuits.
另一個巡迴區的地方法院可以參考第一個法院的推理，但不一定受到拘束。美國最高法院日後可能解決不同巡迴區之間的法律分歧。

## 理解測驗

   具有拘束力的法律權威與具有說服力的法律權威有什麼不同？
2. Is dicta always binding?
   附帶意見是否一定具有拘束力？
3. Does distinguishing a case mean that the earlier case was overruled?
   區別判例是否代表先前判例已被推翻？

**Answers:** 1. Binding authority must generally be followed; persuasive authority may be considered. 2. No. 3. No.
**答案：** 1. 拘束性權威通常必須遵循；說服性權威可以參考。2. 不是。3. 不是。
