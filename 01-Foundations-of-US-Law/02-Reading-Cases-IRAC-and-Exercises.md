# Reading Cases, IRAC, and Beginner Exercises
# 閱讀判例、IRAC 及初學者練習

## 1. Why read cases?
## 1. 為什麼要閱讀判例？

A case is a dispute decided by a court. Reading cases helps you see how judges identify legal questions, select rules, evaluate facts, and explain a result.
判例是由法院裁決的爭議。閱讀判例可以讓你了解法官如何辨識法律問題、選擇規則、評估事實，以及解釋裁判結果。

A case is not merely a story. The same event can lead to different legal outcomes depending on the jurisdiction, the governing rule, and the evidence.
判例不只是故事。同一事件可能因司法管轄區、適用規則及證據不同，而產生不同法律結果。

## 2. The seven parts of a case
## 2. 判例的七個部分

- **Facts:** What happened that matters legally?
  **事實：** 發生了什麼具有法律重要性的事情？
- **Procedural history:** Which courts heard the case, and what happened before?
  **程序經過：** 哪些法院審理過案件，之前發生了什麼？
- **Issue:** What precise legal question must the court answer?
  **爭點：** 法院必須回答哪一個具體法律問題？
- **Rule:** What constitutional provision, statute, regulation, or precedent applies?
  **規則：** 適用哪項憲法條文、制定法、行政規章或判例？
- **Holding:** How did the court answer the legal issue?
  **裁判要旨：** 法院如何回答法律爭點？
- **Reasoning:** Why did the court reach that answer?
  **判決推理：** 法院為什麼得出這個答案？
- **Disposition:** What happened to the case after the decision?
  **裁判結果：** 判決後案件如何處理？

## 3. IRAC
## 3. IRAC 法律分析法

IRAC is a common structure for organizing legal analysis: Issue, Rule, Application, and Conclusion.
IRAC 是一種常見的法律分析架構，分別代表爭點、規則、適用及結論。

**Issue:** State the legal question in one sentence.
**爭點：** 用一句話說明法律問題。

**Rule:** State the governing legal rule accurately and identify its source.
**規則：** 準確說明適用的法律規則，並指出來源。

**Application:** Compare each important fact with the elements of the rule. Explain both supporting and opposing arguments.
**適用：** 將每項重要事實與法律規則的要件比較，並解釋支持及反對的論點。

**Conclusion:** Give a reasoned answer, while acknowledging uncertainty or missing facts.
**結論：** 提出有理由支持的答案，同時承認不確定性或缺少的事實。

## 4. Worked example
## 4. 完整示範

**Hypothetical facts:** A city requires a permit for every public demonstration. A resident is denied a permit solely because the official dislikes the resident's criticism of the mayor.
**假設事實：** 某城市要求所有公開示威活動都必須取得許可。一名居民僅因官員不喜歡他對市長的批評，就被拒絕發出許可。

**Issue:** Does denying the permit because of the speaker's viewpoint violate the First Amendment?
**爭點：** 僅因發言者的觀點而拒絕許可，是否違反第一修正案？

**Rule:** Government generally may not discriminate against speech because of its viewpoint. The precise analysis depends on the type of forum, the permit scheme, and applicable doctrine.
**規則：** 政府通常不得因言論觀點而歧視言論。具體分析取決於言論場所類型、許可制度及適用法律原則。

**Application:** The stated reason is the resident's criticism of the mayor, which suggests viewpoint discrimination. The court would also examine the actual permit rules, the official's authority, and the full record.
**適用：** 已知拒絕理由是居民批評市長，這可能顯示觀點歧視。法院也會檢查實際許可規則、官員權限及完整紀錄。

**Conclusion:** The facts raise a serious First Amendment concern. A final conclusion requires the complete facts and governing precedent.
**結論：** 這些事實引發嚴重的第一修正案問題。最終結論仍需要完整事實及適用判例。

## 5. Common beginner mistakes
## 5. 初學者常見錯誤

- Do not write a conclusion before identifying the rule.
  不要在確認法律規則之前就直接寫結論。
- Do not treat an allegation as a proven fact.
  不要把指控當成已經證明的事實。
- Do not quote a case without checking its jurisdiction and date.
  不要在未核實司法管轄區及日期前引用判例。
- Do not assume that one case answers every similar question.
  不要假設一宗判例可以回答所有相似問題。
- Do not confuse a court's holding with every sentence in its opinion.
  不要把法院的裁判要旨與判決意見中的每一句話混為一談。

## 6. Practice
## 6. 練習題

**Question 1:** A state trial court follows a U.S. Supreme Court decision interpreting the federal Constitution. Is that decision generally binding on the state court?
**問題一：** 州初審法院遵循美國最高法院對聯邦憲法的解釋。該判決通常是否對州法院具有拘束力？

**Answer:** Yes, on the federal constitutional issue, the U.S. Supreme Court's controlling holding binds state courts.
**答案：** 是。就聯邦憲法問題而言，美國最高法院具有控制力的裁判要旨會拘束州法院。

**Question 2:** A court mentions a broad policy idea that was not necessary to decide the case. Is that necessarily the holding?
**問題二：** 法院提到一項並非解決案件所必需的廣泛政策觀點。這是否必然屬於裁判要旨？

**Answer:** No. It may be dicta.
**答案：** 不是。它可能屬於附帶意見。

**Question 3:** What should you do if a legal rule may have changed?
**問題三：** 如果法律規則可能已經改變，你應該怎麼做？

**Answer:** Check the current official statute or rule and later controlling cases.
**答案：** 查閱現行官方制定法或規則，以及後續具有控制力的判例。
