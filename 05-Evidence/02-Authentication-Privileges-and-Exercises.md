# Authentication, Privileges, and Evidence Exercises
# 真實性認證、證據特權及證據練習

## 1. Authentication and identification
## 1. 真實性認證及識別

Rule 901 requires evidence sufficient to support a finding that an item is what its proponent claims. A witness with personal knowledge may identify a photograph; metadata or system evidence may help authenticate a digital record.
第901條規則要求提供足夠證據，支持裁決者認定某物品確實是提出者所聲稱的內容。具有親身知識的證人可以識別照片；中繼資料或系統證據也可能有助於認證數位紀錄。

Rule 902 identifies categories of evidence that may be self-authenticating, subject to the rule's conditions.
第902條規則列出某些在符合規定條件時可以自行認證的證據類別。

## 2. Best evidence rule
## 2. 最佳證據規則

Rules 1001–1008 address proving the content of writings, recordings, and photographs. Rule 1002 generally requires an original when a party seeks to prove the content of a writing, recording, or photograph, subject to exceptions.
第1001至1008條規則處理如何證明文字、錄音及照片的內容。第1002條通常要求當事人在證明文字、錄音或照片內容時提出原件，但仍存在例外。

The rule does not mean that an original is required every time a document is mentioned. The key question is whether a party is trying to prove the content itself.
這項規則並不代表每次提及文件都必須提出原件。核心問題是當事人是否正在試圖證明文件本身的內容。

## 3. Privileges
## 3. 證據特權

A privilege may protect certain confidential communications from compelled disclosure. The exact privilege depends on governing law and the relationship involved.
證據特權可能保護某些機密溝通，使其免於被強制披露。具體特權取決於適用法律及相關關係。

Attorney-client privilege generally protects confidential communications between a client and lawyer made for the purpose of seeking or providing legal advice. It does not automatically protect every document a lawyer receives or every fact a client knows.
律師與委託人特權通常保護委託人與律師之間，為尋求或提供法律意見而作出的機密溝通。它不會自動保護律師收到的每份文件，也不會保護委託人知道的每一項事實。

The work-product doctrine is different. It generally protects certain materials prepared in anticipation of litigation, subject to rules and exceptions.
工作成果原則則有所不同。它通常保護為預期訴訟而準備的某些資料，但仍受到規則及例外限制。

## 4. Digital evidence
## 4. 數位證據

Digital evidence may include emails, text messages, logs, location data, photographs, cloud records, and computer files. A party may need to establish authenticity, relevance, hearsay status, and compliance with privacy or discovery rules.
數位證據可能包括電子郵件、簡訊、日誌、位置資料、照片、雲端紀錄及電腦檔案。當事人可能需要證明真實性、相關性、傳聞性質，以及符合隱私或證據開示規則。

A screenshot alone may not answer who created a message, whether it was altered, or whether the displayed account belongs to the alleged sender. Context and corroboration matter.
單獨一張螢幕截圖可能無法回答訊息由誰建立、是否遭到修改，或顯示的帳戶是否屬於被指稱的發送者。上下文及佐證十分重要。

## Exercise 1 — Hearsay purpose
## 練習一——傳聞陳述的提出目的

A witness testifies that a person said, “The floor is wet,” to show that the store had notice of a dangerous condition, not to prove that the floor was actually wet. Is the statement necessarily hearsay?
證人作證指出某人曾說「地板是濕的」，提出這句話是為了證明商店已收到危險情況的通知，而不是為了證明地板當時真的濕了。這句話是否必然屬於傳聞？

**Answer:** Not necessarily. If offered to show notice rather than the truth of the statement, it may be non-hearsay for that purpose. The court still considers relevance and other rules.
**答案：** 不一定。如果提出陳述是為了證明通知，而不是證明內容真實，它可能不屬於該目的下的傳聞。法院仍須考慮相關性及其他規則。

## Exercise 2 — Authentication
## 練習二——真實性認證

A party offers a digital chat screenshot but provides no witness, device information, account evidence, or metadata. What issue should the opponent raise?
一方提出數位聊天截圖，卻沒有提供證人、裝置資料、帳戶證據或中繼資料。對方應提出什麼問題？

**Answer:** Whether there is sufficient evidence to authenticate the screenshot under Rule 901. The proponent may be able to supply additional evidence.
**答案：** 是否有足夠證據依第901條規則認證截圖。提出證據的一方可能可以補充其他資料。

## Exercise 3 — Attorney-client privilege
## 練習三——律師與委託人特權

A client tells a lawyer confidentially, “I need legal advice about this contract.” Is the communication potentially privileged?
委託人向律師保密地說：「我需要就這份契約取得法律意見。」這項溝通是否可能受到特權保護？

**Answer:** Yes, if the communication is confidential and made for the purpose of seeking legal advice, and no waiver or exception applies.
**答案：** 是，但前提是該溝通具有機密性，且目的是尋求法律意見，同時不存在放棄特權或例外情況。
