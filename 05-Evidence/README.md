# Evidence
# 證據法

**Level: beginner | Main framework: Federal Rules of Evidence**
**程度：初學者｜主要架構：《聯邦證據規則》**

Evidence law determines what information may be presented to a factfinder and how it may be used. This course introduces federal evidence rules; states may have different rules.
證據法決定哪些資料可以提交給事實裁決者，以及資料可以如何使用。本課程介紹聯邦證據規則；各州可能採用不同規則。
