# Evidence
# 證據法

## What this course is about
## 本課程內容

Begin after you understand facts, witnesses, and the burden of proof.
先理解事實、證人及舉證責任，再開始本課。

This is an introductory course guide, not a complete textbook or legal opinion.
本頁是入門課程指南，並非完整教科書或法律意見。

## Start with three ideas
## 從三個概念開始

### 1. Relevance
### 1. 關聯性

Learn why some information helps prove a fact and other information does not.
學習為什麼某些資訊有助於證明事實，而另一些資訊則沒有幫助。

### 2. Hearsay
### 2. 傳聞證據

Learn the basic idea of out-of-court statements and why exceptions exist.
學習庭外陳述的基本概念，以及傳聞例外為什麼存在。

### 3. Witnesses and experts
### 3. 證人與專家

Understand testimony, credibility, and expert evidence at an introductory level.
以入門程度理解證言、可信度及專家證據。


## Beginner practice
## 初學者練習

Explain the difference between a witness's own observation and a statement the witness heard from another person.
解釋證人親自觀察的內容與從他人聽來的陳述有何不同。

## How to continue
## 如何繼續學習

Study one topic at a time and write down unfamiliar terms.
一次只學習一個主題，並記錄不熟悉的術語。

For every case, identify the court, jurisdiction, date, issue, rule, holding, and reasoning.
閱讀每一宗判例時，辨識法院、司法管轄區、日期、爭點、規則、裁判要旨及推理。

Always verify the law that applies to the relevant jurisdiction and date.
務必核實相關司法管轄區及日期所適用的法律。
