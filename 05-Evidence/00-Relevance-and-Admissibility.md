# Relevance and Admissibility
# 證據相關性及可採性

## 1. What is evidence law?
## 1. 什麼是證據法？

Evidence rules govern what information a court may consider and the purpose for which it may be considered. The judge often decides admissibility, while the jury evaluates the weight of admitted evidence in a jury trial.
證據規則規範法院可以考慮哪些資料，以及可以為何種目的考慮這些資料。在陪審團審判中，法官通常決定證據是否可採，而陪審團評估已採納證據的證明力。

Evidence can include testimony, documents, photographs, recordings, physical objects, and digital information.
證據可以包括證人證言、文件、照片、錄音、實體物品及數位資料。

## 2. Relevance — Rules 401 and 402
## 2. 相關性——第401及402條規則

Under Rule 401, evidence is relevant if it has any tendency to make a fact of consequence more or less probable than it would be without the evidence.
依第401條規則，如果某項證據能使一項具有後果重要性的事實，相較於沒有該證據時更可能或更不可能成立，該證據便具有相關性。

Rule 402 generally makes relevant evidence admissible unless the Constitution, a statute, the Evidence Rules, or other applicable rules provide otherwise. Irrelevant evidence is not admissible.
第402條規則通常規定相關證據可以採納，除非憲法、制定法、《證據規則》或其他適用規則另有規定。不相關的證據不得採納。

## 3. Rule 403 balancing
## 3. 第403條權衡

A court may exclude relevant evidence if its probative value is substantially outweighed by dangers such as unfair prejudice, confusing the issues, misleading the jury, undue delay, wasting time, or needlessly presenting cumulative evidence.
如果相關證據的證明價值被不公平偏見、混淆爭點、誤導陪審團、不當延誤、浪費時間或不必要重複證據等危險實質性地超過，法院可以排除該證據。

Unfair prejudice does not mean that evidence is harmful to one side. Most useful evidence harms one side's case. The concern is an improper basis for decision, such as emotional reaction rather than rational evaluation.
不公平偏見並不是指證據對某一方不利。大多數有用證據都會削弱其中一方的案件。真正的問題是不當的裁決依據，例如以情緒反應取代理性評估。

## 4. Character and other acts
## 4. 品格及其他行為

Rule 404 generally limits using a person's character to prove that the person acted in accordance with that character on a particular occasion.
第404條規則通常限制以某人的品格來證明該人在某個特定場合依照該品格行事。

Other-act evidence may be admissible for a proper non-character purpose, such as motive, opportunity, intent, preparation, plan, knowledge, identity, or absence of mistake, subject to the rule and other limits.
其他行為證據可能為適當的非品格目的而獲准採納，例如動機、機會、意圖、準備、計畫、知識、身分或排除錯誤，但仍須符合規則及其他限制。

## 5. Specialized exclusions
## 5. 特定證據排除規則

The Federal Rules contain special rules for subsequent remedial measures (Rule 407), compromise offers and negotiations (Rule 408), offers to pay medical expenses (Rule 409), pleas and plea discussions (Rule 410), and liability insurance (Rule 411).
《聯邦證據規則》對後續補救措施（第407條）、和解提議及談判（第408條）、支付醫療費用的提議（第409條）、認罪及認罪討論（第410條），以及責任保險（第411條）設有特別規則。

These rules often restrict a particular use of evidence rather than creating a universal rule that the information can never be used for any purpose.
這些規則通常限制證據的特定用途，而不是建立一項普遍規則，認定相關資料在任何目的下都永遠不能使用。

## 6. Authentication
## 6. 真實性認證

Before a document, photograph, recording, or other item can be admitted, the proponent generally must produce evidence sufficient to support a finding that the item is what the proponent claims it is.
文件、照片、錄音或其他物品要被採納，提出證據的一方通常必須提供足夠資料，支持裁決者認定該物品確實是其所聲稱的內容。

Authentication does not automatically prove that the content is true. It addresses identity or genuineness, not every issue of accuracy or weight.
真實性認證不會自動證明內容真實。它處理的是身分或真實性問題，而不是所有準確性或證明力問題。

## 理解測驗

   依第401條規則，什麼會使證據具有相關性？

   第403條規則是否排除所有對一方不利的證據？

   真實性認證是否證明文件中的陳述都是真的？
