# Witnesses and Hearsay
# 證人及傳聞證據

## 1. Witness competence and personal knowledge
## 1. 證人資格及親身知識

Rule 601 generally presumes that every person is competent to be a witness unless a rule provides otherwise. Rule 602 requires evidence sufficient to support a finding that a witness has personal knowledge of the matter.
第601條規則通常推定每個人都有資格作證，除非規則另有規定。第602條規則要求提供足夠證據，支持證人對所述事項具有親身知識的認定。

A witness should testify about what the witness perceived, not merely repeat speculation.
證人應就自己親身感知的事情作證，而不是單純重複猜測。

## 2. Credibility and impeachment
## 2. 可信度及彈劾

Rule 607 allows any party, including the party that called the witness, to attack the witness's credibility.
第607條規則允許任何一方，包括傳召該證人的一方，質疑證人的可信度。

Rules 608 and 609 address certain forms of character evidence and prior convictions used to evaluate credibility. Their requirements and limits must be checked carefully.
第608及609條規則處理某些用來評估可信度的品格證據及先前定罪。必須仔細核實其要求及限制。

## 3. Lay and expert opinions
## 3. 一般證人及專家意見

Rule 701 governs certain opinions by lay witnesses. Such opinions generally must be rationally based on the witness's perception, helpful, and not based on specialized knowledge covered by Rule 702.
第701條規則規範一般證人的某些意見。這些意見通常必須合理地建立在證人的感知之上、有助於理解證言，而且不能依賴第702條所規範的專業知識。

Rule 702 governs expert testimony. The court must assess whether the expert's specialized knowledge will help the factfinder and whether the testimony meets reliability requirements.
第702條規則規範專家證言。法院必須評估專家的專業知識是否有助於事實裁決者，以及證言是否符合可靠性要求。

## 4. What is hearsay?
## 4. 什麼是傳聞證據？

Under Rule 801, hearsay generally involves an out-of-court statement offered to prove the truth of what the statement asserts.
依第801條規則，傳聞通常涉及在法庭外作出的陳述，而提出該陳述的目的是證明其內容所主張的事情為真。

The definition has technical parts. A statement may be spoken, written, or nonverbal conduct intended as an assertion.
這項定義包含技術性要素。陳述可能是口頭、書面，或意圖表達主張的非語言行為。

Not every statement made outside court is hearsay. If a statement is offered for another relevant purpose, such as notice or its effect on the listener, it may not be offered for its truth.
並非每一項在法庭外作出的陳述都是傳聞。如果提出陳述是為了其他相關目的，例如證明通知或對聽者造成的影響，而不是證明內容為真，它可能不屬於為真實性目的提出的傳聞。

## 5. Exclusions and exceptions
## 5. 排除於傳聞定義之外的陳述及例外

Rule 801(d) identifies certain statements that are treated as not hearsay under the rule, including specified prior statements and opposing-party statements.
第801(d)條規則列出某些依該規則視為非傳聞的陳述，包括特定先前陳述及對方當事人陳述。

Rules 803 and 804 contain many hearsay exceptions. Some exceptions do not require the declarant to be unavailable; others require unavailability and additional conditions.
第803及804條規則包含許多傳聞例外。有些例外不要求陳述者無法出庭；另一些則要求陳述者無法出庭，並符合其他條件。

Examples include present-sense impressions, excited utterances, business records, public records, and former testimony, each with specific requirements.
例子包括即時感知陳述、激動陳述、業務紀錄、公務紀錄及先前證言，每一項都有具體要求。

## 6. Confrontation Clause in criminal cases
## 6. 刑事案件中的對質條款

The Sixth Amendment Confrontation Clause may restrict testimonial hearsay offered against a criminal defendant. The analysis is separate from the ordinary hearsay rules.
第六修正案對質條款可能限制在刑事案件中對被告提出的證言性傳聞。這項分析與一般傳聞規則是分開的。

A statement may satisfy a hearsay exception and still raise a constitutional confrontation issue. Always analyze both questions.
一項陳述即使符合傳聞例外，仍可能引發憲法上的對質問題。務必分別分析這兩個問題。

## Check your understanding
## 理解測驗

1. Is every out-of-court statement hearsay?
   每一項法庭外陳述都是傳聞嗎？
2. What is the difference between Rule 801 and Rules 803–804?
   第801條與第803至804條規則有什麼不同？
3. Can a statement satisfy a hearsay exception but still raise a Confrontation Clause issue?
   一項陳述是否可能符合傳聞例外，卻仍引發對質條款問題？

**Answers:** 1. No; purpose matters. 2. Rule 801 defines hearsay and exclusions; Rules 803–804 provide exceptions. 3. Yes.
**答案：** 1. 不是；提出陳述的目的很重要。2. 第801條定義傳聞及排除情況；第803至804條規定例外。3. 可以。
