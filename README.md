# Ocean-learning
# Ocean-learning 美國法律零基礎學習專案

**Start from zero. Learn U.S. law step by step, with every English explanation followed immediately by Traditional Chinese.**
**從零開始，循序漸進學習美國法律；每段英文說明後面都立即附上繁體中文。**

This project is designed for a learner with no legal education or prior knowledge of the U.S. legal system.
本專案是為沒有法律教育背景、也不熟悉美國法律制度的初學者設計。

You do not need to understand every legal word before beginning. Learn the basic idea first, then build vocabulary and legal reasoning step by step.
開始時不需要理解每一個法律術語。先學會基本概念，再逐步累積詞彙及法律推理能力。

## How to study
## 如何學習

Study for 20–30 minutes at a time. Read one English sentence, then read the Traditional Chinese translation immediately below it.
每次學習 20 至 30 分鐘。先讀一句英文，再立即閱讀其下方的繁體中文翻譯。

Explain each concept in your own simple words before memorizing its technical definition.
在背誦專業定義之前，先用自己的簡單話語解釋每個概念。

The United States has federal law and state law; the applicable rule can differ by jurisdiction.
美國同時存在聯邦法與州法；適用規則可能因司法管轄區而異。

This project is educational only. It does not provide legal advice or guarantee JD admission or bar eligibility.
本專案僅供教育學習，不提供法律意見，也不保證 JD 入學或律師考試資格。
