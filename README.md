# Ocean-learning
# Ocean-learning 美國法律零基礎學習專案

**Start from zero. Learn U.S. law step by step, with every English explanation followed immediately by Traditional Chinese.**
**從零開始，循序漸進學習美國法律；每段英文說明後面都立即附上繁體中文。**

This project is designed for learners with no legal education or prior knowledge of the U.S. legal system. It introduces legal ideas in plain English, explains important terms, and uses cases and exercises to build legal reasoning.
本專案是為沒有法律教育背景、也不熟悉美國法律制度的初學者設計。內容以淺白英文介紹法律概念、解釋重要術語，並透過判例及練習建立法律推理能力。

You do not need to understand every legal word before beginning. Learn the basic idea first, then build vocabulary and legal reasoning step by step.
開始時不需要理解每一個法律術語。先學會基本概念，再逐步累積詞彙及法律推理能力。

## How to study
## 如何學習

Study for 20–30 minutes at a time. Read one English sentence, then read the Traditional Chinese translation immediately below it.
每次學習 20 至 30 分鐘。先讀一句英文，再立即閱讀其下方的繁體中文翻譯。

Explain each concept in your own simple words before memorizing its technical definition. For each case, identify the court, jurisdiction, date, facts, issue, rule, holding, and reasoning.
在背誦專業定義之前，先用自己的簡單話語解釋每個概念。閱讀每宗判例時，辨識法院、司法管轄區、日期、事實、爭點、規則、裁判要旨及推理。

Use official primary sources whenever possible, and verify the current law before relying on a legal proposition.
盡可能使用官方第一手資料，並在依賴任何法律命題前核實現行法律。
