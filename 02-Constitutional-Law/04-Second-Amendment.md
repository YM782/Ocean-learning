# Second Amendment — The Right to Keep and Bear Arms
# 第二修正案——持有及攜帶武器的權利

## 1. The constitutional text
## 1. 憲法原文

“A well regulated Militia, being necessary to the security of a free State, the right of the people to keep and bear Arms, shall not be infringed.”
「紀律良好的民兵組織對自由州的安全十分必要，因此人民持有及攜帶武器的權利不得受到侵犯。」

This amendment concerns the right to keep and bear arms. Its meaning and the constitutional limits on firearm regulation have been interpreted by the Supreme Court.
這項修正案涉及持有及攜帶武器的權利。最高法院曾對其含義及槍械管制的憲法界限作出解釋。

## 2. What does “keep and bear arms” mean?
## 2. 「持有及攜帶武器」是什麼意思？

In *District of Columbia v. Heller* (2008), the Supreme Court recognized an individual right to possess a handgun in the home for lawful purposes such as self-defense.
在2008年的 *District of Columbia v. Heller* 案中，最高法院承認個人有權為合法目的（例如自我防衛）在家中持有手槍。

The Court did not say that the right is unlimited.
法院並沒有說這項權利毫無限制。

## 3. Does the Second Amendment apply to the states?
## 3. 第二修正案是否適用於各州？

In *McDonald v. Chicago* (2010), the Supreme Court held that the Second Amendment right recognized in *Heller* applies to state and local governments through the Fourteenth Amendment.
在2010年的 *McDonald v. Chicago* 案中，最高法院裁定，*Heller* 所承認的第二修正案權利，透過第十四修正案適用於州及地方政府。

## 4. The modern test: text, history, and tradition
## 4. 現代審查方法：文本、歷史及傳統

In *New York State Rifle & Pistol Association v. Bruen* (2022), the Court described a framework for Second Amendment challenges.
在2022年的 *New York State Rifle & Pistol Association v. Bruen* 案中，最高法院說明了審查第二修正案爭議的法律架構。

First, ask whether the Second Amendment's plain text covers the person's proposed conduct.
首先，判斷第二修正案的明確文本是否涵蓋當事人所主張的行為。

If it does, the government must justify the regulation by showing that it is consistent with the Nation's historical tradition of firearm regulation.
如果涵蓋，政府就必須證明該管制措施與美國歷史上的槍械管制傳統相符。

This approach does not mean that every modern law must be an exact copy of an eighteenth-century law. Courts examine relevant historical principles and whether the modern rule is sufficiently analogous.
這種方法並不代表每一項現代法律都必須完全複製十八世紀的法律。法院會檢查相關歷史原則，以及現代規則是否具有足夠的類比性。

## 5. United States v. Rahimi (2024)
## 5. United States v. Rahimi（2024年）

The Supreme Court held that a person found by a court to pose a credible threat to another person's physical safety may be temporarily disarmed consistent with the Second Amendment.
最高法院裁定，如果法院認定某人對他人的人身安全構成可信威脅，暫時禁止該人持有槍械可以符合第二修正案。

The case is important because it shows that *Bruen* does not make firearm rights absolute. Historical analysis can support some modern regulations.
本案十分重要，因為它說明 *Bruen* 並沒有使持槍權變成絕對權利。歷史分析仍可能支持某些現代管制措施。

## 6. What the amendment does not automatically mean
## 6. 這項修正案不代表什麼？

It does not mean every person may possess every weapon in every place.
它不代表每個人都可以在任何地方持有任何武器。

It does not mean every firearm restriction is constitutional.
它也不代表所有槍械限制都符合憲法。

A court must examine the text, the historical tradition, the type of regulation, the person's circumstances, and controlling precedent.
法院必須檢查憲法文本、歷史傳統、管制類型、當事人的具體情況及具有拘束力的判例。

## 7. A simple case-analysis example
## 7. 簡單的判例分析例子

A state prohibits a person who has been found by a court, after appropriate proceedings, to pose a credible threat of physical violence from possessing firearms for a limited period.
某州規定，經適當程序後被法院認定對他人構成可信暴力威脅的人，在有限期間內不得持有槍械。

**Issue:** Does the restriction violate the Second Amendment?
**爭點：** 這項限制是否違反第二修正案？

**Rule:** Under *Bruen*, the government must justify covered firearm regulations by reference to the Nation's historical tradition. *Rahimi* recognizes that temporary disarmament of a person judicially found to pose a credible threat can be constitutional.
**規則：** 依 *Bruen*，政府必須參照美國歷史上的管制傳統，為受第二修正案涵蓋的槍械管制提供正當理由。*Rahimi* 承認，對經法院認定構成可信威脅的人實施暫時禁槍，可能符合憲法。

**Application:** The court would examine the statutory criteria, the reliability and process of the threat finding, the duration of the restriction, and historical analogues.
**適用：** 法院會檢查法律標準、威脅認定的可靠性及程序、限制期限，以及相關歷史類比。

**Conclusion:** *Rahimi* is highly relevant, but the result in a different case depends on its exact facts and the law being challenged.
**結論：** *Rahimi* 與本案高度相關，但不同案件的結果仍取決於確切事實及受到挑戰的法律。

## 8. Important cases
## 8. 重要判例

- *District of Columbia v. Heller* (2008) — individual right to possess a handgun in the home for self-defense.
  *District of Columbia v. Heller*（2008年）——承認個人在家中為自我防衛而持有手槍的個人權利。
- *McDonald v. Chicago* (2010) — applies the Second Amendment right to states and local governments.
  *McDonald v. Chicago*（2010年）——第二修正案權利適用於州及地方政府。
- *New York State Rifle & Pistol Association v. Bruen* (2022) — text-and-historical-tradition framework.
  *New York State Rifle & Pistol Association v. Bruen*（2022年）——文本及歷史傳統審查架構。
- *United States v. Rahimi* (2024) — temporary disarmament of a person judicially found to pose a credible threat.
  *United States v. Rahimi*（2024年）——對經法院認定構成可信威脅的人實施暫時禁槍。
