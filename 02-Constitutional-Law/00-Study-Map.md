# U.S. Constitutional Law — Beginner Study Map
# 美國憲法學——零基礎學習地圖

**Level:** Absolute beginner to introductory case analysis.
**程度：** 從完全零基礎到初步判例分析。

**Language:** English first, followed immediately by Traditional Chinese.
**語言：** 先英文，後面立即附上繁體中文。

**Recommended pace:** 30–40 minutes a day, five days a week. Take longer whenever a topic feels unfamiliar.
**建議進度：** 每天30至40分鐘，每週五天。遇到不熟悉的內容，可以放慢速度。

## What you will learn
## 你將學會什麼

Constitutional law asks two basic questions: what powers does government have, and what limits does the Constitution place on those powers?
憲法學主要研究兩個基本問題：政府擁有哪些權力？憲法又對這些權力設下哪些限制？

You will learn how constitutional text, statutes, government action, and court decisions work together.
你將學習憲法文本、制定法、政府行為及法院判決如何共同運作。

You will also learn that a constitutional right is not always absolute. Its scope, exceptions, and enforcement depend on the text, legal doctrine, facts, and jurisdiction.
你也會學到，憲法權利並不一定是絕對的。權利的範圍、例外及執行方式，取決於憲法文本、法律原則、案件事實及司法管轄區。

## A six-week learning plan
## 六週學習計畫

### Week 1 — Understand the Constitution
### 第一週——理解憲法

- Day 1: Read the Constitution's purpose and structure.
  第一天：閱讀憲法的目的及架構。
- Day 2: Learn federalism and the three branches.
  第二天：學習聯邦制及政府三個部門。
- Day 3: Learn judicial review and the state-action requirement.
  第三天：學習司法審查及政府行為原則。
- Day 4: Learn strict scrutiny, intermediate scrutiny, and rational-basis review at an introductory level.
  第四天：初步學習嚴格審查、中度審查及合理基礎審查。
- Day 5: Explain these ideas without looking at your notes.
  第五天：不看筆記，試著用自己的話解釋上述概念。

### Week 2 — Freedom of religion and expression
### 第二週——宗教及表達自由

- Day 1–2: Establishment Clause and Free Exercise Clause.
  第一天至第二天：政教分立條款及宗教自由行使條款。
- Day 3–4: Speech, press, public forums, and limits on speech.
  第三天至第四天：言論、新聞、公共論壇及言論限制。
- Day 5: Analyze a simple school or public-park example.
  第五天：分析一個簡單的學校或公共公園案例。

### Week 3 — The Second and Fourth Amendments
### 第三週——第二及第四修正案

- Day 1–2: Individual right to keep and bear arms; constitutional limits.
  第一天至第二天：個人持有及攜帶武器的權利，以及憲法限制。
- Day 3: Searches, seizures, and warrants.
  第三天：搜查、扣押及搜查令。
- Day 4: Common warrant exceptions.
  第四天：常見的無令狀搜查例外。
- Day 5: Apply the Fourth Amendment to a phone or location-data scenario.
  第五天：將第四修正案應用於手機或定位資料情境。

### Week 4 — The Fifth and Sixth Amendments
### 第四週——第五及第六修正案

- Day 1: Self-incrimination and Miranda.
  第一天：不得自證其罪及 Miranda 警告。
- Day 2: Double jeopardy and due process.
  第二天：禁止雙重危險及正當法律程序。
- Day 3: Right to counsel.
  第三天：律師協助權。
- Day 4: Jury, confrontation, and speedy trial.
  第四天：陪審團、對質權及迅速審判權。
- Day 5: Explain the difference between police questioning and a criminal trial.
  第五天：解釋警方訊問與刑事審判的區別。

### Week 5 — The Eighth and Fourteenth Amendments
### 第五週——第八及第十四修正案

- Day 1: Bail, fines, and punishment.
  第一天：保釋金、罰金及刑罰。
- Day 2: Citizenship and the structure of the Fourteenth Amendment.
  第二天：公民身分及第十四修正案架構。
- Day 3: Procedural due process and substantive due process.
  第三天：程序性正當程序及實質性正當程序。
- Day 4: Equal protection and levels of scrutiny.
  第四天：平等保護及審查標準。
- Day 5: Explain how many Bill of Rights protections apply to states.
  第五天：解釋《權利法案》的許多保障如何適用於各州。

### Week 6 — Case analysis
### 第六週——判例分析

Read the case studies, complete the exercises, and write two short IRAC answers.
閱讀判例分析，完成練習題，並撰寫兩份簡短的 IRAC 法律分析。

## A method for every study session
## 每次學習的固定方法

1. Read one English paragraph.
   閱讀一段英文。
2. Read the Traditional Chinese translation immediately below it.
   立即閱讀下方的繁體中文翻譯。
3. Write down no more than five new legal terms.
   每次最多記錄五個新法律術語。
4. Explain the rule in plain language.
   用日常語言解釋法律規則。
5. Apply it to one simple example.
   將規則應用於一個簡單例子。

## What you should be able to do at the end
## 完成後應具備的能力

You should be able to identify the relevant amendment, distinguish the constitutional issue from the factual dispute, explain the basic rule, recognize important exceptions, and write a short IRAC analysis.
你應該能夠找出相關修正案、區分憲法問題與事實爭議、解釋基本規則、辨識重要例外，並撰寫簡短的 IRAC 分析。

You are not expected to act as a lawyer or give legal advice. This course teaches legal literacy and introductory legal reasoning.
本課程不要求你以律師身分行事或提供法律意見，而是培養法律素養及初步法律推理能力。
