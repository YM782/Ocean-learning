# Eighth Amendment — Bail, Fines, and Punishment
# 第八修正案——保釋金、罰金及刑罰限制

## 1. The constitutional text
## 1. 憲法原文

“Excessive bail shall not be required, nor excessive fines imposed, nor cruel and unusual punishments inflicted.”
「不得要求過高的保釋金，不得科處過高的罰金，也不得施加殘酷及不尋常的刑罰。」

The Eighth Amendment places constitutional limits on certain parts of the criminal justice system.
第八修正案對刑事司法制度中的某些部分設下憲法限制。

## 2. Excessive bail
## 2. 過高保釋金

Bail is a legal mechanism that may allow a person charged with a crime to remain out of custody while a case is pending, subject to conditions.
保釋是一種法律機制，可能允許受到刑事指控的人在案件審理期間暫時不被羈押，但仍須遵守相關條件。

The Excessive Bail Clause prohibits bail set higher than reasonably necessary under the applicable legal framework.
禁止過高保釋金條款，禁止在適用法律架構下設定超過合理需要的保釋金。

**Important:** The Constitution does not create an unlimited right to bail in every case. Some defendants may be detained before trial under valid laws and procedures.
**重要提醒：** 憲法並沒有建立每宗案件都可以無條件保釋的權利。某些被告可能依有效法律及程序，在審判前受到羈押。

## 3. Excessive fines
## 3. 過高罰金

The Excessive Fines Clause limits government financial penalties that are grossly disproportionate under the governing constitutional standard.
禁止過高罰金條款，限制依適用憲法標準而言極不相稱的政府金錢處罰。

In *Timbs v. Indiana* (2019), the Supreme Court held that the Excessive Fines Clause applies to state and local governments through the Fourteenth Amendment.
在2019年的 *Timbs v. Indiana* 案中，最高法院裁定，禁止過高罰金條款透過第十四修正案適用於州及地方政府。

A fine may be criminal or civil in form. Courts examine its nature and purpose, not merely the label attached to it.
罰金在形式上可能屬於刑事或民事處罰。法院會檢查其性質及目的，而不只是看它被貼上什麼名稱。

## 4. Cruel and unusual punishment
## 4. 殘酷及不尋常的刑罰

The Cruel and Unusual Punishments Clause limits the punishments government may impose after criminal conviction.
禁止殘酷及不尋常刑罰條款，限制政府在刑事定罪後可以施加的刑罰。

The Supreme Court has interpreted the clause in light of constitutional text, history, precedent, and standards governing particular categories of punishment.
最高法院會結合憲法文本、歷史、判例，以及特定刑罰類別的適用標準來解釋這項條款。

The doctrine has addressed issues such as:
相關法律原則處理過以下問題：

- Methods and procedures of capital punishment.
  死刑的執行方式及程序。
- Whether certain categories of people may receive the death penalty.
  某些類別的人是否可以被判處死刑。
- Punishment of juveniles for particular crimes.
  對未成年人特定犯罪的刑罰。
- Proportionality of certain prison sentences.
  某些監禁刑期是否符合比例原則。
- Conditions of confinement for convicted prisoners.
  已定罪囚犯的監禁環境。

## 5. Proportionality and punishment
## 5. 比例原則與刑罰

The Constitution does not require every sentence to be identical for similar conduct. Sentencing depends on statutes, facts, criminal history, and constitutional limits.
憲法並不要求所有相似行為都必須判處完全相同的刑罰。量刑取決於制定法、案件事實、犯罪紀錄及憲法限制。

The Supreme Court has recognized a narrow proportionality principle in noncapital cases. A sentence being harsh does not automatically make it unconstitutional.
最高法院在非死刑案件中承認一項範圍有限的比例原則。刑罰嚴厲並不會自動使其違憲。

## 6. Prison conditions and the Eighth Amendment
## 6. 監獄環境與第八修正案

For convicted prisoners, the Eighth Amendment can restrict deliberate indifference to serious medical needs and certain unsafe or inhumane conditions.
對已定罪囚犯而言，第八修正案可能限制監獄管理者對嚴重醫療需求及某些不安全或不人道環境故意漠視的行為。

A pretrial detainee has not yet been convicted. Claims about pretrial conditions are generally analyzed under the Fourteenth Amendment's Due Process Clause rather than the Eighth Amendment's post-conviction punishment rule.
審前被羈押者尚未被定罪。對審前羈押環境提出的主張，通常依第十四修正案正當法律程序條款分析，而不是直接依第八修正案的定罪後刑罰規則分析。

## 7. Important cases
## 7. 重要判例

- *United States v. Salerno* (1987): Addressed preventive pretrial detention under the federal Bail Reform Act and rejected the claim that the Act was facially invalid under the Due Process Clause and Excessive Bail Clause.
  *United States v. Salerno*（1987年）：處理聯邦《保釋改革法》下的預防性審前羈押，並駁回該法在表面上違反正當法律程序及禁止過高保釋金條款的主張。
- *Timbs v. Indiana* (2019): Applied the Excessive Fines Clause to the states.
  *Timbs v. Indiana*（2019年）：裁定禁止過高罰金條款適用於各州。
- *Graham v. Florida* (2010): Held that the Eighth Amendment prohibits life without parole for juveniles convicted of non-homicide offenses.
  *Graham v. Florida*（2010年）：裁定對犯下非殺人罪的未成年人，不得判處不得假釋的終身監禁。
- *Roper v. Simmons* (2005): Held that the death penalty may not be imposed for crimes committed by people under eighteen.
  *Roper v. Simmons*（2005年）：裁定不得對犯罪時未滿十八歲的人判處死刑。

## 8. A practical example
## 8. 實際例子

A city imposes a very large financial penalty on a person for a minor offense. The penalty is far greater than the value involved and appears designed to punish rather than compensate.
某城市因一項輕微違規，對某人科處極高金額的罰款。罰款遠遠超過涉及的財物價值，而且看起來主要是為了懲罰，而不是補償。

**Issue:** Does the fine raise an Excessive Fines Clause question?
**爭點：** 這項罰款是否引發禁止過高罰金條款的問題？

**Rule:** The Eighth Amendment prohibits excessive fines, and the protection applies to state and local governments.
**規則：** 第八修正案禁止過高罰金，而且這項保障適用於州及地方政府。

**Application:** A court would examine the penalty's punitive purpose, the seriousness of the offense, the relationship between the penalty and the conduct, and the applicable proportionality doctrine.
**適用：** 法院會檢查處罰的懲罰性目的、違規嚴重程度、罰款與行為之間的關係，以及適用的比例原則。

**Conclusion:** The facts may raise a constitutional issue, but the amount alone does not decide the case.
**結論：** 這些事實可能引發憲法問題，但不能只憑罰款金額就決定案件結果。
