# First Amendment — Speech, Press, Assembly, and Petition
# 第一修正案——言論、新聞、集會及請願自由

## 1. The constitutional text
## 1. 憲法原文

“Congress shall make no law ... abridging the freedom of speech, or of the press; or the right of the people peaceably to assemble, and to petition the Government for a redress of grievances.”
「國會不得制定……限制言論或新聞自由的法律；也不得剝奪人民和平集會及向政府請願、要求救濟的權利。」

The First Amendment protects several related freedoms. It does not mean that every statement is protected in every setting or that every restriction is unconstitutional.
第一修正案保障數項彼此相關的自由。但這並不代表所有言論在任何場合都受到保護，也不代表所有限制都違憲。

## 2. Who is restricted?
## 2. 它限制誰？

The First Amendment primarily restricts government action. It generally does not require a private person, private employer, or private website to publish or host every opinion.
第一修正案主要限制政府行為。它通常不要求私人、私人雇主或私人網站必須刊登或容納所有意見。

A government restriction may come from a statute, regulation, police action, public-school rule, licensing system, or official decision.
政府限制可能來自制定法、行政規章、警方行動、公立學校規則、許可制度或公職人員決定。

## 3. Content-based and viewpoint-based restrictions
## 3. 基於內容及觀點的限制

A **content-based restriction** treats speech differently because of what the speaker is discussing.
**內容歧視性限制（Content-Based Restriction）** 是指政府因為言論討論的內容不同，而採取不同待遇。

A **viewpoint-based restriction** treats speech differently because of the position or opinion expressed.
**觀點歧視性限制（Viewpoint-Based Restriction）** 是指政府因為發言者表達的立場或意見不同，而採取不同待遇。

Viewpoint discrimination is especially disfavored. Content-based restrictions in public forums generally face strict scrutiny, subject to the relevant doctrine and recognized categories.
觀點歧視尤其受到憲法排斥。在公共論壇中，基於內容的限制通常面臨嚴格審查，但仍須考慮相關法律原則及已被承認的例外類別。

## 4. Protected speech and narrow categories of unprotected speech
## 4. 受到保障的言論及少數不受保障的言論類別

Political criticism, unpopular opinions, and offensive ideas are often protected by the First Amendment. The government cannot simply ban speech because officials dislike its message.
政治批評、不受歡迎的意見及令人反感的觀點，通常受到第一修正案保障。政府不能僅僅因為官員不喜歡某種訊息，就禁止相關言論。

The Supreme Court has recognized narrow categories that may receive little or no First Amendment protection, including:
最高法院承認少數可能不受第一修正案保障，或只獲得有限保障的類別，包括：

- **Incitement:** Advocacy directed to producing imminent lawless action and likely to produce it.
  **煽動：** 旨在引發迫在眉睫的違法行動，而且很可能造成該行動的言論。
- **True threats:** Serious expressions of intent to commit unlawful violence against a person or group, subject to the governing constitutional test.
  **真實威脅：** 嚴肅表達對個人或群體實施非法暴力的意圖，具體標準須依適用憲法原則判斷。
- **Obscenity:** A narrowly defined category of legally obscene material.
  **淫穢內容：** 依法律嚴格定義的淫穢材料類別。
- **Defamation:** Certain legally actionable false statements that harm reputation, subject to constitutional protections and standards.
  **誹謗：** 某些依法可追究、損害名譽的虛假陳述，但仍受到憲法保障及標準限制。
- **Fighting words:** A narrow category of personally abusive face-to-face words historically treated as likely to provoke immediate violence.
  **挑釁性辱罵言詞：** 歷史上被視為可能立即引發暴力的狹義當面辱罵言詞。

Do not turn these categories into broad labels. For example, “hate speech” is not automatically outside First Amendment protection merely because it is hateful or offensive.
不要將這些類別擴大解釋。例如，言論不會僅僅因為具有仇恨或冒犯性，就自動失去第一修正案保障。

## 5. Incitement: Brandenburg test
## 5. 煽動：Brandenburg 標準

In *Brandenburg v. Ohio* (1969), the Court held that advocacy of force or law violation may be punished as incitement only when it is directed to inciting or producing imminent lawless action and is likely to incite or produce such action.
在1969年的 *Brandenburg v. Ohio* 案中，最高法院認為，只有當鼓吹使用暴力或違法的言論旨在煽動或造成迫在眉睫的違法行動，而且很可能造成該行動時，才可能作為煽動行為受到處罰。

A vague prediction that violence may happen someday is not the same as the Brandenburg standard.
模糊地預測未來某一天可能發生暴力，並不等同於 Brandenburg 標準。

## 6. Public forums and government property
## 6. 公共論壇及政府財產

A **traditional public forum** includes places such as public streets and parks that have historically been open for public expression.
**傳統公共論壇（Traditional Public Forum）** 包括歷史上一直開放供公眾表達意見的地方，例如公共街道及公園。

In a traditional public forum, government generally may impose reasonable time, place, and manner restrictions if they are content-neutral, narrowly tailored to serve a significant governmental interest, and leave open ample alternative channels of communication.
在傳統公共論壇中，政府通常可以施加合理的時間、地點及方式限制，但限制必須保持內容中立、經過狹義限定以服務重要政府利益，並保留充分的替代表達途徑。

A **limited public forum** or **nonpublic forum** may allow government to reserve property for particular purposes, but restrictions still must satisfy the applicable constitutional standard and cannot be arbitrary viewpoint discrimination.
**有限公共論壇（Limited Public Forum）** 或**非公共論壇（Nonpublic Forum）** 可能允許政府將場所保留給特定用途，但限制仍須符合適用的憲法標準，也不能任意進行觀點歧視。

## 7. Freedom of the press
## 7. 新聞自由

The Press Clause protects the press from certain government restrictions. It supports the public's ability to receive information, investigate public affairs, and criticize government.
新聞自由條款保障新聞媒體免受某些政府限制，並支持公眾獲取資訊、調查公共事務及批評政府的能力。

Press freedom does not mean that journalists are immune from every generally applicable law. The legal question depends on the law, the conduct, and the constitutional doctrine involved.
新聞自由並不代表記者可以免受所有普遍適用法律的約束。法律問題取決於具體法律、行為及相關憲法原則。

## 8. Defamation and public officials
## 8. 誹謗與公職人員

In *New York Times Co. v. Sullivan* (1964), the Court held that a public official suing over defamatory false statements about official conduct must prove **actual malice**.
在1964年的 *New York Times Co. v. Sullivan* 案中，最高法院裁定，公職人員若因涉及其公務行為的誹謗性虛假陳述提起訴訟，必須證明**實際惡意（Actual Malice）**。

Actual malice has a specific legal meaning: the defendant knew the statement was false or acted with reckless disregard for whether it was false.
實際惡意具有特定法律含義：被告明知陳述不實，或罔顧陳述是否真實而作出陳述。

It does not simply mean personal hatred, spite, or a wish to hurt someone's feelings.
它並不只是指個人仇恨、惡意或希望傷害他人感情。

## 9. Assembly and petition
## 9. 集會及請願

The right to peaceably assemble protects people who gather for expressive purposes, including demonstrations and public meetings, subject to lawful and constitutional time, place, and manner rules.
和平集會權保障人們為表達意見而聚集，包括示威及公共會議，但仍須遵守合法且符合憲法的時間、地點及方式規則。

The right to petition protects the ability to ask government to address grievances. Examples include petitions, complaints, and certain forms of organized advocacy.
請願權保障人民向政府提出訴求、要求處理不滿的能力。例子包括請願書、投訴及某些有組織的倡議活動。

## 10. Important cases
## 10. 重要判例

- *Tinker v. Des Moines* (1969): Public-school students do not “shed their constitutional rights to freedom of speech or expression at the schoolhouse gate”; schools may regulate student expression under applicable standards when it materially and substantially disrupts school operations or invades others' rights.
  *Tinker v. Des Moines*（1969年）：公立學校學生不會因為走進校門就失去言論及表達自由；但若學生表達依適用標準對學校運作造成重大且實質的干擾，或侵犯他人權利，學校可能依法加以限制。
- *Brandenburg v. Ohio* (1969): Established the modern imminent-lawless-action incitement standard.
  *Brandenburg v. Ohio*（1969年）：建立現代「迫在眉睫的違法行動」煽動標準。
- *New York Times Co. v. Sullivan* (1964): Established the actual-malice standard for public officials' defamation claims about official conduct.
  *New York Times Co. v. Sullivan*（1964年）：建立公職人員就公務行為提出誹謗請求時的實際惡意標準。
- *Kennedy v. Bremerton School District* (2022): Addressed a public employee's private religious expression and the Establishment Clause.
  *Kennedy v. Bremerton School District*（2022年）：處理公職人員私人宗教表達與政教分立條款之間的關係。

## 11. Mini example
## 11. 迷你案例

A mayor orders police to remove a peaceful protest because the protesters criticize the mayor's policies. The protest does not block roads or threaten anyone.
某市長命令警方驅散一場和平示威，理由是示威者批評市長的政策。示威沒有阻塞道路，也沒有威脅任何人。

**Issue:** Is the government restricting speech because of its viewpoint?
**爭點：** 政府是否因為言論立場而限制表達？

**Rule:** Government viewpoint discrimination is strongly disfavored under the First Amendment.
**規則：** 第一修正案強烈反對政府基於觀點進行差別對待。

**Application:** The stated reason is the protesters' criticism, not a neutral safety or traffic rule.
**適用：** 已知理由是示威者的批評內容，而不是中立的安全或交通規則。

**Conclusion:** The order raises a serious First Amendment issue. A complete analysis would examine the location, actual conduct, applicable forum rules, and any additional facts.
**結論：** 這項命令引發嚴重的第一修正案問題。完整分析仍須檢查地點、實際行為、適用的論壇規則及其他事實。
