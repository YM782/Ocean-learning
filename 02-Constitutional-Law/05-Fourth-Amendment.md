# Fourth Amendment — Searches, Seizures, and Privacy
# 第四修正案——搜查、扣押及隱私權

## 1. The constitutional text
## 1. 憲法原文

“The right of the people to be secure in their persons, houses, papers, and effects, against unreasonable searches and seizures, shall not be violated, and no Warrants shall issue, but upon probable cause, supported by Oath or affirmation, and particularly describing the place to be searched, and the persons or things to be seized.”
「人民的人身、住宅、文件及財物不受不合理搜查及扣押的權利，不得受到侵犯；除非具有合理根據，並以宣誓或確認作為支持，且具體描述要搜查的地點及要扣押的人或物，否則不得簽發搜查令。」

The Fourth Amendment is a limit on government searches and seizures. It is especially important when police investigate crime.
第四修正案限制政府進行搜查及扣押。警方調查犯罪時，這項修正案尤其重要。

## 2. What is a search?
## 2. 什麼是搜查？

A search occurs when government conduct intrudes on an interest protected by the Fourth Amendment.
當政府行為侵犯第四修正案所保障的利益時，就可能構成搜查。

The Supreme Court has used both property-based principles and the idea of a **reasonable expectation of privacy**.
最高法院既使用以財產權為基礎的原則，也使用**合理隱私期待（Reasonable Expectation of Privacy）**的概念。

A person generally has a strong privacy interest in the inside of a home. But privacy expectations depend on the place, information, technology, and circumstances.
個人通常對住宅內部享有強烈的隱私利益。但隱私期待仍取決於場所、資訊、科技及具體情況。

## 3. What is a seizure?
## 3. 什麼是扣押？

A seizure of a person occurs when government action restrains that person's freedom of movement in the constitutional sense.
當政府行為在憲法意義上限制某人的行動自由時，就可能構成對人的扣押。

A seizure of property occurs when government meaningfully interferes with a person's possessory interest in property.
當政府實質干預某人對財產的占有利益時，就可能構成對物的扣押。

A police conversation is not automatically a seizure. Courts examine whether a reasonable person would feel free to leave or otherwise terminate the encounter, considering all the circumstances.
警方與某人交談並不會自動構成扣押。法院會綜合考慮所有情況，判斷合理的人是否會覺得自己可以離開或結束接觸。

## 4. Warrant and probable cause
## 4. 搜查令與合理根據

A **warrant** is a judicial authorization for a search or arrest.
**令狀（Warrant）** 是法院對搜查或逮捕作出的授權。

A valid search warrant generally must be issued by a neutral judicial officer, supported by probable cause, and sufficiently particular about the place and items involved.
有效的搜查令通常必須由中立的司法官員簽發，具有合理根據，並充分具體描述相關地點及物品。

**Probable cause** means a practical, fact-based probability that evidence of a crime will be found or that a person committed an offense. It is more than a mere hunch, but it is less than proof beyond a reasonable doubt.
**合理根據（Probable Cause）** 是指根據實際事實，存在相當可能性認為能找到犯罪證據，或某人實施了犯罪。它高於單純猜測，但低於排除合理懷疑的刑事定罪證明標準。

## 5. Is a warrant always required?
## 5. 是否每次搜查都必須有令狀？

No. A warrant is generally preferred, and warrantless searches are often presumed unreasonable, but the Supreme Court recognizes several exceptions.
不是。一般而言，令狀是較受憲法偏好的方式；沒有令狀的搜查通常會被推定為不合理，但最高法院承認多種例外。

Common examples include:
常見例子包括：

- **Consent:** A person with appropriate authority voluntarily agrees to the search.
  **同意搜查：** 具有適當權限的人自願同意搜查。
- **Exigent circumstances:** An emergency makes it impractical to obtain a warrant first, such as an immediate threat to life or imminent destruction of evidence under the governing test.
  **緊急情況：** 緊急狀況使警方無法先取得令狀，例如生命受到即時威脅，或依適用標準存在證據即將被毀滅的情況。
- **Search incident to lawful arrest:** Officers may conduct certain searches connected to a lawful arrest, within defined limits.
  **合法逮捕附帶搜查：** 執法人員可以在特定界限內，進行與合法逮捕相關的搜查。
- **Automobile exception:** Under defined conditions, probable cause may permit a vehicle search without a warrant.
  **汽車例外：** 在特定條件下，合理根據可能允許警方無令狀搜查車輛。
- **Plain view:** Officers who are lawfully present may seize certain evidence whose incriminating character is immediately apparent and which they have lawful access to.
  **明顯可見原則：** 執法人員在合法位置時，如果某物的犯罪性質立即明顯，而且他們有合法接觸該物的權限，就可能依法扣押。

These are not blanket permissions. Each exception has its own requirements.
這些並不是無條件授權。每一項例外都有自己的法律要求。

## 6. Stop, frisk, and arrest are different
## 6. 臨時攔查、拍身搜查及逮捕並不相同

A brief investigative stop may be justified by **reasonable suspicion**, a lower standard than probable cause but more than an unparticularized hunch.
短暫的調查性攔查可能以**合理懷疑（Reasonable Suspicion）**為依據。它低於合理根據，但高於沒有具體事實支持的猜測。

Under *Terry v. Ohio* (1968), an officer who reasonably suspects criminal activity may briefly stop a person. A limited pat-down for weapons requires a reasonable basis to believe the person is armed and dangerous.
依1968年的 *Terry v. Ohio* 案，警員在合理懷疑存在犯罪活動時，可以短暫攔查某人。若要進行有限的武器拍身搜查，則必須有合理理由相信該人攜帶武器且具有危險性。

An arrest generally requires probable cause.
逮捕通常需要合理根據。

## 7. What happens if police violate the Fourth Amendment?
## 7. 如果警方違反第四修正案，會怎麼樣？

The **exclusionary rule** may prevent certain evidence obtained through unconstitutional searches or seizures from being used by the prosecution in a criminal trial.
**排除證據規則（Exclusionary Rule）** 可能阻止控方在刑事審判中使用透過違憲搜查或扣押取得的某些證據。

The rule has exceptions. One important example is the **good-faith exception**, which may allow evidence when officers reasonably relied on a warrant or legal authority later found defective.
這項規則存在例外。其中一個重要例子是**善意例外（Good-Faith Exception）**：如果警員合理依賴一份後來被認定存在缺陷的令狀或法律權限，證據仍可能被採納。

A constitutional violation does not automatically mean the entire criminal case is dismissed. The remedy depends on the violation, the evidence, the defendant's claim, and the applicable doctrine.
違反憲法並不會自動導致整宗刑事案件被撤銷。救濟方式取決於違法情況、證據、被告的主張及適用法律原則。

## 8. Digital privacy: phones and location records
## 8. 數位隱私：手機及定位紀錄

In *Riley v. California* (2014), the Supreme Court held that police generally must obtain a warrant before searching the digital contents of a cell phone seized incident to arrest.
在2014年的 *Riley v. California* 案中，最高法院裁定，警方通常必須先取得令狀，才能搜查因逮捕而扣押的手機內數位內容。

In *Carpenter v. United States* (2018), the Court held that obtaining certain historical cell-site location information is a Fourth Amendment search and generally requires a warrant supported by probable cause, subject to the limits of the decision.
在2018年的 *Carpenter v. United States* 案中，最高法院裁定，取得某些歷史手機基地台定位資訊構成第四修正案意義上的搜查，通常需要有合理根據支持的令狀，但仍須遵守該判決的適用界限。

### Recent example: Chatrie v. United States (2026)
### 最新例子：Chatrie v. United States（2026年）

On June 29, 2026, the Supreme Court held that police conducted a Fourth Amendment search when they obtained a person's Google Location History data through a geofence process. The Court recognized a reasonable expectation of privacy in the cell-phone location information at issue.
2026年6月29日，最高法院裁定，警方透過地理圍欄程序取得某人的 Google 位置歷史資料，構成第四修正案意義上的搜查。法院承認，當事人對相關手機定位資訊享有合理隱私期待。

**Important limit:** The Court did not decide whether the good-faith exception would still permit admission of the evidence. It left that issue for the lower court to consider. Do not summarize the case as automatically suppressing all geofence evidence.
**重要限制：** 最高法院並沒有決定善意例外是否仍允許採納該證據，而是將這個問題留給下級法院考慮。不要把本案簡化成所有地理圍欄證據都必須自動排除。

## 9. A practical example
## 9. 實際例子

Police take a person's phone after arrest and immediately open photo albums, messages, location history, and banking apps without a warrant.
警方逮捕某人後扣押其手機，並在沒有令狀的情況下立即打開相簿、訊息、定位歷史及銀行應用程式。

**Issue:** Does the search raise a Fourth Amendment question?
**爭點：** 這次搜查是否引發第四修正案問題？

**Rule:** *Riley* generally requires a warrant before police search the digital contents of a cell phone seized incident to arrest.
**規則：** 依 *Riley*，警方通常必須先取得令狀，才能搜查因逮捕而扣押的手機數位內容。

**Application:** The facts describe a search of extensive digital information. The prosecution would need to identify a valid warrant or a legally recognized exception.
**適用：** 這些事實涉及大量數位資訊的搜查。控方必須指出有效令狀或依法承認的例外。

**Conclusion:** The search raises a substantial Fourth Amendment issue. A final answer requires more facts about the arrest, warrant, consent, and any claimed exception.
**結論：** 這次搜查引發重大第四修正案問題。最終判斷仍須了解逮捕、令狀、同意及警方主張的例外。
