# Constitutional Foundations — How to Read the U.S. Constitution
# 憲法基礎——如何閱讀美國憲法

## 1. What is a constitution?
## 1. 什麼是憲法？

A constitution is a country's foundational legal framework. It establishes public institutions, distributes government powers, and sets limits on what government may do.
憲法是一個國家的根本法律架構。它建立公共機關、分配政府權力，並限制政府可以採取的行動。

The U.S. Constitution is not simply a list of rights. It first creates a system of government and then limits that system through structural rules and individual-rights protections.
《美國憲法》並不只是一份權利清單。它首先建立政府制度，再透過制度性規則及個人權利保障限制政府。

## 2. The Constitution's basic structure
## 2. 美國憲法的基本架構

The Constitution has a Preamble, seven original Articles, and twenty-seven Amendments.
美國憲法包含序言、原始七條正文，以及二十七條修正案。

- Article I creates Congress and describes legislative powers.
  第一條建立國會並規定立法權。
- Article II creates the presidency and executive powers.
  第二條建立總統職位並規定行政權。
- Article III establishes the federal judiciary.
  第三條建立聯邦司法機關。
- Article IV addresses relationships among states and other federal-state matters.
  第四條處理各州之間的關係及其他聯邦與州的事項。
- Article V provides the amendment process.
  第五條規定修憲程序。
- Article VI addresses constitutional supremacy, federal obligations, and related matters.
  第六條涉及憲法最高效力、聯邦義務及相關事項。
- Article VII set out the original ratification process.
  第七條規定原始憲法的批准程序。

## 3. Separation of powers
## 3. 權力分立

The federal government has three branches: legislative, executive, and judicial.
聯邦政府分為三個部門：立法、行政及司法。

**Congress makes federal statutes.**
**國會制定聯邦法律。**

**The President and executive agencies administer and enforce federal law.**
**總統及行政機關負責管理及執行聯邦法律。**

**Federal courts decide cases and controversies and interpret applicable law.**
**聯邦法院審理案件及爭議，並解釋適用法律。**

The branches are separate, but they are not completely isolated. The Constitution gives each branch certain ways to check the others.
三個部門彼此分立，但並非完全隔離。憲法賦予各部門某些制衡其他部門的方式。

**Checks and balances** means that one branch can limit certain actions of another branch.
**權力制衡（Checks and Balances）** 是指一個政府部門可以限制另一個部門的某些行動。

Example: The President may veto a bill, but Congress may override that veto with the constitutionally required vote.
例子：總統可以否決法案，但國會可以依憲法規定的票數推翻否決。

## 4. Federalism: federal government and state governments
## 4. 聯邦制：聯邦政府與州政府

The United States has both a federal government and state governments. They have different sources and areas of legal authority.
美國同時存在聯邦政府及州政府。兩者的法律權限來源及範圍並不相同。

The federal government must act within powers granted by the Constitution.
聯邦政府必須在憲法授予的權力範圍內行事。

States have broad powers over many areas of daily life, such as general criminal law, family law, property law, and local government, subject to federal constitutional and statutory limits.
各州對許多日常生活領域具有廣泛權力，例如一般刑法、家庭法、財產法及地方政府，但仍受到聯邦憲法及有效聯邦法律的限制。

The Supremacy Clause makes the Constitution and valid federal laws supreme over conflicting state law.
最高效力條款規定，憲法及有效聯邦法律優先於與其牴觸的州法。

**Preemption** is the doctrine that determines whether federal law displaces state law in a particular area.
**聯邦優先適用（Preemption）** 是判斷聯邦法律是否在特定領域排除州法適用的原則。

Do not assume that every federal rule cancels every state rule. The text, scope, purpose, and structure of the federal law matter.
不要假設每一項聯邦規則都會取消所有州法。聯邦法律的文本、範圍、目的及架構都很重要。

## 5. Who does the Constitution restrict?
## 5. 憲法限制誰？

Most constitutional rights are protections against government action, not general rules governing every private disagreement.
大多數憲法權利是用來限制政府行為的保障，而不是管理所有私人爭議的一般規則。

For example, the First Amendment generally restricts government censorship. A private newspaper's editorial choice is usually not government censorship.
例如，第一修正案通常限制政府審查。私人報紙自行決定刊登或不刊登某篇文章，通常不屬於政府審查。

The **state-action doctrine** asks whether the conduct being challenged is legally attributable to government.
**政府行為原則（State-Action Doctrine）** 是判斷受到挑戰的行為在法律上是否可以歸屬於政府。

There are narrow situations in which private conduct may be treated as state action, such as certain joint actions with government or functions traditionally and exclusively performed by government. These exceptions are fact-specific.
在少數情況下，私人行為可能被視為政府行為，例如某些與政府共同進行的行動，或傳統上且專屬由政府執行的職能。這些例外必須依具體事實判斷。

## 6. The Fourteenth Amendment and incorporation
## 6. 第十四修正案與權利納入適用

The original Bill of Rights was understood to restrict the federal government. The Fourteenth Amendment later became the main constitutional route through which many Bill of Rights protections apply to state and local governments.
原始《權利法案》主要被理解為限制聯邦政府。後來，第十四修正案成為許多《權利法案》保障適用於州及地方政府的主要憲法途徑。

This process is called **incorporation**.
這個過程稱為**權利納入適用（Incorporation）**。

Most relevant protections have been incorporated through the Fourteenth Amendment's Due Process Clause. Not every provision has been incorporated in exactly the same way; the Grand Jury Clause of the Fifth Amendment, for example, has not been incorporated against the states.
許多相關保障是透過第十四修正案的正當法律程序條款納入適用的。但並非每一項條文都以完全相同方式納入；例如，第五修正案的大陪審團條款並未適用於各州。

## 7. Judicial review
## 7. 司法審查

**Judicial review** is the power of courts to determine whether government action is consistent with the Constitution in a case properly before them.
**司法審查（Judicial Review）** 是法院在依法受理的案件中，判斷政府行為是否符合憲法的權力。

The landmark case is *Marbury v. Madison* (1803).
重要判例是1803年的 *Marbury v. Madison*。

Courts do not normally issue advisory opinions simply because someone asks an abstract constitutional question. There must be a real case or controversy that satisfies legal requirements.
法院通常不會僅因有人提出抽象的憲法問題，就發布諮詢性意見。案件必須是真實爭議，並符合相關法律要求。

Some important justiciability concepts include:
重要的可司法審理性概念包括：

- **Standing:** Does this person have a sufficiently concrete and personal injury that the court can address?
  **訴訟資格（Standing）：** 此人是否受到足夠具體且個人的損害，以便法院處理？
- **Ripeness:** Is the dispute ready for judicial decision, or is it too early?
  **成熟性（Ripeness）：** 爭議是否已經適合司法裁決，還是提出得太早？
- **Mootness:** Does a live dispute still exist?
  **失去實益（Mootness）：** 現在是否仍然存在實際爭議？
- **Political question:** Is the issue one that constitutional doctrine leaves to the political branches rather than judicial resolution?
  **政治問題原則（Political Question）：** 依憲法原則，該問題是否應由政治部門處理，而非由法院裁決？

## 8. How courts interpret constitutional text
## 8. 法院如何解釋憲法文本

Judges and scholars use different methods to interpret constitutional provisions. The methods may overlap, and judges may disagree about how much weight each deserves.
法官及學者會使用不同方法解釋憲法條文。這些方法可能互相重疊，法官也可能對每種方法應占多大比重存在分歧。

- **Textual analysis:** What do the words mean in context?
  **文本分析：** 這些文字在上下文中代表什麼意思？
- **Original meaning:** What public meaning did the words have when adopted?
  **原始含義：** 條文通過時，這些文字在當時具有什麼公共含義？
- **Precedent:** How have earlier courts interpreted the provision?
  **判例先例：** 先前法院如何解釋這項條文？
- **Constitutional structure:** How does the provision fit with the rest of the Constitution?
  **憲法架構：** 這項條文如何與憲法其他部分配合？
- **Historical practice and tradition:** What relevant historical practices help explain the provision?
  **歷史實踐及傳統：** 哪些相關歷史做法有助於理解條文？
- **Practical consequences:** What are the likely effects of competing interpretations?
  **實際後果：** 不同解釋可能造成什麼結果？

Do not assume that all judges use one identical method or that one method always decides every case.
不要假設所有法官都使用完全相同的方法，也不要假設任何一種方法都能決定每一宗案件。

## 9. Levels of scrutiny — a first introduction
## 9. 憲法審查標準——初步認識

Courts use different levels of review in some constitutional cases. The correct standard depends on the right, classification, and doctrine involved.
在某些憲法案件中，法院會採用不同程度的審查。適用哪一種標準，取決於涉及的權利、分類及法律原則。

### Strict scrutiny
### 嚴格審查

The government generally must show a compelling governmental interest and narrow tailoring.
政府通常必須證明具有迫切的重要政府利益，且措施經過嚴格限定。

This standard commonly applies to racial classifications by government and certain fundamental-rights burdens.
這項標準通常適用於政府的種族分類，以及對某些基本權利的限制。

### Intermediate scrutiny
### 中度審查

The government generally must show an important governmental objective and a substantial relationship between the classification and that objective.
政府通常必須證明具有重要政府目標，而且分類方式與該目標之間存在實質關聯。

This standard commonly applies to sex-based classifications.
這項標準通常適用於基於性別的分類。

### Rational-basis review
### 合理基礎審查

The government generally needs a legitimate governmental interest and a rational relationship between the challenged rule and that interest.
政府通常只需要證明具有正當政府利益，而且受到挑戰的規則與該利益之間存在合理關聯。

This is the usual standard for many classifications that do not trigger a higher level of review.
對於許多不會觸發更高審查標準的分類，通常採用這項標準。

These are introductory descriptions, not mechanical formulas. Some constitutional doctrines use different tests.
以上只是入門說明，不是機械式公式。某些憲法原則會使用不同測試方法。

## 10. A beginner's constitutional checklist
## 10. 初學者憲法分析清單

When someone says, “The government violated my constitutional rights,” ask:
當有人說「政府侵犯了我的憲法權利」時，請先問：

1. Which government actor acted — federal, state, or local?
   是哪一級政府採取行動——聯邦、州，還是地方？
2. What exactly did the government do?
   政府究竟做了什麼？
3. Which constitutional provision may apply?
   哪一項憲法條文可能適用？
4. Is the conduct government action?
   該行為是否屬於政府行為？
5. What legal test or standard applies?
   適用哪一項法律測試或標準？
6. What facts are missing?
   還缺少哪些事實？
7. What court has authority to decide the dispute?
   哪個法院有權審理這項爭議？
8. What remedy could the court legally provide?
   法院依法可能提供什麼救濟？

**Educational use only; not legal advice.**
**僅供教育學習，不構成法律意見。**
