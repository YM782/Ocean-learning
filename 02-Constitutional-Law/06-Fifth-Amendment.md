# Fifth Amendment — Self-Incrimination, Double Jeopardy, and Due Process
# 第五修正案——不得自證其罪、禁止雙重危險及正當法律程序

## 1. The constitutional text
## 1. 憲法原文

The Fifth Amendment contains several protections. Its text begins:
第五修正案包含多項保障。其開頭規定：

“No person shall be held to answer for a capital, or otherwise infamous crime, unless on a presentment or indictment of a Grand Jury ...”
「任何人不得因死刑罪或其他重大犯罪而被追究刑事責任，除非經大陪審團提出起訴……」

It later provides that no person may be twice put in jeopardy for the same offense, compelled to be a witness against himself in a criminal case, or deprived of life, liberty, or property without due process of law.
後文還規定，任何人不得因同一罪行而兩次陷入刑事危險，不得在刑事案件中被迫作出不利於自己的證言，也不得未經正當法律程序就被剝奪生命、自由或財產。

The Amendment also includes the Takings Clause, which requires just compensation when private property is taken for public use.
該修正案還包含徵收條款，規定政府為公共用途徵收私人財產時，必須提供公平補償。

## 2. The Grand Jury Clause
## 2. 大陪審團條款

A **grand jury** is a group of citizens that considers whether there is sufficient basis to issue an indictment in certain serious federal criminal cases.
**大陪審團（Grand Jury）** 是由公民組成的團體，在某些嚴重的聯邦刑事案件中，負責考慮是否有足夠依據提出正式起訴。

A grand jury does not decide whether the accused is guilty at trial. Its role is different from that of a trial jury.
大陪審團不負責在審判中決定被告是否有罪。它的職能與審判陪審團不同。

**Important limit:** The Grand Jury Clause has not been incorporated against the states. States may use different charging procedures under their own constitutions and laws.
**重要限制：** 大陪審團條款並未納入適用於各州。各州可以依自己的州憲法及法律採用不同的起訴程序。

## 3. Double Jeopardy Clause
## 3. 禁止雙重危險條款

The Double Jeopardy Clause protects a person from certain repeated prosecutions or punishments for the same offense.
禁止雙重危險條款保障個人免受某些針對同一罪行的重複起訴或處罰。

At a beginner level, remember three common protections:
初學者可以先記住三項常見保障：

- A second prosecution after an acquittal is generally barred.
  已經無罪判決後，通常不得再次就同一罪行起訴。
- A second prosecution after a conviction is generally barred, subject to the governing rules.
  已經定罪後，通常不得再次就同一罪行起訴，但仍須考慮適用法律原則。
- Multiple punishments for the same offense in the same proceeding may be restricted.
  在同一程序中，對同一罪行施加多重刑罰可能受到限制。

**Do not overgeneralize:** The separate-sovereigns doctrine may allow successive federal and state prosecutions for the same conduct when the offenses are prosecuted by separate sovereigns. The doctrine is complex and has limits.
**不要過度概括：** 如果聯邦及州政府被視為不同主權者，分別追訴同一行為，獨立主權原則可能允許聯邦及州先後起訴。這項原則相當複雜，也有其限制。

The Double Jeopardy Clause applies to state governments through the Fourteenth Amendment.
禁止雙重危險條款透過第十四修正案適用於州政府。

## 4. Privilege against self-incrimination
## 4. 不得自證其罪的特權

The Fifth Amendment protects a person from being compelled to provide testimonial evidence that may incriminate that person in a criminal case.
第五修正案保障個人不會被強迫提供可能使自己在刑事案件中受到不利追訴的證言性證據。

The privilege focuses on compelled testimonial communication. It does not automatically prevent the government from collecting every kind of physical evidence.
這項特權主要針對被迫作出的證言性溝通。它不會自動阻止政府收集所有種類的物理證據。

For example, fingerprints, photographs, and certain physical samples are generally treated differently from compelled testimonial answers.
例如，指紋、照片及某些身體樣本，通常會與被迫提供的證言答案受到不同的法律處理。

A person may invoke the privilege in an appropriate setting, but the timing and method of invocation matter.
個人在適當情況下可以主張這項特權，但主張的時間及方式十分重要。

## 5. Miranda warnings
## 5. Miranda 權利告知

In *Miranda v. Arizona* (1966), the Supreme Court required law-enforcement officers to provide warnings before **custodial interrogation**.
在1966年的 *Miranda v. Arizona* 案中，最高法院要求執法人員在進行**羈押狀態下的訊問（Custodial Interrogation）**之前，作出權利告知。

The familiar warnings include:
常見的權利告知包括：

- You have the right to remain silent.
  你有權保持沉默。
- Anything you say may be used against you in court.
  你所說的話可能在法庭上被用作對你不利的證據。
- You have the right to consult with an attorney and to have an attorney present during questioning.
  你有權諮詢律師，並有權在訊問期間由律師陪同。
- If you cannot afford an attorney, one may be appointed under the applicable rules.
  如果你無力聘請律師，在符合適用條件時可以為你指定律師。

**Two conditions matter:** custody and interrogation.
**兩項條件十分重要：** 羈押狀態及訊問。

A routine conversation with police is not automatically custodial interrogation. A traffic stop is not automatically the same as formal custody for Miranda purposes. The facts matter.
與警方進行一般交談，不會自動構成 Miranda 意義上的羈押訊問。交通攔查也不會自動等同於正式羈押。必須看具體事實。

A Miranda violation does not automatically dismiss a criminal case. The usual remedy concerns the use of an unwarned statement in the prosecution's case-in-chief, subject to recognized exceptions and other rules.
違反 Miranda 規則並不會自動撤銷刑事案件。一般救濟主要涉及控方是否可以在主要舉證階段使用未經權利告知取得的陳述，但仍須考慮已被承認的例外及其他規則。

## 6. Due Process Clause
## 6. 正當法律程序條款

The Fifth Amendment's Due Process Clause limits the federal government. The Fourteenth Amendment contains a similar clause that limits state governments.
第五修正案的正當法律程序條款限制聯邦政府。第十四修正案也包含類似條款，用來限制州政府。

**Procedural due process** concerns the fairness of government decision-making procedures.
**程序性正當法律程序（Procedural Due Process）** 關注政府作出決定時，所使用的程序是否公平。

Depending on the protected interest and circumstances, required procedures may include notice, an opportunity to respond, and a neutral decision-maker.
依受保障利益及具體情況，所需程序可能包括通知、回應機會及中立的決策者。

**Substantive due process** concerns certain fundamental liberty interests that government may not infringe without adequate constitutional justification.
**實質性正當法律程序（Substantive Due Process）** 涉及某些基本自由利益；政府若要限制這些利益，必須具有充分的憲法正當理由。

These doctrines are complex and have changed through Supreme Court decisions. Do not assume every unfair government decision automatically violates due process.
這些法律原則相當複雜，並隨最高法院判決而發展。不要假設每一項不公平的政府決定都自動違反正當法律程序。

## 7. Takings Clause
## 7. 徵收條款

The Takings Clause says that private property may not be taken for public use without just compensation.
徵收條款規定，政府不得在沒有公平補償的情況下，為公共用途徵收私人財產。

A **taking** may involve direct physical appropriation or, in some circumstances, government regulation that goes so far that it is treated as a taking under constitutional doctrine.
**徵收（Taking）** 可能涉及直接實際取得財產；在某些情況下，政府管制嚴重到一定程度，也可能依憲法原則被視為徵收。

The Takings Clause applies to state and local governments through the Fourteenth Amendment.
徵收條款透過第十四修正案適用於州及地方政府。

## 8. Important cases
## 8. 重要判例

- *Miranda v. Arizona* (1966): Warnings required before custodial interrogation.
  *Miranda v. Arizona*（1966年）：在羈押狀態下訊問前必須作出權利告知。
- *Benton v. Maryland* (1969): The Double Jeopardy Clause applies to the states.
  *Benton v. Maryland*（1969年）：禁止雙重危險條款適用於各州。
- *Mathews v. Eldridge* (1976): A balancing framework for certain procedural due-process questions.
  *Mathews v. Eldridge*（1976年）：為某些程序性正當法律程序問題建立衡量架構。
- *Kelo v. City of New London* (2005): Addressed the meaning of public use under the Takings Clause.
  *Kelo v. City of New London*（2005年）：處理徵收條款中「公共用途」的含義。

## 9. Mini example
## 9. 迷你案例

A person is arrested. While the person is in custody, detectives question the person about the crime without giving Miranda warnings.
某人被逮捕後處於羈押狀態，偵查人員在沒有作出 Miranda 權利告知的情況下，就犯罪問題對其進行訊問。

**Issue:** Does the Fifth Amendment and Miranda doctrine apply?
**爭點：** 第五修正案及 Miranda 原則是否適用？

**Rule:** Miranda warnings are generally required before custodial interrogation.
**規則：** 羈押狀態下訊問前，通常必須作出 Miranda 權利告知。

**Application:** The facts indicate custody and questioning. More facts are needed to determine whether an exception applies and what remedy is available.
**適用：** 已知事實顯示當事人處於羈押狀態並受到訊問。仍需要更多事實，才能判斷是否適用例外及可以取得什麼救濟。

**Conclusion:** The statement may be subject to exclusion from the prosecution's case-in-chief, but the entire prosecution is not automatically dismissed.
**結論：** 該陳述可能不得在控方主要舉證階段使用，但整宗刑事追訴不會因此自動撤銷。
