# Constitutional Law — Exercises and Answer Key
# 憲法學——練習題及答案解析

Try to answer before reading the explanation. The goal is to understand the legal reasoning, not to memorize letters.
請先自行回答，再閱讀解析。學習目標是理解法律推理，而不是背答案字母。

## Part A — Multiple choice
## A部分——選擇題

### 1. What is the main purpose of the U.S. Constitution?
### 1. 美國憲法的主要目的為何？

A. It only lists individual rights. / 它只列出個人權利。
B. It creates and limits government institutions. / 它建立並限制政府機關。
C. It replaces all state law. / 它取代所有州法。
D. It governs only criminal trials. / 它只規範刑事審判。

**Answer: B.** The Constitution creates government structures, allocates powers, and limits government action.
**答案：B。** 憲法建立政府架構、分配權力並限制政府行為。

### 2. Which amendment contains the Establishment and Free Exercise Clauses?
### 2. 哪一條修正案包含政教分立條款及宗教自由行使條款？

A. First / 第一修正案
B. Fourth / 第四修正案
C. Fifth / 第五修正案
D. Eighth / 第八修正案

**Answer: A.** Both religion clauses are part of the First Amendment.
**答案：A。** 兩項宗教條款都屬於第一修正案。

### 3. Is every offensive statement unprotected by the First Amendment?
### 3. 所有令人反感的言論都不受第一修正案保障嗎？

A. Yes. / 是。
B. No. / 否。

**Answer: B.** Offensive or unpopular speech is often protected. Only narrow categories, such as legally defined incitement or true threats, may fall outside ordinary protection.
**答案：B。** 令人反感或不受歡迎的言論通常仍受保障。只有法律嚴格界定的少數類別，例如煽動或真實威脅，才可能不受一般保障。

### 4. What framework did Bruen establish for Second Amendment challenges?
### 4. Bruen 案為第二修正案爭議建立了什麼審查架構？

A. A simple balancing of cost and benefit. / 簡單衡量成本及利益。
B. Text and historical tradition. / 文本及歷史傳統。
C. Automatic approval of all gun regulations. / 自動批准所有槍械管制。
D. Automatic invalidation of all gun regulations. / 自動判定所有槍械管制違憲。

**Answer: B.** The Court asks whether the text covers the conduct and whether the government can justify the regulation through historical tradition.
**答案：B。** 法院會判斷憲法文本是否涵蓋相關行為，以及政府能否透過歷史傳統為管制措施提供正當理由。

### 5. What is probable cause?
### 5. 什麼是合理根據？

A. A mere guess. / 單純猜測。
B. A fact-based probability sufficient under the legal standard. / 依法律標準足夠的事實性可能性。
C. Proof beyond a reasonable doubt. / 排除合理懷疑的證明。
D. A conviction. / 定罪。

**Answer: B.** Probable cause is more than a hunch but less than proof beyond a reasonable doubt.
**答案：B。** 合理根據高於單純猜測，但低於排除合理懷疑的證明標準。

### 6. Does police possession of a phone automatically permit a search of all its digital contents?
### 6. 警方扣押手機後，是否自動可以搜查其中所有數位內容？

A. Yes. / 是。
B. No. / 否。

**Answer: B.** Under Riley, police generally need a warrant to search the digital contents of a phone seized incident to arrest, subject to applicable exceptions.
**答案：B。** 依 Riley 案，警方通常需要令狀才能搜查因逮捕而扣押的手機數位內容，但仍須考慮適用例外。

### 7. Does Miranda apply to every conversation with police?
### 7. Miranda 是否適用於與警方的每一次交談？

A. Yes. / 是。
B. No. / 否。

**Answer: B.** The familiar rule concerns custodial interrogation. Both custody and interrogation matter.
**答案：B。** 一般 Miranda 規則涉及羈押狀態下的訊問。羈押及訊問兩項條件都很重要。

### 8. Which amendment contains the right to counsel in criminal prosecutions?
### 8. 哪一條修正案包含刑事追訴中的律師協助權？

A. Second / 第二修正案
B. Sixth / 第六修正案
C. Eighth / 第八修正案
D. Tenth / 第十修正案

**Answer: B.** The Sixth Amendment protects the right to assistance of counsel in criminal prosecutions.
**答案：B。** 第六修正案保障刑事追訴中的律師協助權。

### 9. Does the Constitution guarantee bail in every criminal case?
### 9. 憲法是否保證每宗刑事案件都可以保釋？

A. Yes. / 是。
B. No. / 否。

**Answer: B.** The Eighth Amendment prohibits excessive bail; it does not create an unlimited right to bail in every case.
**答案：B。** 第八修正案禁止過高保釋金，但沒有建立每宗案件都可以無條件保釋的權利。

### 10. Which amendment contains the Equal Protection Clause?
### 10. 哪一條修正案包含平等保護條款？

A. First / 第一修正案
B. Fourth / 第四修正案
C. Fourteenth / 第十四修正案
D. Twenty-First / 第二十一修正案

**Answer: C.** The Equal Protection Clause is in Section 1 of the Fourteenth Amendment.
**答案：C。** 平等保護條款位於第十四修正案第一節。

## Part B — True or false
## B部分——是非題

### 11. The First Amendment generally restricts government action, not every private decision.
### 11. 第一修正案通常限制政府行為，而不是所有私人決定。

**Answer: True.** The state-action requirement is a basic starting point.
**答案：對。** 政府行為原則是憲法分析的基本起點。

### 12. A warrantless search is always unconstitutional.
### 12. 沒有令狀的搜查永遠違憲。

**Answer: False.** Recognized exceptions exist, but each has specific requirements.
**答案：錯。** 法律承認多種例外，但每一項例外都有具體要求。

### 13. The Fifth Amendment Grand Jury Clause applies to every state in the same way.
### 13. 第五修正案大陪審團條款以相同方式適用於每一州。

**Answer: False.** The Grand Jury Clause has not been incorporated against the states.
**答案：錯。** 大陪審團條款並未納入適用於各州。

### 14. The Sixth Amendment right to counsel and Miranda warnings are exactly the same right.
### 14. 第六修正案律師協助權與 Miranda 權利告知完全相同。

**Answer: False.** They arise under different doctrines and have different triggers.
**答案：錯。** 兩者源自不同法律原則，適用起點也不同。

### 15. The Fourteenth Amendment is one important route through which many Bill of Rights protections apply to states.
### 15. 第十四修正案是《權利法案》許多保障適用於各州的重要途徑。

**Answer: True.** This process is called incorporation.
**答案：對。** 這個過程稱為權利納入適用。

## Part C — Short-answer reasoning
## C部分——簡答及法律推理

### 16. A city allows a political group to hold a peaceful rally but bans another group solely because city officials dislike its message. What issue arises?
### 16. 某城市允許一個政治團體舉行和平集會，卻僅僅因為官員不喜歡另一個團體的訊息而禁止其集會。這引發什麼問題？

**Sample answer:** The decision may be viewpoint discrimination under the First Amendment. The analysis should identify the forum, the government's stated reason, and any neutral rules that apply.
**參考答案：** 這項決定可能構成第一修正案下的觀點歧視。分析時應辨識場所類型、政府提出的理由，以及是否存在中立適用的規則。

### 17. Police stop a person briefly after observing specific conduct that reasonably suggests criminal activity. Is this automatically an arrest?
### 17. 警方觀察到具體行為，合理懷疑存在犯罪活動後短暫攔查某人。這是否自動構成逮捕？

**Sample answer:** No. A brief Terry stop may be based on reasonable suspicion. An arrest generally requires probable cause. The scope and duration of the encounter matter.
**參考答案：** 不是。依 Terry 原則，短暫攔查可能以合理懷疑為依據；逮捕通常需要合理根據。接觸的範圍及持續時間都很重要。

### 18. A person is questioned in custody without Miranda warnings. Does the entire criminal case automatically disappear?
### 18. 某人在羈押狀態下受到訊問，但警方沒有作出 Miranda 權利告知。整宗刑事案件會自動消失嗎？

**Sample answer:** No. The statement may be excluded from the prosecution's case-in-chief, subject to exceptions. Other evidence and the rest of the case must be analyzed separately.
**參考答案：** 不會。該陳述可能不得在控方主要舉證階段使用，但仍須考慮例外。其他證據及案件其他部分必須分開分析。

### 19. A state law treats people differently based on race. What level of scrutiny generally applies?
### 19. 某州法律基於種族而區別對待不同的人。通常適用哪一種審查標準？

**Sample answer:** Strict scrutiny generally applies to government racial classifications. The government must show a compelling interest and narrow tailoring.
**參考答案：** 政府的種族分類通常受到嚴格審查。政府必須證明具有迫切的重要利益，而且措施經過嚴格限定。

## Part D — IRAC exercise
## D部分——IRAC 練習

**Facts:** A public school suspends a student for peacefully criticizing a government policy on a T-shirt. The student does not interrupt class or threaten anyone.
**事實：** 一所公立學校因學生在 T 恤上和平批評政府政策而將其停學。學生沒有干擾課堂，也沒有威脅任何人。

**Issue:** Does the suspension raise a First Amendment issue?
**爭點：** 這項停學處分是否引發第一修正案問題？

**Rule:** Under *Tinker*, student expression is generally protected unless it materially and substantially disrupts school operations or invades the rights of others, subject to the relevant school-speech doctrine.
**規則：** 依 *Tinker*，學生表達通常受到保障，除非其對學校運作造成重大且實質的干擾，或侵犯他人權利，但仍須考慮相關校園言論原則。

**Application:** The facts say the expression was peaceful and did not interrupt class or threaten anyone. The school would need to identify a constitutionally sufficient reason under the applicable standard.
**適用：** 已知事實顯示學生的表達是和平的，沒有干擾課堂或威脅他人。學校必須依適用標準提出充分的憲法理由。

**Conclusion:** The suspension raises a serious First Amendment concern. More facts about the school setting, policy, and any actual disruption may matter.
**結論：** 這項停學處分引發嚴重的第一修正案疑問。學校環境、校規及是否實際造成干擾等更多事實，仍可能影響結果。
