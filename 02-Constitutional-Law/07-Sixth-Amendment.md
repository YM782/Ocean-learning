# Sixth Amendment — Rights of the Accused in Criminal Cases
# 第六修正案——刑事被告的審判權利

## 1. The constitutional text
## 1. 憲法原文

“In all criminal prosecutions, the accused shall enjoy the right to a speedy and public trial, by an impartial jury ... and to be informed of the nature and cause of the accusation; to be confronted with the witnesses against him; to have compulsory process for obtaining witnesses in his favor, and to have the Assistance of Counsel for his defence.”
「在所有刑事追訴中，被告有權獲得迅速且公開的審判，由公正的陪審團審理……有權獲知對其指控的性質及原因；有權與不利於自己的證人對質；有權強制傳喚有利於自己的證人，並有權獲得律師協助進行辯護。」

The Sixth Amendment protects a person after the government begins a criminal prosecution. It helps make the trial fair.
第六修正案保障政府開始刑事追訴後的被告，目的是協助確保審判公平。

## 2. When does the right attach?
## 2. 這些權利何時開始適用？

Many Sixth Amendment rights attach when formal adversarial judicial proceedings begin, such as through indictment, information, arraignment, or an initial appearance, depending on the right and circumstances.
許多第六修正案權利會在正式對抗性司法程序開始時適用，例如提出起訴書、正式控告、提訊或首次出庭，但仍須視具體權利及情況而定。

The Sixth Amendment right to counsel is different from the Miranda right to counsel during custodial interrogation. The two protections can overlap, but they have different triggers and rules.
第六修正案的律師協助權，與 Miranda 規則中羈押訊問時的律師權利並不相同。兩者可能重疊，但適用起點及規則不同。

## 3. Speedy trial
## 3. 迅速審判權

The government should not leave a person facing criminal charges under unjustified delay.
政府不應在沒有正當理由的情況下，讓面臨刑事指控的人長期等待審判。

In *Barker v. Wingo* (1972), the Supreme Court described a balancing approach that considers:
在1972年的 *Barker v. Wingo* 案中，最高法院提出衡量方法，考慮以下因素：

- The length of the delay.
  延誤的時間長度。
- The reason for the delay.
  延誤的原因。
- Whether and how the defendant asserted the right.
  被告是否主張迅速審判權，以及如何主張。
- Prejudice to the defendant.
  延誤對被告造成的損害。

A delay does not automatically violate the Constitution. Courts examine all relevant circumstances.
審判延誤並不會自動違憲。法院會檢查所有相關情況。

## 4. Public trial
## 4. 公開審判權

A public trial allows the public and press to observe court proceedings, supporting transparency and accountability.
公開審判讓公眾及新聞媒體能夠旁聽法院程序，促進透明度及問責。

The right is not unlimited. Courts may close portions of a proceeding only under demanding legal standards and with appropriate findings.
這項權利並非毫無限制。法院只有在符合嚴格法律標準並作出適當認定時，才可能關閉部分審判程序。

## 5. Impartial jury
## 5. 公正陪審團

A jury must be impartial. The jury-selection process cannot use unconstitutional discrimination.
陪審團必須保持公正。陪審團遴選程序不得採用違憲歧視。

The constitutional jury-trial right generally applies to serious criminal offenses, not every minor offense. The details of what counts as a serious offense are determined by doctrine.
憲法上的陪審團審判權通常適用於嚴重刑事犯罪，而不是每一項輕微違法行為。何謂嚴重犯罪，須依法律原則判斷。

In *Ramos v. Louisiana* (2020), the Supreme Court held that the Sixth Amendment requires unanimous jury verdicts to convict in serious criminal cases in state and federal courts.
在2020年的 *Ramos v. Louisiana* 案中，最高法院裁定，州及聯邦法院在嚴重刑事案件中，陪審團必須一致同意才能作出有罪裁決。

## 6. Notice of the accusation
## 6. 指控通知權

The accused must be informed of the nature and cause of the accusation.
被告有權獲知指控的性質及原因。

This requirement helps the defendant understand what conduct the government alleges and prepare a defense.
這項要求有助於被告了解政府指控的具體行為，並準備辯護。

## 7. Confrontation Clause
## 7. 對質條款

The Confrontation Clause protects a defendant's opportunity to confront witnesses against them.
對質條款保障被告與不利於自己的證人對質的機會。

In *Crawford v. Washington* (2004), the Supreme Court held that testimonial statements by an unavailable witness generally cannot be used against a criminal defendant unless the defendant had a prior opportunity for cross-examination, subject to the doctrine's details.
在2004年的 *Crawford v. Washington* 案中，最高法院裁定，如果證人無法出庭，其證言性陳述通常不得用來對付刑事被告，除非被告先前有機會進行交叉詢問，但仍須考慮相關法律原則的具體細節。

The rule does not mean every out-of-court statement is automatically excluded. Courts must determine whether the statement is testimonial and whether a recognized doctrine applies.
這項規則並不代表所有庭外陳述都自動排除。法院必須判斷陳述是否屬於證言性陳述，以及是否適用已被承認的法律原則。

## 8. Compulsory process
## 8. 強制傳喚證人權

The defendant has a right to use lawful compulsory process to obtain witnesses in the defendant's favor.
被告有權透過合法的強制程序，取得有利於自己的證人。

This right helps the defense present its side of the case rather than relying only on the prosecution's evidence.
這項權利讓辯方能夠提出自己的證據，而不是只能依賴控方的證據。

## 9. Assistance of counsel
## 9. 律師協助權

In *Gideon v. Wainwright* (1963), the Supreme Court held that states must provide counsel to indigent defendants charged with serious crimes when the constitutional right applies.
在1963年的 *Gideon v. Wainwright* 案中，最高法院裁定，在憲法權利適用的情況下，各州必須為無力聘請律師、且被控嚴重犯罪的被告提供律師。

The right to appointed counsel is not a universal promise of a free lawyer for every civil or criminal matter. Its scope depends on the type of proceeding and the possible punishment.
指定律師權並不是承諾所有民事或刑事事項都能免費獲得律師。其適用範圍取決於程序類型及可能面臨的刑罰。

The right to effective assistance of counsel is also protected. Under *Strickland v. Washington* (1984), a defendant generally must show deficient performance and prejudice to establish ineffective assistance.
有效律師協助權也受到保障。依1984年的 *Strickland v. Washington* 案，被告通常必須證明律師表現低於合理專業標準，並且因此受到損害，才能成立無效律師協助主張。

## 10. A practical example
## 10. 實際例子

A person is charged with a serious felony, cannot afford a lawyer, and the court tells the person to represent himself without first explaining the right to appointed counsel.
某人被控嚴重重罪，無力聘請律師，但法院沒有先解釋指定律師權，就要求此人自行辯護。

**Issue:** Does the Sixth Amendment require appointed counsel?
**爭點：** 第六修正案是否要求法院指定律師？

**Rule:** Under *Gideon*, an indigent defendant charged with a serious crime has a constitutional right to appointed counsel in state criminal proceedings.
**規則：** 依 *Gideon*，在州刑事程序中，無力聘請律師且被控嚴重犯罪的被告享有憲法上的指定律師權。

**Application:** The facts indicate a serious felony and inability to afford counsel. More facts are needed about waiver, eligibility, and the precise stage of the proceedings.
**適用：** 已知事實顯示案件涉及嚴重重罪，而且當事人無力聘請律師。仍需要更多關於放棄權利、資格及程序階段的事實。

**Conclusion:** The court's conduct raises a serious Sixth Amendment concern.
**結論：** 法院的做法引發嚴重的第六修正案疑問。
