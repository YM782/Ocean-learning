# First Amendment — Religion
# 第一修正案——宗教自由

## 1. The constitutional text
## 1. 憲法原文

**English text:**
**英文原文：**

“Congress shall make no law respecting an establishment of religion, or prohibiting the free exercise thereof ...”
「國會不得制定關於確立國教的法律，也不得禁止人民自由信奉宗教……」

**Learning translation:**
**學習用中文翻譯：**

「國會不得制定確立國教的法律，也不得禁止人民自由實踐宗教信仰……」

The First Amendment's religion protections are commonly discussed through two clauses: the Establishment Clause and the Free Exercise Clause.
第一修正案的宗教保障通常透過兩項條款討論：政教分立條款及宗教自由行使條款。

## 2. Establishment Clause
## 2. 政教分立條款

The Establishment Clause limits government involvement in establishing, sponsoring, or officially favoring religion.
政教分立條款限制政府建立、支持或正式偏袒宗教。

It does not mean that religious people must be excluded from public life. Government must respect constitutional limits while treating religious and nonreligious people fairly.
這並不代表有宗教信仰的人必須被排除在公共生活之外。政府必須尊重憲法限制，並公平對待有宗教信仰及沒有宗教信仰的人。

**Example:** A public school cannot simply require students to participate in a government-composed prayer as an official school activity.
**例子：** 公立學校不能僅憑行政安排，就要求學生參加由政府編寫、作為學校官方活動的祈禱。

## 3. Free Exercise Clause
## 3. 宗教自由行使條款

The Free Exercise Clause protects religious belief and religious practice against certain forms of government interference.
宗教自由行使條款保障宗教信念及宗教實踐，使其免受某些政府干預。

A government may not target a religious practice for unfavorable treatment simply because it is religious.
政府不能僅僅因為某種行為具有宗教性，就特意對它作出不利待遇。

A law that is neutral toward religion and generally applies to everyone may often be enforced even if it incidentally burdens a religious practice. But a law that is not neutral or not generally applicable may trigger a more demanding constitutional review.
一項對宗教保持中立、且普遍適用於所有人的法律，即使附帶影響某種宗教實踐，通常仍可能獲得執行。但如果法律並不中立，或並非普遍適用，就可能觸發更嚴格的憲法審查。

This is a simplified introduction. Exceptions and the applicable test depend on the facts and controlling cases.
這只是簡化的入門說明。例外及適用標準取決於具體事實及具有拘束力的判例。

## 4. Religious belief and religious conduct
## 4. 宗教信念與宗教行為

Religious belief receives very strong constitutional protection. Conduct associated with religion may still be subject to neutral laws in some circumstances.
宗教信念受到非常強的憲法保障。與宗教有關的行為，在某些情況下仍可能受到中立法律規範。

For example, the government may enforce ordinary health and safety rules in appropriate circumstances, but it cannot selectively burden religious conduct without a constitutionally sufficient reason.
例如，在適當情況下，政府可以執行一般健康及安全規則，但不能在沒有充分憲法理由的情況下，選擇性地加重宗教行為的負擔。

## 5. Religion in public schools
## 5. 公立學校中的宗教

Public schools are government institutions. Their officials must respect the First Amendment.
公立學校屬於政府機構，其工作人員必須遵守第一修正案。

A school may teach about religion as an academic subject in an objective educational manner. That is different from sponsoring religious worship.
學校可以以客觀的教育方式教授宗教相關知識。這與學校支持宗教崇拜並不相同。

Students generally retain their own religious expression rights, subject to applicable school rules and constitutional limits.
學生通常保有自己的宗教表達權，但仍須遵守適用的校規及憲法限制。

A government employee's private religious expression is not automatically the government's religious speech. The context, job duties, audience, and whether the government appears to endorse the expression all matter.
政府僱員的私人宗教表達，不會自動變成政府的宗教言論。必須考慮具體情境、工作職責、受眾，以及政府是否看起來在支持該宗教表達。

## 6. Important cases
## 6. 重要判例

### Engel v. Vitale (1962)
### Engel v. Vitale（1962年）

The Supreme Court held that government-composed prayer in public schools violated the Establishment Clause, even when participation was described as voluntary.
最高法院裁定，即使政府將參與描述為自願，公立學校中的政府編寫祈禱仍違反政教分立條款。

### Employment Division v. Smith (1990)
### Employment Division v. Smith（1990年）

The Court held that the Free Exercise Clause generally does not require exemptions from neutral, generally applicable laws merely because they incidentally burden religious practice.
法院認為，對於中立且普遍適用的法律，僅因其附帶影響宗教實踐，宗教自由行使條款通常不會自動要求豁免。

### Church of the Lukumi Babalu Aye v. Hialeah (1993)
### Church of the Lukumi Babalu Aye v. Hialeah（1993年）

The Court invalidated ordinances that targeted a religious practice rather than neutrally regulating conduct.
最高法院判定，針對特定宗教實踐而非中立規範行為的地方條例違憲。

### Kennedy v. Bremerton School District (2022)
### Kennedy v. Bremerton School District（2022年）

The Court ruled for a public-school football coach who engaged in personal prayer after games. The decision rejected use of the former *Lemon* test and the related endorsement test as the governing Establishment Clause framework, emphasizing historical practices and understandings.
最高法院支持一名在比賽後進行個人祈禱的公立學校足球教練。該判決不再採用過去的 *Lemon* 測試及相關認可測試作為政教分立條款的主導架構，而是強調歷史實踐及歷史理解。

**Important:** This case does not mean every religious activity by a public employee is automatically lawful. The facts and the distinction between private expression and government-sponsored religious activity matter.
**重要提醒：** 本案並不代表公職人員或公立學校僱員的所有宗教活動都自動合法。必須考慮具體事實，以及私人表達與政府支持宗教活動之間的區別。

## 7. How to analyze a religion question
## 7. 如何分析宗教自由問題

Ask these questions:
請提出以下問題：

1. Is the actor the government or a private person?
   行為者是政府還是私人？
2. Is the government establishing, sponsoring, or favoring religion?
   政府是否建立、支持或偏袒宗教？
3. Is the government restricting a person's religious belief or practice?
   政府是否限制某人的宗教信念或實踐？
4. Is the rule neutral and generally applicable?
   該規則是否保持中立並普遍適用？
5. What facts and precedent control the result?
   哪些事實及判例對結果具有決定性影響？

## 8. Mini example
## 8. 迷你案例

A city allows community groups to reserve a public meeting room. It allows secular clubs to meet but refuses a religious club solely because the club is religious.
某城市允許社區團體預約公共會議室。它允許世俗社團使用，卻僅僅因為某個社團具有宗教性，就拒絕該團體使用。

**Issue:** Does the city's unequal treatment raise a Free Exercise or speech issue?
**爭點：** 城市的差別待遇是否引發宗教自由行使或言論自由問題？

**Rule:** Government generally may not exclude a group from a generally available public benefit solely because of its religious character, subject to the details of the program and controlling doctrine.
**規則：** 政府通常不能僅僅因為某個團體具有宗教性，就將它排除在普遍開放的公共利益之外，但仍須考慮計畫細節及具有拘束力的法律原則。

**Application:** Compare the treatment of religious and nonreligious groups and examine the eligibility rules.
**適用：** 比較宗教及非宗教團體受到的待遇，並檢查實際資格規則。

**Conclusion:** The refusal may raise a serious constitutional concern. More facts are needed before reaching a final legal conclusion.
**結論：** 這項拒絕可能引發嚴重的憲法疑問。作出最終法律結論前，仍需要更多事實。

## Key terms
## 重要詞彙

- Establishment Clause — 政教分立條款
- Free Exercise Clause — 宗教自由行使條款
- Religious neutrality — 宗教中立
- Government endorsement — 政府支持／認可
- Generally applicable law — 普遍適用的法律
- Religious accommodation — 宗教合理調整／豁免安排
