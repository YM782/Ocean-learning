# Constitutional Law — Guided Case Studies
# 憲法學——引導式判例分析

These case notes are learning summaries. They do not replace the full judicial opinions.
以下判例筆記是學習用摘要，不能取代完整的法院判決意見。

## How to read every case
## 如何閱讀每一宗判例

- **Facts:** What happened?
  **事實：** 發生了什麼事？
- **Procedural history:** How did the case reach this court?
  **程序經過：** 案件如何來到這個法院？
- **Issue:** What constitutional question did the court answer?
  **爭點：** 法院回答了什麼憲法問題？
- **Rule:** What legal principle did the court apply?
  **規則：** 法院適用了什麼法律原則？
- **Holding:** What did the court decide?
  **裁判要旨：** 法院作出了什麼決定？
- **Reasoning:** Why did the court decide that way?
  **推理：** 法院為什麼作出這個決定？
- **Scope:** What does the decision not decide?
  **適用範圍：** 這項判決沒有決定什麼？

## First Amendment cases
## 第一修正案判例

### 1. Tinker v. Des Moines Independent Community School District (1969)
### 1. Tinker v. Des Moines Independent Community School District（1969年）

**Facts:** Students wore black armbands to protest the Vietnam War. The school suspended them under a policy adopted to prevent the protest.
**事實：** 學生佩戴黑色臂章，抗議越南戰爭。學校為阻止這項抗議而制定規則，並停學處分學生。

**Issue:** Did the school's action violate the students' First Amendment rights?
**爭點：** 學校的行為是否侵犯學生的第一修正案權利？

**Holding:** The Supreme Court ruled for the students. Student expression is protected unless it materially and substantially disrupts school operations or invades the rights of others under the applicable standard.
**裁判要旨：** 最高法院支持學生。依適用標準，學生表達受到保障，除非其對學校運作造成重大且實質的干擾，或侵犯他人權利。

**Lesson:** Public schools are government actors, but the school setting may affect how constitutional rules apply.
**學習重點：** 公立學校屬於政府機關，但學校環境可能影響憲法規則的適用方式。

### 2. Brandenburg v. Ohio (1969)
### 2. Brandenburg v. Ohio（1969年）

**Issue:** When may government punish advocacy of force or lawbreaking?
**爭點：** 政府在什麼情況下可以處罰鼓吹暴力或違法的言論？

**Holding:** Advocacy may be punished as incitement only when it is directed to inciting or producing imminent lawless action and is likely to produce such action.
**裁判要旨：** 只有當鼓吹行為旨在煽動或造成迫在眉睫的違法行動，而且很可能造成該行動時，才可能作為煽動而受到處罰。

**Lesson:** Political extremity or offensive language alone does not automatically satisfy the incitement test.
**學習重點：** 政治立場極端或言論令人反感，本身並不會自動符合煽動標準。

### 3. New York Times Co. v. Sullivan (1964)
### 3. New York Times Co. v. Sullivan（1964年）

**Issue:** What must a public official prove to recover damages for defamatory statements about official conduct?
**爭點：** 公職人員若要因涉及公務行為的誹謗性陳述獲得賠償，必須證明什麼？

**Holding:** The official must prove actual malice — knowledge of falsity or reckless disregard for truth — under the constitutional standard.
**裁判要旨：** 公職人員必須依憲法標準證明實際惡意，也就是明知虛假或罔顧真偽。

**Lesson:** Actual malice is a technical legal term, not simply personal hostility.
**學習重點：** 實際惡意是一個專業法律術語，不只是個人敵意。

### 4. Kennedy v. Bremerton School District (2022)
### 4. Kennedy v. Bremerton School District（2022年）

**Issue:** Did a public school violate the Establishment Clause by disciplining a coach for personal post-game prayer?
**爭點：** 公立學校因教練在賽後進行個人祈禱而處分他，是否違反政教分立條款？

**Holding:** The Supreme Court ruled for the coach on the facts presented and emphasized that the Establishment Clause must be interpreted by reference to historical practices and understandings, not the former *Lemon* test.
**裁判要旨：** 最高法院依本案事實支持教練，並強調政教分立條款應參照歷史實踐及歷史理解來解釋，而不是使用過去的 *Lemon* 測試。

**Lesson:** Distinguish private religious expression from government-sponsored religious activity.
**學習重點：** 必須區分私人宗教表達與政府支持的宗教活動。

## Second Amendment cases
## 第二修正案判例

### 5. District of Columbia v. Heller (2008)
### 5. District of Columbia v. Heller（2008年）

**Holding:** The Second Amendment protects an individual right to possess a handgun in the home for lawful purposes such as self-defense.
**裁判要旨：** 第二修正案保障個人為自我防衛等合法目的在家中持有手槍的權利。

**Lesson:** The right is individual, but the Court also explained that it is not unlimited.
**學習重點：** 這是一項個人權利，但最高法院也指出它並非毫無限制。

### 6. New York State Rifle & Pistol Association v. Bruen (2022)
### 6. New York State Rifle & Pistol Association v. Bruen（2022年）

**Holding:** The Court set out a text-and-historical-tradition framework for assessing Second Amendment challenges.
**裁判要旨：** 最高法院建立了以憲法文本及歷史傳統為基礎的第二修正案審查架構。

**Lesson:** A court examines whether the text covers the conduct and whether the government can justify the regulation by historical tradition.
**學習重點：** 法院會檢查憲法文本是否涵蓋相關行為，以及政府能否以歷史傳統為管制措施提供正當理由。

### 7. United States v. Rahimi (2024)
### 7. United States v. Rahimi（2024年）

**Holding:** A person found by a court to pose a credible threat to another person's physical safety may be temporarily disarmed consistent with the Second Amendment.
**裁判要旨：** 對經法院認定構成可信人身安全威脅的人實施暫時禁槍，可能符合第二修正案。

**Lesson:** *Bruen* does not make the right unlimited; historical principles may support some modern regulations.
**學習重點：** *Bruen* 並沒有使持槍權變成無限制權利；歷史原則可能支持某些現代管制措施。

## Fourth Amendment cases
## 第四修正案判例

### 8. Terry v. Ohio (1968)
### 8. Terry v. Ohio（1968年）

**Holding:** An officer may briefly stop a person based on reasonable suspicion of criminal activity. A limited frisk for weapons requires a reasonable basis to believe the person is armed and dangerous.
**裁判要旨：** 警員可以基於對犯罪活動的合理懷疑，短暫攔查某人。若要有限度地拍身搜查武器，必須有合理理由相信該人攜帶武器且具有危險性。

**Lesson:** Reasonable suspicion is lower than probable cause, but it requires specific, articulable facts.
**學習重點：** 合理懷疑低於合理根據，但仍必須有具體且能夠說明的事實。

### 9. Mapp v. Ohio (1961)
### 9. Mapp v. Ohio（1961年）

**Holding:** The exclusionary rule applies to state criminal proceedings through the Fourteenth Amendment.
**裁判要旨：** 排除證據規則透過第十四修正案適用於州刑事程序。

**Lesson:** A constitutional search violation may affect whether evidence can be used at trial, but exceptions exist.
**學習重點：** 違憲搜查可能影響證據能否在審判中使用，但仍存在例外。

### 10. Riley v. California (2014)
### 10. Riley v. California（2014年）

**Holding:** Police generally need a warrant to search the digital contents of a cell phone seized incident to arrest.
**裁判要旨：** 警方通常需要令狀，才能搜查因逮捕而扣押的手機數位內容。

**Lesson:** A modern phone contains much more personal information than an ordinary physical object.
**學習重點：** 現代手機包含的個人資訊遠多於一般實體物品。

### 11. Carpenter v. United States (2018)
### 11. Carpenter v. United States（2018年）

**Holding:** Government acquisition of certain historical cell-site location information is a Fourth Amendment search and generally requires a warrant supported by probable cause.
**裁判要旨：** 政府取得某些歷史手機基地台定位資料，構成第四修正案意義上的搜查，通常需要有合理根據支持的令狀。

**Lesson:** Digital records held by third parties may still involve significant privacy interests.
**學習重點：** 即使數位紀錄由第三方持有，也可能涉及重要的隱私利益。

### 12. Chatrie v. United States (2026)
### 12. Chatrie v. United States（2026年）

**Holding:** The Supreme Court held that obtaining the person's Google Location History data through the geofence process at issue was a Fourth Amendment search.
**裁判要旨：** 最高法院裁定，透過本案所涉及的地理圍欄程序取得當事人的 Google 位置歷史資料，構成第四修正案意義上的搜查。

**Important limit:** The Court did not decide whether the good-faith exception would permit admission of the evidence. That issue remained for the lower court.
**重要限制：** 最高法院沒有決定善意例外是否允許採納該證據；這個問題仍留給下級法院處理。

**Lesson:** Constitutional privacy rules continue to develop as surveillance technology changes.
**學習重點：** 隨著監控科技改變，憲法上的隱私規則仍會持續發展。

## Fifth and Sixth Amendment cases
## 第五及第六修正案判例

### 13. Miranda v. Arizona (1966)
### 13. Miranda v. Arizona（1966年）

**Holding:** Before custodial interrogation, police generally must give warnings protecting the privilege against compelled self-incrimination.
**裁判要旨：** 在羈押狀態下訊問之前，警方通常必須作出保障不得被迫自證其罪特權的權利告知。

**Lesson:** Custody and interrogation are both important; not every police conversation triggers Miranda.
**學習重點：** 羈押狀態及訊問兩者都很重要；並非每次警方交談都會觸發 Miranda 規則。

### 14. Gideon v. Wainwright (1963)
### 14. Gideon v. Wainwright（1963年）

**Holding:** States must provide counsel to indigent defendants in serious criminal cases where the constitutional right applies.
**裁判要旨：** 在憲法權利適用的嚴重刑事案件中，各州必須為無力聘請律師的被告提供律師。

**Lesson:** A fair criminal trial requires meaningful legal representation in covered cases.
**學習重點：** 在受保障的案件中，公平刑事審判需要有實質的法律代表協助。

### 15. Crawford v. Washington (2004)
### 15. Crawford v. Washington（2004年）

**Holding:** Testimonial statements by an unavailable witness generally cannot be admitted against a defendant unless the defendant had a prior opportunity to cross-examine, subject to the doctrine's details.
**裁判要旨：** 無法出庭的證人所作證言性陳述，通常不得用來對付被告，除非被告先前有機會交叉詢問，但仍須考慮相關原則的具體細節。

**Lesson:** The Confrontation Clause is not simply a general rule excluding all hearsay.
**學習重點：** 對質條款並不是一項排除所有傳聞證據的一般規則。

## Fourteenth Amendment cases
## 第十四修正案判例

### 16. Brown v. Board of Education (1954)
### 16. Brown v. Board of Education（1954年）

**Holding:** Racial segregation in public schools violates the Equal Protection Clause.
**裁判要旨：** 公立學校中的種族隔離違反平等保護條款。

**Lesson:** The Constitution can be used to challenge state laws and practices that impose racial inequality.
**學習重點：** 憲法可以用來挑戰造成種族不平等的州法律及政府做法。

### 17. Loving v. Virginia (1967)
### 17. Loving v. Virginia（1967年）

**Holding:** State laws banning interracial marriage violated the Fourteenth Amendment.
**裁判要旨：** 州政府禁止跨種族婚姻的法律違反第十四修正案。

**Lesson:** Equal protection and liberty principles can both matter in constitutional analysis.
**學習重點：** 在憲法分析中，平等保護及自由原則可能同時發揮作用。

### 18. Timbs v. Indiana (2019)
### 18. Timbs v. Indiana（2019年）

**Holding:** The Eighth Amendment's Excessive Fines Clause applies to state and local governments through the Fourteenth Amendment.
**裁判要旨：** 第八修正案的禁止過高罰金條款透過第十四修正案適用於州及地方政府。

**Lesson:** The Fourteenth Amendment is a major route for applying federal constitutional protections to states.
**學習重點：** 第十四修正案是讓聯邦憲法保障適用於各州的重要途徑。

## Practice: write a case brief
## 練習：撰寫判例摘要

Choose one case and complete the following:
選擇一宗判例並完成以下內容：

- Case name and year — 案件名稱及年份
- Court — 法院
- Facts — 事實
- Procedural history — 程序經過
- Constitutional provision — 憲法條文
- Issue — 爭點
- Rule — 法律規則
- Holding — 裁判要旨
- Reasoning — 判決理由
- Limits — 判決的適用限制
- Your own explanation — 用自己的話解釋

Do not copy a case summary and assume that you understand the case. Explain why the rule applies to the facts.
不要只是複製判例摘要就認為自己理解了案件。你必須解釋為什麼該法律規則適用於相關事實。
