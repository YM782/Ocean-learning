# Constitutional Law
# 憲法學

## What this course is about
## 本課程內容

Begin after you understand the three branches of government and federalism.
先理解政府三個部門及聯邦制，再開始本課。

This is an introductory course guide, not a complete textbook or legal opinion.
本頁是入門課程指南，並非完整教科書或法律意見。

## Start with three ideas
## 從三個概念開始

### 1. Constitutional structure
### 1. 憲法架構

Learn how the Constitution creates government institutions and divides power.
學習憲法如何建立政府機關及分配權力。

### 2. Individual rights
### 2. 個人權利

Begin with the First, Fourth, Fifth, Sixth, and Fourteenth Amendments.
先從第一、第四、第五、第六及第十四修正案開始。

### 3. Judicial review
### 3. 司法審查

Learn how courts decide whether government action is consistent with the Constitution.
學習法院如何判斷政府行為是否符合憲法。


## Beginner practice
## 初學者練習

Identify one constitutional right and describe a government action that might raise a question about it.
指出一項憲法權利，並描述一項可能引發相關問題的政府行為。

## How to continue
## 如何繼續學習

Study one topic at a time and write down unfamiliar terms.
一次只學習一個主題，並記錄不熟悉的術語。

For every case, identify the court, jurisdiction, date, issue, rule, holding, and reasoning.
閱讀每一宗判例時，辨識法院、司法管轄區、日期、爭點、規則、裁判要旨及推理。

Always verify the law that applies to the relevant jurisdiction and date.
務必核實相關司法管轄區及日期所適用的法律。

**Educational use only; not legal advice.**
**僅供教育學習，不構成法律意見。**
