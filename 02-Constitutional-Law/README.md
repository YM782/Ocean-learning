# Constitutional Law — Complete Beginner Course
# 憲法學——零基礎完整課程

**Start with ordinary language. Learn the constitutional text, then the legal rule, then the cases.**
**先從日常語言開始，再學憲法原文、法律規則及相關判例。**

This course is designed for learners who have never studied law. You do not need to know legal terminology in advance.
本課程是為從未學過法律的人設計的。開始之前不需要預先掌握法律術語。

## What you will learn
## 你將學會什麼

- How the U.S. Constitution creates and limits government.
  美國憲法如何建立及限制政府。
- Why many constitutional rights restrict government action rather than every private decision.
  為什麼許多憲法權利限制的是政府行為，而不是所有私人決定。
- How the First, Second, Fourth, Fifth, Sixth, Eighth, and Fourteenth Amendments work.
  第一、第二、第四、第五、第六、第八及第十四修正案如何運作。
- How the Supreme Court interprets constitutional text and develops legal tests.
  最高法院如何解釋憲法文本及發展法律測試。
- How to read a case and write a short IRAC analysis.
  如何閱讀判例及撰寫簡短的 IRAC 分析。

## How to study each lesson
## 每一課的學習方法

1. Read the English paragraph once without stopping at every unfamiliar word.
   先讀一遍英文段落，不要每遇到陌生詞就停下來。
2. Read the Traditional Chinese explanation immediately below it.
   立即閱讀下方的繁體中文解釋。
3. Learn the key legal terms listed at the end.
   學習每課末尾列出的重要法律術語。
4. Explain the rule in your own words.
   用自己的話解釋法律規則。
5. Apply the rule to the example and identify what facts are missing.
   將規則應用於案例，並指出還缺少哪些事實。

## Important distinctions for beginners
## 初學者必須理解的重要區別

A constitutional right is not always absolute.
憲法權利並不一定是絕對的。

A constitutional violation does not automatically mean that a criminal case is dismissed or that a person receives money damages.
憲法受到侵犯並不會自動導致刑事案件撤銷，也不代表當事人必然獲得金錢賠償。

Federal constitutional rules and state laws may interact. The correct answer can depend on jurisdiction, facts, procedural posture, and the date of the rule.
聯邦憲法規則可能與州法互相影響。正確答案可能取決於司法管轄區、案件事實、程序階段及法律規則的日期。

A case summary is a learning aid. The court's actual opinion is the primary source for what the court decided.
判例摘要只是學習輔助。法院實際判決意見才是核實法院裁決內容的第一手資料。

## Scope and next topics
## 課程範圍及後續主題

This first deep-dive focuses on constitutional structure and selected amendments that are especially useful for understanding individual rights and criminal justice. It is not a complete treatment of every amendment or every area of constitutional litigation.
本階段首先深入憲法架構，以及特別有助於理解個人權利與刑事司法的部分修正案。這並不是對所有修正案或所有憲法訴訟領域的完整研究。

Future lessons can add the Third, Seventh, Ninth, Tenth, Eleventh, and other amendments, as well as Commerce Clause litigation, presidential powers, federal courts, and state constitutional protections.
後續課程可以增加第三、第七、第九、第十、第十一及其他修正案，以及商業條款訴訟、總統權力、聯邦法院及州憲法保障等內容。

**Content review date: November 23, 2026.** Legal rules may change after this date; verify current primary authority before relying on any rule.
**內容核對日期：2026年11月23日。** 此日期之後法律規則可能改變；依賴任何規則前，請核實現行第一手法律資料。
