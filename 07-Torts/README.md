# Torts
# 侵權法

**Level: beginner | Focus: civil liability for legally recognized harms**
**程度：初學者｜重點：對法律承認的損害承擔民事責任**

Tort law addresses civil wrongs that cause legally recognized harm. A tort claim may seek compensation or, in some situations, an injunction.
侵權法處理造成法律承認損害的民事不法行為。侵權請求可能尋求金錢賠償，某些情況下也可能尋求禁制令。

Tort rules are largely state-based. The examples in this course are general teaching models; always check the law of the relevant state.
侵權法主要由各州規範。本課程中的例子是一般教學模型；務必核實相關州的法律。
