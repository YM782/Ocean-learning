# Torts
# 侵權法

## What this course is about
## 本課程內容

Begin after you understand civil cases, duties, and causation.
先理解民事案件、義務及因果關係，再開始本課。

This is an introductory course guide, not a complete textbook or legal opinion.
本頁是入門課程指南，並非完整教科書或法律意見。

## Start with three ideas
## 從三個概念開始

### 1. Negligence
### 1. 過失

Learn duty, breach, causation, and damages.
學習注意義務、義務違反、因果關係及損害。

### 2. Intentional torts
### 2. 故意侵權

Study basic examples such as battery, assault, and trespass.
學習毆打、威脅性侵害及侵入等基本例子。

### 3. Defenses and liability
### 3. 抗辯與責任

Understand that defenses and liability rules can vary by jurisdiction.
理解抗辯及責任規則可能因司法管轄區而異。


## Beginner practice
## 初學者練習

Analyze a simple bicycle accident by identifying possible duty, breach, causation, and harm.
透過簡單的自行車事故，辨識可能的注意義務、義務違反、因果關係及損害。

## How to continue
## 如何繼續學習

Study one topic at a time and write down unfamiliar terms.
一次只學習一個主題，並記錄不熟悉的術語。

For every case, identify the court, jurisdiction, date, issue, rule, holding, and reasoning.
閱讀每一宗判例時，辨識法院、司法管轄區、日期、爭點、規則、裁判要旨及推理。

Always verify the law that applies to the relevant jurisdiction and date.
務必核實相關司法管轄區及日期所適用的法律。

**僅供教育學習，不構成法律意見。**
