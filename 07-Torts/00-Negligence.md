# Negligence
# 過失侵權

## 1. What is negligence?
## 1. 什麼是過失？

Negligence is a failure to use the level of care required by law, causing legally recognized harm. A plaintiff generally must prove duty, breach, causation, and damages.
過失是未能採取法律要求的注意程度，並因此造成法律承認的損害。原告通常必須證明注意義務、義務違反、因果關係及損害。

The exact formulation and defenses differ among states. Treat the four-part framework as a starting point, not a universal code.
具體表述及抗辯因州而異。應將四要件架構視為起點，而不是全美統一法典。

## 2. Duty
## 2. 注意義務

Duty asks whether the defendant owed the plaintiff a legally recognized obligation to act with reasonable care in the circumstances.
注意義務是問：在具體情況下，被告是否對原告負有法律承認的合理注意義務。

Courts may consider foreseeability, relationships, public policy, statutes, and the jurisdiction's approach to duty. Some relationships or activities create special duties.
法院可能考慮可預見性、雙方關係、公共政策、制定法，以及該司法管轄區對注意義務的處理方式。某些關係或活動會產生特殊義務。

## 3. Breach
## 3. 違反注意義務

Breach asks whether the defendant acted as a reasonably careful person would have acted under similar circumstances.
違反注意義務是問：被告的行為是否符合處於類似情況下合理謹慎之人應有的行為。

A court may consider the likelihood and seriousness of harm, the burden of precautions, and relevant customs or statutes. Custom can be evidence, but it is not always conclusive.
法院可能考慮損害發生的可能性及嚴重程度、採取預防措施的負擔，以及相關慣例或制定法。行業慣例可以作為證據，但不一定具有決定性。

## 4. Causation
## 4. 因果關係

Factual cause asks whether the harm would have occurred without the defendant's breach. Courts may use a but-for test or another test when multiple causes are involved.
事實因果關係會問：如果沒有被告的違反義務行為，損害是否仍會發生。法院可能採用「若非如此」測試；涉及多重原因時，也可能使用其他測試。

Proximate cause limits liability to harms sufficiently connected to the conduct under the jurisdiction's foreseeability and policy rules.
法律上的近因則依司法管轄區的可預見性及政策規則，將責任限制在與行為具有足夠關聯的損害範圍內。

## 5. Damages
## 5. 損害

A negligence plaintiff generally must show actual legally recognized harm. Damages may include medical expenses, lost income, property loss, and pain and suffering, depending on the claim and law.
過失侵權原告通常必須證明實際發生法律承認的損害。依請求類型及法律，賠償可能包括醫療費、收入損失、財產損失及痛苦。

## 6. Common doctrines
## 6. 常見法律原則

**Negligence per se:** A statute may supply the standard of conduct when the plaintiff and harm fall within the statute's protected class and purpose, subject to state law.
**法定過失：** 如果原告及損害屬於制定法所保護的對象及目的範圍，制定法可能提供行為標準，但仍須符合州法。

**Res ipsa loquitur:** In appropriate circumstances, the nature of an accident may support an inference of negligence when the event ordinarily would not occur without negligence and other requirements are met.
**事實自證過失：** 在適當情況下，如果某種事故通常不會在沒有過失時發生，而且符合其他要求，事故本身的性質可能支持過失推論。

**Comparative fault:** A plaintiff's own negligence may reduce recovery under the state's comparative-fault system. States differ on whether and how a plaintiff's fault bars recovery.
**比較過失：** 依州的比較過失制度，原告自身的過失可能減少賠償。各州對原告過失是否及如何阻止取得賠償，規則不同。

## 理解測驗

1. What are the four common negligence elements?
   過失侵權常見的四個要件是什麼？
2. Is factual cause the same as proximate cause?
   事實因果關係是否等同法律上的近因？
3. Does a plaintiff's own negligence always eliminate recovery?
   原告自身有過失是否一定完全失去賠償？

**Answers:** 1. Duty, breach, causation, and damages. 2. No. 3. No; comparative-fault rules differ.
**答案：** 1. 注意義務、違反、因果關係及損害。2. 不是。3. 不一定；比較過失規則因州而異。
