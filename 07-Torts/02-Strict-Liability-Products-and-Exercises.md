# Strict Liability, Products, and Exercises
# 嚴格責任、產品責任及練習題

## 1. Strict liability
## 1. 嚴格責任

Strict liability may impose responsibility without proof of ordinary negligence when the legal requirements for the category are met. It does not mean automatic liability for every injury.
如果符合特定類別的法律要求，嚴格責任可能在不必證明一般過失的情況下使當事人承擔責任。它不代表每一項傷害都會自動產生責任。

Some jurisdictions recognize strict liability for abnormally dangerous activities and certain animal-related harms.
某些司法管轄區對異常危險活動及某些動物造成的損害承認嚴格責任。

## 2. Products liability
## 2. 產品責任

Products-liability claims may be based on manufacturing defects, design defects, or inadequate warnings or instructions. The elements and tests differ among states.
產品責任請求可能基於製造缺陷、設計缺陷，或警告及說明不足。各州的構成要件及測試可能不同。

A manufacturing defect generally means that a particular product departed from its intended design.
製造缺陷通常是指某一特定產品偏離其預定設計。

A design-defect claim challenges the product's design itself. Jurisdictions use different tests, including risk-utility or consumer-expectation approaches.
設計缺陷請求質疑產品本身的設計。各司法管轄區採用不同測試，包括風險效益測試或消費者期待測試。

A warning claim asks whether a foreseeable risk required an adequate warning or instruction under the governing law.
警告不足請求會問：依適用法律，對可預見的風險是否必須提供充分警告或說明。

## 3. Vicarious liability
## 3. 替代責任

Under respondeat superior, an employer may be vicariously liable for an employee's tort committed within the scope of employment, subject to the governing jurisdiction's rules.
依僱主替代責任原則，如果僱員在職務範圍內實施侵權行為，僱主可能承擔替代責任，但仍須遵守適用司法管轄區的規則。

The employee may also remain personally liable. Employer liability and employee liability are separate questions.
僱員本人也可能繼續承擔個人責任。僱主責任與僱員責任是不同問題。

## 4. Damages
## 4. 損害賠償

Compensatory damages aim to compensate legally recognized losses. Punitive damages may be available in limited cases to punish and deter especially wrongful conduct, subject to constitutional and state-law limits.
補償性賠償旨在補償法律承認的損失。在有限情況下，懲罰性賠償可能用來懲罰及阻止特別不當的行為，但受到憲法及州法限制。

## Exercise 1 — Negligence or strict liability?
## 練習一——過失還是嚴格責任？

A plaintiff is injured by an activity that the state classifies as abnormally dangerous. The plaintiff cannot prove that the defendant failed to use reasonable care. What theory may still be relevant?
原告因某項州法分類為異常危險的活動而受傷，但無法證明被告未採取合理注意。仍可能涉及什麼責任理論？

**Answer:** Strict liability may be relevant if the activity and harm satisfy the jurisdiction's requirements.
**答案：** 如果活動及損害符合該司法管轄區的要求，可能涉及嚴格責任。

## Exercise 2 — Product defect
## 練習二——產品缺陷

A single product leaves the factory with a missing safety component, even though the approved design includes it. What type of defect is most directly implicated?
某件產品出廠時缺少安全零件，儘管核准設計本來包含該零件。最直接涉及哪一種缺陷？

**Answer:** A manufacturing defect.
**答案：** 製造缺陷。

## IRAC practice
## IRAC 練習

**Issue:** Is a manufacturer potentially liable for a product-related injury?
**爭點：** 製造商是否可能對產品造成的傷害承擔責任？

**Rule:** Identify the state's products-liability test and the relevant defect category.
**規則：** 找出該州的產品責任測試及相關缺陷類型。

**Application:** Examine the product, its design, manufacturing process, warnings, foreseeable use, causation, and injury.
**適用：** 檢查產品、設計、製造過程、警告、可預見用途、因果關係及傷害。

**Conclusion:** State a conditional conclusion and identify facts still needed.
**結論：** 提出有條件的結論，並指出仍需要哪些事實。
