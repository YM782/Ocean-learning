# Intentional Torts and Defenses
# 故意侵權及抗辯

## 1. What makes a tort intentional?
## 1. 什麼使侵權行為構成故意？

An intentional tort generally requires that the defendant intended the relevant contact, act, or consequence described by the tort. Intent does not always mean that the defendant wanted to cause every resulting injury.
故意侵權通常要求被告意圖作出該侵權類型所描述的相關接觸、行為或結果。故意並不一定代表被告希望造成後來發生的每一項傷害。

The required intent varies by tort and jurisdiction. Read the elements of the specific claim.
必要故意因侵權類型及司法管轄區而異。應閱讀具體請求的構成要件。

## 2. Battery and assault
## 2. 殴打侵權及威脅侵權

Battery generally involves intentional harmful or offensive contact with another person without valid consent or privilege.
殴打侵權通常涉及未經有效同意或特權，而故意與他人發生有害或冒犯性的身體接觸。

Assault generally involves intentionally causing another person to reasonably anticipate imminent harmful or offensive contact. Actual physical contact is not always required.
威脅侵權通常涉及故意使他人合理預期即將發生有害或冒犯性的身體接觸。並非每次都需要實際身體接觸。

## 3. False imprisonment
## 3. 非法拘禁

False imprisonment generally involves intentional confinement of a person within boundaries, without lawful privilege, when the person is aware of the confinement or is harmed by it under the applicable rule.
非法拘禁通常涉及在沒有合法特權的情況下，故意將某人限制在一定範圍內；依適用規則，當事人須知道自己受到限制，或因此受到損害。

## 4. Trespass and conversion
## 4. 侵入土地及侵占動產

Trespass to land generally involves an intentional physical invasion of another person's possessory interest in land.
土地侵入通常涉及故意侵入他人對土地所享有的占有利益。

Conversion generally involves serious interference with another person's right to control personal property. Lesser interference may support a different claim, such as trespass to chattels.
動產侵占通常涉及對他人控制個人財產的權利作出嚴重干預。較輕微的干預可能支持其他請求，例如動產侵擾。

## 5. Defamation
## 5. 誹謗

Defamation generally involves a false statement of fact communicated to a third person that harms reputation, subject to defenses and constitutional limits.
誹謗通常涉及向第三人傳達虛假的事實陳述，並損害他人名譽，但仍受到抗辯及憲法限制。

Public-official and public-figure plaintiffs face the constitutional actual-malice requirement for covered claims. Actual malice means knowledge of falsity or reckless disregard for truth, not merely ill will.
在適用的請求中，公職人員及公眾人物原告必須符合憲法上的實際惡意要求。實際惡意是指明知陳述虛假，或罔顧真偽，而不只是心懷敵意。

## 6. Defenses
## 6. 抗辯

Consent may defeat a claim when it is valid and covers the conduct. Self-defense and defense of others may justify reasonable force under the applicable rules.
如果同意有效且涵蓋相關行為，可能排除侵權請求。依適用規則，正當防衛及保護他人可能使合理武力具有正當性。

Necessity, privilege, and statutory defenses may apply in particular settings. The burden and elements depend on the jurisdiction.
在特定情況下，緊急避難、特權及制定法抗辯可能適用。舉證責任及構成要件取決於司法管轄區。

   殴打侵權是否一定要求嚴重身體傷害？

   威脅侵權是否一定要求身體接觸？

   在憲法誹謗法中，實際惡意代表什麼？
