# Ocean-learning Content Standard
# Ocean-learning 課程內容標準

Every lesson is written for a learner with no prior legal education.
每一課都以沒有法律教育背景的學習者為對象。

Every substantive English sentence must be followed immediately by its Traditional Chinese translation.
每一句實質英文內容後面都必須立即接續繁體中文翻譯。

Introduce a plain-language explanation before using advanced legal terminology.
使用進階法律術語之前，先提供白話解釋。

Explain the jurisdiction and date whenever a legal rule may vary.
法律規則可能因司法管轄區而異時，必須說明適用地區及日期。

Separate primary legal authority from secondary educational commentary.
必須區分第一手法律權威與第二手教育性評論。

For each case, distinguish facts, procedural history, issue, rule, holding, reasoning, and disposition.
每宗判例都必須區分事實、程序經過、爭點、規則、裁判要旨、推理及裁判結果。

Exercises must include answer explanations, not only the correct letter or conclusion.
練習題必須附上答案解析，而不只是正確選項或結論。

Do not promise that self-study materials qualify a person for JD admission or bar admission.
不得承諾自學資料能使任何人取得 JD 入學資格或律師准入資格。

Educational use only; not legal advice.
僅供教育學習，不構成法律意見。
