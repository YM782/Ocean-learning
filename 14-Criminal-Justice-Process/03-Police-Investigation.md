# Police Investigation
# 警方調查

## 1. What is an investigation?
## 1. 什麼是調查？

An investigation is the process of gathering information about a possible crime. It may begin with a report from a victim, a witness, a business, another agency, or information discovered by officers.
調查是收集可能犯罪相關資料的過程。調查可能始於被害人、證人、企業、其他機關的報告，或執法人員自行發現的線索。

At this stage, the government may not yet know whether a crime occurred or who committed it.
在這個階段，政府可能還不知道是否真的發生犯罪，也可能尚未知道誰是行為人。

## 2. Common investigative methods
## 2. 常見調查方法

Investigators may interview witnesses, review public records, inspect a scene, collect voluntarily provided materials, use informants, analyze financial records, or seek judicial authorization for certain searches or surveillance.
調查人員可能訪問證人、查閱公開紀錄、檢查現場、收集自願提供的資料、使用線人、分析金融紀錄，或為某些搜查及監控申請司法授權。

Different methods have different legal requirements. A voluntary interview is not the same as a compelled interrogation, and a public observation is not automatically the same as a search.
不同調查方法有不同法律要求。自願接受訪問不等於被迫訊問；在公共場所觀察也不會自動等同於搜查。

## 3. The role of evidence
## 3. 證據的作用

Investigators should preserve evidence in a reliable way. This may include documenting who collected an item, when and where it was collected, how it was stored, and who later handled it.
調查人員應以可靠方式保存證據，包括記錄由誰收集、何時何地收集、如何保存，以及之後由誰接觸或處理。

This record helps establish chain of custody. A break in the chain does not automatically make evidence inadmissible, but it may affect authenticity, reliability, or weight.
這份紀錄有助於建立證據保管鏈。保管鏈出現中斷，不會自動導致證據不得採納，但可能影響證據的真實性、可靠性或證明力。

## 4. Digital investigations
## 4. 數位調查

Digital investigations may involve phones, computers, cloud accounts, location information, messages, logs, or network records. The Fourth Amendment may protect certain privacy interests in digital information.
數位調查可能涉及手機、電腦、雲端帳戶、位置資料、訊息、日誌或網路紀錄。第四修正案可能保障數位資料中的某些隱私利益。

Do not assume that all information held by a service provider is automatically free from constitutional protection. The legal analysis depends on the type of data, how it was obtained, the person's privacy interest, and current precedent.
不要假設服務提供者持有的所有資料都自動不受憲法保障。法律分析取決於資料種類、取得方式、當事人的隱私利益及現行判例。

## 5. Voluntary cooperation and the right to remain silent
## 5. 自願配合與保持沉默的權利

A person may choose to speak with investigators, decline a voluntary interview, or ask whether they are free to leave. The legal consequences of a statement depend on the circumstances and applicable rules.
個人可以選擇與調查人員交談、拒絕自願訪問，或詢問自己是否可以離開。陳述的法律後果取決於具體情況及適用規則。

Miranda warnings are generally required before custodial interrogation, not before every conversation with an officer. Custody and interrogation are separate requirements.
Miranda 權利告知通常必須在羈押狀態下訊問之前作出，而不是每次與警員交談前都必須作出。羈押狀態及訊問是兩個不同的適用要件。

## 6. Investigation does not equal guilt
## 6. 調查不等於有罪

A person can be investigated and never charged. A person can be charged and later acquitted. The government must prove the charged offense at trial under the required burden of proof.
一個人可能受到調查，但最終從未被起訴。一個人可能受到起訴，之後也可能被判無罪。政府必須在審判中依規定的舉證責任證明被控罪名。

## Case connection: Kyles v. Whitley (1995)
## 判例連結：Kyles v. Whitley（1995年）

The Supreme Court explained that the prosecution's disclosure duty includes favorable evidence known to others acting on the government's behalf in the case, including police.
最高法院指出，控方的披露義務包括案件中代表政府行事的其他人員所掌握的有利證據，其中包括警方。

**Why it matters:** Investigative agencies and prosecutors cannot treat favorable evidence as irrelevant merely because it is held by a different member of the prosecution team.
**重要性：** 調查機關及檢察官不能只因有利證據由控方團隊中的另一個成員持有，就將其視為無關資料。

## Questions to ask
## 分析時應提出的問題

1. What started the investigation?
   調查是由什麼事情開始的？
2. What evidence was collected?
   收集了哪些證據？
3. Was the evidence obtained lawfully?
   證據是否依法取得？
4. Was the evidence preserved and documented?
   證據是否妥善保存及記錄？
5. Does the evidence support guilt, innocence, or both?
   證據支持有罪、無罪，還是兩者都有可能？
