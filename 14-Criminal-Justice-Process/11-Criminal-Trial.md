# The Criminal Trial
# 刑事審判

## 1. The burden of proof
## 1. 舉證責任

In a criminal trial, the government bears the burden of proving the defendant's guilt beyond a reasonable doubt. The defendant is presumed innocent and does not have to prove innocence.
在刑事審判中，政府承擔排除合理懷疑地證明被告有罪的責任。被告享有無罪推定，也不必證明自己無罪。

The burden applies to the elements of the charged offense. The prosecution cannot replace proof with suspicion, public pressure, or the fact that an arrest occurred.
舉證責任適用於被控罪名的構成要件。控方不能用懷疑、輿論壓力，或曾經發生逮捕的事實來取代證明。

## 2. Jury trial or bench trial
## 2. 陪審團審判或法官獨任審判

In a jury trial, jurors decide the facts and return a verdict under the judge's legal instructions. The judge decides legal questions and manages the trial.
在陪審團審判中，陪審員依照法官的法律指示認定事實並作出裁決。法官負責裁決法律問題及管理審判程序。

A bench trial is decided by a judge without a jury. In federal court, waiver of a jury trial must satisfy Rule 23 and requires the relevant approvals.
法官獨任審判是由法官而非陪審團作出裁決。在聯邦法院，放棄陪審團審判必須符合第23條規則，並取得相關批准。

## 3. Jury selection — voir dire
## 3. 陪審團遴選——voir dire

The court and lawyers question prospective jurors to identify bias, conflicts, or other grounds for disqualification. The goal is to select an impartial jury under applicable law.
法院及律師會詢問候選陪審員，以辨識偏見、利益衝突或其他取消資格的理由。目標是依適用法律選出公正的陪審團。

In *Batson v. Kentucky* (1986), the Supreme Court limited the use of peremptory challenges to exclude jurors on the basis of race.
在1986年的 *Batson v. Kentucky* 案中，最高法院限制以種族為理由使用無因迴避權排除陪審員。

## 4. A simplified trial sequence
## 4. 簡化審判流程

1. Jury selection.
   陪審團遴選。
2. Opening statements.
   開庭陳述。
3. Prosecution's case-in-chief.
   控方主要舉證。
4. Defense cross-examination of prosecution witnesses.
   辯方交叉詢問控方證人。
5. Defense case, if the defense chooses to present one.
   如辯方選擇提出證據，則進行辯方舉證。
6. Rebuttal evidence, if permitted.
   如獲准，控方提出反駁證據。
7. Closing arguments.
   結案陳詞。
8. Jury instructions.
   法官向陪審團作出法律指示。
9. Deliberations and verdict.
   陪審團評議及裁決。

The exact order may vary under the rules and the judge's management of the case.
具體順序可能依規則及法官對案件的管理而有所不同。

## 5. Confrontation and cross-examination
## 5. 對質及交叉詢問

The Sixth Amendment Confrontation Clause protects the defendant's right to confront witnesses against them. Cross-examination allows the defense to test a witness's perception, memory, bias, and accuracy.
第六修正案的對質條款保障被告與不利於自己的證人對質的權利。交叉詢問讓辯方檢驗證人的觀察能力、記憶、偏見及準確性。

In *Crawford v. Washington* (2004), the Supreme Court developed an important rule for testimonial statements by witnesses who do not appear for cross-examination, subject to the doctrine's details.
在2004年的 *Crawford v. Washington* 案中，最高法院針對無法出庭接受交叉詢問的證人所作證言性陳述，建立重要規則，但仍須考慮該原則的具體細節。

## 6. Unanimous verdicts
## 6. 一致裁決

In serious federal criminal cases, a jury verdict must be unanimous. In *Ramos v. Louisiana* (2020), the Supreme Court held that the Sixth Amendment requires unanimous jury verdicts to convict in serious criminal cases in state and federal courts.
在嚴重的聯邦刑事案件中，陪審團裁決必須一致同意。在2020年的 *Ramos v. Louisiana* 案中，最高法院裁定，第六修正案要求州及聯邦法院在嚴重刑事案件中必須由陪審團一致同意才能定罪。

## 7. Verdicts
## 7. 裁決

A jury may return a guilty verdict or a not-guilty verdict. A hung jury occurs when the jury cannot reach the required agreement. The court may declare a mistrial, and the possibility of retrial depends on the circumstances and double-jeopardy rules.
陪審團可能作出有罪或無罪裁決。如果陪審團無法達成必要的一致意見，就可能構成僵局陪審團。法院可能宣告審判無效，而能否重新審判取決於具體情況及禁止雙重危險規則。

A not-guilty verdict generally prevents the government from retrying the defendant for the same offense in the same sovereign's court, subject to the governing doctrine.
無罪裁決通常會阻止同一主權政府在其法院就同一罪行再次審判被告，但仍須考慮適用法律原則。

## 8. What if the defendant is found guilty?
## 8. 如果被告被裁定有罪？

A guilty verdict does not itself explain the final punishment. The court moves to sentencing, where statutory penalties, the sentencing framework, the record, and the parties' submissions are considered.
有罪裁決本身並不會說明最終刑罰。法院接著進入量刑階段，考慮法定刑罰、量刑架構、案件紀錄及雙方提出的資料。
