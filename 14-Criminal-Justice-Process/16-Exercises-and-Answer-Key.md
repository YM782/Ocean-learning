# Exercises and Answer Key
# 練習題及答案解析

Try each question before reading the answer. The explanations are more important than memorizing a letter.
閱讀答案之前，先嘗試自行作答。理解解析比記住選項字母更重要。

## Part A — Multiple choice
## A部分——選擇題

### 1. Does an investigation automatically mean a person has been charged?
### 1. 受到調查是否代表已經被起訴？

A. Yes. / 是。
B. No. / 否。

**Answer: B.** Investigation, arrest, charging, and conviction are separate stages.
**答案：B。** 調查、逮捕、起訴及定罪是不同階段。

### 2. What standard generally supports a brief Terry stop?
### 2. 一般而言，短暫 Terry 攔查需要什麼標準？

A. Beyond a reasonable doubt. / 排除合理懷疑。
B. Reasonable suspicion. / 合理懷疑。
C. A conviction. / 已經定罪。
D. A grand-jury verdict. / 大陪審團裁決。

**Answer: B.** A brief investigative stop generally requires specific facts supporting reasonable suspicion.
**答案：B。** 短暫調查性攔查通常需要具體事實支持合理懷疑。

### 3. What does probable cause establish?
### 3. 合理根據可以確立什麼？

A. Guilt beyond a reasonable doubt. / 排除合理懷疑地證明有罪。
B. A legal basis for certain government actions, such as arrest. / 為逮捕等政府行動提供法律基礎。
C. A final conviction. / 最終定罪。
D. A sentence. / 刑罰。

**Answer: B.** Probable cause is not the trial burden of proof.
**答案：B。** 合理根據不是審判中的舉證標準。

### 4. When are Miranda warnings generally required?
### 4. 一般何時必須作出 Miranda 權利告知？

A. Before every conversation with police. / 每次與警方交談之前。
B. Before custodial interrogation. / 羈押狀態下訊問之前。
C. Only after trial. / 只在審判後。
D. Only after sentencing. / 只在量刑後。

**Answer: B.** Custody and interrogation are both required under the familiar Miranda framework.
**答案：B。** 在一般 Miranda 架構下，羈押狀態及訊問兩項條件都必須符合。

### 5. What does a federal grand jury decide?
### 5. 聯邦大陪審團決定什麼？

A. Whether the defendant is guilty beyond a reasonable doubt. / 被告是否已被排除合理懷疑地證明有罪。
B. Whether probable cause supports an indictment. / 是否有合理根據支持起訴書。
C. The final sentence. / 最終刑罰。
D. Whether an appeal succeeds. / 上訴是否成功。

**Answer: B.** The trial jury or judge decides guilt at trial; the grand jury screens the charge.
**答案：B。** 審判陪審團或法官在審判中決定有罪與否；大陪審團負責初步審查起訴。

### 6. What is the main function of arraignment?
### 6. 正式提訊的主要功能是什麼？

A. To enter a plea to the formal charge. / 對正式指控作出答辯。
B. To decide the appeal. / 決定上訴。
C. To select the sentencing guideline. / 選擇量刑指南。
D. To conduct the police investigation. / 進行警方調查。

**Answer: A.** Arraignment informs the defendant of the charge and addresses the plea.
**答案：A。** 正式提訊會告知被告指控內容，並處理答辯。

### 7. What is Brady evidence?
### 7. 什麼是 Brady 證據？

A. Any evidence the defendant wants. / 被告想要的任何證據。
B. Favorable evidence material to guilt or punishment that the prosecution must disclose under the constitutional rule. / 依憲法規則控方必須披露、且對有罪與否或刑罰具有重要性的有利證據。
C. Only evidence proving guilt. / 只有證明有罪的證據。
D. Only evidence found after trial. / 只有審判後才發現的證據。

**Answer: B.** Brady concerns material favorable evidence; Giglio addresses material impeachment evidence.
**答案：B。** Brady 涉及重要的有利證據；Giglio 涉及重要的彈劾證據。

### 8. Does the judge generally participate in federal plea negotiations?
### 8. 法官通常會參與聯邦認罪協商嗎？

A. Yes, as a negotiator. / 會，作為談判者。
B. No, Rule 11 generally prohibits court participation in plea discussions. / 不會，第11條規則通常禁止法院參與認罪協商。
C. Only the jury participates. / 只有陪審團參與。
D. The police decide the agreement. / 警方決定協議。

**Answer: B.** The judge later evaluates the plea under Rule 11.
**答案：B。** 法官之後會依第11條規則評估認罪。

### 9. Who bears the burden of proving guilt at trial?
### 9. 誰承擔在審判中證明有罪的責任？

A. The defendant. / 被告。
B. The government. / 政府。
C. The victim alone. / 只有被害人。
D. The jury. / 陪審團。

**Answer: B.** The government must prove guilt beyond a reasonable doubt.
**答案：B。** 政府必須排除合理懷疑地證明有罪。

### 10. Is a direct appeal normally a completely new trial?
### 10. 直接上訴通常是一次完整的新審判嗎？

A. Yes. / 是。
B. No. / 否。

**Answer: B.** An appeal usually reviews the existing record for legal error.
**答案：B。** 上訴通常是根據既有紀錄審查法律錯誤。

## Part B — True or false
## B部分——是非題

### 11. An indictment proves guilt.
### 11. 起訴書可以證明有罪。

**Answer: False.** It reflects a probable-cause charging decision, not a trial verdict.
**答案：錯。** 起訴書反映的是以合理根據提出指控的決定，不是審判裁決。

### 12. Every warrantless search is unconstitutional.
### 12. 所有沒有令狀的搜查都違憲。

**Answer: False.** Recognized exceptions exist, but each has specific limits.
**答案：錯。** 法律承認某些例外，但每項例外都有具體限制。

### 13. The defendant must prove innocence.
### 13. 被告必須證明自己無罪。

**Answer: False.** The government bears the burden of proof in a criminal trial.
**答案：錯。** 刑事審判中的舉證責任由政府承擔。

### 14. A Miranda violation automatically dismisses the entire prosecution.
### 14. 違反 Miranda 會自動撤銷整宗刑事追訴。

**Answer: False.** The usual issue concerns use of a statement, subject to exceptions and other evidence.
**答案：錯。** 一般爭點涉及陳述能否使用，仍須考慮例外及其他證據。

### 15. Federal criminal procedure and state criminal procedure are always identical.
### 15. 聯邦刑事程序與州刑事程序永遠相同。

**Answer: False.** State rules and procedures may differ.
**答案：錯。** 各州規則及程序可能不同。

## Part C — Short-answer reasoning
## C部分——簡答及法律推理

### 16. A person is arrested, but the prosecutor later declines to file charges. What does this teach?
### 16. 某人遭到逮捕，但檢察官之後決定不提出指控。這說明什麼？

**Sample answer:** Arrest and charging are separate decisions. An arrest does not prove guilt, and a prosecutor must independently evaluate whether charges are supported and appropriate.
**參考答案：** 逮捕及起訴是不同決定。逮捕不能證明有罪，檢察官必須獨立評估證據是否支持起訴，以及起訴是否適當。

### 17. A grand jury returns an indictment. Can the defendant still plead not guilty and demand a trial?
### 17. 大陪審團提出起訴書後，被告仍可以不認罪並要求審判嗎？

**Sample answer:** Yes, subject to the applicable procedural rules. An indictment is a formal accusation, not a final determination of guilt.
**參考答案：** 可以，但須符合適用程序規則。起訴書是正式指控，不是最終有罪認定。

### 18. The police have a witness statement that tends to show the defendant did not commit the crime. The prosecutor does not personally know about it. What issue may arise?
### 18. 警方持有一份傾向於證明被告沒有犯罪的證人陳述，但檢察官本人不知道這份資料。可能引發什麼問題？

**Sample answer:** Under *Kyles*, the prosecution's disclosure responsibility may include favorable evidence known to members of the prosecution team, including police. Materiality and the complete facts must be analyzed.
**參考答案：** 依 *Kyles* 案，控方的披露責任可能包括控方團隊成員所掌握的有利證據，其中包括警方。仍須分析證據重要性及完整事實。

### 19. A defendant is convicted and files an appeal. Does the appellate court automatically hear all witnesses again?
### 19. 被告被定罪後提出上訴。上訴法院會自動重新聽取所有證人作證嗎？

**Sample answer:** No. A direct appeal usually reviews the trial record and legal rulings. A new trial may be ordered only if the legal standard for that remedy is satisfied.
**參考答案：** 不會。直接上訴通常審查審判紀錄及法律裁決。只有符合該項救濟的法律標準時，才可能命令重新審判。

## Part D — IRAC exercise
## D部分——IRAC 練習

**Facts:** Police stop a person based on a vague feeling that the person looks suspicious. The officer cannot identify specific conduct suggesting crime. The officer then searches the person's backpack without consent or a warrant.
**事實：** 警方只是因為覺得某人看起來可疑，就將其攔查。警員無法指出任何暗示犯罪的具體行為。之後，警員在沒有取得同意或令狀的情況下搜查該人的背包。

**Issue:** Does the stop and search raise a Fourth Amendment issue?
**爭點：** 這次攔查及搜查是否引發第四修正案問題？

**Rule:** A Terry stop generally requires specific and articulable facts supporting reasonable suspicion. A search requires a warrant or a recognized exception, depending on the circumstances.
**規則：** Terry 攔查通常需要具體且能夠說明的事實支持合理懷疑。搜查則須依情況取得令狀或符合法律承認的例外。

**Application:** The officer's vague feeling may be insufficient for reasonable suspicion. The backpack search also requires a separate legal justification. The court would examine whether any exception applies and whether the defendant's own rights were violated.
**適用：** 警員模糊的感覺可能不足以構成合理懷疑。背包搜查也需要獨立的法律依據。法院會檢查是否適用任何例外，以及被告本人的權利是否受到侵犯。

**Conclusion:** The facts raise substantial Fourth Amendment questions, but the final result depends on the full circumstances and applicable doctrine.
**結論：** 這些事實引發重要的第四修正案問題，但最終結果取決於完整情況及適用法律原則。
