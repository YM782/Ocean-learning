# Arraignment and Pretrial Motions
# 正式提訊及審前動議

## 1. What is arraignment?
## 1. 什麼是正式提訊？

Arraignment is a court proceeding in which the defendant is informed of the formal charge and asked to enter a plea. In federal court, Rule 10 governs arraignment.
正式提訊是法院告知被告正式指控內容，並要求被告作出答辯的程序。在聯邦法院，第10條規則規範正式提訊。

A common plea is not guilty. A defendant may also plead guilty or, with the court's consent, nolo contendere.
常見答辯是不認罪。被告也可以認罪，或在法院同意下作出不抗辯答辯（nolo contendere）。

## 2. Initial appearance is not arraignment
## 2. 首次出庭不等於正式提訊

The initial appearance focuses on identity, notice of charges, rights, counsel, and release or detention. Arraignment focuses on the formal charge and the plea.
首次出庭主要處理身分確認、指控告知、權利、律師協助及釋放或羈押。正式提訊則主要處理正式指控及被告答辯。

The two hearings may occur close together or be combined in some circumstances, but their legal functions are different.
兩項聽證可能在接近的時間舉行，某些情況下也可能合併處理，但兩者的法律職能不同。

## 3. Pretrial motions
## 3. 審前動議

A motion is a formal request asking the court to make a ruling or take an action. Pretrial motions can resolve legal questions before a jury is selected.
動議是正式要求法院作出裁決或採取行動的請求。審前動議可以在陪審團遴選前解決法律問題。

Common motions include:
常見動議包括：

- Motion to suppress evidence.
  排除證據動議。
- Motion to dismiss a charge.
  撤銷指控動議。
- Motion to sever defendants or counts.
  分開審理被告或罪名的動議。
- Motion for a bill of particulars.
  要求提供具體指控事項的動議。
- Motion to change venue.
  變更審判地點動議。
- Motion in limine.
  審前限制或准許特定證據的動議。

## 4. Motion to suppress
## 4. 排除證據動議

A defendant may ask the court to exclude evidence allegedly obtained in violation of the Constitution or other applicable law.
被告可以要求法院排除據稱違反憲法或其他適用法律取得的證據。

The court may hold an evidentiary hearing to resolve disputed facts. The result may affect whether particular evidence can be used at trial.
法院可能舉行證據聽證，以解決存在爭議的事實。裁決結果可能影響特定證據能否在審判中使用。

## 5. Speedy Trial Act
## 5. 《迅速審判法》

Federal law includes time limits for indictment and trial. Under 18 U.S.C. § 3161, the statute generally provides a 30-day period for filing an indictment or information after arrest or summons in connection with the charges, and a 70-day period for trial measured from the later of specified triggering events.
聯邦法律對提出起訴及開始審判設有期限。依18 U.S.C. § 3161，法律通常規定在與相關指控有關的逮捕或傳票後30天內提出起訴書或正式控告書；審判期限通常為70天，並從法定列明的較後觸發日期起計算。

These are not simple calendar promises. The Act contains exclusions, continuances, and other rules that affect the calculation. A real case requires a date-by-date calculation.
這些並不是簡單的日曆承諾。《迅速審判法》包含不計入期限的期間、延期及其他影響計算的規則。實際案件需要逐日計算。

## 6. Pretrial conference and trial preparation
## 6. 審前會議及審判準備

The court may schedule conferences to organize the case, address evidence issues, set deadlines, and reduce unnecessary delay.
法院可能安排審前會議，以整理案件、處理證據問題、設定期限及減少不必要的延誤。

The parties may also subpoena witnesses and documents under the applicable rules.
雙方也可以依適用規則傳喚證人及要求提交文件。

## 7. Why pretrial procedure matters
## 7. 為什麼審前程序重要？

A trial is not simply a contest of stories. The court must determine which evidence is legally admissible, what issues remain disputed, and what instructions the jury should receive.
審判並不只是雙方故事的競賽。法院必須決定哪些證據在法律上可以採納、還有哪些問題存在爭議，以及應向陪審團提供什麼法律指示。
