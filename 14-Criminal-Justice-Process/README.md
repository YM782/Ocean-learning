# U.S. Criminal Justice Process — Beginner Course
# 美國刑事司法程序——零基礎完整課程

**Understand the full path of a federal criminal case, from investigation to appeal.**
**從調查到上訴，完整理解一宗聯邦刑事案件的流程。**

This course is designed for a learner with no prior legal education. It explains the federal criminal process in plain English, with a Traditional Chinese translation immediately after each substantive English passage.
本課程是為沒有法律教育背景的初學者設計，以淺白英文解釋聯邦刑事司法程序，並在每段實質英文內容後立即附上繁體中文翻譯。

## Scope: federal procedure first
## 範圍：先以聯邦程序為主

The United States has a federal criminal justice system and separate criminal justice systems in the states. This course uses federal law and the Federal Rules of Criminal Procedure as its main teaching framework. State constitutions, statutes, court rules, bail systems, charging procedures, and deadlines may differ.
美國有聯邦刑事司法制度，也有各州各自的刑事司法制度。本課程以聯邦法律及《聯邦刑事訴訟規則》作為主要教學架構。各州的州憲法、制定法、法院規則、保釋制度、起訴程序及期限可能不同。

Do not treat a federal rule as a nationwide rule unless the Constitution or a controlling federal law makes it applicable. Always identify the jurisdiction before applying a rule.
除非憲法或具有控制效力的聯邦法律使某項規則適用，否則不要把聯邦規則當成全美統一規則。應先確認司法管轄區，再適用法律。

## Learning outcomes
## 學習目標

By the end of this course, you should be able to:
完成本課程後，你應能夠：

- Describe the major stages of a federal criminal case.
  說明聯邦刑事案件的主要階段。
- Distinguish an investigation, a stop, an arrest, a charge, an indictment, and a conviction.
  區分調查、攔查、逮捕、刑事指控、大陪審團起訴及定罪。
- Explain the different roles of police, prosecutors, defense counsel, judges, grand juries, trial juries, and probation officers.
  解釋警察、檢察官、辯護律師、法官、大陪審團、審判陪審團及緩刑官的不同職責。
- Identify the major constitutional and procedural protections at each stage.
  找出每個階段主要的憲法保障及程序保障。
- Explain why a probable-cause decision is not a finding of guilt.
  解釋為什麼合理根據的認定並不等於有罪判決。
- Use IRAC to analyze a basic criminal-procedure problem.
  使用 IRAC 分析基本刑事程序問題。

## How to study
## 如何學習

1. Read one short English section first.
   先閱讀一小段英文。
2. Read the Traditional Chinese translation immediately below it.
   隨即閱讀緊接在下方的繁體中文翻譯。
3. Write down no more than five new terms per study session.
   每次學習最多記錄五個新術語。
4. Explain the rule in your own words before reading the case example.
   在閱讀案例之前，先用自己的話解釋法律規則。
5. For every case, identify the jurisdiction, court, date, issue, rule, holding, reasoning, and result.
   閱讀每宗判例時，辨識司法管轄區、法院、日期、爭點、規則、裁判要旨、推理及結果。

## Important legal distinctions
## 重要法律區別

An investigation is not an arrest. An arrest is not a charge. A charge is not a conviction. A grand jury indictment is not a trial verdict.
調查不等於逮捕。逮捕不等於起訴。起訴不等於定罪。大陪審團提出起訴書也不等於審判有罪裁決。

Probable cause is a lower standard than proof beyond a reasonable doubt. A judge's probable-cause finding does not decide guilt.
合理根據的證明標準低於排除合理懷疑。法官認定存在合理根據，並不是決定被告有罪。

A constitutional violation does not automatically dismiss an entire case. The remedy depends on the right, the violation, the evidence, procedural rules, and the applicable jurisdiction.
憲法受到侵犯並不會自動導致整宗案件撤銷。救濟方式取決於相關權利、違法情況、證據、程序規則及適用的司法管轄區。
