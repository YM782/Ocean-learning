# The People and Institutions in a Criminal Case
# 刑事案件中的人員及司法機關

## 1. Law-enforcement officers
## 1. 執法人員

Police officers and federal agents investigate suspected crimes. They may interview witnesses, collect evidence, seek warrants, make lawful stops, and make arrests when the legal requirements are met.
警察及聯邦探員調查涉嫌犯罪的案件。他們可能訪問證人、收集證據、申請令狀、依法攔查，並在符合法律要求時逮捕嫌疑人。

Officers do not decide the defendant's final guilt. Their investigation produces evidence that may later be evaluated by prosecutors, judges, and juries.
執法人員不能決定被告最終是否有罪。他們的調查會產生證據，之後可能由檢察官、法官及陪審團評估。

## 2. Prosecutors
## 2. 檢察官

A federal prosecutor, usually an Assistant United States Attorney, represents the United States in federal criminal proceedings. Prosecutors evaluate evidence, decide whether and what charges to bring under applicable law and policy, litigate motions, negotiate pleas, and present the government's case.
聯邦檢察官通常是助理聯邦檢察官，在聯邦刑事程序中代表美國政府。檢察官會評估證據、依適用法律及政策決定是否起訴及提出哪些指控、處理動議、協商認罪協議，並在法庭上提出控方案件。

A prosecutor is not the judge. The prosecutor is an advocate for the government and is also subject to constitutional, statutory, professional, and ethical disclosure obligations.
檢察官不是法官。檢察官代表政府進行訴訟，同時也受到憲法、制定法、專業規則及倫理上的證據披露義務約束。

## 3. Defense counsel
## 3. 辯護律師

Defense counsel advises the defendant, investigates the case, challenges unlawful evidence, tests the government's proof, negotiates when appropriate, and represents the defendant at hearings and trial.
辯護律師向被告提供法律建議、調查案件、挑戰非法取得的證據、檢驗政府的證明、在適當情況下進行協商，並在聽證及審判中代表被告。

A defendant who cannot afford counsel may qualify for appointed counsel in proceedings where the constitutional or statutory right applies. The right is not a universal promise of a free lawyer for every legal problem.
在憲法或制定法保障的程序中，無力聘請律師的被告可能符合指定律師資格。但這並不代表所有法律問題都保證可以免費獲得律師。

## 4. Judges
## 4. 法官

Judges decide legal questions, supervise court proceedings, rule on motions, protect procedural rights, instruct juries, and impose sentences when appropriate.
法官負責裁決法律問題、主持法院程序、處理動議、保障程序權利、向陪審團作出法律指示，並在適當情況下判處刑罰。

A judge is not supposed to act as the prosecutor or as the defendant's personal lawyer.
法官不應充當檢察官，也不應成為被告的私人律師。

## 5. Grand jury
## 5. 大陪審團

A federal grand jury is a group of citizens that considers evidence presented by the prosecutor and decides whether probable cause supports an indictment.
聯邦大陪審團由公民組成，負責考慮檢察官提出的證據，並決定是否有合理根據支持起訴。

A grand jury is not the trial jury. It does not decide guilt beyond a reasonable doubt. Grand-jury proceedings are generally secret, and the defendant usually does not present a full adversarial defense at that stage.
大陪審團並不是審判陪審團。它不會以排除合理懷疑的標準決定有罪與否。大陪審團程序通常具有保密性，被告一般不會在該階段提出完整的對抗式辯護。

## 6. Trial jury
## 6. 審判陪審團

A trial jury evaluates the evidence presented in court and decides whether the government has proved guilt beyond a reasonable doubt, under the judge's instructions.
審判陪審團依照法官的法律指示，評估在法庭上提出的證據，並決定政府是否已排除合理懷疑地證明被告有罪。

In serious federal criminal cases, the jury verdict must be unanimous. The right to a jury trial does not apply in exactly the same way to every minor offense.
在嚴重的聯邦刑事案件中，陪審團裁決必須一致同意。陪審團審判權並非以完全相同方式適用於每一項輕微違法行為。

## 7. Pretrial services and probation officers
## 7. 審前服務及緩刑官

Pretrial services officers may assess release risks, monitor court-ordered conditions, and report compliance to the court. Probation officers may prepare presentence reports and supervise people placed on probation or supervised release.
審前服務官員可能評估釋放風險、監督法院命令的條件，並向法院報告遵守情況。緩刑官可能編製量刑前報告，並監督被判緩刑或監督釋放的人。

They do not replace the judge's decision-making role.
他們不能取代法官的裁決職責。

## 8. Victims and witnesses
## 8. 被害人及證人

Victims and witnesses may provide information, testify, and participate in certain proceedings under applicable law. Federal crime victims have statutory rights under the Crime Victims' Rights Act, but those rights do not make the victim the prosecutor or the final decision-maker.
被害人及證人可能提供資料、作證，並依適用法律參與某些程序。聯邦犯罪被害人依《犯罪被害人權利法》享有法定權利，但這些權利不會使被害人成為檢察官或最終決策者。

## Remember the division of roles
## 記住職責分工

- Police investigate.
  警方負責調查。
- Prosecutors decide whether to bring charges and present the government's case.
  檢察官決定是否提出指控，並代表政府提出案件。
- Defense counsel protects the defendant's legal interests.
  辯護律師保障被告的法律利益。
- Judges decide legal questions and supervise fair proceedings.
  法官裁決法律問題並監督公平程序。
- Grand juries decide whether probable cause supports an indictment.
  大陪審團決定是否有合理根據支持起訴。
- Trial juries decide whether guilt has been proved beyond a reasonable doubt.
  審判陪審團決定是否已排除合理懷疑地證明有罪。
