# Criminal Justice — Legal English Glossary
# 刑事司法——法律英語詞彙表

| English term | 繁體中文 | Plain English definition | 中文解釋 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Investigation | 調查 | Gathering information about possible crime. | 收集可能犯罪的相關資料。 |
| Law enforcement | 執法機關 | Police and other agencies that enforce laws. | 負責執行法律的警察及其他機關。 |
| Suspect | 嫌疑人 | A person suspected of involvement in an offense. | 被懷疑涉及某項犯罪的人。 |
| Witness | 證人 | A person who may provide relevant information. | 可能提供相關資料的人。 |
| Informant | 線人 | A person who provides information to authorities. | 向執法機關提供情報的人。 |
| Search | 搜查 | Government examination of a protected place or information. | 政府檢查受保障的場所或資料。 |
| Seizure | 扣押 | Government taking of property or restraint of a person. | 政府取得財物或限制人身自由。 |
| Warrant | 令狀 | Judicial authorization for specified government action. | 法院對特定政府行動作出的授權。 |
| Probable cause | 合理根據 | Facts supporting a fair probability that an offense or evidence is involved. | 支持犯罪或證據具有相當可能性的事實基礎。 |
| Reasonable suspicion | 合理懷疑 | Specific facts supporting a brief investigative stop. | 支持短暫調查性攔查的具體事實。 |
| Terry stop | Terry 攔查 | A brief investigative stop based on reasonable suspicion. | 基於合理懷疑而進行的短暫調查性攔查。 |
| Frisk | 拍身搜查 | A limited pat-down for weapons when the legal standard is met. | 在符合法律標準時進行的有限度武器拍身搜查。 |
| Arrest | 逮捕 | Taking a person into custody under legal authority. | 依法律權限將某人拘捕及控制。 |
| Booking | 登記程序 | Administrative processing after arrest. | 逮捕後的行政登記處理。 |
| Custody | 羈押狀態 | A legally significant restraint on freedom. | 具有法律意義的人身自由限制狀態。 |
| Interrogation | 訊問 | Police questioning or its functional equivalent under Miranda. | Miranda 原則下的警方提問或功能上等同訊問的行為。 |
| Miranda warnings | Miranda 權利告知 | Warnings generally required before custodial interrogation. | 羈押狀態下訊問前通常必須作出的權利告知。 |
| Complaint | 刑事申訴書 | A charging document stating essential facts. | 載明犯罪必要事實的起訴文件。 |
| Information | 正式控告書 | A formal charging document filed by a prosecutor. | 由檢察官提出的正式起訴文件。 |
| Indictment | 起訴書 | A formal charge returned by a grand jury. | 由大陪審團提出的正式指控文件。 |
| Grand jury | 大陪審團 | Citizens who decide whether probable cause supports an indictment. | 決定是否有合理根據支持起訴的公民團體。 |
| Trial jury | 審判陪審團 | Jurors who decide guilt at trial. | 在審判中決定有罪與否的陪審員。 |
| Preliminary hearing | 初步聽證 | A hearing to assess probable cause when required. | 在法律要求時評估合理根據的聽證。 |
| Initial appearance | 首次出庭 | The first court appearance after arrest in the relevant process. | 相關程序中逮捕後的首次法院出庭。 |
| Arraignment | 正式提訊 | A proceeding where the charge is presented and a plea is entered. | 告知正式指控並作出答辯的程序。 |
| Bail | 保釋 | A legal mechanism for pretrial release, often subject to conditions. | 審前釋放的法律機制，通常附帶條件。 |
| Detention hearing | 羈押聽證 | A hearing to decide whether pretrial detention is justified. | 決定審前羈押是否具有正當理由的聽證。 |
| Discovery | 證據開示 | Exchange or disclosure of specified case information before trial. | 審判前依規則交換或披露特定案件資料。 |
| Exculpatory evidence | 無罪方向證據 | Evidence tending to show innocence or reduce culpability. | 傾向於證明無罪或減輕責任的證據。 |
| Impeachment evidence | 彈劾證據 | Evidence used to challenge a witness's credibility. | 用來質疑證人可信度的證據。 |
| Brady material | Brady 證據 | Material favorable evidence subject to constitutional disclosure. | 依憲法必須披露的重要有利證據。 |
| Suppression motion | 排除證據動議 | A request to exclude evidence from trial. | 要求法院排除審判證據的動議。 |
| Plea bargaining | 認罪協商 | Negotiation to resolve a criminal case by a plea agreement. | 透過認罪協議解決刑事案件的協商。 |
| Guilty plea | 認罪答辯 | A formal admission of guilt to a charge. | 對指控正式承認有罪。 |
| Nolo contendere | 不抗辯答辯 | A plea not contesting the charge, subject to court consent. | 在法院同意下不爭辯指控的答辯方式。 |
| Voir dire | 陪審團遴選詢問 | Questioning prospective jurors. | 對候選陪審員進行詢問。 |
| Cross-examination | 交叉詢問 | Questioning an opposing party's witness. | 對對方提出的證人進行詢問。 |
| Verdict | 裁決 | The jury's or judge's decision on guilt. | 陪審團或法官對有罪與否作出的決定。 |
| Hung jury | 僵局陪審團 | A jury unable to reach the required verdict. | 無法達成必要裁決的陪審團。 |
| Sentencing | 量刑 | The court's decision about punishment after conviction. | 定罪後法院決定刑罰的程序。 |
| Presentence report | 量刑前報告 | A report prepared to assist sentencing. | 協助法院量刑的報告。 |
| Sentencing Guidelines | 量刑指南 | A structured framework for calculating a guideline range. | 用於計算指南刑期範圍的結構化架構。 |
| Mandatory minimum | 強制最低刑 | A minimum penalty required by statute, subject to exceptions. | 制定法要求的最低刑罰，但仍可能存在例外。 |
| Restitution | 損害賠償 | Payment ordered to compensate a victim for loss. | 法院命令向被害人支付的損害補償。 |
| Supervised release | 監督釋放 | Court-ordered supervision after imprisonment in federal cases. | 聯邦案件中監禁後由法院命令的監督期間。 |
| Direct appeal | 直接上訴 | Review of a judgment by a higher court. | 由上級法院審查下級法院判決。 |
| Habeas corpus | 人身保護令 | A legal procedure challenging unlawful custody. | 挑戰非法羈押的法律程序。 |
| Collateral review | 附帶審查 | Review outside the ordinary direct appeal process. | 一般直接上訴程序以外的審查。 |
| Wrongful conviction | 冤錯定罪 | A conviction that is factually or legally wrongful. | 在事實或法律上錯誤的定罪。 |
| Chain of custody | 證據保管鏈 | Record of who handled evidence and when. | 記錄證據由誰及何時保管或處理的紀錄。 |
| Burden of proof | 舉證責任 | The duty to establish facts under a legal standard. | 依法律標準證明事實的責任。 |
| Beyond a reasonable doubt | 排除合理懷疑 | The criminal-trial standard of proof. | 刑事審判的證明標準。 |
| Acquittal | 無罪判決 | A judgment that the defendant is not guilty. | 法院裁定被告無罪。 |
| Conviction | 定罪 | A formal finding or plea of guilt. | 正式認定有罪或認罪後的定罪。 |
