# Sentencing
# 量刑

## 1. When does sentencing happen?
## 1. 什麼時候量刑？

Sentencing generally follows a guilty plea or guilty verdict. The court determines the legal punishment after considering the governing statutes, constitutional limits, sentencing rules, and the facts of the case.
量刑通常發生在被告認罪或被裁定有罪之後。法院會考慮適用制定法、憲法限制、量刑規則及案件事實，決定法律上的刑罰。

## 2. Presentence investigation and report
## 2. 量刑前調查及報告

In many federal felony cases, a probation officer prepares a presentence investigation report. The report may describe the offense, the defendant's background, criminal history, relevant sentencing calculations, and other information required by the rules.
在許多聯邦重罪案件中，緩刑官會編製量刑前調查報告。報告可能描述犯罪行為、被告背景、犯罪紀錄、相關量刑計算，以及規則要求的其他資料。

The parties may object to disputed facts or legal calculations. The report assists the court but does not replace the judge's independent responsibility.
雙方可以對有爭議的事實或法律計算提出異議。報告有助於法院處理案件，但不能取代法官獨立作出決定的責任。

## 3. Federal Sentencing Guidelines
## 3. 聯邦量刑指南

The United States Sentencing Guidelines provide a structured framework for calculating a guideline range. The calculation may consider the offense guideline, offense characteristics, adjustments, criminal history, and other provisions.
美國量刑指南提供一套結構化架構，用來計算指南刑期範圍。計算可能考慮犯罪指南、犯罪特徵、調整因素、犯罪紀錄及其他規定。

After *United States v. Booker* (2005), the federal Guidelines are advisory rather than mandatory. The sentencing court must correctly calculate and consider the Guidelines, together with the statutory factors in 18 U.S.C. § 3553(a).
在2005年的 *United States v. Booker* 案之後，聯邦量刑指南屬於建議性而非強制性。量刑法院必須正確計算並考慮量刑指南，以及18 U.S.C. § 3553(a) 所列的法定因素。

For this package, the current reference is the 2026 Guidelines Manual, effective November 1, 2026. Always confirm the manual and amendments applicable on the sentencing date.
本文件包採用的現行參考資料是2026年11月1日起生效的《2026年量刑指南》。務必核實量刑日期適用的指南版本及修正內容。

## 4. Statutory factors
## 4. 法定量刑因素

Under 18 U.S.C. § 3553(a), the court considers factors such as the nature and circumstances of the offense, the defendant's history and characteristics, the purposes of punishment, available sentences, the Guidelines, avoiding unwarranted disparities, and restitution.
依18 U.S.C. § 3553(a)，法院會考慮犯罪的性質及情況、被告的歷史及特徵、刑罰目的、可用刑罰、量刑指南、避免不合理的刑罰差異，以及賠償。

## 5. Types of sentences
## 5. 刑罰種類

Depending on the statute and case, a sentence may include:
依制定法及案件情況，刑罰可能包括：

- Imprisonment.
  監禁。
- Probation.
  緩刑。
- Supervised release after imprisonment.
  監禁後的監督釋放。
- Fines and special assessments.
  罰金及特別評估金。
- Restitution to victims.
  向被害人支付賠償。
- Forfeiture of property when authorized by law.
  在法律授權時沒收財產。

A sentence may combine more than one form of punishment.
一項刑罰可能結合多種處罰方式。

## 6. The defendant's opportunity to speak
## 6. 被告陳述意見的機會

Rule 32 requires the court to allow the defendant and counsel to speak before sentence is imposed. A victim who is present must be allowed to be reasonably heard under the rule.
第32條規則要求法院在判刑前給予被告及律師陳述機會。依該規則，在場的被害人也必須獲得合理陳述機會。

## 7. Mandatory minimums and judicial discretion
## 7. 強制最低刑及司法裁量

Some federal statutes establish mandatory minimum penalties. These statutory requirements can limit the court's sentencing options, subject to any applicable safety valve, substantial-assistance provision, or other lawful exception.
某些聯邦制定法設有強制最低刑。這些法定要求可能限制法院的量刑選擇，但仍須考慮適用的安全閥、重大協助條款或其他合法例外。

## 8. Sentencing is not a second trial
## 8. 量刑不是第二次審判

The sentencing stage has different rules from the guilt phase. The information a court may consider at sentencing can be broader than the evidence admissible at trial, subject to constitutional and statutory limits.
量刑階段的規則與有罪認定階段不同。法院在量刑時可以考慮的資料，可能比審判中可採納的證據更廣，但仍受到憲法及制定法限制。
