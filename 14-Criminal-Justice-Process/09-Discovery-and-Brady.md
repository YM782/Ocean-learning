# Discovery, Disclosure, and Brady
# 證據開示、披露及 Brady 義務

## 1. What is discovery?
## 1. 什麼是證據開示？

Discovery is the pretrial process through which parties obtain certain information and materials from each other under governing rules.
證據開示是審判前雙方依適用規則向對方取得特定資料及材料的程序。

Criminal discovery is not identical to civil discovery. Federal criminal discovery is governed by specific rules and statutes, and it is generally more limited than broad civil discovery.
刑事證據開示與民事證據開示並不相同。聯邦刑事證據開示由特定規則及制定法規範，通常比廣泛的民事證據開示更有限。

## 2. Federal Rule of Criminal Procedure 16
## 2. 《聯邦刑事訴訟規則》第16條

Rule 16 identifies categories of information the government must disclose when its requirements are met. These may include certain defendant statements, documents and objects, examination and test reports, and expert information.
第16條規則列出在符合規定時政府必須披露的資料類別。這些資料可能包括被告的某些陳述、文件及物品、檢查與測試報告，以及專家資訊。

The rule also contains reciprocal disclosure obligations for the defense in specified circumstances. It is not a rule that every item in the government's files must automatically be handed over.
在特定情況下，該規則也對辯方設有相互披露義務。它並不是要求政府自動交出檔案中每一項資料的規則。

## 3. Brady evidence
## 3. Brady 證據

Under *Brady v. Maryland* (1963), the prosecution violates due process when it suppresses evidence favorable to the accused that is material to guilt or punishment.
依1963年的 *Brady v. Maryland* 案，如果控方隱瞞對被告有利、而且對有罪與否或刑罰具有重要性的證據，就可能違反正當法律程序。

Favorable evidence includes evidence that tends to show innocence or reduce culpability. The constitutional duty is not limited to evidence that the defense formally requests.
有利證據包括傾向於證明無罪或減輕責任的證據。憲法上的披露義務並不只限於辯方正式提出要求的證據。

Materiality is a legal standard. Not every undisclosed item automatically establishes a constitutional violation; the court examines whether the evidence was material under the governing test.
重要性是一項法律標準。並非每一項未披露資料都自動構成憲法違反；法院會依適用標準檢查該證據是否具有重要性。

## 4. Giglio impeachment evidence
## 4. Giglio 彈劾證據

Under *Giglio v. United States* (1972), material evidence that could impeach a government witness may fall within the prosecution's disclosure duty.
依1972年的 *Giglio v. United States* 案，可能用來彈劾政府證人的重要證據，可能屬於控方必須披露的範圍。

Examples may include a material promise of leniency, a benefit given to a witness, or information that bears on a witness's credibility, depending on the facts and materiality standard.
依具體事實及重要性標準，例子可能包括給予證人的重要寬待承諾、利益，或影響證人可信度的資料。

## 5. Kyles and the prosecution team
## 5. Kyles 案與控方團隊

In *Kyles v. Whitley* (1995), the Supreme Court explained that the prosecutor's responsibility includes favorable evidence known to others acting on the government's behalf in the case, including police.
在1995年的 *Kyles v. Whitley* 案中，最高法院解釋，檢察官的責任包括案件中代表政府行事的其他人員所掌握的有利證據，其中包括警方。

A prosecutor cannot avoid the constitutional duty simply because the information is held by an investigator rather than personally by the prosecutor.
檢察官不能只因資料由調查人員持有、而不是由檢察官本人持有，就逃避憲法上的披露義務。

## 6. Jencks Act and witness statements
## 6. 《Jencks 法》及證人陳述

Federal law and Rule 26.2 address production of certain prior statements of witnesses after the witness has testified on direct examination, subject to the rules' definitions and procedures.
聯邦法律及第26.2條規則規範證人在直接詢問作證後，某些先前陳述的提交問題，但仍須符合規則中的定義及程序。

This is different from a general right to inspect every investigative note before trial.
這與審判前普遍有權查閱所有調查筆記並不相同。

## 7. Why disclosure matters
## 7. 為什麼證據披露很重要？

The defense cannot test the government's case fairly if material favorable evidence is hidden. Disclosure rules help reduce wrongful convictions and improve the reliability of the adversarial process.
如果重要的有利證據被隱瞞，辯方就無法公平檢驗控方案件。披露規則有助於減少冤錯定罪，並提高對抗式訴訟程序的可靠性。

## Mini scenario
## 迷你情境

A key prosecution witness tells investigators that the witness received a promise of favorable treatment. The prosecutor's file does not contain the note, but the police investigator has it.
控方的重要證人告訴調查人員，自己曾獲得有利待遇的承諾。檢察官的檔案中沒有這份筆記，但警方調查人員持有該筆記。

**Issue:** Could the information be relevant under Brady or Giglio?
**爭點：** 這項資料是否可能與 Brady 或 Giglio 義務有關？

**Analysis:** It may be impeachment information. The court would examine the prosecution team's knowledge, the credibility issue, materiality, timing, and any resulting prejudice.
**分析：** 這可能是彈劾證據。法院會檢查控方團隊的知情情況、證人可信度問題、證據重要性、披露時間及因此造成的損害。
