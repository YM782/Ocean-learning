# Charging, Grand Jury, and Preliminary Hearing
# 起訴、大陪審團及初步聽證

## 1. Who brings a federal criminal case?
## 1. 誰提出聯邦刑事案件？

A federal criminal prosecution is brought by the government, usually through the United States Attorney's Office. Law-enforcement agencies investigate, but the prosecutor makes the charging decision under applicable law and policy.
聯邦刑事追訴由政府提出，通常透過聯邦檢察官辦公室進行。執法機關負責調查，但檢察官依適用法律及政策作出起訴決定。

A private person generally cannot personally prosecute a federal criminal case in the name of the United States.
一般而言，私人不能以美國政府名義親自提出聯邦刑事追訴。

## 2. Three common charging documents
## 2. 三種常見起訴文件

### Criminal complaint
### 刑事申訴書

A complaint is a written statement of essential facts constituting the offense charged, made under oath or otherwise supported as required by the rules.
刑事申訴書是載明被控罪名必要事實的書面文件，並須依規則宣誓或以其他要求的方式支持。

### Information
### 正式控告書

An information is a formal charging document filed by a prosecutor. In a federal felony case, prosecution by information generally requires the defendant to waive indictment in open court after being advised of the charge and the relevant rights.
正式控告書是由檢察官提出的正式起訴文件。在聯邦重罪案件中，通常必須由被告在獲告知指控及相關權利後，於公開法庭放棄大陪審團起訴，才可以透過正式控告書進行追訴。

### Indictment
### 起訴書

An indictment is a formal accusation returned by a grand jury. For federal offenses punishable by death or imprisonment for more than one year, Rule 7 generally requires an indictment, subject to a valid waiver.
起訴書是由大陪審團提出的正式指控。對於可判處死刑或超過一年監禁的聯邦罪行，第7條規則通常要求提出起訴書，但有效放棄權利的情況除外。

## 3. What does a grand jury do?
## 3. 大陪審團負責什麼？

A federal grand jury reviews evidence presented by the prosecutor and decides whether there is probable cause to charge the person.
聯邦大陪審團審查檢察官提出的證據，並決定是否有合理根據對當事人提出指控。

The grand jury does not determine whether the defendant is guilty beyond a reasonable doubt. That is a different question for a trial jury or judge at trial.
大陪審團不會以排除合理懷疑的標準判斷被告是否有罪。這是審判時由審判陪審團或法官處理的另一個問題。

Grand-jury proceedings are generally secret. The defendant usually does not have the same opportunity to cross-examine witnesses or present a full defense that exists at trial.
大陪審團程序通常具有保密性。被告通常沒有審判階段那種交叉詢問證人或提出完整辯護的機會。

## 4. Grand jury versus trial jury
## 4. 大陪審團與審判陪審團的區別

| Grand jury | Trial jury |
|---|---|
| Considers whether probable cause supports an indictment. | Decides whether guilt is proved beyond a reasonable doubt. |
| 考慮是否有合理根據支持起訴書。 | 決定是否已排除合理懷疑地證明有罪。 |
| Usually hears the government's presentation. | Evaluates evidence presented by both sides under court rules. |
| 通常聽取政府提出的證據。 | 依法院規則評估雙方提出的證據。 |
| Does not conduct the trial. | Participates in the trial and returns a verdict. |
| 不負責進行審判。 | 參與審判並作出裁決。 |

## 5. Preliminary hearing — Rule 5.1
## 5. 初步聽證——第5.1條規則

When required, a preliminary hearing allows a magistrate judge to decide whether probable cause supports proceeding with the case. The defendant may cross-examine adverse witnesses and introduce evidence under Rule 5.1.
在法律要求的情況下，初步聽證讓治安法官決定是否有合理根據支持案件繼續進行。依第5.1條規則，被告可以交叉詢問不利證人並提出證據。

Under the federal rule, the hearing generally must occur within 14 days after the initial appearance if the defendant is in custody, or within 21 days if the defendant is not in custody, subject to permitted extensions and exceptions.
依聯邦規則，如果被告遭羈押，聽證通常必須在首次出庭後14天內舉行；如果被告沒有遭羈押，通常必須在21天內舉行，但仍須考慮獲准的延期及例外。

A preliminary hearing is generally unnecessary when the defendant has already been indicted or when a qualifying information has been filed under the rule.
如果被告已經受到大陪審團起訴，或依規則已提出符合條件的正式控告書，通常不需要再進行初步聽證。

## 6. Why an indictment is not proof of guilt
## 6. 為什麼起訴書不是有罪證明？

An indictment means the grand jury found probable cause to charge. It does not mean the government has proved every element of the offense at trial.
起訴書代表大陪審團認為有合理根據提出指控，並不代表政府已經在審判中證明犯罪的每一項要件。

The defendant remains presumed innocent unless and until guilt is established under the applicable trial standard.
除非並且直到依適用審判標準證明有罪，否則被告仍享有無罪推定。

## 7. Federal versus state grand juries
## 7. 聯邦與州的大陪審團

The Fifth Amendment Grand Jury Clause applies to federal prosecutions, but it has not been incorporated against the states. State charging systems therefore vary.
第五修正案的大陪審團條款適用於聯邦刑事追訴，但尚未納入適用於各州。因此，各州的起訴制度有所不同。
