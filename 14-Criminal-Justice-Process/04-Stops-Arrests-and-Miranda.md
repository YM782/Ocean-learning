# Stops, Arrests, and Miranda
# 攔查、逮捕及 Miranda 權利告知

## 1. A voluntary encounter
## 1. 自願接觸

A voluntary encounter generally occurs when an officer approaches a person and asks questions without restraining the person's freedom in a way that amounts to a seizure.
自願接觸通常是指警員接近某人並提出問題，但沒有以構成扣留的方式限制該人的自由。

The key question is whether a reasonable person in the circumstances would feel free to decline the officer's requests or end the encounter. The full context matters.
關鍵問題是，在當時情況下，理性的人是否會認為自己可以拒絕警員的要求或結束接觸。必須考慮整體情境。

## 2. Investigative stop — Terry stop
## 2. 調查性攔查——Terry 攔查

Under *Terry v. Ohio* (1968), an officer may briefly stop a person when the officer has reasonable suspicion, based on specific and articulable facts, that criminal activity may be occurring.
依1968年的 *Terry v. Ohio* 案，警員若基於具體且能夠說明的事實，合理懷疑可能正在發生犯罪活動，可以短暫攔查某人。

Reasonable suspicion is more than a hunch but less than probable cause. The stop must remain reasonably related in scope and duration to the reason for the stop.
合理懷疑高於單純猜測，但低於合理根據。攔查的範圍及持續時間必須與攔查理由保持合理關聯。

A limited frisk for weapons requires an additional reasonable basis to believe the person is armed and dangerous. A frisk is not a general evidence search.
如要進行有限度的武器拍身搜查，還必須有合理理由相信當事人攜帶武器且具有危險性。拍身搜查不是一般性的證據搜查。

## 3. Arrest
## 3. 逮捕

An arrest is a more substantial restraint on a person's liberty. An arrest generally requires probable cause that the person committed an offense.
逮捕是對個人自由作出更大程度的限制。逮捕通常需要合理根據相信該人犯下某項罪行。

Probable cause is a practical, fact-based standard. It is not proof beyond a reasonable doubt and does not establish guilt at trial.
合理根據是一種依實際事實作判斷的標準。它不是排除合理懷疑的證明，也不會確立審判中的有罪結論。

The Fourth Amendment places additional limits on certain arrests, including entry into a home to make an arrest. The exact rule depends on the location, warrant, consent, and any recognized exigent circumstances.
第四修正案對某些逮捕設下額外限制，包括進入住宅逮捕。具體規則取決於地點、令狀、同意及是否存在法律承認的緊急情況。

## 4. Miranda warnings
## 4. Miranda 權利告知

Under *Miranda v. Arizona* (1966), police generally must provide warnings before custodial interrogation. The familiar warning explains the right to remain silent, that statements may be used in court, and the right to counsel.
依1966年的 *Miranda v. Arizona* 案，警方通常必須在羈押狀態下訊問前作出權利告知。常見告知內容包括保持沉默的權利、所說的話可能在法庭上被使用，以及獲得律師協助的權利。

Two conditions matter:
兩項條件十分重要：

- **Custody:** The person is in custody under the Miranda test.
  **羈押狀態：** 依 Miranda 標準，當事人處於羈押狀態。
- **Interrogation:** Police conduct questioning or its functional equivalent that is reasonably likely to elicit an incriminating response.
  **訊問：** 警方進行提問，或採取在功能上等同訊問、且合理可能引出不利陳述的行為。

Not every police conversation is custodial interrogation. A traffic stop is not automatically Miranda custody.
並非每次與警方交談都構成羈押訊問。交通攔查也不會自動構成 Miranda 意義上的羈押。

## 5. Invoking the right
## 5. 主張權利

A person in custodial interrogation may invoke the right to remain silent or the Miranda right to counsel. The rules for invocation, questioning, and any later waiver are technical and fact-sensitive.
處於羈押訊問中的人可以主張保持沉默的權利，或主張 Miranda 下的律師協助權。權利主張、後續訊問及任何其後的放棄，都有技術性且取決於具體事實的規則。

The Fifth Amendment Miranda right to counsel is not identical to the Sixth Amendment right to counsel. The Sixth Amendment right generally attaches after formal adversarial criminal proceedings begin and is offense-specific.
第五修正案 Miranda 下的律師協助權，與第六修正案律師協助權並不相同。第六修正案權利通常在正式對抗性刑事程序開始後適用，而且具有特定罪名範圍。

## 6. What happens after a Miranda violation?
## 6. 違反 Miranda 後會怎樣？

A Miranda violation does not automatically dismiss the entire case. The usual issue is whether an unwarned statement may be used in the prosecution's case-in-chief, subject to recognized exceptions and other rules.
違反 Miranda 規則不會自動撤銷整宗案件。一般爭點是未經權利告知取得的陳述能否在控方主要舉證階段使用，但仍須考慮法律承認的例外及其他規則。

Other evidence may remain admissible if it was obtained independently and lawfully.
如果其他證據是獨立且合法取得的，仍可能被採納。

## Mini scenario
## 迷你情境

An officer stops a person based on specific facts suggesting a recent robbery. During the brief stop, the officer asks questions. The person is not handcuffed, has not been told they are under arrest, and the encounter remains brief.
警員基於具體事實，懷疑某人涉及最近發生的搶劫案，因此將其攔查。短暫攔查期間，警員提出問題。當事人沒有被戴上手銬，也沒有被告知已遭逮捕，而且接觸時間仍然短暫。

**Question:** Does Miranda automatically apply?
**問題：** Miranda 是否自動適用？

**Analysis:** Not necessarily. The person may be subject to a Terry stop, but whether the person is in Miranda custody requires a separate objective analysis of the circumstances. The stop itself does not automatically trigger Miranda.
**分析：** 不一定。當事人可能正處於 Terry 攔查，但是否構成 Miranda 羈押狀態，需要根據具體情況作獨立的客觀分析。攔查本身不會自動觸發 Miranda。
