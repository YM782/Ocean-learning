# Booking, Initial Appearance, and Pretrial Release
# 登記程序、首次出庭及審前釋放

## 1. Booking
## 1. 登記程序

After an arrest, the person may be taken to a police or detention facility for booking. Booking commonly records identity information, the alleged offense, fingerprints, photographs, and custody information.
逮捕後，當事人可能被帶到警察局或拘留設施辦理登記。登記通常記錄身分資料、涉嫌罪名、指紋、照片及羈押資訊。

Booking is an administrative step. It is not a judicial finding that the person is guilty.
登記程序屬於行政性步驟，並不是法院認定當事人有罪。

## 2. Initial appearance — Rule 5
## 2. 首次出庭——第5條規則

In a federal case, a person arrested in the United States generally must be taken before a magistrate judge without unnecessary delay.
在聯邦案件中，在美國境內遭逮捕的人，通常必須在沒有不必要延誤的情況下被帶到治安法官面前。

At the initial appearance, the judge advises the defendant of the complaint or charges, explains important rights, addresses counsel, and considers release or detention under the applicable law.
在首次出庭時，法官會告知被告刑事申訴書或指控內容、解釋重要權利、處理律師協助問題，並依適用法律考慮釋放或羈押。

The initial appearance is not the same as a trial. The judge is not deciding guilt beyond a reasonable doubt at this hearing.
首次出庭並不是審判。法官不會在這場聽證中以排除合理懷疑的標準決定被告是否有罪。

## 3. Right to counsel
## 3. 律師協助權

A defendant who cannot afford counsel may qualify for appointed counsel under the applicable constitutional and statutory rules. The judge must address the right to counsel in the relevant proceedings.
無力聘請律師的被告，可能依適用憲法及制定法規則符合指定律師資格。法官必須在相關程序中處理律師協助權問題。

The scope of appointed counsel depends on the type of case, possible punishment, and governing law. Do not assume that every civil or minor criminal matter carries the same right.
指定律師權的適用範圍取決於案件類型、可能刑罰及適用法律。不要假設每宗民事案件或輕微刑事案件都享有相同權利。

## 4. Pretrial release and detention
## 4. 審前釋放及羈押

Under the federal Bail Reform Act, the court considers whether conditions can reasonably assure the defendant's appearance and the safety of other people and the community.
依聯邦《保釋改革法》，法院會考慮是否可以透過附帶條件，合理確保被告按時出庭，以及保障其他人和社區的安全。

Possible outcomes include:
可能結果包括：

- Release on personal recognizance.
  以個人保證方式釋放。
- Release on an unsecured appearance bond.
  以無擔保出庭保證書方式釋放。
- Release with conditions, such as reporting, travel restrictions, or monitoring.
  附帶條件釋放，例如定期報到、旅行限制或監控。
- Temporary detention or detention pending trial when statutory requirements are met.
  在符合法定要求時，暫時羈押或審前羈押。

Federal pretrial release is not simply a system in which every defendant pays a fixed cash amount. The statute directs courts to consider release conditions and detention criteria.
聯邦審前釋放並不是所有被告支付固定現金金額就可以獲釋的制度。制定法要求法院考慮釋放條件及羈押標準。

## 5. Detention hearing
## 5. 羈押聽證

At a detention hearing, the court considers the statutory factors and evidence relevant to appearance and safety. The prosecution and defense may present arguments and information.
在羈押聽證中，法院會考慮與出庭及安全有關的法定因素及證據。控辯雙方可以提出主張及相關資料。

The government may seek detention in categories of cases specified by statute. Some cases involve rebuttable presumptions, but the precise legal effect depends on the statute and the facts.
政府可能在制定法列明的某些案件類別中申請羈押。某些案件涉及可反駁的推定，但其具體法律效果取決於制定法及案件事實。

## 6. Important distinction: release is not acquittal
## 6. 重要區別：獲釋不等於無罪

A person released before trial remains subject to the criminal case and must follow court conditions. A person detained before trial has not thereby been convicted.
審前獲釋的人仍然受到刑事案件程序約束，必須遵守法院條件。審前遭羈押的人也不會因此被視為已經定罪。
