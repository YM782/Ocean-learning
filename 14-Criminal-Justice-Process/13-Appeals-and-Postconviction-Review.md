# Appeals and Post-Conviction Review
# 上訴及定罪後審查

## 1. What is a direct appeal?
## 1. 什麼是直接上訴？

A direct appeal asks a higher court to review a lower court's judgment for legal error. It is usually based on the existing trial record, not a completely new trial with all evidence presented again.
直接上訴是要求上級法院審查下級法院判決是否存在法律錯誤。上訴通常以既有審判紀錄為基礎，而不是把所有證據重新提出並進行一次完整新審判。

A court of appeals may affirm, reverse, vacate, or remand a decision, depending on the issue and the governing standard.
上訴法院可能依爭點及適用審查標準，維持、撤銷、廢棄或發回原審法院重新處理。

## 2. What can be raised on appeal?
## 2. 上訴可以提出什麼問題？

An appeal may raise issues such as:
上訴可能提出以下問題：

- A constitutional violation.
  憲法違反。
- An incorrect interpretation of a statute or rule.
  對制定法或規則作出錯誤解釋。
- Improper admission or exclusion of evidence.
  不當採納或排除證據。
- Incorrect jury instructions.
  陪審團法律指示錯誤。
- Insufficient evidence under the applicable legal standard.
  依適用法律標準，證據不足。
- A legally unreasonable sentence.
  在法律上不合理的刑罰。

## 3. Preservation of error
## 3. 保留法律異議

A party generally must raise an objection at the appropriate time in the trial court to preserve many issues for appeal. Failure to object may lead to a more demanding standard of appellate review.
訴訟當事人通常必須在適當時間向原審法院提出異議，才能保留許多上訴問題。未提出異議可能導致上訴法院採用更嚴格的審查標準。

There are exceptions, including certain forms of plain-error review. The applicable standard depends on the issue and procedural history.
仍存在例外，包括某些明顯錯誤審查。適用標準取決於爭點及程序經過。

## 4. Deadline for a federal criminal appeal
## 4. 聯邦刑事上訴期限

Under Federal Rule of Appellate Procedure 4(b), a defendant's notice of appeal generally must be filed within 14 days after entry of the judgment or order being appealed, subject to the rule's details and permitted extensions.
依《聯邦上訴程序規則》第4(b)條，被告通常必須在被上訴判決或命令正式登錄後14天內提出上訴通知，但仍須考慮規則細節及獲准的延期。

Deadlines can be strict. The calculation may be affected by certain post-trial motions. Always check the current rule and local appellate requirements.
期限可能相當嚴格。某些審判後動議可能影響期限計算。務必核對現行規則及當地上訴要求。

## 5. Habeas corpus and collateral review
## 5. 人身保護令及附帶審查

Collateral review is different from direct appeal. It allows a person to raise certain claims through a separate legal procedure after the ordinary appeal process, subject to demanding statutory and procedural requirements.
附帶審查與直接上訴不同。它讓當事人在一般上訴程序之後，依另一項法律程序提出某些主張，但受到嚴格的制定法及程序要求限制。

Two important federal statutes are:
兩項重要聯邦制定法是：

- **28 U.S.C. § 2255:** A federal prisoner may use this procedure to challenge certain aspects of a federal sentence or conviction.
  **28 U.S.C. § 2255：** 聯邦囚犯可以透過此程序，挑戰聯邦定罪或刑罰的某些方面。
- **28 U.S.C. § 2254:** A person in custody under a state-court judgment may seek federal habeas relief subject to statutory requirements, including exhaustion of available state remedies.
  **28 U.S.C. § 2254：** 受到州法院判決羈押的人，可以在符合制定法要求的情況下申請聯邦人身保護救濟，包括用盡可用的州級救濟程序。

These procedures have strict filing rules, limitation periods, exhaustion requirements, and restrictions on successive petitions. They are not general opportunities to retry the case.
這些程序具有嚴格的提出規則、期限、救濟用盡要求，以及對重複申請的限制。它們不是讓當事人普遍重新審判案件的機會。

## 6. New trial and post-trial motions
## 6. 新審判及審判後動議

The Federal Rules of Criminal Procedure provide specific post-trial motions, including motions for judgment of acquittal and motions for a new trial. The rules set deadlines and grounds.
《聯邦刑事訴訟規則》提供特定審判後動議，包括無罪判決動議及新審判動議。規則會設定期限及提出理由。

Newly discovered evidence does not automatically produce a new trial. The defendant must satisfy the governing legal test and procedural requirements.
新發現的證據不會自動導致重新審判。被告必須符合適用法律測試及程序要求。

## 7. What does an appeal not do?
## 7. 上訴不代表什麼？

An appeal does not automatically release a defendant, erase the conviction, or guarantee a new trial. The result depends on the legal error, its effect, preservation, and available remedy.
上訴不會自動釋放被告、抹除定罪或保證重新審判。結果取決於法律錯誤、錯誤造成的影響、異議是否保留，以及可用救濟。
