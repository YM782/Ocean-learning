# Searches, Warrants, and Digital Evidence
# 搜查、令狀及數位證據

## 1. The Fourth Amendment
## 1. 第四修正案

The Fourth Amendment protects people against unreasonable searches and seizures by government actors. The legal question is not simply whether police found evidence; it is whether the government action was constitutionally reasonable.
第四修正案保障人民免受政府人員不合理的搜查及扣押。法律問題不只是警方是否找到證據，而是政府行為在憲法上是否合理。

## 2. Search warrants
## 2. 搜查令

A search warrant is judicial authorization for a defined search. A warrant generally must be supported by probable cause and must particularly describe the place to be searched and the persons or things to be seized.
搜查令是法院對特定搜查作出的授權。搜查令通常必須有合理根據支持，並且具體描述要搜查的地點，以及要扣押的人或物品。

The warrant requirement helps place a neutral judicial officer between the police and the person's protected privacy interests.
令狀要求有助於在警方與個人受保障的隱私利益之間，加入中立的司法審查者。

## 3. Common exceptions to the warrant requirement
## 3. 令狀要求的常見例外

Some searches may be lawful without a warrant when a recognized exception applies. Examples include:
在法律承認的例外適用時，某些搜查可能不需要令狀。例子包括：

- **Consent:** A person with valid authority voluntarily consents to the search.
  **同意：** 具有適當權限的人自願同意搜查。
- **Exigent circumstances:** A genuine emergency or urgent situation justifies immediate action under the applicable doctrine.
  **緊急情況：** 依適用法律原則，真實緊急事件或急迫情況足以支持立即行動。
- **Search incident to lawful arrest:** A limited search may be permitted under specific rules connected to a lawful arrest.
  **合法逮捕附帶搜查：** 依特定規則，合法逮捕可能允許有限度的附帶搜查。
- **Automobile exception:** Under defined conditions, probable cause may permit a vehicle search without a warrant.
  **汽車例外：** 在符合特定條件時，合理根據可能允許無令狀搜查車輛。
- **Plain view:** Officers who are lawfully in a position to observe an item may seize it when the legal requirements are met.
  **明顯可見原則：** 執法人員合法處於能夠觀察物品的位置，並且符合相關法律要求時，可能可以扣押該物品。
- **Inventory search:** A standardized, non-investigatory inventory may be permitted under governing rules.
  **財物清點搜查：** 依適用規則，標準化且非以調查為目的的財物清點可能獲准。

These exceptions are not blank checks. Each has limits, and the government must satisfy the requirements of the particular doctrine.
這些例外並不是無限制授權。每一項例外都有界限，政府必須符合該項法律原則的具體要求。

## 4. Digital devices
## 4. 數位裝置

A modern phone may contain messages, photographs, location history, financial information, contacts, and years of personal records. The Supreme Court recognized that searching digital phone contents raises distinctive privacy concerns.
現代手機可能包含訊息、照片、位置歷史、財務資料、聯絡人及多年個人紀錄。最高法院承認，搜查手機數位內容會引發特殊的隱私問題。

In *Riley v. California* (2014), the Court generally required a warrant to search digital information on a phone seized incident to arrest, subject to applicable exceptions.
在2014年的 *Riley v. California* 案中，最高法院一般要求警方取得令狀，才能搜查因逮捕而扣押的手機數位資料，但仍須考慮適用例外。

In *Carpenter v. United States* (2018), the Court held that obtaining certain historical cell-site location information was a Fourth Amendment search and generally required a warrant supported by probable cause.
在2018年的 *Carpenter v. United States* 案中，最高法院裁定，取得某些歷史手機基地台位置資料構成第四修正案意義上的搜查，通常需要有合理根據支持的令狀。

## 5. Suppression and the exclusionary rule
## 5. 證據排除及排除證據規則

The exclusionary rule may prevent certain evidence obtained through unconstitutional government conduct from being used in a criminal trial. The rule is subject to limitations and exceptions.
排除證據規則可能阻止某些透過違憲政府行為取得的證據在刑事審判中使用。但該規則受到限制，也存在例外。

A defendant may file a motion to suppress evidence. The court may need to determine whether the defendant's own Fourth Amendment rights were violated, whether the evidence was obtained through the violation, and whether an exception applies.
被告可以提出排除證據動議。法院可能需要判斷被告本人的第四修正案權利是否受到侵犯、證據是否因該違法行為而取得，以及是否適用例外。

Important doctrines include:
重要法律原則包括：

- **Good-faith exception:** *United States v. Leon* (1984).
  **善意例外：** *United States v. Leon*（1984年）。
- **Independent source doctrine:** Evidence may be admitted if obtained through a genuinely independent lawful source.
  **獨立來源原則：** 如果證據是透過真正獨立的合法來源取得，可能仍可採納。
- **Inevitable discovery:** Evidence may be admitted if the government establishes it would inevitably have been discovered lawfully under the applicable test.
  **必然發現原則：** 如果政府依適用標準證明證據必然會透過合法方式被發現，可能仍可採納。
- **Attenuation:** A sufficiently attenuated connection between unlawful conduct and evidence may affect exclusion.
  **因果關係減弱原則：** 非法行為與證據之間的因果關係若已充分減弱，可能影響是否排除證據。

## 6. Do not assume every violation excludes everything
## 6. 不要假設每一項違法都會排除所有證據

A court may exclude one statement or item while allowing other evidence. A suppression ruling does not automatically end the prosecution.
法院可能排除某一項陳述或物品，同時允許其他證據使用。排除證據的裁決不會自動終止刑事追訴。

## Mini IRAC
## 迷你 IRAC 分析

**Facts:** Police arrest a person and seize a phone. Without a warrant or a recognized exception, an officer opens the phone and searches old messages.
**事實：** 警方逮捕某人並扣押其手機。警員沒有令狀，也沒有適用法律承認的例外，就打開手機搜尋舊訊息。

**Issue:** Does the phone search raise a Fourth Amendment issue?
**爭點：** 搜查手機是否引發第四修正案問題？

**Rule:** Under *Riley*, police generally need a warrant to search digital contents of a phone seized incident to arrest, subject to exceptions.
**規則：** 依 *Riley* 案，警方通常需要令狀才能搜查因逮捕而扣押的手機數位內容，但仍須考慮例外。

**Application:** The facts indicate a search of digital contents without a warrant. The court must examine whether a recognized exception applies.
**適用：** 已知事實顯示警方在沒有令狀的情況下搜查數位內容。法院必須檢查是否適用法律承認的例外。

**Conclusion:** The search raises a substantial Fourth Amendment question; the remedy depends on the complete facts and applicable doctrine.
**結論：** 這次搜查引發重要的第四修正案問題；救濟方式取決於完整事實及適用法律原則。
