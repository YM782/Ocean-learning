# Plea Bargaining and Guilty Pleas
# 認罪協商及認罪答辯

## 1. What is plea bargaining?
## 1. 什麼是認罪協商？

Plea bargaining is a negotiated resolution in which the defendant agrees to plead guilty or no contest under specified terms, and the government makes an agreed charging or sentencing position.
認罪協商是一種透過協商解決案件的方式。被告同意依特定條件認罪或作出不抗辯答辯，而政府則同意採取約定的起訴或量刑立場。

A plea agreement may involve dismissal of some charges, a recommendation about a sentence, or an agreed sentencing disposition, depending on the type of agreement and the court's role.
認罪協議可能涉及撤銷部分指控、提出量刑建議，或約定量刑結果，具體取決於協議類型及法院的角色。

## 2. Why do cases resolve by plea?
## 2. 為什麼案件會透過認罪解決？

A plea may reduce uncertainty, avoid the time and expense of trial, and provide a defined resolution. The government may avoid trial risks, and the defendant may receive concessions under the agreement.
認罪可能減少不確定性、避免審判的時間及成本，並提供明確的案件結果。政府可以避免審判風險，而被告可能依協議取得某些讓步。

But a plea also involves giving up important trial rights. The defendant must understand the charge, the consequences, and the rights being waived.
但認罪也涉及放棄重要的審判權利。被告必須理解指控、法律後果及自己放棄的權利。

## 3. Rule 11 and the court's role
## 3. 第11條規則及法院的角色

Under Federal Rule of Criminal Procedure 11, the court must address the defendant personally in open court before accepting a guilty or nolo contendere plea and ensure that the plea is entered under the required conditions.
依《聯邦刑事訴訟規則》第11條，法院在接受認罪或不抗辯答辯之前，必須在公開法庭親自詢問被告，並確保答辯符合規定條件。

The court must explain and determine that the defendant understands important rights, including the right to a jury trial, counsel, confrontation, and protection against compelled self-incrimination.
法院必須解釋並確認被告理解重要權利，包括陪審團審判權、律師協助權、對質權及免受強迫自證其罪的保障。

The court must also determine that there is a factual basis for the plea.
法院還必須確認認罪具有事實基礎。

## 4. Three common Rule 11 agreement types
## 4. 第11條規則下三種常見協議類型

- **Rule 11(c)(1)(A):** The government agrees to dismiss or not bring other charges.
  **第11(c)(1)(A)條：** 政府同意撤銷或不提出其他指控。
- **Rule 11(c)(1)(B):** The government recommends or does not oppose a sentencing position; the recommendation does not bind the court.
  **第11(c)(1)(B)條：** 政府提出量刑建議或不反對被告提出的量刑立場；該建議不會拘束法院。
- **Rule 11(c)(1)(C):** The parties agree that a specific sentence or sentencing range is the appropriate disposition; if the court accepts the agreement, it is binding as provided by the rule.
  **第11(c)(1)(C)條：** 雙方同意特定刑罰或量刑範圍是適當結果；法院接受協議後，依規則產生拘束力。

## 5. The judge does not negotiate the plea
## 5. 法官不參與認罪協商

Rule 11 generally prohibits the court from participating in plea-agreement discussions. The judge's role is to assess the plea under the rule, not to bargain as one of the parties.
第11條規則通常禁止法院參與認罪協議的協商。法官的職責是依規則評估認罪，而不是像訴訟當事人一樣參與談判。

## 6. Voluntariness and consequences
## 6. 自願性及法律後果

A guilty plea must satisfy constitutional and procedural requirements. A plea may be challenged in limited circumstances, but withdrawing a plea after sentencing is difficult and usually requires direct appeal or collateral review.
認罪必須符合憲法及程序要求。在有限情況下，認罪可能受到挑戰；但量刑後要撤回認罪相當困難，通常需要透過直接上訴或附帶審查處理。

Immigration consequences can be especially serious for noncitizens. *Padilla v. Kentucky* (2010) held that defense counsel has duties concerning advice about clear immigration consequences of a plea under the applicable standard.
對非公民而言，移民後果可能特別嚴重。2010年的 *Padilla v. Kentucky* 案裁定，辯護律師依適用標準，對認罪可能產生的明確移民後果負有告知義務。

## 7. Plea bargaining and effective counsel
## 7. 認罪協商與有效律師協助

In *Missouri v. Frye* (2012) and *Lafler v. Cooper* (2012), the Supreme Court addressed ineffective assistance in the plea-bargaining process.
在2012年的 *Missouri v. Frye* 及 *Lafler v. Cooper* 案中，最高法院處理了認罪協商過程中的無效律師協助問題。

These cases show that the Sixth Amendment right to effective counsel can matter before trial, including during plea negotiations.
這些案件說明，第六修正案的有效律師協助權不只在審判期間重要，在審判前的認罪協商過程中也可能發揮作用。

## 8. Do not assume a plea is automatically fair or unfair
## 8. 不要假設認罪必然公平或不公平

The legal analysis depends on the defendant's understanding, voluntariness, counsel's performance, the agreement's terms, the court's inquiry, and the record.
法律分析取決於被告的理解程度、自願性、律師表現、協議條款、法院詢問及相關紀錄。
