# Primary Sources and Case Law
# 第一手資料及重要判例

## Source hierarchy
## 資料來源層級

For legal research, start with the Constitution, statutes, court rules, and judicial opinions. Government manuals and court education pages can explain procedures, but they do not all have the same legal force.
進行法律研究時，應從憲法、制定法、法院規則及司法判決開始。政府手冊及法院教育頁面可以解釋程序，但它們並不具有相同的法律效力。

A Department of Justice policy manual is not the same as a statute or a court rule. It may describe internal prosecutorial policy, but it does not automatically create an enforceable right for a defendant.
司法部政策手冊不等於制定法或法院規則。它可能描述檢察官內部政策，但不會自動為被告創設可強制執行的權利。

## How to verify a case
## 如何核實判例

1. Search the exact case name and reporter citation.
   搜尋確切案件名稱及判例集引證。
2. Confirm the court and year.
   確認法院及年份。
3. Read the majority opinion and identify the holding.
   閱讀多數意見並找出裁判要旨。
4. Check concurring and dissenting opinions when relevant.
   在相關情況下檢查協同意見及不同意見。
5. Search later cases to determine whether the rule was limited, distinguished, or overruled.
   搜尋後續判例，確認該規則是否受到限制、區別或推翻。
6. Record the jurisdiction and the date you checked the law.
   記錄司法管轄區及核實法律的日期。

## Translation policy
## 翻譯原則

The English legal terms are retained where useful. Traditional Chinese translations are learning translations, not official government translations.
在有幫助的情況下保留英文法律術語。繁體中文翻譯僅供學習使用，並非政府官方譯文。
