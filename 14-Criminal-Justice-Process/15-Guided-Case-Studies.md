# Guided Case Studies in Criminal Procedure
# 刑事程序引導式判例分析

These are concise learning summaries. Always read the full judicial opinion before relying on a case in legal research.
以下是簡明的學習摘要。進行法律研究時，依賴任何判例前都應閱讀完整的法院判決意見。

## 1. Terry v. Ohio, 392 U.S. 1 (1968)
## 1. Terry v. Ohio（1968年）

**Facts:** An officer observed conduct that led him to suspect that people were preparing to commit a robbery. He stopped and frisked them.
**事實：** 警員觀察到某些行為，因此懷疑有人正在準備搶劫。他攔查並拍身搜查相關人士。

**Issue:** When may an officer briefly stop and frisk a person without a full arrest warrant?
**爭點：** 警員在什麼情況下可以不依正式逮捕程序，短暫攔查及拍身搜查某人？

**Holding:** A brief stop may be justified by reasonable suspicion; a frisk requires a reasonable basis to believe the person is armed and dangerous.
**裁判要旨：** 合理懷疑可能支持短暫攔查；拍身搜查則需要合理理由相信當事人攜帶武器且具有危險性。

**Lesson:** Reasonable suspicion and probable cause are different standards.
**學習重點：** 合理懷疑與合理根據是不同的法律標準。

## 2. Mapp v. Ohio, 367 U.S. 643 (1961)
## 2. Mapp v. Ohio（1961年）

**Issue:** Does the exclusionary rule apply to state criminal proceedings?
**爭點：** 排除證據規則是否適用於州刑事程序？

**Holding:** The exclusionary rule applies to the states through the Fourteenth Amendment.
**裁判要旨：** 排除證據規則透過第十四修正案適用於各州。

**Lesson:** An unconstitutional search may affect whether evidence can be used at trial, subject to exceptions.
**學習重點：** 違憲搜查可能影響證據能否在審判中使用，但仍須考慮例外。

## 3. Miranda v. Arizona, 384 U.S. 436 (1966)
## 3. Miranda v. Arizona（1966年）

**Holding:** Before custodial interrogation, police generally must provide warnings protecting the privilege against compelled self-incrimination.
**裁判要旨：** 在羈押狀態下訊問前，警方通常必須作出保障不得被迫自證其罪特權的權利告知。

**Lesson:** Miranda is not triggered by every police conversation; custody and interrogation matter.
**學習重點：** Miranda 不會因每次警方交談而觸發；羈押狀態及訊問都很重要。

## 4. Gideon v. Wainwright, 372 U.S. 335 (1963)
## 4. Gideon v. Wainwright（1963年）

**Holding:** The Sixth Amendment requires states to provide counsel to indigent defendants in serious criminal cases where the right applies.
**裁判要旨：** 在權利適用的嚴重刑事案件中，第六修正案要求各州為無力聘請律師的被告提供律師。

**Lesson:** Legal representation is a core protection in covered criminal prosecutions.
**學習重點：** 在受保障的刑事追訴中，法律代表是核心保障。

## 5. Brady v. Maryland, 373 U.S. 83 (1963)
## 5. Brady v. Maryland（1963年）

**Holding:** Suppression of material favorable evidence violates due process.
**裁判要旨：** 隱瞞重要的有利證據會違反正當法律程序。

**Lesson:** The prosecution's duty includes material evidence favorable to the accused.
**學習重點：** 控方的義務包括對被告有利的重要證據。

## 6. Giglio v. United States, 405 U.S. 150 (1972)
## 6. Giglio v. United States（1972年）

**Holding:** Material impeachment evidence concerning a government witness may fall within the constitutional disclosure duty.
**裁判要旨：** 涉及政府證人的重要彈劾證據，可能屬於憲法上的披露義務範圍。

**Lesson:** Witness credibility can be legally important.
**學習重點：** 證人的可信度可能具有重要法律意義。

## 7. Strickland v. Washington, 466 U.S. 668 (1984)
## 7. Strickland v. Washington（1984年）

**Holding:** A defendant generally must show deficient counsel performance and prejudice to establish ineffective assistance.
**裁判要旨：** 被告通常必須證明律師表現存在缺陷，並且因此受到損害，才能成立無效律師協助主張。

**Lesson:** A poor outcome alone is not enough.
**學習重點：** 結果不好本身並不足夠。

## 8. Apprendi v. New Jersey, 530 U.S. 466 (2000)
## 8. Apprendi v. New Jersey（2000年）

**Holding:** Other than the fact of a prior conviction, a fact that increases the penalty beyond the prescribed statutory maximum generally must be submitted to a jury and proved beyond a reasonable doubt.
**裁判要旨：** 除先前定罪事實外，任何使刑罰超過法定最高刑的事實，通常必須提交陪審團並排除合理懷疑地證明。

**Lesson:** Some facts affecting punishment are subject to jury-trial and proof protections.
**學習重點：** 某些影響刑罰的事實也受到陪審團審判及證明標準的保障。

## 9. United States v. Booker, 543 U.S. 220 (2005)
## 9. United States v. Booker（2005年）

**Holding:** The federal Sentencing Guidelines became advisory rather than mandatory in the constitutional framework established by the Court.
**裁判要旨：** 在最高法院建立的憲法架構下，聯邦量刑指南改為建議性而非強制性。

**Lesson:** Judges must consider the Guidelines but also apply statutory sentencing factors.
**學習重點：** 法官必須考慮量刑指南，同時適用法定量刑因素。

## 10. Missouri v. Frye, 566 U.S. 134 (2012)
## 10. Missouri v. Frye（2012年）

**Holding:** Defense counsel's duty of effective assistance can apply to communicating formal plea offers.
**裁判要旨：** 辯護律師的有效協助義務可能適用於正式認罪提議的告知。

**Lesson:** Plea negotiations are an important stage of criminal representation.
**學習重點：** 認罪協商是刑事辯護的重要階段。

## 11. Lafler v. Cooper, 566 U.S. 156 (2012)
## 11. Lafler v. Cooper（2012年）

**Holding:** The Court addressed prejudice where deficient advice caused a defendant to reject a plea offer and proceed to trial.
**裁判要旨：** 最高法院處理了律師不當建議導致被告拒絕認罪提議並進入審判時，如何判斷損害的問題。

**Lesson:** The consequences of ineffective advice may arise before trial.
**學習重點：** 無效法律建議造成的後果可能在審判前就已經出現。

## 12. Ramos v. Louisiana, 590 U.S. 83 (2020)
## 12. Ramos v. Louisiana（2020年）

**Holding:** The Sixth Amendment requires unanimous jury verdicts to convict in serious criminal cases in state and federal courts.
**裁判要旨：** 第六修正案要求州及聯邦法院在嚴重刑事案件中，陪審團必須一致同意才能定罪。

**Lesson:** Jury verdict rules are constitutional protections, not merely courtroom customs.
**學習重點：** 陪審團裁決規則是憲法保障，而不只是法庭慣例。

   事實。
2. Procedural history.

3. Issue.
   爭點。

   法律規則。
5. Holding.
   裁判要旨。
6. Reasoning.
   判決推理。
7. Limits of the decision.
   判決的適用限制。
8. Why the case matters in a real criminal case.
   該判例在真實刑事案件中的重要性。
