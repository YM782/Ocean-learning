# Wrongful Convictions and Legal Remedies
# 冤錯定罪及法律救濟

## 1. What is a wrongful conviction?
## 1. 什麼是冤錯定罪？

A wrongful conviction occurs when an innocent person is convicted, or when a conviction is legally unreliable or unjust under the governing law. The term can cover different factual and legal situations.
冤錯定罪是指無辜者被定罪，或某項定罪依適用法律而言缺乏可靠性或不公正。這個詞可能涵蓋不同的事實及法律情況。

A person may be factually innocent, or may have been convicted after a serious procedural or constitutional violation. These are related but not identical concepts.
一個人可能在事實上無罪，也可能在存在嚴重程序或憲法違反的情況下被定罪。兩者相關，但並不完全相同。

## 2. Common contributing factors
## 2. 常見成因

Research and case reviews have identified recurring problems that can contribute to wrongful convictions:
研究及案件檢討指出，以下反覆出現的問題可能造成冤錯定罪：

- Mistaken eyewitness identification.
  錯誤的目擊者辨認。
- False or unreliable confessions.
  虛假或不可靠的認罪陳述。
- Informant testimony affected by incentives.
  受到利益誘因影響的線人證言。
- Unreliable or overstated forensic evidence.
  不可靠或被過度解讀的鑑識證據。
- Suppression of material exculpatory or impeachment evidence.
  隱瞞重要的無罪方向證據或彈劾證據。
- Ineffective assistance of counsel.
  無效的律師協助。
- Official misconduct or investigative tunnel vision.
  公務人員不當行為或調查上的隧道視野。

No single factor proves that a particular conviction is wrongful. Each case requires evidence and legal analysis.
任何單一因素都不能直接證明某項定罪必然錯誤。每宗案件都需要證據及法律分析。

## 3. Brady violations
## 3. Brady 違反

If the prosecution suppresses favorable evidence that is material to guilt or punishment, the defendant may raise a due-process claim under *Brady v. Maryland*.
如果控方隱瞞對被告有利、而且對有罪與否或刑罰具有重要性的證據，被告可能依 *Brady v. Maryland* 提出正當法律程序主張。

The court examines materiality and the effect of the suppression under the governing legal test. A violation does not automatically establish factual innocence, but it may undermine the fairness or reliability of the conviction.
法院會依適用法律測試，檢查證據的重要性及隱瞞行為造成的影響。違反披露義務不會自動證明當事人在事實上無罪，但可能削弱定罪的公平性或可靠性。

## 4. Ineffective assistance of counsel
## 4. 無效律師協助

Under *Strickland v. Washington* (1984), a defendant generally must show that counsel's performance was deficient and that the deficient performance caused prejudice under the applicable standard.
依1984年的 *Strickland v. Washington* 案，被告通常必須證明律師表現存在缺陷，而且該缺陷依適用標準造成了損害。

A disappointing result alone does not prove ineffective assistance. Courts examine counsel's conduct and the effect on the proceeding.
結果令人失望本身並不能證明律師協助無效。法院會檢查律師的行為及其對程序造成的影響。

## 5. New evidence and DNA testing
## 5. 新證據及 DNA 檢測

Newly discovered evidence, including reliable DNA evidence in an appropriate case, may support a motion for a new trial or a post-conviction claim. The availability of testing and relief depends on statutes, procedural rules, preservation of evidence, and the facts.
新發現的證據，包括在適當案件中可靠的 DNA 證據，可能支持新審判動議或定罪後主張。能否進行檢測及取得救濟，取決於制定法、程序規則、證據保存情況及案件事實。

## 6. Possible remedies
## 6. 可能的救濟

Depending on the case, remedies may include:
依案件情況，可能的救濟包括：

- Suppression of unlawfully obtained evidence before trial.
  在審判前排除非法取得的證據。
- Dismissal of charges when the legal requirements are met.
  在符合法律要求時撤銷指控。
- A new trial.
  重新審判。
- Reversal of a conviction on direct appeal.
  在直接上訴中撤銷定罪。
- Relief through a properly filed habeas petition.
  透過依法提出的人身保護令申請取得救濟。
- Executive clemency or pardon, where available.
  在可用情況下取得行政寬赦或赦免。
- Compensation under a federal or state compensation statute, if the person satisfies its requirements.
  如果符合聯邦或州賠償制定法的要求，可能取得相應賠償。

## 7. Why media attention is not a legal remedy by itself
## 7. 為什麼媒體關注本身不是法律救濟？

Media coverage may bring attention to a case, help locate witnesses, or encourage review. But a newspaper article or public campaign does not itself vacate a conviction or compel a court to grant relief.
媒體報導可能讓案件受到關注、協助尋找證人，或促使有關方面重新檢查案件。但新聞文章或公眾運動本身不會撤銷定罪，也不會強迫法院批准救濟。

The legal remedy must be pursued through the correct court, statute, procedure, and deadline.
法律救濟必須透過正確的法院、制定法、程序及期限提出。

## 8. A careful way to evaluate a possible wrongful conviction
## 8. 評估可能冤錯定罪的謹慎方法

1. Obtain the judgment and complete procedural history.
   取得判決及完整程序經過。
2. Identify the exact conviction and statutory elements.
   確認具體定罪罪名及法定構成要件。
3. Review the trial record and evidence.
   檢查審判紀錄及證據。
4. Identify any undisclosed, unreliable, or newly discovered evidence.
   找出任何未披露、不可靠或新發現的證據。
5. Identify the legal claim and the proper procedural route.
   確認法律主張及適當程序途徑。
6. Check all deadlines and exhaustion rules.
   核實所有期限及救濟用盡規則。
7. Consult a qualified lawyer in the relevant jurisdiction.
   諮詢相關司法管轄區的合資格律師。
