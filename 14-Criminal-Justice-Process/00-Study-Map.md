# Six-Week Study Map
# 六週學習地圖

## Week 1 — The system and its actors
## 第一週——司法制度及參與者

Read the process map and the roles chapter. Learn the difference between federal and state jurisdiction, and memorize the basic roles of police, prosecutors, defense counsel, judges, grand juries, and trial juries.
閱讀刑事流程圖及司法角色章節。理解聯邦與州的司法管轄權差異，並掌握警察、檢察官、辯護律師、法官、大陪審團及審判陪審團的基本職責。

**Checkpoint:** Explain why the police do not decide whether a person is legally guilty.
**檢查點：** 解釋為什麼警方不能自行決定一個人在法律上是否有罪。

## Week 2 — Investigation, stops, arrest, and searches
## 第二週——調查、攔查、逮捕及搜查

Study reasonable suspicion, probable cause, warrants, Miranda custody, and the major exceptions to the warrant requirement. Pay special attention to the difference between a voluntary conversation, a Terry stop, and an arrest.
學習合理懷疑、合理根據、搜查令、Miranda 羈押狀態，以及令狀要求的主要例外。特別注意自願交談、Terry 攔查及正式逮捕之間的差異。

**Checkpoint:** Given a short scenario, identify whether the facts suggest a voluntary encounter, a stop, or an arrest.
**檢查點：** 根據簡短情境，判斷事實較可能構成自願接觸、攔查還是逮捕。

## Week 3 — First court appearances, charging, and pretrial
## 第三週——首次出庭、起訴及審前程序

Study booking, initial appearance, release or detention, complaint, information, indictment, grand jury, preliminary hearing, arraignment, and pretrial motions.
學習登記程序、首次出庭、釋放或羈押、刑事申訴書、正式控告書、起訴書、大陪審團、初步聽證、正式提訊及審前動議。

**Checkpoint:** Explain why an indictment is not proof of guilt and why an initial appearance is not the same as arraignment.
**檢查點：** 解釋為什麼起訴書不是有罪證明，以及為什麼首次出庭不等於正式提訊。

## Week 4 — Discovery and plea bargaining
## 第四週——證據開示及認罪協商

Study Rule 16, Brady, Giglio, Jencks Act, and the basic structure of federal plea agreements under Rule 11.
學習第16條規則、Brady、Giglio、《Jencks 法》及第11條規則下聯邦認罪協議的基本架構。

**Checkpoint:** Explain the difference between ordinary discovery, exculpatory evidence, and impeachment evidence.
**檢查點：** 解釋一般證據開示、無罪方向證據及彈劾證據之間的差異。

## Week 5 — Trial and sentencing
## 第五週——審判及量刑

Study jury selection, prosecution evidence, cross-examination, defense evidence, jury instructions, verdict, presentence reports, sentencing guidelines, and the judge's sentencing decision.
學習陪審團遴選、控方證據、交叉詢問、辯方證據、陪審團指示、裁決、量刑前報告、量刑指南及法官的量刑決定。

**Checkpoint:** Explain who bears the burden of proof and what “beyond a reasonable doubt” means.
**檢查點：** 解釋誰承擔舉證責任，以及「排除合理懷疑」代表什麼。

## Week 6 — Appeals, post-conviction relief, and wrongful convictions
## 第六週——上訴、定罪後救濟及冤錯定罪

Study direct appeal, preservation of objections, standards of review, habeas corpus, 28 U.S.C. §§ 2254 and 2255, and common causes of wrongful convictions.
學習直接上訴、異議保留、審查標準、人身保護令、28 U.S.C. §§ 2254 及 2255，以及冤錯定罪的常見原因。

**Checkpoint:** Explain why an appeal is usually not a new trial and why newly discovered evidence does not automatically overturn a conviction.
**檢查點：** 解釋為什麼上訴通常不是重新審判，以及為什麼新發現的證據不會自動推翻定罪。

## Weekly routine
## 每週學習習慣

- Day 1–2: Read the English and Chinese explanations.
  第1至2天：閱讀英文及中文解釋。
- Day 3: Review the legal vocabulary.
  第3天：複習法律詞彙。
- Day 4: Read the cited case or rule excerpt.
  第4天：閱讀引用的判例或規則節錄。
- Day 5: Write a short IRAC answer.
  第5天：撰寫簡短 IRAC 答案。
- Day 6: Complete the practice questions.
  第6天：完成練習題。
- Day 7: Review errors and explain the topic aloud in simple English.
  第7天：複習錯題，並嘗試用簡單英文口頭解釋主題。
