# Primary Sources for U.S. Constitutional Law
# 美國憲法學第一手資料來源

Use primary legal sources whenever possible. A textbook, news article, or online explainer can help you understand a subject, but it does not replace the Constitution, a statute, or a controlling judicial opinion.
只要可以，請優先使用第一手法律資料。教科書、新聞文章或網路解說可以協助理解主題，但不能取代憲法、制定法或具有拘束力的司法判決。

## 6. How to verify a constitutional rule
## 6. 如何核實憲法規則

1. Identify the exact constitutional provision.
   找出確切的憲法條文。
2. Identify whether the government is federal, state, or local.
   確認涉及聯邦、州或地方政府。
3. Find the Supreme Court case that explains the rule.
   找出解釋該規則的最高法院判例。
4. Read the holding, not only a headline or case summary.
   閱讀裁判要旨，不要只看新聞標題或判例摘要。
5. Check later cases to see whether the rule was limited, distinguished, or overruled.
   檢查後續判例，確認該規則是否受到限制、區別或推翻。
6. Record the date and jurisdiction.
   記錄查閱日期及司法管轄區。

## 7. Source and translation policy
## 7. 資料及翻譯原則

The English constitutional text is reproduced from public government sources. The Traditional Chinese translations in this course are learning translations prepared for comprehension and are not official government translations.
本課程的英文憲法文本取自政府公開資料。本課程的繁體中文翻譯是為了協助理解而編寫的學習譯文，並非美國政府官方譯文。

Legal rules can change through amendments and judicial decisions. Always verify the current law before relying on it.
法律規則可能因修憲及司法判決而改變。依賴法律規則前，務必核實現行法律。

**Educational use only; not legal advice.**
**僅供教育學習，不構成法律意見。**
