# Official Starting Resources for Beginners
# 初學者官方法律資源

Start with official government and court sources whenever possible.
只要可以，請優先使用政府及法院官方資料。

Educational websites explain legal ideas, but they do not replace the controlling law.
教育網站可以解釋法律概念，但不能取代具有控制效力的法律。

## 1. U.S. Courts — Educational Resources
## 1. 美國聯邦法院——教育資源

https://www.uscourts.gov/about-federal-courts/educational-resources

Learn about federal courts, judges, juries, and the court system.
了解聯邦法院、法官、陪審團及法院制度。

## 2. Congress — Constitution Annotated
## 2. 美國國會——憲法注釋

https://constitution.congress.gov/

Read explanations of constitutional text and important judicial interpretations.
閱讀憲法文本及重要司法解釋的說明。

## 3. United States Code
## 3. 美國法典

https://uscode.house.gov/

Find federal statutes and check the relevant title and section.
查找聯邦制定法，並核對相關編章及條文。

## 4. Electronic Code of Federal Regulations
## 4. 電子版《聯邦法規彙編》

https://www.ecfr.gov/current

Read the current text of federal regulations and check update information.
閱讀聯邦行政規章的現行文本，並核對更新資訊。

## 5. Supreme Court of the United States
## 5. 美國聯邦最高法院

https://www.supremecourt.gov/

Find official information and published Supreme Court opinions.
查找最高法院官方資訊及已公布的判決意見。

## 6. Law School Admission Council
## 6. 法學院入學委員會

https://www.lsac.org/applying-law-school

Learn about JD applications and law-school admission steps.
了解 JD 申請及法學院入學流程。

## 7. American Bar Association — Legal Education
## 7. 美國律師協會——法律教育

https://www.americanbar.org/groups/legal_education/

Learn about legal education and accreditation information.
了解法律教育及認證資訊。

Bar eligibility is determined by the relevant jurisdiction, not by the ABA alone.
律師考試資格由相關司法管轄區決定，並非僅由 ABA 決定。

## 8. National Conference of Bar Examiners
## 8. 全國律師考試委員會

https://www.ncbex.org/jurisdictions

Find the official bar-admission authority for a particular state or territory.
查找特定州或地區的官方律師准入機關。

## 9. Cornell Legal Information Institute — Wex
## 9. 康乃爾大學法律資訊研究所——Wex

https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex

Use this free legal encyclopedia and dictionary to understand unfamiliar terms.
使用這個免費法律百科全書及字典理解陌生術語。

Wex is a secondary educational source, not an official court or statute database.
Wex 是教育性第二手資料，不是官方法院或制定法資料庫。

## Source rule
## 資料來源原則

Use explanations to understand a topic, then verify important legal propositions in the Constitution, statutes, regulations, court rules, or judicial opinions.
先利用解說理解主題，再到憲法、制定法、行政規章、法院規則或司法判決中核實重要法律命題。

Always record the jurisdiction and the date on which you checked a legal rule.
務必記錄法律規則所屬的司法管轄區，以及核實該規則的日期。
