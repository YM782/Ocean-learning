# Contracts
# 契約法

## What this course is about
## 本課程內容

Begin after you understand civil disputes and basic legal duties.
先理解民事爭議及基本法律義務，再開始本課。

This is an introductory course guide, not a complete textbook or legal opinion.
本頁是入門課程指南，並非完整教科書或法律意見。

## Start with three ideas
## 從三個概念開始

### 1. Formation
### 1. 契約成立

Learn offer, acceptance, and consideration.
學習要約、承諾及對價。

### 2. Performance and breach
### 2. 履行與違約

Understand promises, duties, conditions, and failure to perform.
理解承諾、義務、條件及未履行的情況。

### 3. Remedies
### 3. 救濟

Learn damages and other possible remedies through basic examples.
透過基本例子學習損害賠償及其他可能的救濟。


## Beginner practice
## 初學者練習

Use a simple purchase example to identify an offer, acceptance, and possible breach.
使用簡單的購買例子，辨識要約、承諾及可能的違約。

## How to continue
## 如何繼續學習

Study one topic at a time and write down unfamiliar terms.
一次只學習一個主題，並記錄不熟悉的術語。

For every case, identify the court, jurisdiction, date, issue, rule, holding, and reasoning.
閱讀每一宗判例時，辨識法院、司法管轄區、日期、爭點、規則、裁判要旨及推理。

Always verify the law that applies to the relevant jurisdiction and date.
務必核實相關司法管轄區及日期所適用的法律。

**僅供教育學習，不構成法律意見。**
