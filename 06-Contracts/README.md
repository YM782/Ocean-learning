**Level: beginner | Focus: formation, defenses, breach, and remedies**
**程度：初學者｜重點：契約成立、抗辯、違約及救濟**

Contract law studies legally enforceable agreements. U.S. contract law is shaped by state common law, statutes, and the Uniform Commercial Code for many sales of goods.
契約法研究具有法律強制執行力的協議。美國契約法受到各州普通法、制定法，以及規範許多貨物買賣的《統一商法典》影響。
