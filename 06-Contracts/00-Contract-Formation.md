# Contract Formation
# 契約成立

## 1. What is a contract?
## 1. 什麼是契約？

A contract is an agreement that the law recognizes as enforceable. Not every promise is a contract; the law requires particular elements and may impose formal requirements.
契約是法律承認具有強制執行力的協議。並非每一項承諾都是契約；法律要求特定構成要件，也可能要求形式要件。

The basic common-law model often asks whether there was mutual assent, consideration, capacity, and a lawful purpose. The precise test depends on the jurisdiction and type of agreement.
普通法的基本模式通常會檢查是否存在雙方合意、對價、締約能力及合法目的。具體測試取決於司法管轄區及協議類型。

## 2. Offer
## 2. 要約

An offer is a communication showing a present willingness to enter a bargain on sufficiently definite terms, so that another person's acceptance can create an agreement.
要約是一種溝通，表明提出者目前願意依足夠明確的條款進行交易，使另一方接受後可以成立協議。

Advertisements, price lists, and preliminary negotiations are often invitations to negotiate rather than offers, although the wording and context control.
廣告、價目表及初步談判通常是邀請協商，而不是要約，但仍須依文字及上下文判斷。

## 3. Acceptance
## 3. 承諾

Acceptance is an objective expression of agreement to the offer's terms. The required method and timing may depend on the offer and governing law.
承諾是以客觀方式表示同意要約條款。所要求的方式及時間可能取決於要約內容及適用法律。

Under the common-law mirror-image approach, an acceptance generally must match the offer; a material change may be a counteroffer. The UCC has different rules for many sales-of-goods contracts.
依普通法的鏡像原則，承諾通常必須與要約一致；重大修改可能構成反要約。對於許多貨物買賣契約，《統一商法典》採取不同規則。

## 4. Consideration
## 4. 對價

Consideration is a bargained-for exchange. Each side gives or promises something the law recognizes as value, such as money, goods, services, or a promise to refrain from doing something.
對價是經協商形成的交換。雙方各自提供或承諾提供法律承認具有價值的事物，例如金錢、貨物、服務，或承諾不做某件事。

A promise to do something one is already legally required to do may not be new consideration. A past act, by itself, generally is not consideration for a later promise.
承諾履行自己原本就有法律義務履行的事情，可能不構成新的對價。單純過去已完成的行為，通常不能成為日後承諾的對價。

## 5. Objective theory of assent
## 5. 合意的客觀理論

Courts generally evaluate outward words and conduct rather than a person's unexpressed private thoughts. The question is how a reasonable person would understand the parties' objective manifestations in context.
法院通常評估外在言語及行為，而不是某人未表達的內心想法。問題在於，合理的人在上下文中會如何理解當事人客觀表達的意思。

## 6. UCC Article 2 and sales of goods
## 6. 《統一商法典》第2篇及貨物買賣

Article 2 of the Uniform Commercial Code governs many transactions in goods. Goods generally means movable, tangible items identified to the contract.
《統一商法典》第2篇規範許多貨物交易。貨物通常指已被確定為契約標的、可移動且有形的物品。

The UCC may relax some common-law formation rules, including rules about definite terms and acceptance. The applicable state's enacted version must be checked.
《統一商法典》可能放寬部分普通法契約成立規則，包括條款明確性及承諾規則。必須查閱適用州實際制定的版本。

## 7. Electronic contracts
## 7. 電子契約

Electronic agreements can be enforceable. The analysis may consider notice, assent, the design of the interface, the user's actions, and applicable electronic-transactions statutes.
電子協議可能具有法律強制執行力。分析時可能考慮通知、同意、介面設計、使用者行為及適用的電子交易制定法。

A click alone does not answer every question. The court may examine whether the terms were reasonably presented and whether the user objectively manifested assent.
單純點擊本身並不能回答所有問題。法院可能檢查條款是否以合理方式呈現，以及使用者是否客觀表示同意。

## Check your understanding
## 理解測驗

1. Is every promise legally enforceable?
   每一項承諾都可以依法強制執行嗎？
2. What is consideration?
   什麼是對價？
3. Does Article 2 govern every type of contract?
   《統一商法典》第2篇是否規範所有類型的契約？

**Answers:** 1. No. 2. A bargained-for exchange. 3. No; it governs many transactions in goods.
**答案：** 1. 不是。2. 經協商形成的交換。3. 不是；它規範許多貨物交易。
