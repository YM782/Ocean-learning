# Contract Defenses and Interpretation
# 契約抗辯及解釋

## 1. Capacity
## 1. 締約能力

A person must have the legal capacity required by the governing law to enter an enforceable agreement. Rules involving minors, mental incapacity, and intoxication vary and may allow a contract to be voidable rather than automatically void.
一個人必須具備適用法律所要求的締約能力，才能訂立可強制執行的協議。涉及未成年人、精神能力不足及醉酒的規則可能不同，並可能使契約得撤銷，而不是自動無效。

## 2. Duress and undue influence
## 2. 脅迫及不當影響

Duress may exist when improper threats leave a party with no reasonable alternative and induce assent under the applicable test.
如果不當威脅使一方沒有合理替代選擇，並依適用測試促使其同意，可能構成脅迫。

Undue influence may arise when unfair persuasion overcomes a person's free judgment in a relationship involving special trust or vulnerability.
如果在具有特殊信任或脆弱性的關係中，不公平的勸誘壓倒某人的自由判斷，可能構成不當影響。

## 3. Misrepresentation and fraud
## 3. 不實陳述及詐欺

A material misrepresentation may affect whether an agreement is enforceable. Fraud generally requires additional elements such as a false statement, the required state of mind, reliance, and legally recognized harm.
重大不實陳述可能影響協議是否具有強制執行力。詐欺通常還要求其他要件，例如虛假陳述、必要主觀狀態、信賴及法律承認的損害。

The elements and available remedies vary by jurisdiction and claim.
構成要件及可用救濟因司法管轄區及請求類型而異。

## 4. Mistake
## 4. 錯誤

A mutual mistake may justify avoidance when both parties shared a basic mistaken assumption that materially affects the exchange, subject to the applicable rules.
如果雙方共同基於一項重大錯誤的基本假設訂立契約，並且該錯誤實質影響交換內容，在符合適用規則時，可能構成撤銷契約的理由。

A unilateral mistake is more difficult and usually requires additional circumstances.
單方錯誤較難成立，通常需要其他特殊情況。

## 5. Statute of Frauds
## 5. 防止詐欺法

The Statute of Frauds requires certain categories of agreements to be evidenced by a sufficient writing or electronic record. Common categories may include certain land transactions and agreements that cannot be performed within one year, but the exact statute varies.
防止詐欺法要求某些類型的協議必須有足夠的書面或電子紀錄作為證明。常見類別可能包括某些土地交易，以及無法在一年內履行的協議，但具體制定法可能不同。

The rule does not mean that every contract must be written. It applies only to specified categories and contains exceptions.
這項規則並不代表每份契約都必須採用書面形式。它只適用於特定類別，並存在例外。

## 6. Interpreting contract terms
## 6. 契約條款解釋

Courts interpret contract language in context, considering the text, structure, and sometimes admissible evidence of the circumstances. Jurisdictions differ on when outside evidence may be considered.
法院會結合上下文解釋契約文字，考慮文本、結構，有時也會考慮可採納的背景證據。各司法管轄區對何時可以考慮外部證據存在差異。

The parol evidence rule generally limits the use of prior or contemporaneous agreements to contradict or supplement a final integrated writing, subject to exceptions and the governing jurisdiction's approach.
口頭證據規則通常限制使用先前或同時期的協議，來否定或補充一份最終整合的書面契約，但仍存在例外，而且各司法管轄區的處理方式可能不同。

## 7. Unconscionability and public policy
## 7. 顯失公平及公共政策

A court may refuse to enforce a term or agreement that is unconscionable under the applicable test. The analysis may consider unfair surprise, oppressive terms, bargaining power, and the circumstances of formation.
如果某項條款或協議依適用測試顯失公平，法院可能拒絕執行。分析可能考慮不公平突襲、壓迫性條款、議價能力及締約情況。

A contract may also be unenforceable when enforcement would violate a sufficiently strong public policy recognized by law.
如果執行契約會違反法律承認的重要公共政策，契約也可能無法強制執行。
