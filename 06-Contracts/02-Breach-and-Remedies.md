# Breach of Contract and Remedies
# 契約違約及法律救濟

## 1. What is breach?
## 1. 什麼是違約？

A breach occurs when a party fails to perform a contractual duty when performance is due, without a legally sufficient excuse.
當履行期限到來時，一方未履行契約義務，而且沒有充分法律理由時，便可能構成違約。

The plaintiff generally must establish the contract, the plaintiff's performance or excuse, the defendant's breach, and legally recognized damages or another basis for relief.
原告通常必須證明契約存在、原告已履行或有正當理由不履行、被告違約，以及法律承認的損害或其他救濟依據。

## 2. Material breach
## 2. 重大違約

A material breach is serious enough to affect the other party's central contractual expectations. Whether a breach is material depends on the circumstances and governing law.
重大違約嚴重到影響另一方的核心契約期待。違約是否重大取決於具體情況及適用法律。

A minor breach may permit damages without excusing the other party from all remaining performance.
輕微違約可能允許受害方請求賠償，但不一定使另一方免除所有剩餘履行義務。

## 3. Expectation damages
## 3. 期待利益賠償

Expectation damages generally aim to place the injured party in the position that party would have occupied if the contract had been performed.
期待利益賠償通常旨在使受害方處於契約已獲履行時本來可以處於的經濟位置。

The plaintiff must still satisfy rules on causation, foreseeability, reasonable certainty, and mitigation.
原告仍須符合因果關係、可預見性、合理確定性及減損義務等規則。

## 4. Reliance and restitution
## 4. 信賴利益及返還利益

Reliance damages may compensate reasonable expenses incurred because the party relied on the agreement.
信賴利益賠償可能補償當事人因信賴協議而合理支出的費用。

Restitution focuses on preventing unjust retention of a benefit. It may require a party to return a benefit received when the legal conditions are met.
返還利益著重於防止不當保留利益。在符合法律條件時，可能要求一方返還已取得的利益。

## 5. Specific performance and injunctions
## 5. 強制履行及禁制令

Specific performance is an equitable remedy ordering a party to perform the contract. It may be available when money damages are inadequate, such as in some disputes involving unique property.
強制履行是一種衡平法救濟，法院命令一方履行契約。如果金錢賠償不足以彌補損害，例如某些涉及獨特財產的爭議，可能適用強制履行。

Courts generally do not order specific performance of ordinary personal-service contracts. Injunctions may be available to prevent or require particular conduct under equitable standards.
法院通常不會命令強制履行一般個人服務契約。依衡平法標準，禁制令可能用來禁止或要求特定行為。

## 6. Liquidated damages and penalties
## 6. 約定損害賠償及懲罰性條款

A liquidated-damages clause sets an agreed amount or method for calculating damages if a breach occurs. Courts often examine whether the amount was a reasonable forecast of difficult-to-measure loss under the governing law.
約定損害賠償條款會預先設定違約時的賠償金額或計算方法。法院通常會依適用法律檢查該金額是否合理預估難以計算的損失。

A clause designed primarily to punish breach may be treated as an unenforceable penalty.
主要目的在於懲罰違約的條款，可能被視為不可執行的懲罰性條款。

## 7. UCC sales remedies
## 7. 《統一商法典》貨物買賣救濟

Article 2 contains remedies for buyers and sellers in sales-of-goods disputes. Examples include cover, market damages, rejection, revocation of acceptance, and seller remedies, each subject to specific requirements.
《統一商法典》第2篇為貨物買賣爭議中的買方及賣方提供救濟。例子包括替代購買、市場價差賠償、拒收、撤銷接受，以及賣方救濟，每一項都有具體要求。

## Check your understanding
## 理解測驗

1. What do expectation damages try to accomplish?
   期待利益賠償希望達到什麼效果？
2. What is the difference between reliance and restitution?
   信賴利益與返還利益有什麼不同？
3. Is every liquidated-damages clause enforceable?
   每一項約定損害賠償條款都可以執行嗎？

**Answers:** 1. Put the injured party in the position expected if performance occurred. 2. Reliance compensates reasonable reliance loss; restitution returns a benefit. 3. No; an unenforceable penalty may be rejected.
**答案：** 1. 使受害方處於契約獲履行時的預期位置。2. 信賴利益補償合理信賴損失；返還利益則返還已取得的利益。3. 不是；不可執行的懲罰性條款可能被法院拒絕。
