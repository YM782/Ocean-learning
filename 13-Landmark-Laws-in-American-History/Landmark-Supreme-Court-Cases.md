# Landmark Supreme Court Cases for Beginners
# 美國最高法院重要判例：零基礎入門

A statute is a law enacted by a legislature. A judicial opinion is a court's explanation of its decision. Both matter, but they are not the same kind of legal authority.
制定法是由立法機關通過的法律；司法判決意見是法院對其裁判的說明。兩者都很重要，但不是同一種類型的法律權威。

## 1. Marbury v. Madison (1803)
## 1. Marbury v. Madison（1803年）

**Topic: Judicial review.**
**主題：司法審查。**

William Marbury asked the Supreme Court to order Secretary of State James Madison to deliver his judicial commission.
William Marbury 請求最高法院命令國務卿 James Madison 交付他的法官任命證書。

The Court concluded that Marbury had a right and that a legal remedy could exist, but it could not issue the requested writ under its original jurisdiction because the relevant statutory provision conflicted with Article III of the Constitution.
法院認為 Marbury 享有權利，而且法律可能提供救濟；但由於相關制定法條文與憲法第三條相牴觸，最高法院不能依其原始管轄權核發所請求的命令。

**Why it matters:** The case established the Supreme Court's role in reviewing the constitutionality of federal legislation in a case properly before it.
**重要性：** 本案確立最高法院在依法受理的案件中，審查聯邦立法是否符合憲法的角色。

Primary source: https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/marbury-v-madison
第一手資料：美國國家檔案館 Marbury 判例。

## 2. McCulloch v. Maryland (1819)
## 2. McCulloch v. Maryland（1819年）

**Topic: Implied federal powers and federal supremacy.**
**主題：聯邦默示權力及聯邦優先效力。**

The case concerned Congress's authority to create a national bank and Maryland's attempt to tax that bank.
本案涉及國會設立國家銀行的權力，以及馬里蘭州對該銀行課稅的做法。

The Supreme Court recognized that Congress has implied powers under the Necessary and Proper Clause to carry out its enumerated powers, and that a state may not use its taxing power to obstruct a valid federal instrument.
最高法院承認，國會可以依必要與適當條款行使完成列舉權力所需的默示權力；州政府也不能利用課稅權阻礙有效的聯邦機關運作。

**Why it matters:** It explains why federal power is not limited to powers written as a detailed list of specific actions, while still requiring a constitutional basis.
**重要性：** 它解釋了為什麼聯邦權力不只限於逐項明文列出的具體行為，但仍然必須有憲法依據。

Primary source: https://constitution.congress.gov/
第一手資料：美國國會《憲法注釋》。

## 3. Dred Scott v. Sandford (1857)
## 3. Dred Scott v. Sandford（1857年）

**Topic: Citizenship, slavery, and constitutional failure.**
**主題：公民身分、奴隸制度及憲法制度的失敗。**

Dred Scott and his wife Harriet sued for freedom after living in places where slavery was prohibited. The Supreme Court's majority held that people of African descent whose ancestors had been brought to the United States and enslaved were not citizens within the meaning of the federal Constitution, and it also held that Congress lacked power to prohibit slavery in the federal territories at issue.
Dred Scott 與妻子 Harriet 曾在禁止奴隸制度的地區居住，之後提出自由請求。最高法院多數意見認為，祖先被帶到美國並遭奴役的非裔人士不屬於聯邦憲法所稱的公民；法院也認為國會無權禁止相關聯邦領地中的奴隸制度。

The decision was profoundly unjust and intensified the national crisis over slavery. The Thirteenth and Fourteenth Amendments later changed the constitutional rules on slavery and citizenship.
這項判決極不公正，並加劇了全國圍繞奴隸制度的危機。第十三及第十四修正案後來改變了奴隸制度及公民身分方面的憲法規則。

**Why it matters:** A Supreme Court decision can have enormous consequences and can later be superseded by constitutional amendments.
**重要性：** 最高法院判決可能造成巨大影響，而後來的憲法修正案也可能改變其法律基礎。

Primary source: https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/dred-scott-v-sandford
第一手資料：美國國家檔案館 Dred Scott 判例。

## 4. Plessy v. Ferguson (1896)
## 4. Plessy v. Ferguson（1896年）

**Topic: Racial segregation and the “separate but equal” doctrine.**
**主題：種族隔離及「隔離但平等」原則。**

The Supreme Court upheld a Louisiana law requiring racial segregation in railway accommodations and accepted the separate-but-equal doctrine.
最高法院維持路易斯安那州要求鐵路設施實施種族隔離的法律，並接受「隔離但平等」原則。

In practice, segregation entrenched racial inequality. Brown v. Board of Education later rejected this doctrine in public education.
在實際生活中，種族隔離制度鞏固了種族不平等。Brown v. Board of Education 後來在公立教育領域否定了這項原則。

**Why it matters:** It demonstrates that judicial decisions can uphold unjust systems; legal history must include both rights-protecting and rights-denying decisions.
**重要性：** 它說明司法判決也可能維護不公正制度；法律史必須同時研究保障權利及否定權利的判決。

Source: https://guides.loc.gov/plessy-ferguson/introduction
資料來源：美國國會圖書館 Plessy 判例研究指南。

## 5. Brown v. Board of Education (1954)
## 5. Brown v. Board of Education（1954年）

**Topic: Equal protection and public-school segregation.**
**主題：平等保護及公立學校種族隔離。**

Black children and their families challenged laws and practices that separated public-school students by race.
非裔兒童及其家庭挑戰依種族將公立學校學生分開的法律及做法。

The Supreme Court held that racial segregation in public schools violated the Equal Protection Clause of the Fourteenth Amendment. It rejected the idea that separate public schools could be equal in the constitutional sense.
最高法院裁定，公立學校的種族隔離違反第十四修正案的平等保護條款，並否定了公立學校可以在憲法意義上做到「隔離但平等」的觀念。

**Why it matters:** It became a landmark in the constitutional law of equality and the civil-rights movement.
**重要性：** 本案成為平等憲法法及民權運動的重要里程碑。

Official educational source: https://www.uscourts.gov/educational-resources/educational-activities/access-education-rule-law
官方教育資料：美國聯邦法院 Brown 判例教學資料。

## 6. Gideon v. Wainwright (1963)
## 6. Gideon v. Wainwright（1963年）

**Topic: Right to counsel in serious criminal cases.**
**主題：重大刑事案件中的律師協助權。**

Clarence Gideon was charged with a felony in state court and could not afford a lawyer. The trial court refused to appoint one under the law then applied.
Clarence Gideon 在州法院被控重罪，但無力聘請律師。當時適用的法律下，初審法院拒絕為他指定律師。

The Supreme Court held that the Sixth Amendment right to counsel is fundamental and applies to the states through the Fourteenth Amendment. Indigent defendants charged with serious crimes must be provided counsel.
最高法院認為，第六修正案的律師協助權是一項基本權利，並透過第十四修正案適用於各州。無力聘請律師而被控重大犯罪的被告，必須獲得律師協助。

**Important limit:** This case is not a rule that the government must appoint a free lawyer for every person in every kind of dispute. Its holding concerns criminal prosecutions and the constitutional right to counsel.
**重要限制：** 本案並不是規定政府必須為所有人、所有類型的爭議都免費指定律師。其裁判涉及刑事追訴及憲法上的律師協助權。

Official source: https://www.uscourts.gov/about-federal-courts/educational-resources/educational-activities/sixth-amendment-activities/gideon-v-wainwright/facts-and-case-summary-gideon-v-wainwright
官方資料：美國聯邦法院 Gideon 判例摘要。

## 7. Mapp v. Ohio (1961)
## 7. Mapp v. Ohio（1961年）

**Topic: The exclusionary rule and the Fourth Amendment.**
**主題：排除證據規則及第四修正案。**

The Supreme Court held that evidence obtained through unconstitutional searches and seizures is generally inadmissible in state criminal trials under the exclusionary rule, subject to later-developed exceptions.
最高法院裁定，在州刑事審判中，透過違憲搜查及扣押取得的證據通常依排除證據規則不得採用，但仍須考慮後來發展的例外。

**Why it matters:** It shows that constitutional rights may be enforced through rules about what evidence the government can use in court.
**重要性：** 它說明憲法權利可以透過限制政府在法院使用何種證據的規則來執行。

Official case access: https://www.supremecourt.gov/
官方判決查詢：美國聯邦最高法院。

## 8. Miranda v. Arizona (1966)
## 8. Miranda v. Arizona（1966年）

**Topic: Safeguards during custodial interrogation.**
**主題：受拘束訊問時的權利保障。**

The Supreme Court required law-enforcement officers to give specified warnings before custodial interrogation, including the right to remain silent and the right to consult with an attorney.
最高法院要求執法人員在對處於羈押狀態的人進行訊問前，提供特定權利告知，包括保持沉默的權利及諮詢律師的權利。

**Important limit:** Miranda warnings are not automatically required during every police conversation, traffic stop, or arrest. The legal test focuses on custody and interrogation.
**重要限制：** 並不是每次警察交談、交通攔查或逮捕都自動需要 Miranda 警告。法律判斷重點在於是否屬於羈押狀態及訊問。

Official educational source: https://www.uscourts.gov/about-federal-courts/educational-resources/supreme-court-landmarks
官方教育資料：美國聯邦法院最高法院重要判例。

## 9. New York Times Co. v. Sullivan (1964)
## 9. New York Times Co. v. Sullivan（1964年）

**Topic: Freedom of speech and press; defamation claims by public officials.**
**主題：言論及新聞自由；公職人員提出的誹謗請求。**

The Supreme Court held that a public official suing over defamatory false statements concerning official conduct must prove “actual malice” under the constitutional standard. Actual malice means knowledge that a statement was false or reckless disregard for whether it was false.
最高法院裁定，公職人員若因涉及其公務行為的誹謗性虛假陳述提起訴訟，必須依憲法標準證明「實際惡意」。實際惡意是指明知陳述不實，或罔顧其真偽而作出陳述。

**Important limit:** Actual malice does not simply mean personal hatred or bad feelings. The legal definition is specific.
**重要限制：** Actual malice 並不只是指個人仇恨或惡劣情緒；它有特定的法律定義。

**Why it matters:** It gives strong protection to public debate and criticism of government officials while leaving room for defamation law under the applicable standards.
**重要性：** 它在適用法律標準下，為公共討論及批評政府官員提供強有力的保障，同時仍保留誹謗法的適用空間。

Official landmark case overview: https://www.uscourts.gov/about-federal-courts/educational-resources/supreme-court-landmarks
官方重要判例概覽：美國聯邦法院。

## A case-reading checklist
## 判例閱讀清單

For every case, identify:
閱讀每宗判例時，找出：

- **Court and year** — Which court decided it, and when?
  **法院及年份**——哪個法院在什麼時候作出判決？
- **Facts** — What happened?
  **事實**——發生了什麼事？
- **Procedural history** — How did the case reach this court?
  **程序經過**——案件如何來到這個法院？
- **Issue** — What legal question was presented?
  **爭點**——案件提出了什麼法律問題？
- **Rule** — What legal rule did the court apply?
  **規則**——法院適用了什麼法律規則？
- **Holding** — What did the court decide?
  **裁判要旨**——法院作出了什麼決定？
- **Reasoning** — Why?
  **推理**——法院為什麼這樣判決？
- **Scope** — Which courts and situations does the decision control?
  **適用範圍**——這項判決拘束哪些法院及情況？

A case summary is not a substitute for the court's opinion. When possible, read the majority opinion and check whether later cases limited, distinguished, or overruled it.
判例摘要不能取代法院判決意見正文。只要可能，應閱讀多數意見，並核對後來的判例是否限制、區別或推翻該判決。
