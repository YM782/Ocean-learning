# How U.S. Law Is Made, Organized, and Read
# 美國法律如何制定、編排及閱讀

## 1. First, identify what kind of legal document you are reading
## 1. 首先，確認你正在閱讀哪一種法律文件

A **constitution** establishes the basic structure and limits of government.
**憲法（Constitution）** 建立政府的基本架構及權力限制。

A **constitutional amendment** formally changes or adds to the Constitution through the amendment process.
**憲法修正案（Constitutional Amendment）** 透過修憲程序正式修改或增加憲法內容。

A **bill** is a proposal considered by a legislature. It is not yet an enacted statute.
**法案草案（Bill）** 是立法機關審議的提案；它尚未成為正式制定法。

A **statute** is a law enacted by a legislature.
**制定法（Statute）** 是由立法機關正式通過的法律。

A **regulation** is a rule issued by an administrative agency under authority granted by a constitution or statute.
**行政規章（Regulation）** 是行政機關依憲法或制定法授予的權限發布的規則。

A **judicial opinion** explains a court's decision in a case.
**司法判決意見（Judicial Opinion）** 是法院對某一案件裁決的說明。

A **court rule** governs procedure in a particular court system.
**法院規則（Court Rule）** 規範特定法院制度中的訴訟程序。

## 2. How a federal bill can become law
## 2. 聯邦法案如何成為法律

A member of Congress introduces a bill in the House or Senate, subject to constitutional and chamber rules.
國會議員依憲法及議院規則，在眾議院或參議院提出法案。

The bill may be referred to a committee, where members study it, hold hearings, and propose changes.
法案可能被交由委員會審查，委員會成員會研究法案、舉行聽證會並提出修改。

The House and Senate generally must pass the same text.
眾議院及參議院通常必須通過相同的法案文本。

The bill is presented to the President. The President may sign it, veto it, or take no action under the constitutional rules.
法案會提交總統。總統可以簽署、否決，或依憲法規則不採取行動。

Congress may override a presidential veto with the constitutionally required two-thirds vote in each chamber.
國會可以在兩院各自取得憲法規定的三分之二票數後，推翻總統否決。

**Important:** This is a simplified outline. Special procedures, budget rules, deadlines, and constitutional details can affect the process.
**重要提醒：** 這是簡化流程。特殊程序、預算規則、期限及憲法細節都可能影響實際立法過程。

Official explanation: https://www.congress.gov/legislative-process
官方說明：美國國會立法程序。

## 3. Federal law and state law
## 3. 聯邦法與州法

The federal government has only the powers granted to it by the Constitution.
聯邦政府只能行使憲法授予它的權力。

States retain broad authority over many matters, subject to the U.S. Constitution and valid federal law.
各州對許多事項保有廣泛權限，但仍受美國憲法及有效聯邦法律限制。

The Supremacy Clause provides that the Constitution and valid federal laws made under it are supreme over conflicting state law.
最高效力條款規定，憲法及依憲法制定的有效聯邦法律，優先於與其牴觸的州法。

**Preemption** is the legal doctrine used to determine when federal law displaces state law.
**聯邦優先適用（Preemption）** 是用來判斷聯邦法何時排除州法適用的法律原則。

Do not assume that federal law covers every detail. State law may provide additional protections where federal law allows it.
不要假設聯邦法規定了所有細節。在聯邦法允許的範圍內，州法可能提供額外保障。

## 4. Where federal statutes are published
## 4. 聯邦制定法刊載在哪裡

The **United States Code (U.S.C.)** organizes federal statutes by subject into titles.
**《美國法典》（United States Code, U.S.C.）** 按照主題將聯邦制定法編排成不同編章。

The **Statutes at Large** publish federal laws in the order they were enacted.
**《美國法律總集》（Statutes at Large）** 按照聯邦法律通過的先後順序刊載法律。

A law's public-law number identifies the enacted law in a particular Congress. For example, “Pub. L. 88-352” identifies the Civil Rights Act of 1964.
法律的公法編號用來識別某一屆國會通過的法律。例如，「Pub. L. 88-352」是《1964年民權法》的公法編號。

A statute citation such as “42 U.S.C. § 2000e” points to a section of the United States Code. The symbol “§” means “section.”
像「42 U.S.C. § 2000e」這樣的制定法引證，指向《美國法典》中的一個條文。「§」符號代表「條」。

Official sources: https://uscode.house.gov/ and https://www.govinfo.gov/app/collection/statute
官方資料：《美國法典》及 GovInfo《美國法律總集》。

## 5. How to read a statute
## 5. 如何閱讀制定法

Do not start by translating every word. First find the statute's structure.
不要一開始就逐字翻譯。先找出制定法的結構。

1. **Short title:** What is the law called?
   **簡稱：** 這項法律叫什麼名字？
2. **Findings or purpose:** Does Congress explain the problem or purpose?
   **認定事實或立法目的：** 國會是否說明問題或立法目的？
3. **Definitions:** How does the law define important words?
   **定義：** 法律如何定義重要詞語？
4. **Scope:** Who and what are covered?
   **適用範圍：** 哪些人及事項受到規範？
5. **Main rule:** What conduct is required, allowed, or prohibited?
   **主要規則：** 哪些行為是必須、允許或禁止的？
6. **Exceptions:** Are there exemptions or special conditions?
   **例外：** 是否有豁免或特別條件？
7. **Enforcement:** Which agency or court enforces the rule?
   **執行：** 哪個行政機關或法院負責執行規則？
8. **Remedies and penalties:** What can happen if the rule is violated?
   **救濟及處罰：** 違反規則會有什麼後果？
9. **Effective date:** When does the rule take effect?
   **生效日期：** 規則從什麼時候開始生效？
10. **Amendments:** Has Congress changed the law since enactment?
    **修正：** 國會通過後是否曾修改這項法律？

## 6. Words that change legal meaning
## 6. 會改變法律含義的詞語

**May** often indicates permission or discretion, but context matters.
**May** 通常表示許可或裁量權，但仍須看上下文。

**Must** commonly expresses a mandatory requirement.
**Must** 通常表示強制性要求。

**Shall** is often used to express a duty, but its legal effect depends on the statute and jurisdiction.
**Shall** 常用來表達義務，但其法律效果取決於制定法及司法管轄區。

**Unless** introduces an exception or condition.
**Unless** 用來引出例外或條件。

**Except** identifies something excluded from a general rule.
**Except** 指出一般規則中的排除事項。

**Subject to** signals that a rule is limited or controlled by another provision.
**Subject to** 表示某項規則受到另一條文限制或支配。

**Notwithstanding** often signals that a provision operates despite another rule, but the exact priority must be read in context.
**Notwithstanding** 通常表示即使存在另一項規則，該條文仍可能適用；但確切優先關係必須結合上下文判斷。

## 7. How to read a court opinion
## 7. 如何閱讀法院判決意見

A judicial opinion is not the same as a statute. It applies or interprets law in a specific dispute.
司法判決意見並不等同於制定法。它是在特定爭議中適用或解釋法律。

Read the caption to identify the parties and court.
閱讀判決首頁的案件名稱及標題，確認當事人和法院。

Find the procedural history to understand how the case reached that court.
找出程序經過，了解案件如何來到該法院。

Identify the question presented, the governing rule, the holding, and the reasoning.
找出提出的問題、適用規則、裁判要旨及推理。

A **majority opinion** states the court's controlling opinion when it has the required support.
**多數意見（Majority Opinion）** 是獲得法院所需多數支持的控制性意見。

A **concurrence** agrees with the result but may give different reasons.
**協同意見（Concurrence）** 同意裁判結果，但可能提出不同理由。

A **dissent** disagrees with the result or reasoning.
**反對意見（Dissent）** 不同意裁判結果或推理。

A headnote is an editor's summary, not the court's own legal reasoning.
判例摘要（Headnote）是編輯整理的摘要，不是法院本身的法律推理。

## 8. Binding and persuasive authority
## 8. 具有拘束力與說服力的法律權威

**Binding authority** is legal authority a court must follow under the relevant hierarchy and jurisdiction.
**拘束性法律權威（Binding Authority）** 是法院依相關層級及司法管轄區必須遵循的法律權威。

**Persuasive authority** may help a court reason but does not necessarily have to be followed.
**說服性法律權威（Persuasive Authority）** 可以協助法院進行推理，但不一定必須遵循。

A U.S. Supreme Court holding on federal constitutional law generally binds lower federal and state courts on the federal question.
美國聯邦最高法院對聯邦憲法問題作出的裁判要旨，通常對處理該聯邦法律問題的下級聯邦法院及州法院具有拘束力。

A decision of one state's highest court on that state's own law generally does not bind courts in every other state.
某一州最高法院對該州本地法律作出的判決，通常不會拘束其他所有州的法院。

## 9. A worked mini-example
## 9. 一個完整的迷你例子

A city passes a rule that prohibits people from criticizing the mayor in a public park.
某城市通過一項規則，禁止人們在公共公園批評市長。

**Question:** Could the rule violate the First Amendment?
**問題：** 這項規則是否可能違反第一修正案？

**Rule:** The First Amendment protects speech from certain government restrictions, subject to recognized constitutional doctrines and exceptions.
**規則：** 第一修正案保護言論免受某些政府限制，但仍須考慮已被承認的憲法原則及例外。

**Application:** A rule targeting criticism of a public official because of its viewpoint raises a serious constitutional concern. The exact analysis would require facts about the rule, the location, and any claimed justification.
**適用：** 如果規則因為觀點內容而針對對公職人員的批評，就會引發嚴重的憲法疑問。確切分析仍需要了解規則內容、場所及政府提出的理由。

**Conclusion:** The rule may be unconstitutional, but a lawyer must examine the exact text, facts, and controlling precedent before reaching a final legal conclusion.
**結論：** 這項規則可能違憲，但律師必須檢查確切文本、事實及具有拘束力的判例，才能作出最終法律結論。

## Beginner checklist
## 初學者檢查清單

- [ ] I know whether I am reading a Constitution, amendment, bill, statute, regulation, or judicial opinion.
  我知道自己閱讀的是憲法、修正案、法案草案、制定法、行政規章，還是司法判決意見。
- [ ] I know whether the rule is federal or state law.
  我知道該規則屬於聯邦法還是州法。
- [ ] I checked the jurisdiction and effective date.
  我核對了司法管轄區及生效日期。
- [ ] I identified definitions, scope, exceptions, and enforcement.
  我找出了定義、適用範圍、例外及執行方式。
- [ ] I distinguished the court's holding from a headnote or commentary.
  我區分了法院裁判要旨與判例摘要或評論。

僅供教育學習，不構成法律意見。
