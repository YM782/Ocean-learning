# Exercises — Landmark Laws and U.S. Legal Institutions
# 練習題——美國重要法律與法律制度

Try each question before reading the answer. Explain why the other options are incorrect.
請先自行回答每道題目，再閱讀答案。並嘗試解釋其他選項為什麼不正確。

## Part A — Multiple choice
## A部分——選擇題

### 1. Which document established the basic structure of the federal government?
### 1. 哪一份文件建立了聯邦政府的基本架構？

A. Declaration of Independence / 《獨立宣言》
B. U.S. Constitution / 《美國憲法》
C. Sherman Act / 《謝爾曼法》
D. Social Security Act / 《社會保障法》

**Answer: B.** The Constitution created the federal framework and divided federal powers.
**答案：B。** 美國憲法建立聯邦制度架構，並分配聯邦權力。

### 2. What is the Bill of Rights?
### 2. 什麼是《權利法案》？

A. The first ten constitutional amendments / 憲法前十條修正案
B. Ten federal criminal statutes / 十項聯邦刑事法律
C. A Supreme Court case / 一宗最高法院判例
D. A state constitution / 一部州憲法

**Answer: A.** The first ten amendments were ratified in 1791.
**答案：A。** 憲法前十條修正案於1791年獲批准。

### 3. Which amendment abolished slavery?
### 3. 哪一條修正案廢除了奴隸制度？

A. 10th / 第十修正案
B. 13th / 第十三修正案
C. 14th / 第十四修正案
D. 15th / 第十五修正案

**Answer: B.** The Thirteenth Amendment abolished slavery and involuntary servitude, subject to its criminal-punishment exception.
**答案：B。** 第十三修正案廢除了奴隸制度及非自願奴役，但保留其刑罰例外。

### 4. What did the Fourteenth Amendment add?
### 4. 第十四修正案增加了什麼重要保障？

A. A national bank / 國家銀行
B. Birthright citizenship, due process, and equal protection / 出生公民權、正當法律程序及平等保護
C. A federal minimum wage / 聯邦最低工資
D. A national park / 國家公園

**Answer: B.** These are central parts of Section 1 of the Fourteenth Amendment.
**答案：B。** 這些是第十四修正案第一節的重要內容。

### 5. Does the Sherman Act prohibit a business only because it is large?
### 5. 企業只因為規模很大，就會違反《謝爾曼法》嗎？

A. Yes / 是
B. No / 否

**Answer: B.** The law focuses on specified anticompetitive conduct and monopolization, not size alone.
**答案：B。** 該法針對特定反競爭行為及壟斷，而不是只看企業規模。

### 6. Which law established important federal minimum-wage and overtime standards?
### 6. 哪一項法律建立了重要的聯邦最低工資及加班標準？

A. Fair Labor Standards Act / 《公平勞動標準法》
B. Chinese Exclusion Act / 《排華法案》
C. Judiciary Act / 《司法法》
D. Voting Rights Act / 《投票權法》

**Answer: A.** The FLSA establishes wage, overtime, recordkeeping, and child-labor standards for covered employment.
**答案：A。**《公平勞動標準法》為受涵蓋的工作建立工資、加班、紀錄及童工標準。

### 7. Did the 2013 Shelby County decision repeal the entire Voting Rights Act?
### 7. 2013年的 Shelby County 判決是否廢除了整部《投票權法》？

A. Yes / 是
B. No / 否

**Answer: B.** It invalidated the coverage formula for a particular preclearance mechanism; it did not repeal the entire statute.
**答案：B。** 該判決判定特定預先審查機制的涵蓋公式違憲，並沒有廢除整部法律。

## Part B — True or false
## B部分——是非題

### 8. The Declaration of Independence and the Constitution are the same legal document.
### 8. 《獨立宣言》與《美國憲法》是同一份法律文件。

**Answer: False.** They have different purposes and legal roles.
**答案：錯。** 兩者具有不同目的及法律角色。

### 9. The Bill of Rights originally restricted the federal government.
### 9. 《權利法案》最初主要限制聯邦政府。

**Answer: True.** Many of its protections were later applied to states through Fourteenth Amendment doctrine.
**答案：對。** 其中許多保障後來透過第十四修正案相關原則適用於各州。

### 10. A Supreme Court case can never be changed by a constitutional amendment.
### 10. 最高法院判例永遠不可能被憲法修正案改變。

**Answer: False.** Constitutional amendments can change the constitutional text and supersede a prior decision's constitutional basis.
**答案：錯。** 憲法修正案可以改變憲法文本，並取代先前判決所依據的憲法基礎。

## Part C — Short-answer reasoning
## C部分——簡答及法律推理

### 11. A worker works 45 hours in one workweek. Does the worker automatically receive overtime for five hours?
### 11. 一名勞工在一個工作週工作45小時，是否自動可以取得五小時加班費？

**Sample answer:** Not automatically. First determine whether the worker and employer are covered by the FLSA, whether the worker is exempt, how the workweek and regular rate are calculated, and whether state law provides additional protections.
**參考答案：** 不一定。首先要判斷勞工及雇主是否受《公平勞動標準法》涵蓋、勞工是否屬於豁免類別、工作週及正常工資如何計算，以及州法是否提供額外保障。

### 12. A state law conflicts with a valid federal statute. What should you investigate?
### 12. 州法與一項有效的聯邦制定法發生衝突時，你應該調查什麼？

**Sample answer:** Identify the federal statute's constitutional basis, its scope, any express or implied preemption, the nature of the conflict, and relevant Supreme Court decisions.
**參考答案：** 找出聯邦法律的憲法依據、適用範圍、明示或默示的聯邦優先適用、衝突性質，以及相關最高法院判例。

### 13. A city bans criticism of the mayor in a public park. What legal issue might arise?
### 13. 某城市禁止人們在公共公園批評市長，可能產生什麼法律爭點？

**Sample answer:** The rule may discriminate based on viewpoint and raise a First Amendment issue. The exact analysis requires the text of the rule, the forum, the facts, and controlling precedent.
**參考答案：** 該規則可能基於觀點內容而作出差別對待，因而引發第一修正案問題。確切分析需要檢查規則文本、言論場所、事實及具有拘束力的判例。

### 14. Why should a legal researcher check the date of a statute?
### 14. 為什麼法律研究者必須核對制定法的日期？

**Sample answer:** A statute may have been amended, repealed, delayed, or interpreted by later courts. The current rule may differ from the original version.
**參考答案：** 制定法可能經過修正、廢除、延後生效，或受到後來法院解釋。現行規則可能與最初版本不同。

## Part D — Mini IRAC
## D部分——迷你 IRAC 練習

**Facts:** A state university excludes an applicant solely because of the applicant's race. The applicant challenges the decision.
**事實：** 一所州立大學僅因申請人的種族而拒絕錄取。申請人對此提出法律挑戰。

**Issue:** Does the state university's decision raise an Equal Protection issue under the Fourteenth Amendment?
**爭點：** 州立大學的決定是否引發第十四修正案平等保護條款的問題？

**Rule:** The Equal Protection Clause restricts state governments. Race-based governmental classifications are subject to strict scrutiny under modern constitutional doctrine.
**規則：** 平等保護條款限制州政府。依現代憲法原則，政府基於種族作出的分類通常受到嚴格審查。

**Application:** The university is a state institution, and the decision expressly uses race. A full analysis would examine the policy, the asserted governmental interest, and whether the policy is narrowly tailored under controlling precedent.
**適用：** 該大學是州立機構，而且決定明確使用種族因素。完整分析必須檢查政策內容、政府提出的利益，以及依具有拘束力的判例，該政策是否經過嚴格限定。

**Conclusion:** The facts raise a serious constitutional issue, but a final answer requires reviewing the exact policy and current controlling law.
**結論：** 這些事實引發嚴重的憲法問題，但最終答案仍須審查確切政策及現行具有拘束力的法律。
