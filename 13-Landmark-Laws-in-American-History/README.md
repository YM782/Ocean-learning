# Landmark Laws in American History
# 美國歷史上影響深遠的法律與憲法文件

> **零基礎程度｜每段英文後面立即附上繁體中文**

## How to use this chapter
## 如何使用本章

This chapter is not a list of dates to memorize. It explains why important legal documents were created, what they changed, who benefited, who was harmed, and how their effects developed over time.
本章不是要求你死記年份的清單，而是解釋重要法律文件為什麼出現、改變了什麼、誰因此受益、誰受到傷害，以及它們的影響如何隨時間發展。

For each item, read the historical background first, then the legal change, then the limits and consequences. Finally, explain the idea in your own words.
每個項目都先閱讀歷史背景，再閱讀法律變化，接著了解限制及後果，最後用自己的話解釋概念。

**Important distinction:** The documents below are not all the same kind of law. Some are constitutional documents, some are amendments to the Constitution, some are statutes enacted by Congress, and some are judicial decisions. Do not call all of them “Acts.”
**重要區別：** 以下文件並不是同一種類型的法律。有些是憲法文件，有些是憲法修正案，有些是國會制定的法律，另一些則是法院判決。不要把它們全部都稱為「法案（Acts）」。

## Part I — Founding the legal system
## 第一部分——建立美國法律制度

### 1. Declaration of Independence (1776)
### 1. 《獨立宣言》（1776年）

**Type: Founding political document, not the U.S. Constitution and not an ordinary statute.**
**類型：建國政治文件；它不是美國憲法，也不是一般國會制定法。**

The Declaration announced that the thirteen colonies regarded themselves as independent states. It explained the political case for separation from Great Britain and expressed ideas about equality, rights, and government by consent.
《獨立宣言》宣布十三個殖民地將自己視為獨立的州。它說明了脫離英國的政治理由，並表達平等、權利及政府須獲人民同意等理念。

Its famous statement that people possess unalienable rights became a lasting part of American political language. However, the Declaration did not itself create the modern federal court system or operate as a complete code of enforceable individual rights.
其中關於人民享有不可剝奪權利的著名表述，成為美國政治語言的重要部分。不過，《獨立宣言》本身並沒有建立現代聯邦法院制度，也不是一部完整、可直接執行的個人權利法典。

**Why it matters:** It explains the ideals used to justify independence, while later constitutional law created the actual institutions and legal rules of government.
**重要性：** 它解釋了美國獨立所依據的理念；後來的憲法法律才建立政府的實際機關及法律規則。

Primary source: https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/declaration-transcript
第一手資料：美國國家檔案館《獨立宣言》原文。

### 2. Constitution of the United States (drafted 1787; effective 1789)
### 2. 《美國憲法》（1787年起草；1789年開始施行）

**Type: Constitution — the foundational legal framework of the federal government.**
**類型：憲法——聯邦政府的根本法律架構。**

The Constitution created the federal government, divided federal power among three branches, established relationships between the federal government and the states, and provided a method for amendment.
美國憲法建立聯邦政府，將聯邦權力分配給三個部門，規定聯邦政府與各州之間的關係，並建立修憲程序。

The Constitution does not give the federal government unlimited power. Federal institutions must identify constitutional authority for the actions they take.
憲法並沒有賦予聯邦政府無限制的權力。聯邦機關採取行動時，必須指出其憲法權限依據。

**Everyday example:** Congress cannot simply claim that it may regulate every subject. It must act within a power granted by the Constitution, and constitutional limits still apply.
**生活例子：** 國會不能只憑自己宣稱就可以管理所有事項。國會必須在憲法授予的權力範圍內行事，而且仍然受到憲法限制。

**Key ideas:** Federalism, separation of powers, checks and balances, enumerated powers, and constitutional supremacy.
**重要概念：** 聯邦制、權力分立、權力制衡、列舉權力及憲法最高效力。

Primary source: https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/constitution-transcript
第一手資料：美國國家檔案館《美國憲法》全文。

### 3. Judiciary Act of 1789
### 3. 《1789年司法法》

**Type: Federal statute.**
**類型：聯邦制定法。**

The Constitution established the federal judicial branch but left Congress to organize much of the lower federal court system. The Judiciary Act of 1789 created the initial structure of federal district courts and circuit courts and provided for the office of Attorney General.
憲法建立了聯邦司法部門，但將下級聯邦法院制度的許多組織工作交由國會處理。《1789年司法法》建立了聯邦地方法院及巡迴法院的初步架構，並規定了司法部長職位。

**Why it matters:** A constitutional promise needs institutions and procedures. This Act helped turn the Constitution's judicial branch into a functioning court system.
**重要性：** 憲法上的制度安排需要機關及程序才能運作。這項法律協助將憲法所設立的司法部門轉化為實際運作的法院制度。

**Do not confuse:** The Attorney General is the head of the Department of Justice today, but the Department of Justice was not created by the 1789 Act; it became an executive department in 1870.
**不要混淆：** 今天的司法部長是司法部首長，但司法部並不是由1789年司法法建立的；司法部於1870年成為行政部門。

Introductory source: https://www.uscourts.gov/about-federal-courts/educational-resources
入門資料：美國聯邦法院教育資源。

### 4. Bill of Rights (1791)
### 4. 《權利法案》（1791年）

**Type: The first ten amendments to the Constitution.**
**類型：美國憲法的前十條修正案。**

The Bill of Rights was ratified on December 15, 1791. It protects important liberties and sets limits on government power.
《權利法案》於1791年12月15日獲批准。它保障重要自由，並限制政府權力。

Examples include freedom of speech and religion, protection against unreasonable searches, criminal-procedure rights, and limits on excessive bail and cruel punishment.
例子包括言論與宗教自由、免受不合理搜查的保障、刑事程序權利，以及對過高保釋金和殘酷刑罰的限制。

**A crucial beginner detail:** The Bill of Rights originally restricted the federal government. Through later interpretation of the Fourteenth Amendment, many — but not all in precisely the same way — of these protections came to apply to state and local governments.
**初學者必須理解的細節：**《權利法案》最初主要限制聯邦政府。透過後來對第十四修正案的司法解釋，其中許多保障逐步適用於州及地方政府，但並不是每一項權利都以完全相同的方式適用。

**Everyday example:** The Fourth Amendment is relevant when government officers search a home. It does not mean every search is automatically illegal; the legal question depends on warrants, exceptions, consent, and the circumstances.
**生活例子：** 政府執法人員搜查住宅時，第四修正案可能適用。但這不代表所有搜查都自動違法；法律判斷取決於令狀、例外情況、同意及具體情境。

Primary source: https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/bill-of-rights-transcript
第一手資料：美國國家檔案館《權利法案》全文。

## Part II — Expansion, slavery, and constitutional change
## 第二部分——領土擴張、奴隸制度與憲法變革

### 5. Indian Removal Act (1830)
### 5. 《1830年印第安人遷移法》

**Type: Federal statute.**
**類型：聯邦制定法。**

The Act authorized the President to negotiate removal treaties with Native nations living east of the Mississippi River. In practice, federal and state policies led to forced displacement of Native peoples from their homelands.
這項法律授權總統與居住在密西西比河以東的原住民族協商遷移條約。實際上，聯邦及州政府政策導致原住民族被迫離開世代居住的土地。

The Cherokee removal of 1838–1839 became known as the Trail of Tears. The National Archives reports that approximately 4,000 of about 16,000 Cherokees died during the forced removal.
1838至1839年間切羅基人的強制遷移後來被稱為「血淚之路」。美國國家檔案館指出，約16,000名切羅基人中，有約4,000人在強制遷移過程中死亡。

**Why it matters:** This law is an example of how government policy and legal authority can be used to dispossess vulnerable communities. A law's formal enactment does not prove that its consequences are just.
**重要性：** 這項法律說明政府政策及法律權力可能被用來剝奪弱勢群體的土地與權益。法律經過正式制定，不代表其後果就是公正的。

Primary source: https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/jacksons-message-to-congress-on-indian-removal
第一手資料：美國國家檔案館關於印第安人遷移政策的歷史文件。

### 6. Compromise of 1850 and Fugitive Slave Act of 1850
### 6. 《1850年妥協案》及《1850年逃奴法》

**Type: A package of five federal statutes; one strengthened the Fugitive Slave Act.**
**類型：由五項聯邦法律組成的法案組合，其中一項強化了逃奴法。**

The Compromise of 1850 attempted to reduce conflict between free and slave states. Its provisions addressed California statehood, territorial governments, the Texas boundary, the slave trade in Washington, D.C., and the return of people who escaped slavery.
《1850年妥協案》試圖緩和自由州與蓄奴州之間的衝突。其內容涉及加利福尼亞州加入聯邦、領地政府、德克薩斯州邊界、華盛頓特區的奴隸貿易，以及逃離奴役者的遣返。

The strengthened Fugitive Slave Act required federal and local officials in free as well as slave states to assist in the capture and return of alleged fugitives. It penalized people who helped them escape.
強化後的《逃奴法》要求自由州及蓄奴州的聯邦與地方官員協助抓捕並遣返被指稱逃離奴役的人，並處罰協助他們逃亡的人。

**Why it matters:** The law nationalized enforcement of slavery-related claims and intensified resistance and sectional conflict. It shows how federal law can force states and individuals to participate in a national policy.
**重要性：** 這項法律將與奴隸制度有關的追捕制度擴展到全國，激化了反抗及南北矛盾。它說明聯邦法律可能要求各州及個人參與全國性政策。

Primary source: https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/compromise-of-1850
第一手資料：美國國家檔案館《1850年妥協案》。

### 7. Homestead Act (1862)
### 7. 《1862年宅地法》

**Type: Federal statute.**
**類型：聯邦制定法。**

The Homestead Act allowed eligible applicants to claim surveyed public land, subject to filing, residence, and improvement requirements. It encouraged settlement and agricultural development in western territories.
《宅地法》允許符合資格的申請人依照登記、居住及土地改良等條件，申請測量後的公共土地。它鼓勵人們移居西部領地及發展農業。

**Why it matters:** It reshaped land ownership and western settlement. However, the story cannot be told only as opportunity for settlers; westward expansion also occurred on Indigenous lands and contributed to dispossession.
**重要性：** 它改變了土地所有權及美國西部的定居格局。不過，這段歷史不能只被描述為移民取得土地的機會；西進擴張也發生在原住民族土地上，並加劇了土地剝奪。

Primary source collection: https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/list
第一手資料：美國國家檔案館重要歷史文件目錄，查找 Homestead Act。

### 8. Reconstruction Amendments: 13th, 14th, and 15th Amendments (1865–1870)
### 8. 重建時期三項憲法修正案：第十三、十四及十五修正案（1865至1870年）

**Type: Constitutional amendments.**
**類型：憲法修正案。**

The Thirteenth Amendment (1865) abolished slavery and involuntary servitude, except as punishment for a crime after due conviction.
第十三修正案（1865年）廢除奴隸制度及非自願奴役，但依法定罪後作為犯罪刑罰的情況除外。

The Fourteenth Amendment (1868) established national birthright citizenship for persons born or naturalized in the United States and subject to its jurisdiction. It also contains the Due Process and Equal Protection Clauses.
第十四修正案（1868年）規定，在美國出生或歸化且受其管轄的人具有美國公民身分。它還包含正當法律程序條款及平等保護條款。

The Fifteenth Amendment (1870) prohibits denying or abridging the right to vote on account of race, color, or previous condition of servitude.
第十五修正案（1870年）禁止因種族、膚色或曾經受奴役的身分而剝奪或限制投票權。

**Important limit:** The Fifteenth Amendment did not establish universal voting rights for women. The Nineteenth Amendment later prohibited denying the vote on account of sex, while other barriers continued to affect many voters.
**重要限制：** 第十五修正案並沒有建立女性普遍投票權。第十九修正案後來禁止因性別而剝奪投票權，但其他障礙仍持續影響許多選民。

**Why they matter:** These amendments changed the Constitution itself. They became central to later civil-rights cases, voting-rights legislation, and the application of many constitutional protections to the states.
**重要性：** 這些修正案直接改變了憲法本身，並成為後來民權案件、投票權立法，以及許多憲法保障適用於各州的重要基礎。

Primary source: https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/amendments-11-27
第一手資料：美國國家檔案館第十一至第二十七修正案原文。

### 9. Chinese Exclusion Act (1882)
### 9. 《1882年排華法案》

**Type: Federal immigration statute.**
**類型：聯邦移民制定法。**

The Act imposed a ten-year ban on the immigration of Chinese laborers. It was the first significant federal law to restrict immigration on the basis of an ethnic working group.
這項法律對中國勞工移民實施十年禁令。它是第一項以特定族裔勞工群體為對象、對移民作出重大限制的聯邦法律。

Congress later extended exclusion. In 1943, Congress repealed the exclusion acts, but a restrictive quota remained. The national-origins quota system was replaced by the 1965 immigration reforms.
國會後來延長了排華限制。1943年，國會廢除了排華法案，但仍保留嚴格的移民配額制度。1965年的移民改革後來取代了國籍來源配額制度。

**Why it matters:** It demonstrates the use of federal immigration law to discriminate against a national-origin and ethnic group. It is also a major part of Chinese American history.
**重要性：** 它說明聯邦移民法曾被用來歧視特定國籍來源及族裔群體，也是美國華人歷史的重要部分。

Primary source: https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/chinese-exclusion-act
第一手資料：美國國家檔案館《排華法案》。

### 10. Pendleton Civil Service Reform Act (1883)
### 10. 《1883年彭德爾頓文官改革法》

**Type: Federal statute.**
**類型：聯邦制定法。**

The Act established a merit-based system for covered federal jobs and used competitive examinations for selection. It also restricted certain politically motivated employment practices.
這項法律為受其涵蓋的聯邦職位建立以能力為基礎的制度，並透過競爭性考試選拔人員，也限制某些出於政治原因的人事處分。

It followed the assassination of President James A. Garfield by a disappointed office seeker.
這項改革發生在總統 James A. Garfield 遭一名求職失敗者刺殺之後。

**Why it matters:** It helped move federal employment away from the spoils system, in which government jobs were distributed as political rewards.
**重要性：** 它協助聯邦公務員制度逐步脫離「政黨分贓制」，也就是把政府職位當作政治獎賞分配的做法。

Primary source: https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/pendleton-act
第一手資料：美國國家檔案館《彭德爾頓法》。

### 11. Sherman Antitrust Act (1890)
### 11. 《1890年謝爾曼反托拉斯法》

**Type: Federal competition statute.**
**類型：聯邦競爭法。**

The Sherman Act targets contracts, combinations, and conspiracies that unreasonably restrain trade, and it prohibits monopolization and attempts or conspiracies to monopolize under its legal standards.
《謝爾曼法》針對不合理限制貿易的契約、聯合及共謀，並依其法律標準禁止壟斷、企圖壟斷及共謀壟斷。

**Beginner warning:** The law does not automatically make a company illegal just because it is large or successful. Legal analysis examines conduct, market circumstances, and the relevant statutory and case-law standards.
**初學者注意：** 企業規模龐大或非常成功，並不會因此自動違法。法律分析會考慮具體行為、市場情況，以及相關制定法和判例標準。

**Everyday example:** Competitors secretly agreeing to fix prices or divide customers may raise serious antitrust concerns.
**生活例子：** 競爭企業私下協議固定價格或瓜分客戶，可能引發嚴重的反托拉斯法律問題。

Primary educational source: https://www.ftc.gov/about-ftc
官方入門資料：美國聯邦貿易委員會。

### 12. Nineteenth Amendment (1920)
### 12. 《第十九修正案》（1920年）

**Type: Constitutional amendment.**
**類型：憲法修正案。**

The Nineteenth Amendment prohibits the United States or a state from denying or abridging the right to vote on account of sex.
第十九修正案禁止聯邦或州政府因性別而剝奪或限制投票權。

It followed decades of organizing, protests, petitions, and political campaigns by women and their allies.
它是女性及其支持者經過數十年組織活動、抗議、請願及政治運動後取得的成果。

**Important limit:** Ratification did not eliminate every voting barrier. Many women of color continued to face racial discrimination and other restrictions.
**重要限制：** 修正案獲批准並沒有消除所有投票障礙。許多有色人種女性仍然面臨種族歧視及其他限制。

Primary source: https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/19th-amendment
第一手資料：美國國家檔案館《第十九修正案》。

## Part III — The modern regulatory and social state
## 第三部分——現代監管制度與社會保障

### 13. Social Security Act (1935)
### 13. 《1935年社會保障法》

**Type: Federal social-welfare and social-insurance statute.**
**類型：聯邦社會福利及社會保險制定法。**

Passed during the Great Depression, the Act created a federal old-age benefits system and established programs involving unemployment compensation and assistance to certain groups, with important roles for state governments.
這項法律在美國大蕭條期間通過，建立聯邦老年福利制度，並設立涉及失業補償及對特定群體援助的計畫，其中州政府扮演重要角色。

The original 1935 Act was not identical to today's Social Security system. Congress amended it many times, and the original coverage excluded some categories of workers.
1935年的原始法律並不等同於今天的社會保障制度。國會後來多次修法，而且原始制度排除了某些類別的勞工。

**Everyday example:** A covered worker may pay Social Security taxes during working life and later qualify for retirement benefits under the applicable rules.
**生活例子：** 受制度涵蓋的勞工可能在工作期間繳納社會保障稅，日後再依適用規則取得退休福利。

Primary sources: https://www.ssa.gov/history/35act.html and https://www.ssa.gov/history/law.html
第一手資料：美國社會保障局1935年法律原文及立法歷史。

### 14. National Labor Relations Act (1935), also called the Wagner Act
### 14. 《1935年國家勞資關係法》，又稱《華格納法》

**Type: Federal labor statute.**
**類型：聯邦勞動法。**

The Act protects many private-sector employees' rights to organize, join unions, and engage in collective bargaining through representatives chosen by employees.
這項法律保障許多私營部門員工組織、加入工會，以及透過員工選出的代表進行集體談判的權利。

It created the National Labor Relations Board (NLRB) to administer and enforce important parts of the law.
它建立了國家勞資關係委員會（NLRB），負責管理及執行法律的重要部分。

**Important limit:** The Act does not cover every worker. Categories such as many public employees, independent contractors, agricultural laborers, and supervisors are treated differently or excluded under the statute.
**重要限制：** 這項法律並不涵蓋所有勞工。許多公務員、獨立承包商、農業勞工及主管等類別，依法律規定可能受到不同待遇或被排除適用。

Primary source: https://www.nlrb.gov/about-nlrb/who-we-are/our-history/1935-passage-of-the-wagner-act
第一手資料：國家勞資關係委員會《華格納法》歷史。

### 15. Fair Labor Standards Act (1938)
### 15. 《1938年公平勞動標準法》

**Type: Federal labor statute.**
**類型：聯邦勞動制定法。**

The FLSA established federal minimum-wage, overtime, recordkeeping, and child-labor standards for covered employment.
《公平勞動標準法》為受法律涵蓋的工作建立聯邦最低工資、加班工資、雇用紀錄及童工標準。

For covered, nonexempt employees, federal law generally requires overtime pay of at least one and one-half times the regular rate for hours worked over forty in a workweek.
對於受法律涵蓋且不屬於豁免類別的員工，聯邦法通常要求每週工作超過四十小時的部分，至少按照正常工資的一點五倍支付加班費。

**Important limit:** Coverage and exemptions matter. State and local laws may provide higher minimum wages or stronger protections, and the more protective applicable rule may control.
**重要限制：** 是否受法律涵蓋及是否屬於豁免類別十分重要。州及地方政府可能規定更高的最低工資或更強的保障，而適用時可能必須遵守較有利於勞工的規則。

Primary sources: https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/flsa and https://www.dol.gov/general/aboutdol/history/flsa1938
第一手資料：美國勞工部《公平勞動標準法》及其歷史。

### 16. Civil Rights Act (1964)
### 16. 《1964年民權法》

**Type: Federal civil-rights statute with multiple titles.**
**類型：包含多個篇章的聯邦民權制定法。**

The Act addressed discrimination in several areas of public life. Different titles cover different settings and protected characteristics.
這項法律處理公共生活多個領域中的歧視問題。不同篇章適用於不同場景及受保護特徵。

Title II addresses discrimination in certain public accommodations based on race, color, religion, or national origin. Title VI addresses discrimination based on race, color, or national origin in programs receiving federal financial assistance. Title VII prohibits covered employment discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, or national origin.
第二篇禁止特定公共場所基於種族、膚色、宗教或國籍來源的歧視。第六篇處理接受聯邦財政援助的計畫中基於種族、膚色或國籍來源的歧視。第七篇禁止受其涵蓋的就業歧視，包括基於種族、膚色、宗教、性別或國籍來源的歧視。

**Everyday example:** A covered employer generally cannot make an employment decision for a reason prohibited by Title VII.
**生活例子：** 受第七篇規範的雇主，通常不能基於第七篇禁止的理由作出僱用決定。

**Important limit:** The exact title, employer coverage, statutory definitions, and available exceptions must be checked. Do not assume every title has the same scope.
**重要限制：** 必須核對具體篇章、雇主適用範圍、法律定義及可適用的例外。不要假設每一篇的適用範圍都相同。

Primary source: https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/civil-rights-act
第一手資料：美國國家檔案館《1964年民權法》。

### 17. Voting Rights Act (1965)
### 17. 《1965年投票權法》

**Type: Federal voting-rights statute enforcing the Fifteenth Amendment.**
**類型：執行第十五修正案的聯邦投票權法律。**

The Act targeted racial discrimination in voting, including literacy tests and other practices used to prevent eligible African Americans from registering and voting.
這項法律針對投票中的種族歧視，包括識字測驗及其他阻止符合資格的非裔美國人登記和投票的做法。

The original Act included federal oversight mechanisms for certain jurisdictions. In 2013, the Supreme Court invalidated the coverage formula used for the preclearance system in Shelby County v. Holder. This did not repeal the entire Voting Rights Act.
原始法律包含針對特定司法管轄區的聯邦監督機制。2013年，最高法院在 Shelby County v. Holder 一案中，判定預先審查制度所使用的涵蓋公式違憲。這並不代表整部《投票權法》被廢除。

**Why it matters:** It shows how Congress can use enforcement legislation to give practical effect to constitutional voting rights, and how later court decisions can change the operation of that legislation.
**重要性：** 它說明國會如何透過執行性立法，使憲法上的投票權得到實際保障，也說明後來的法院判決可能改變該法律的運作方式。

Primary source: https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/voting-rights-act
第一手資料：美國國家檔案館《1965年投票權法》。

### 18. Immigration and Nationality Act of 1952 and the 1965 amendments
### 18. 《1952年移民與國籍法》及1965年修正案

**Type: Federal immigration statute and later amendments.**
**類型：聯邦移民制定法及其後續修正案。**

The 1952 law consolidated immigration and naturalization law and removed certain racial bars to immigration and naturalization, but it retained the national-origins quota system.
1952年的法律整合移民及歸化法律，取消某些基於種族的移民及歸化障礙，但仍保留國籍來源配額制度。

The 1965 amendments replaced the national-origins quota system with a preference system that emphasized family reunification and employment-based immigration. Numerical limits and eligibility rules remained.
1965年的修正案以重視家庭團聚及就業移民的優先制度，取代國籍來源配額制度，但仍保留移民數量限制及資格規則。

**Why it matters:** It changed the structure of legal immigration and had long-term effects on the origins and composition of immigrant communities in the United States.
**重要性：** 它改變了合法移民制度的架構，並長期影響美國移民社群的來源及組成。

Primary source: https://www.uscis.gov/sites/default/files/document/fact-sheets/INSHistory.pdf
官方歷史資料：美國公民及移民服務局《移民局歷史》。

### 19. Fair Housing Act (1968)
### 19. 《1968年公平住房法》

**Type: Federal civil-rights statute, Title VIII of the Civil Rights Act of 1968.**
**類型：聯邦民權制定法，屬於1968年民權法的第八篇。**

The Fair Housing Act prohibits discrimination in many housing-related transactions and activities. Its original protected classes included race, color, religion, and national origin; Congress later added sex, familial status, and disability.
《公平住房法》禁止許多住房交易及相關活動中的歧視。原始受保護類別包括種族、膚色、宗教及國籍來源；國會後來增加性別、家庭狀況及殘障等類別。

**Everyday example:** A covered landlord generally cannot refuse to rent a home because of a protected characteristic.
**生活例子：** 受法律規範的房東通常不能因為租客具有受保護特徵而拒絕出租住宅。

Primary source: https://www.hud.gov/program_offices/fair_housing_equal_opp/fair_housing_act_overview
官方資料：美國住房及城市發展部《公平住房法》概覽。

### 20. Clean Air Act (major 1970 framework; major amendments in 1977 and 1990)
### 20. 《清潔空氣法》（1970年主要架構；1977年及1990年重大修正）

**Type: Federal environmental statute.**
**類型：聯邦環境制定法。**

The 1970 framework greatly expanded the federal role in controlling air pollution. It authorized national air-quality standards and programs addressing pollution from industrial facilities and motor vehicles.
1970年的法律架構大幅擴大聯邦政府在空氣污染管制方面的角色，授權制定全國空氣品質標準，並建立針對工業設施及汽車污染的計畫。

Congress made major amendments in 1977 and 1990. The 1990 amendments addressed issues including acid rain, toxic air pollutants, operating permits, and ozone-depleting chemicals.
國會在1977年及1990年進行重大修法。1990年的修正案處理酸雨、有毒空氣污染物、營運許可及破壞臭氧層的化學物質等問題。

**Important distinction:** The Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) was established in 1970 through a government reorganization plan; do not say that the Clean Air Act itself created the EPA.
**重要區別：** 美國環境保護署（EPA）是在1970年透過政府重組計畫成立的；不要說《清潔空氣法》本身創立了EPA。

Primary sources: https://www.epa.gov/clean-air-act-overview/evolution-clean-air-act and https://www.epa.gov/clean-air-act-overview/clean-air-act-requirements-and-history
第一手資料：美國環境保護署《清潔空氣法》沿革及要求。

### 21. Title IX of the Education Amendments (1972)
### 21. 《1972年教育修正案》第九篇

**Type: Federal statute.**
**類型：聯邦制定法。**

Title IX generally prohibits sex discrimination in education programs or activities receiving federal financial assistance, subject to statutory and regulatory details.
第九篇通常禁止接受聯邦財政援助的教育計畫或活動中的性別歧視，但仍須考慮法律及行政規章的具體規定。

It has affected admissions, educational opportunities, athletics, and the way schools respond to certain forms of sex discrimination.
它影響入學、教育機會、體育活動，以及學校處理某些性別歧視問題的方式。

**Important limit:** Title IX is not a rule that every school must have identical sports teams or identical outcomes. Its legal requirements depend on the program and applicable rules.
**重要限制：** 第九篇並不是要求每所學校都必須有完全相同的運動隊伍或結果。具體法律要求取決於相關計畫及適用規則。

Official resource: https://www.ed.gov/laws-and-policy/civil-rights-laws/title-ix-and-sex-discrimination
官方資料：美國教育部第九篇及性別歧視說明。

### 22. Americans with Disabilities Act (1990)
### 22. 《1990年美國殘疾人法》

**Type: Federal civil-rights statute.**
**類型：聯邦民權制定法。**

The ADA prohibits disability discrimination in important areas including employment, state and local government services, public accommodations, and telecommunications.
《美國殘疾人法》禁止在重要領域歧視殘障人士，包括就業、州及地方政府服務、公共場所及電信服務。

It requires covered entities to follow applicable accessibility and reasonable-accommodation rules.
受法律規範的機構必須遵守適用的無障礙要求及合理調整規則。

**Everyday example:** A covered employer may need to consider a reasonable workplace accommodation for a qualified employee with a disability, unless the law's standards for an exception apply.
**生活例子：** 對符合資格的殘障員工，受法律規範的雇主可能需要考慮提供合理的工作場所調整，除非符合法律規定的例外條件。

Official resource: https://www.ada.gov/topics/intro-to-ada/
官方資料：ADA.gov《美國殘疾人法》入門。

### 23. Affordable Care Act (2010)
### 23. 《2010年平價醫療法》

**Type: Federal health-care statute, formally the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act, as amended.**
**類型：聯邦醫療保健制定法，正式名稱為《患者保護與平價醫療法》及其後續修正。**

The Affordable Care Act made major changes to health-insurance markets. Its provisions include protections related to pre-existing conditions, insurance marketplaces, subsidies for eligible people, and an expansion of Medicaid that states may implement under the governing legal framework.
《平價醫療法》對健康保險市場作出重大改革。其規定包括既有健康狀況相關保障、保險交易平台、符合資格者的補助，以及依適用法律架構由各州實施的 Medicaid 擴張。

**Important limit:** The ACA did not create a single-payer system or guarantee that every person receives identical insurance. Coverage, eligibility, costs, and state implementation vary.
**重要限制：** ACA 並沒有建立單一支付者醫療制度，也沒有保證每個人都取得完全相同的保險。保障範圍、資格、費用及各州實施方式可能不同。

Official resource: https://www.healthcare.gov/glossary/affordable-care-act/
官方資料：HealthCare.gov《平價醫療法》詞彙說明。

## Part IV — How to compare these laws
## 第四部分——如何比較這些法律

Do not ask only, “Was this law good or bad?”
不要只問：「這項法律是好是壞？」

Ask these five questions:
請提出以下五個問題：

1. **Who had legal power to enact it?** Was it Congress, a state legislature, or the people ratifying a constitutional amendment?
   **誰有權制定它？** 是國會、州議會，還是批准憲法修正案的人民？
2. **Who was protected or regulated?** Identify the people, businesses, governments, or activities covered.
   **誰受到保護或規範？** 找出受涵蓋的人、企業、政府機關或活動。
3. **What did the text actually say?** Separate the enacted text from political speeches and later summaries.
   **法律文本實際寫了什麼？** 將正式通過的文本與政治演說及後來的摘要區分開來。
4. **How was it enforced?** Identify the agency, court, procedure, remedy, and penalties where relevant.
   **它如何執行？** 在相關情況下，找出主管機關、法院、程序、救濟及處罰。
5. **What changed later?** Check amendments, judicial interpretations, repeal, and current codification.
   **後來發生了什麼變化？** 核對修正案、司法解釋、廢除情況及現行法典編纂。

## Part V — Beginner review
## 第五部分——初學者複習

**Question 1: Is the Declaration of Independence the same thing as the Constitution?**
**問題一：《獨立宣言》與《美國憲法》是同一份文件嗎？**

No. The Declaration announced independence and expressed political principles; the Constitution established the framework of the federal government.
不是。《獨立宣言》宣布獨立並表達政治原則；《美國憲法》則建立聯邦政府的制度架構。

**Question 2: Did the Bill of Rights originally apply to every state in exactly the same way?**
**問題二：《權利法案》最初是否以完全相同的方式適用於每一州？**

No. It originally restricted the federal government. Many protections were later applied to states through Fourteenth Amendment doctrine.
不是。它最初主要限制聯邦政府。許多保障後來透過第十四修正案相關原則適用於各州。

**Question 3: Does the Sherman Act prohibit every large company?**
**問題三：《謝爾曼法》是否禁止所有大型企業？**

No. It addresses specified anticompetitive conduct and monopolization under legal standards; size alone is not enough.
不是。它依法律標準處理特定反競爭行為及壟斷；企業規模本身並不足以構成違法。

**Question 4: Did the Voting Rights Act disappear after the 2013 Supreme Court decision?**
**問題四：2013年最高法院判決後，《投票權法》是否整部消失？**

No. The Court invalidated a coverage formula used for one federal oversight mechanism; it did not repeal the entire Act.
不是。法院判定某項聯邦監督機制所使用的涵蓋公式違憲，但並沒有廢除整部法律。

**Question 5: Is every law still in force exactly as it was originally enacted?**
**問題五：每一項法律至今都仍然完全按照最初通過時的內容施行嗎？**

No. Congress may amend or repeal statutes, and courts may interpret their meaning or invalidate provisions that violate the Constitution.
不是。國會可以修正或廢除制定法，法院也可能解釋法律含義，或判定違憲條文無效。
