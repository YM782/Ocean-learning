# Landmark U.S. Law — Beginner Glossary
# 美國重要法律——初學者詞彙表

| English | 繁體中文 | Beginner explanation / 初學者解釋 |
|---|---|---|
| Declaration of Independence | 獨立宣言 | The 1776 document announcing independence. / 1776年宣布獨立的文件。 |
| Constitution | 憲法 | The foundational framework of government. / 政府的根本制度架構。 |
| Amendment | 修正案 | A formal change to the Constitution. / 對憲法作出的正式修改。 |
| Bill of Rights | 權利法案 | The first ten constitutional amendments. / 憲法前十條修正案。 |
| Statute | 制定法 | A law enacted by a legislature. / 立法機關通過的法律。 |
| Bill | 法案草案 | A proposal being considered by a legislature. / 立法機關正在審議的提案。 |
| Regulation | 行政規章 | An agency rule issued under legal authority. / 行政機關依法律授權發布的規則。 |
| Federalism | 聯邦制 | A system dividing authority between federal and state governments. / 聯邦與州政府分配權力的制度。 |
| Separation of powers | 權力分立 | Dividing government power among branches. / 將政府權力分配給不同部門。 |
| Checks and balances | 權力制衡 | Each branch can limit certain actions of the others. / 各部門可以限制其他部門的某些行為。 |
| Supremacy Clause | 最高效力條款 | Constitutional rule giving valid federal law priority over conflicting state law. / 規定有效聯邦法優先於相牴觸州法的憲法條款。 |
| Preemption | 聯邦優先適用 | Doctrine determining when federal law displaces state law. / 判斷聯邦法何時排除州法適用的原則。 |
| Judicial review | 司法審查 | Judicial power to review constitutionality in a proper case. / 法院在適當案件中審查合憲性的權力。 |
| Due process | 正當法律程序 | Constitutional protection requiring lawful procedures and, in some contexts, substantive protection. / 要求合法程序並在某些情況下提供實質保障的憲法原則。 |
| Equal protection | 平等保護 | Constitutional restriction on unjustified unequal state treatment. / 限制州政府無正當理由差別對待的憲法原則。 |
| Civil rights | 民權 | Rights protecting equal participation and treatment. / 保障平等參與及待遇的權利。 |
| Discrimination | 歧視 | Unequal treatment that may violate applicable law. / 可能違反適用法律的差別待遇。 |
| Segregation | 種族隔離 | Separating people by race or another characteristic. / 按種族或其他特徵將人群分開。 |
| Citizenship | 公民身分 | Legal membership in a political community. / 一個人依法屬於某政治共同體的身分。 |
| Naturalization | 歸化 | Legal process through which a noncitizen becomes a citizen. / 非公民依法取得公民身分的程序。 |
| Immigration quota | 移民配額 | A numerical limit on certain immigration categories. / 對某些移民類別設定的數量限制。 |
| Antitrust | 反托拉斯／反壟斷 | Laws addressing specified restraints of competition and monopolization. / 處理特定限制競爭及壟斷行為的法律。 |
| Monopoly | 壟斷 | Market power or exclusive control; legal analysis depends on conduct and market conditions. / 市場力量或排他控制；法律判斷取決於行為及市場情況。 |
| Collective bargaining | 集體談判 | Negotiation between an employer and workers' representative. / 雇主與勞工代表之間的協商。 |
| Union | 工會 | An organization representing workers. / 代表勞工的組織。 |
| Minimum wage | 最低工資 | The lowest wage required by applicable law. / 適用法律要求的最低工資。 |
| Overtime | 加班 | Work beyond a legally relevant threshold, often with additional pay rules. / 超過法律相關門檻的工作，通常涉及額外工資規則。 |
| Exemption | 豁免 | A legal exception from a requirement. / 對某項法律要求的例外。 |
| Public accommodation | 公共場所／公共服務場所 | Certain businesses or facilities open to the public. / 向公眾開放的特定企業或設施。 |
| Voting rights | 投票權 | Legal rights relating to registration and voting. / 與選民登記及投票有關的法律權利。 |
| Preclearance | 預先審查 | A former Voting Rights Act mechanism requiring certain jurisdictions to obtain federal approval before specified voting changes. / 《投票權法》曾設立的機制，要求特定地區在實施某些選舉變更前取得聯邦批准。 |
| Public accommodation | 公共場所 | A category of places covered by certain civil-rights laws. / 某些民權法律所涵蓋的場所類別。 |
| Social insurance | 社會保險 | A system of benefits funded through contributions or taxes. / 透過繳費或稅收資助的福利制度。 |
| Administrative agency | 行政機關 | A government body that administers statutes and issues authorized rules. / 負責執行制定法及發布授權規則的政府機關。 |
| Effective date | 生效日期 | The date a legal provision begins to operate. / 法律條文開始生效的日期。 |
| Repeal | 廢除 | Formal removal of a statute or provision. / 正式取消制定法或條文。 |
| Codification | 法典編纂 | Organizing enacted laws into a systematic code. / 將制定法整理編排成有系統的法典。 |
| Legislative history | 立法歷史 | Materials showing how a bill was considered and enacted. / 顯示法案如何被審議及通過的資料。 |
| Primary authority | 第一手法律權威 | Constitution, statute, regulation, or binding judicial decision. / 憲法、制定法、行政規章或具有拘束力的司法判決。 |
| Secondary source | 第二手資料 | A textbook, article, encyclopedia, or commentary explaining the law. / 解釋法律的教科書、文章、百科或評論。 |
| Binding authority | 拘束性法律權威 | Authority a court must follow under the applicable hierarchy. / 法院依適用層級必須遵循的法律權威。 |
| Persuasive authority | 說服性法律權威 | Authority that may influence but does not necessarily bind a court. / 可能影響法院但不一定具有拘束力的法律權威。 |
| Strict scrutiny | 嚴格審查 | A demanding standard of constitutional review used for certain classifications or rights. / 用於某些分類或權利的嚴格憲法審查標準。 |
| Actual malice | 實際惡意 | In the relevant defamation doctrine, knowledge of falsity or reckless disregard for truth. / 在相關誹謗法原則中，指明知虛假或罔顧真偽。 |
| Reasonable accommodation | 合理調整 | A legally required adjustment in covered circumstances, subject to applicable limits. / 在受法律涵蓋的情況下依法要求的調整，但仍有適用限制。 |
| Public Law number | 公法編號 | Identifier assigned to a federal statute as enacted. / 聯邦法律通過後獲得的識別編號。 |
| U.S. Code citation | 美國法典引證 | A reference to a title and section of the U.S. Code. / 指向《美國法典》編章及條文的引證。 |
