# First 50 U.S. Legal Terms for Beginners
# 美國法律初學者最常用的 50 個術語

Learn these terms in context. The Chinese translation is a learning aid; legal meaning can depend on context and jurisdiction.
請在語境中學習這些術語。中文翻譯僅供學習輔助；法律含義可能取決於語境及司法管轄區。

| No. | English | 繁體中文 | Simple English definition | 繁體中文解釋 |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Law | 法律 | A system of rules recognized and enforced by legal institutions. | 由法律機關承認並執行的規則制度。 |
| 2 | Constitution | 憲法 | The foundational legal document of a government. | 政府的根本法律文件。 |
| 3 | Government | 政府 | Public institutions that exercise governmental power. | 行使政府權力的公共機關。 |
| 4 | Federal | 聯邦的 | Related to the national government. | 與全國性政府有關。 |
| 5 | State | 州的 | Related to one U.S. state. | 與美國其中一州有關。 |
| 6 | Congress | 國會 | The legislative branch of the federal government. | 聯邦政府的立法部門。 |
| 7 | President | 總統 | The head of the federal executive branch. | 聯邦行政部門首長。 |
| 8 | Court | 法院 | An institution that resolves legal disputes. | 解決法律爭議的機關。 |
| 9 | Judge | 法官 | A person who presides over a court proceeding. | 主持法院訴訟程序的人。 |
| 10 | Jury | 陪審團 | People selected to decide facts in a trial. | 在審判中負責認定事實的人。 |
| 11 | Plaintiff | 原告 | A party who brings a civil claim. | 提出民事請求的一方。 |
| 12 | Defendant | 被告 | A party responding to a claim or charge. | 回應請求或指控的一方。 |
| 13 | Prosecutor | 檢察官 | A government lawyer who brings criminal prosecutions. | 代表政府提起刑事追訴的法律人員。 |
| 14 | Attorney | 律師 | A person authorized to provide legal services. | 獲授權提供法律服務的人。 |
| 15 | Dispute | 爭議 | A disagreement that may need legal resolution. | 可能需要透過法律解決的分歧。 |
| 16 | Civil case | 民事案件 | A case involving a civil legal claim or dispute. | 涉及民事法律請求或爭議的案件。 |
| 17 | Criminal case | 刑事案件 | A government prosecution for an alleged crime. | 政府追訴涉嫌犯罪行為的案件。 |
| 18 | Statute | 制定法 | A law enacted by a legislature. | 由立法機關制定的法律。 |
| 19 | Regulation | 行政規章 | A rule issued by an agency under legal authority. | 行政機關依法律授權發布的規則。 |
| 20 | Precedent | 判例先例 | An earlier court decision relevant to a later case. | 與後續案件相關的先前法院判決。 |
| 21 | Jurisdiction | 管轄權 | A court's legal authority to hear a case. | 法院審理案件的法律權限。 |
| 22 | Venue | 審判地 | The proper geographic place for a case. | 案件依法適當的地理審理地點。 |
| 23 | Trial | 審判 | A court proceeding to decide disputed issues. | 法院裁決爭議事項的程序。 |
| 24 | Appeal | 上訴 | A request for a higher court to review a decision. | 請求上級法院審查某項決定。 |
| 25 | Evidence | 證據 | Information used to prove or disprove a fact. | 用來證明或否定事實的資訊。 |
| 26 | Witness | 證人 | A person who provides testimony or relevant information. | 提供證言或相關資訊的人。 |
| 27 | Testimony | 證言 | A witness's statement, commonly under oath. | 證人通常在宣誓後作出的陳述。 |
| 28 | Burden of proof | 舉證責任 | The obligation to prove a claim or issue. | 證明某項請求或爭點的責任。 |
| 29 | Beyond a reasonable doubt | 排除合理懷疑 | A high proof standard generally used for criminal convictions. | 刑事定罪通常採用的高度證明標準。 |
| 30 | Contract | 契約 | An agreement that may create enforceable duties. | 可能產生可執行義務的協議。 |
| 31 | Offer | 要約 | A proposal to enter an agreement on stated terms. | 提議依特定條件締結協議。 |
| 32 | Acceptance | 承諾 | Agreement to an offer in the legally required way. | 依法定方式同意要約。 |
| 33 | Consideration | 對價 | Something of legal value exchanged in a contract. | 契約中交換的具有法律價值之事物。 |
| 34 | Breach | 違約／違反 | Failure to perform a legal duty or agreement. | 未履行法律義務或協議。 |
| 35 | Negligence | 過失 | Failure to use legally required care. | 未採取法律要求的注意程度。 |
| 36 | Duty | 義務 | A legal responsibility to act or not act. | 依法必須採取或避免特定行為的責任。 |
| 37 | Damages | 損害賠償 | Money awarded for legally recognized harm. | 針對法律承認的損害所判給的金錢賠償。 |
| 38 | Remedy | 法律救濟 | Relief provided by law or a court. | 法律或法院提供的救濟。 |
| 39 | Right | 權利 | A legally protected interest or entitlement. | 受到法律保障的利益或資格。 |
| 40 | Liability | 法律責任 | Legal responsibility for an act or obligation. | 因行為或義務而產生的法律責任。 |
| 41 | Crime | 犯罪 | Conduct defined as an offense by criminal law. | 由刑法界定為犯罪的行為。 |
| 42 | Arrest | 逮捕 | Taking a person into legal custody. | 依法將某人置於拘束狀態。 |
| 43 | Search | 搜查 | Government examination of a person or place under law. | 政府依法律對人或場所進行檢查。 |
| 44 | Warrant | 令狀 | Formal legal authorization issued by a court or judge. | 由法院或法官核發的正式法律授權文件。 |
| 45 | Due process | 正當法律程序 | Constitutional protection requiring legally adequate procedures. | 要求適當法律程序的憲法保障。 |
| 46 | Equal protection | 平等保護 | A constitutional rule limiting unjustified unequal government treatment. | 限制政府無正當理由差別對待的憲法原則。 |
| 47 | Holding | 裁判要旨 | The legal answer necessary to decide an issue. | 法院解決某項爭點所必要的法律答案。 |
| 48 | Dissent | 反對意見 | A judicial opinion disagreeing with the court's result or reasoning. | 不同意法院結果或推理的司法意見。 |
| 49 | IRAC | 爭點、規則、適用、結論 | A structure for organizing legal analysis. | 組織法律分析的架構。 |
| 50 | Bar admission | 律師准入 | The process for obtaining authorization to practice law. | 取得律師執業授權的程序。 |

Learn five terms a day and write one simple English sentence using each term.
每天學習五個術語，並用每個術語寫一句簡單英文句子。

Review terms after one day, three days, and one week.
在一天後、三天後及一週後複習這些術語。
