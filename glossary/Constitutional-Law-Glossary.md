# U.S. Constitutional Law — Beginner Glossary
# 美國憲法學——初學者詞彙表

| English | 繁體中文 | Plain-language explanation / 白話解釋 |
|---|---|---|
| Constitution | 憲法 | The foundational framework of government. / 政府的根本制度架構。 |
| Amendment | 修正案 | A formal addition or change to the Constitution. / 對憲法作出的正式增加或修改。 |
| Article | 條／條文 | A major numbered part of the original Constitution. / 原始憲法中的主要編號部分。 |
| Federalism | 聯邦制 | Division of authority between federal and state governments. / 聯邦與州政府分配權力的制度。 |
| Separation of powers | 權力分立 | Division of government authority among branches. / 將政府權力分配給不同部門。 |
| Checks and balances | 權力制衡 | Each branch can limit certain actions of the others. / 各部門可以限制其他部門的某些行為。 |
| Judicial review | 司法審查 | Court review of government action for constitutionality. / 法院審查政府行為是否符合憲法。 |
| State action | 政府行為 | Conduct legally attributable to government. / 在法律上可以歸屬於政府的行為。 |
| Incorporation | 權利納入適用 | Applying Bill of Rights protections to states through the Fourteenth Amendment. / 透過第十四修正案使《權利法案》保障適用於各州。 |
| Selective incorporation | 選擇性納入適用 | Applying particular rights to states over time. / 隨時間逐項將特定權利適用於各州。 |
| Standing | 訴訟資格 | A legal requirement that a plaintiff show a sufficiently personal injury. / 原告必須證明自己受到足夠個人化損害的法律要求。 |
| Ripeness | 成熟性 | Whether a dispute is ready for judicial decision. / 爭議是否已經適合司法裁決。 |
| Mootness | 失去實益 | Whether a live dispute still exists. / 是否仍然存在實際爭議。 |
| Strict scrutiny | 嚴格審查 | A demanding constitutional review standard. / 一種要求嚴格的憲法審查標準。 |
| Intermediate scrutiny | 中度審查 | A constitutional review standard between strict and rational-basis review. / 介於嚴格審查與合理基礎審查之間的標準。 |
| Rational-basis review | 合理基礎審查 | A deferential review standard requiring a rational connection to a legitimate interest. / 要求規則與正當政府利益之間具有合理關聯的審查標準。 |
| Establishment Clause | 政教分立條款 | Limits government establishment or sponsorship of religion. / 限制政府建立或支持宗教。 |
| Free Exercise Clause | 宗教自由行使條款 | Protects religious belief and practice against certain government burdens. / 保障宗教信念及實踐免受某些政府限制。 |
| Viewpoint discrimination | 觀點歧視 | Government treats speech differently because of the position expressed. / 政府因言論立場不同而採取差別待遇。 |
| Content-based restriction | 基於內容的限制 | A speech rule based on what the speaker discusses. / 根據發言內容而作出的言論限制。 |
| Prior restraint | 事前限制 | Government action preventing speech before it occurs. / 政府在言論發生前加以阻止的措施。 |
| Public forum | 公共論壇 | Government property opened for public expression under applicable rules. / 依適用規則開放供公眾表達的政府場所。 |
| Incitement | 煽動 | Speech meeting the constitutional test for urging imminent lawless action. / 符合煽動迫在眉睫違法行動憲法標準的言論。 |
| Actual malice | 實際惡意 | Knowledge of falsity or reckless disregard for truth in the relevant defamation doctrine. / 相關誹謗原則中的明知虛假或罔顧真偽。 |
| Keep and bear arms | 持有及攜帶武器 | The right protected by the Second Amendment. / 第二修正案所保障的權利。 |
| Historical tradition | 歷史傳統 | Historical laws and practices used in certain constitutional analysis. / 某些憲法分析中使用的歷史法律及實踐。 |
| Search | 搜查 | Government intrusion into a protected privacy or property interest. / 政府侵犯受保障的隱私或財產利益。 |
| Seizure | 扣押 | Government restraint of a person or meaningful interference with property. / 政府限制人身自由或實質干預財產。 |
| Warrant | 令狀 | Judicial authorization for a search or arrest. / 法院對搜查或逮捕作出的授權。 |
| Probable cause | 合理根據 | A fact-based probability meeting a legal threshold. / 符合法律門檻的事實性可能性。 |
| Reasonable suspicion | 合理懷疑 | Specific facts supporting a brief investigative stop. / 支持短暫調查性攔查的具體事實。 |
| Exclusionary rule | 排除證據規則 | A rule that may prevent certain unlawfully obtained evidence from being used at trial. / 可能阻止某些非法取得的證據在審判中使用的規則。 |
| Good-faith exception | 善意例外 | An exception that may allow evidence when officers reasonably relied on defective legal authority. / 警員合理依賴有缺陷法律權限時，可能允許採納證據的例外。 |
| Custodial interrogation | 羈押狀態下的訊問 | Police questioning when a person is in custody under the Miranda test. / 依 Miranda 標準，對處於羈押狀態的人進行訊問。 |
| Self-incrimination | 自證其罪 | Providing compelled testimonial evidence against oneself. / 被迫提供對自己不利的證言性證據。 |
| Double jeopardy | 雙重危險 | Certain repeated prosecutions or punishments for the same offense. / 對同一罪行進行某些重複起訴或處罰。 |
| Due process | 正當法律程序 | Constitutional protection of fair process and certain liberty interests. / 對公平程序及某些自由利益的憲法保障。 |
| Takings Clause | 徵收條款 | Requires just compensation for takings of private property for public use. / 政府為公共用途徵收私人財產時，要求提供公平補償的條款。 |
| Speedy trial | 迅速審判 | Right to avoid unjustified delay in criminal prosecution. / 避免刑事追訴受到不合理延誤的權利。 |
| Confrontation Clause | 對質條款 | Protects a defendant's ability to confront witnesses against them. / 保障被告與不利證人對質的權利。 |
| Compulsory process | 強制傳喚程序 | Legal process for obtaining witnesses in one's favor. / 取得有利證人的法律程序。 |
| Ineffective assistance | 無效律師協助 | Counsel's performance falling below constitutional standards and causing prejudice. / 律師表現低於憲法標準並造成損害。 |
| Excessive bail | 過高保釋金 | Bail that is excessive under the applicable constitutional standard. / 依適用憲法標準而言過高的保釋金。 |
| Excessive fines | 過高罰金 | Financial penalties that are constitutionally excessive. / 憲法上過高的金錢處罰。 |
| Cruel and unusual punishment | 殘酷及不尋常的刑罰 | Punishment prohibited by the Eighth Amendment. / 第八修正案所禁止的刑罰。 |
| Equal Protection Clause | 平等保護條款 | Requires states to provide equal protection of the laws. / 要求各州提供法律上的平等保護。 |
| Birthright citizenship | 出生公民權 | Citizenship based on birth in the United States, subject to the constitutional text and doctrine. / 依憲法文本及原則，基於在美國出生而取得的公民身分。 |
| Citizenship Clause | 公民身分條款 | The first sentence of Section 1 of the Fourteenth Amendment. / 第十四修正案第一節的第一句。 |
| Procedural due process | 程序性正當法律程序 | Fair procedures before government deprives a protected interest. / 政府剝奪受保障利益前必須遵循的公平程序。 |
| Substantive due process | 實質性正當法律程序 | Protection for certain fundamental liberty interests against unjustified government interference. / 保障某些基本自由利益免受缺乏正當理由的政府干預。 |
| Compelling interest | 迫切的重要利益 | A very strong government interest used in strict scrutiny. / 嚴格審查中要求政府證明的高度重要利益。 |
| Narrow tailoring | 嚴格限定 | A requirement that a measure be carefully fitted to its asserted objective. / 要求措施與所主張目標之間經過仔細限定。 |
| Precedent | 判例先例 | An earlier judicial decision that may guide or bind later courts. / 可能指引或拘束後來法院的先前判決。 |
| Holding | 裁判要旨 | The legal ruling necessary to decide the case. / 裁決案件所必需的法律結論。 |
| Dicta | 附帶意見 | Statements in an opinion not necessary to the holding. / 判決意見中並非裁判所必需的陳述。 |
